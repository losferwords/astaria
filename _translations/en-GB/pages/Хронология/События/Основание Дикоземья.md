# Foundation of Wildlands

> [!timeline] 677 ChE
> [[Аксель Хан|Axel Khan]] encounters the smith-god [[Хангор|Khangor]] and bargains to build the [[Великая Кузня|Great Forge]] in exchange for a new and terrible power. Wars of conquest begin to secure the necessary resources, and a new realm — [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]] — takes shape across the seized territories.
