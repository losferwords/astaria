# Lake Rashah

![[Assets/Images/Rashah_Lake.jpg]]

## Main text

**Rashah** is the north-eastern Garden of [[Кадир|Qadir]], lying among the dry, stony lands before the [[Горный хребет Шафар|Shafar Mountains]]. Water reaches it through underground veins and small seasonal streams, so the level changes more slowly than one might expect in the desert.

Reddish cliffs and groves of date palms surround its shores. Saffron, onions and medicinal plants able to withstand brackish soil are grown in the lowlands. [[Город Арзумат|Arzumat]] and [[Деревня Орзу|Orzu]] serve caravans bound for the eastern passes, and their yards resemble stout caravanserais more than tranquil garden estates.

Rashah belongs to the lands of [[Умар ибн Ла-Ахад|Umar ibn La-Ahad]]. Pupils of the Way of the Desert come here to study stone, wind and the art of finding water where the surface appears utterly dry. They say that the lake teaches one not to trust a first impression: the most important movement happens underground.

The Garden is known for stone-carvers. Its rock is made into spillways, bowls and memorial slabs that acquire a dark sheen over time. Ancient signs appear on the cliffs after sandstorms; some resemble Qadian script, while others cannot be read.

To travellers, Rashah is the last convenient halt before the mountain road. It is best to hire a guide here: the paths have changed hands many times, and an old border marker may lead not to a pass, but to an abandoned outpost.
