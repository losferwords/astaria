# Hapi River

![[Assets/Images/Hapi_River.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Hapi** is one of the principal rivers of [[Амон-Астат|the Empire of the Sun]]. It rises in [[Озеро Нехмет|Lake Nekhmet]], crosses the [[Пустыня Сехет|Sekhet Desert]] and enters the [[Хтоническое море|Chthonic Sea]] through a broad delta, where the port of [[Город Уасет|Waset]] stands.

The river draws a long green band across the sands. Fields, orchards, quays and temples line its banks, while canals carry water to settlements invisible from the main channel. The annual flood leaves fertile silt behind, and the harvest of much of northern Amon-Astat depends upon its height.

Temple keepers and surveyors watch the level of the Hapi. Marks from former floods are cut into stone supports and the readings sent to the capital. Water that rises too high threatens houses and embankments; water that remains too low promises hunger. The first reports from the upper reaches can therefore change the price of grain before the flood itself has reached the delta.

The banks form a busy highway, and boats carry people and cargo more quickly than caravans. Birds, river predators and smugglers familiar with paths around the customs posts hide among the reed channels. Floodwater sometimes exposes fragments of ancient masonry, beginning an argument between scholars and local families over whether the discovery ought to be disturbed.

In 94 NE, the Hapi began to dry up, marking the beginning of the Great Drought. To the Khefat, the disappearance of a river they had thought eternal was a shock no less profound than the famine itself: if the Hapi could die, then the familiar order of the world no longer seemed everlasting.
