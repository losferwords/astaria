# Chapter 028 — Rukh Canyon

*[[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], 104 NE, spring*

[[Томас Робинсон|Tom's]] abduction left no time for argument. The travellers followed the course taken by the enormous [[Рух|Rukh]], and its trail led into a deep canyon near the [[Горный хребет Шафар|Shafar Mountains]].

The cliffs stood close as the walls of another's fortress. A rockfall came without warning, turning every step into a bargain with the mountain: one more breath in exchange for fortune.

Beyond the canyon, a band of nomads awaited them. [[Урист Китин|Urist]] surrendered to his fury, and enemies expecting weary travellers found not prey, but a storm bearing a hammer. He scattered the attackers, leaving fear and dust behind.

[[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] ran after one camel and witnessed from afar what Avgar wrath became when given free rein. Pursuing another camel, [[Мэй Ву|Mei]] noticed an almost sheer slope. Steps had been cut into the rock, climbing towards a great cave.

The Rukh's nest was closer than they wished. That meant Tom might still be alive.
