# Arno Eklund

![[Assets/Images/Arno_Eklund.jpg]]

**Arno Eklund** is a sixth-generation smith and one of the most respected weaponsmiths in [[Город Атафет|the City of Masters]]. He grew up amid the heat of its foundry yards and first took up a hammer at six. By thirteen, Arno was already helping his father make [[Меч Де'лаим|De'laim swords]] and Sa-Tar hammers for newly chosen [[Имитей|Imithei]], although the senior masters would not allow him to place his own mark upon a weapon for many years.

Eklund bears little resemblance to an inspired artist. He speaks sparingly, refuses to haggle once a price has been agreed and may compel a client to repeat the same movement for weeks before deciding which weapon suits them. He believes that a good blade should preserve the character of its owner rather than compensate for their weaknesses. Arno consequently refuses wealthy patrons more often than poor ones and will never accept a commission motivated solely by a desire to impress.

His most celebrated work is the De'laim sword forged for [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth mac Rayne]]. Arno selected metal capable of enduring a [[Горец|Highlander's]] strength without losing flexibility beneath the force of thunder. The weapon was made during a troubled time when war approached Atafet itself, and Eklund's workshop served at once as forge, shelter and a place where the city's defenders repaired their equipment.

Despite his Avgar origins, Arno considers no country his home: that honour belongs to the mountain. He knows almost every old forge in Atafet by its sound and claims that a single clang—a newly made blade laid upon a stone table—is enough to reveal dishonest workmanship.
