# Wolven

## Main text

The **Wolven** are large wolf-like predators belonging to the [[Первые|Firstborn]], far surpassing ordinary wolves in strength, intelligence and coordination. Encountering a pack is dangerous even for people accustomed to the forest and able to defend themselves.

## The pack and the hunt

Wolven live in packs with complex social bonds and a culture of their own. Their intelligence is evident in their coordinated hunting, ability to learn and knowledge of their hunting grounds. Keen hearing and a sharp sense of smell help them find prey and detect approaching strangers.

Howls, growls, body movements and scents form their complex language, through which they communicate within the pack. Wolven do not understand human speech.

Humans can also become their prey. A pack's attack can be coordinated and deliberate; the strength of individual beasts makes such a hunt far more dangerous than an attack by ordinary wolves. Wolven can also assess a threat and retreat. Hunger, danger and the needs of the pack shape their behaviour.

## Lost hunting grounds

Wolven inhabited many forests and mountain ranges long before human settlements appeared. Human expansion pushes the packs deeper into the forests and higher into the mountains. There it is colder and food is harder to find; hunger and the loss of familiar hunting grounds increase their hostility towards humans.

Encounters with humans usually end in an attack or flight, with danger forcing both sides to choose. There is no trade or customary peaceful coexistence between them.

## Range

Wolven can be found on the [[Хельвандский полуостров|Helvand Peninsula]] and [[Туманный остров|Mist Island]], in [[Лес Иомар|Iomar Forest]], [[Сребролесье|Silverwood]], [[Лес Сольвид|Solvid Forest]], [[Веданский лес|Vedan Forest]] and the [[Хвойничье предгорье|Pinewood Foothills]].

On Mist Island, packs inhabit the northern and western reaches of the [[Дымовая Чаща|Smoke Thicket]] and the [[Призрачные горы|Ghost Mountains]]. Humans are driving them into the deep forest and onto harsh mountain slopes. Their howls can be heard even along the coast, making the inhabitants fear the distant woods.
