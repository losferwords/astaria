# Sao Wu

![[Assets/Images/Sao_Wu.jpg]]

## Main text

**Sao Wu** is the young heir of the Wu family and the daughter of [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]]. She presently lives at the family estate near [[Город Чанг-Ша|Chang-Sha]], under the care of her grandfather [[Шень Ву|Shen Wu]].

Sao appears to be about seven years old, although her appearance does not reflect her true age. She grows far more quickly than a human child and is inquisitive, playful and kind to those around her. Her relatives also remark that she is remarkably clever for her years.

To Sao, the estate is an immense world waiting to be explored. She remembers the names of its workers, watches the birds in the rice fields and slips without permission into places the adults have forbidden her to enter. Family relics and tales of the Wu lineage fascinate her most of all. She asks forthright questions which her elders are accustomed to answering with ceremonial silence.

She shares a warm friendship with Shen Wu. The ancient Avatar teaches his young descendant patience, honest labour and respect for fire, without attempting to decide her fate before its time. Sao, in turn, sees him not as a legendary warrior but as a grandfather who can still be coaxed away from painful memories.

Her mother's long absence remains a source of sorrow, though Sao rarely reveals it to strangers. She gathers little gifts for Mei—drawings, pretty stones and pressed leaves—certain that one day she will be able to give them all to her at once. The Wu family regards Sao's unnatural growth with unease, but the girl herself sees no mystery in it. To her, the world is simply too vast to wait until the adults grant her permission to discover it.
