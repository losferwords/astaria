# Mercate

## Main text

Mercate is the chief goddess of the [[Лудаир|Ludair]] pantheon, mistress of night, secrets and dreams. She is bound to the cold and silence of [[Антра|Antra]], and the people of north-western [[Кельн|Keln]] believe that the mightier the Goddess of Night becomes, the longer and colder the nights grow throughout [[Астария|Astaria]].

Together with [[Тарун|Tarun]] and [[Церунна|Cerunna]], Mercate forms the great triad of the ancient Dair pantheon. Tarun recalls the ungovernable power of the heavens and Cerunna the life within the earth; Mercate keeps all that a person hides even from themselves. Travellers invoke her before a journey by night, sleepers ask her protection from evil dreams, and secret oaths that must never be spoken before witnesses are entrusted to her care.

Unlike many gods of Astaria, Mercate appears to mortals comparatively often and in the fullness of her splendour: a woman in a black gown, with immense dark wings at her back. She does not conceal her divinity behind timid signs, and leaves an impression difficult to mistake for an ordinary dream. At times, however, the goddess hides her wings and walks among humankind. Then she cannot be distinguished from a beautiful northern woman until Mercate herself chooses to reveal the truth.

Although Mercate is patron of Lunaar, she is known and honoured in the lands neighbouring Antra: [[Иомар|Iomar]], [[Вактар-Йорден|Vaktar-Jorden]] and [[Обитель|Obitelj]]. Hunters leave black feathers for her; sentries, the last arrow in the quiver. Those who have endured a grievous loss light a lamp at the window so that the departed soul need not fear the dark.

The goddess's faithful companion is an enormous raven named Morogrim, whose eyes are black as a moonless night. Rumour says that he too is one of the [[Тень|Forgotten]] and understands human speech, though he answers only his mistress. Morogrim's appearance above a house may be a warning, an invitation or a sentence—and no interpreter of dreams would dare say with confidence which one it is.

Mercate created the [[Тень|Shadows]], also called the Forgotten: the [[Имитей|Imithei]] of Lunaar. The centre of her worship is the northern city of the [[Храм Меркаты|Temple of Mercate]]. From there, the [[Культ Меркаты|Cult of Mercate]] keeps ancient vows, interprets dreams and sends its servants wherever a secret of the night has become more dangerous than an enemy in plain sight.
