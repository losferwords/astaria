# Edge of Darkness

![[Assets/Images/Edge_Of_Darkness_East_Side.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Edge of Darkness** is a mountain range in western [[Кельн|Keln]], separating the [[Болота Забвения|Marshes of Oblivion]] from the [[Предгорья Битхорн|Bithorn Foothills]]. Its eastern slopes face the lands of the [[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]], while western roads lead towards [[Лунаар|Lunaar]].

The peaks are seldom wholly free of cloud. Rain and snowmelt feed many streams, yet on the western side the water quickly drains into wet lowlands, while the eastern slopes remain stonier and exposed to the wind. Weather changes so sharply that even a familiar path can disappear beneath fog or scree within hours.

The [[Гойдаир|Goidair]] gave the range its name. They believe the mountains shield the clans from the dark enchantments of [[Мерката|Mercate]], worshipped in Lunaar. Another legend holds that the ridges are the bodies of stone giants struck down by the thunder-god [[Тарун|Tarun]]. In certain cliffs one can indeed discern shoulders, bowed backs and enormous faces—if lightning provides the light.

There are almost no reliable crossings through the northern range. The best known is the [[Перевал Путь Забытых|Pass of the Forgotten]], disputed by neighbouring lands for centuries. Other trails are known to shepherds, smugglers and mountain guides, but found markers should not be trusted: some deliberately lead strangers to a precipice or an old shrine no local will approach after sunset.
