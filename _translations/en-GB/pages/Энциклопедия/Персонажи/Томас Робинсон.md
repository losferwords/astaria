# Thomas Robinson

![[Assets/Images/Tomas_Robinson.jpg]]

**Thomas Robinson** grew up as a hunter in the forests along the [[Река Горсафон|Gorsafon]], where reading tracks, waiting patiently and never losing one's way in darkness mattered more than a famous name. After King [[Бодивир Бомейн|Bodivir Beaumain]] was believed murdered, Thomas became the principal suspect. Lapses in his memory left even Thomas unable to reconstruct what had happened with any certainty, and flight from [[Лунаар|Lunaar]] became his only hope of survival.

The road across the [[Пустыня Сехет|Sekhet Desert]] took him to [[Гилас|Gilas]], where Thomas was enslaved as a gladiator in [[Город Ахей|Achaeus]]. His escape from the arena turned the solitary hunter into a companion to people who would otherwise have had little cause to travel together: the smith [[Урист Китин|Urist Kitin]], the scholar [[Хонсу-Намун|Khonsu-Namun]] and the wanderer [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]]. In time, his woodcraft became more than a weapon for the company. It allowed him to guide refugees through forests, find the missing and recognise danger before it showed its face.

The cause of Thomas's missing memories proved to be a concealed part of his identity that bore the name of his dead brother, [[Мэтью Робинсон|Matthew]]. It had emerged to protect a dying child and accepted a bond with [[Мерката|Mercate]] that Thomas himself had not dared to embrace. While saving the weakened goddess, Tom reconciled his divided memories and became himself for the first time, without a second self within him.

Thomas later died in the depths of the [[Храм Дракона|Temple of the Dragon]], defending [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Lang-An]] from [[Артур Бомейн|Arthur Beaumain]] and [[Кион-Чи|Qiong-Qi]]. Mercate restored him to life at the price of complete devotion: Tom became a [[Тень|Shadow]] of the Goddess of Night. The bond deprived him of his former freedom to resist her commands, but granted him power over darkness and dreams. Through it, he can enter the dreams of others and seek a path to those imprisoned within an unnatural sleep.
