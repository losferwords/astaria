# Chapter 051 — The Assault on Nayan

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 104 NE, winter*

[[Ван Сан|Wan San]] arrived in [[Город Найян|Nayan]] shaken: his mother had been taken to the capital under accusation of treason. He asked [[Урист Китин|Urist]] to prepare the evacuation of the people. The city of forges and smoke must become not a fortress, but a passage to safety.

[[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] attempted a hunting rite to find [[Артур Бомейн|Arthur Beaumain]], but the trail led too far east. The rite already demanded a sacrifice for [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari]], and Gilja went to the prison seeking a condemned criminal. The delay proved fatal: the goddess appeared and lunged at the person nearest Gilja—[[Томас Робинсон|Tom]].

[[Ошу Мати|Oshu]] tried to offer Xin in his place. The plan succeeded, but at a dreadful price. Xin, already saved once, died as a sacrifice to the goddess of blood. The temple physician fled, leaving the guilt behind.

At that moment, [[Аксель Хан|Axel Khan's]] army attacked Nayan. Wan San, Urist and [[Ксан Ляньши|Xian Lianshi]] met him at the walls. Axel offered every Imithei the chance to come out and fight honourably, promising to spare the city. The negotiations collapsed.

Gilja went beyond the walls and tried to scatter his spices, but they had no effect upon Axel. A blow from the axe knocked him from his feet, and the assault began without any remaining illusion of easy deliverance.
