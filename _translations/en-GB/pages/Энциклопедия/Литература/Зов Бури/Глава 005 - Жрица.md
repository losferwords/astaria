# Chapter 005 — The Priestess

*The Priestess*

The alarm keeps ringing over [[Город Ахей|Achaeus]]. As the wounded [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth]] is carried from the arena, rebels from [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]] press towards the city. The defenders are hopelessly outnumbered. [[Персефона Раптис|Persephone]] orders a retreat, and within hours Achaeus falls to people whom hunger drives beyond their own borders.

Kenneth and [[Кассиопея Атанатос|Cassia]] remain prisoners. Captured townspeople and captives from the desert are herded into the old barracks. Among them is a girl who knows how to tend the dying. She sees the gravely wounded Highlander and kneels beside him. Nearby lies Cassia, her hands and feet bound, her eyes and mouth covered. Even here, they fear her more than the others.

The girl's name is [[Шани-Хат-Су|Shani]], a priestess of the Sun from the [[Хефат|Khefat]] people. For several days she helps Kenneth drink, tends his wounds and tries to keep him alive. Gradually he finds the strength to give his name. To Shani he is another person who needs help; she does not yet know the hope she will one day place in him.

On the seventh day, a soldier enters the barracks to count the captives. Shani raises her head and recognises [[Рамат-Хат-Су|Ramat]], her elder brother. He stands among the people who hold her prisoner. The joy of recognition gives way to fear: his sister is alive, but her fate lies in his commander's hands. After a long conversation, Ramat promises to arrange her escape. Shani persuades him to free her new companions too. She cannot reach [[Город Тиза|Tiza]] alone, and she cannot leave Kenneth and Cassia in the barracks.

The following evening, a granary near the barracks catches fire. In the confusion Ramat creates, the three prisoners find their chance to leave. Her brother tells them where to find provisions and camels. The [[Пустыня Сехет|Sekhet Desert]] lies ahead; they still have to survive the journey to the capital.

On the road, Shani begins to tell her story. Dawn breaks over her homeland again. Light spreads across the sand, and the air slowly warms; soon there will be little refuge from the heat. For a priestess, the Sun's return should mean life's triumph. Now each morning brings fear of another day.

The Priestess carries her voice: a prayer to [[Аст|Ast]], the God of Light, whom she continues to serve amid hunger and death. Familiar words praising the Sun mingle with dread of its heat. Shani asks for rain, pleading as though one more life depends on whether she is heard this time.

Her story opens upon a home in the lower reaches of the [[Река Хапи|Hapi River]]. Reeds grow there, her father works beside the water, and two sisters and an elder brother are close by. The family is poor, but childhood still has room for joy. Then the water begins to recede. Shani is twelve, watching the familiar world gradually lose everything that sustains it. In the drought's second year, her mother dies of hunger.

Her father takes the children to the port of [[Город Уасет|Waset]]. He and Ramat put out to sea to fish, while his daughters sell the catch in the market. The city is full of families as hungry as their own. The authorities seize part of the goods; what remains must pay for food, fresh water and shelter. Shani knows exactly what each fish is worth.

Yet one day she gives two fish to a hungry boy and takes no payment. Her sisters are angry: they barely have enough to eat themselves. A passing priest of the Sun overhears the argument. That evening he visits their father and offers the youngest daughter a place at the temple. There she will have a roof, water and food. Her father agrees, though parting with her is painful.

At the temple, Shani learns to care for the sick. Day after day she stays beside those who can still be saved and prepares those beyond help for burial. She scarcely sees her family. Once, from her cell window, she glimpses her father and brother in their fishing boat; on other days she fears recognising a familiar face among the dead brought to the priests.

That fear sounds through the chorus. Shani dares to ask the Sun what it is doing to her people. Lives are lost, crops disappear, and each morning the same dazzling light rises over the country. Reproach escapes her alongside her plea for mercy. After five years of despair, she still turns to her god.

Fewer people around her are willing to pray. The [[Кризис веры в Амон-Астате|rebellion]] spreads: the hungry demand water, bread and answers for the deaths of those they love. Fury turns against the temples and their servants. Shani understands where that anger comes from. She knows what an empty home looks like after famine. Now the people who tend the sick and offer prayers for the dead are being killed as well.

The next journey in her story begins when the priests leave Waset and try to reach Tiza under the protection of [[Рамхотеп II|the pharaoh]]. Near [[Город Хесус|Khesus]], rebels attack the caravan. Almost all the priests die; women and children are taken captive. With them, Shani is marched west behind the rebel army until the walls of Achaeus appear ahead.

In the second verse, the approach of war presses into her prayer. Shani sees despair arm people, strip away their faith and lead them into foreign cities. Those who can scarcely feed their children must now fight. A calamity gathers over her homeland, beyond the reach of the familiar words alone.

Beside her rides the Highlander: the same man she tends in the barracks, helping him endure pain and recover his voice. Now Shani knows he can call upon a storm. In the third verse, her appeal finds a new listener. She entrusts Kenneth with her hope of saving her country, a hope that grows harder to hold while looking into an empty sky.

The final chorus returns to the Sun. Shani still prays to Ast, still asks him to hear her people. And beside her, the man she now dares to ask for help continues on his way.

With great difficulty, the travellers cross Sekhet and reach [[Регион Аментир|Amentir]]. Their paths part in Tiza: Kenneth and Cassia set out for [[Город Атафет|Atafet]] to seek the sword. Shani stays to continue her studies and steps once more across the threshold of a temple of the Sun.
