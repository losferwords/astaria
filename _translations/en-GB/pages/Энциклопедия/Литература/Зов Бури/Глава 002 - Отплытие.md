# Chapter 002 — Setting Sail

*Setting Sail*

Having survived his initiation and become a [[Горец|Highlander]], Kenneth must cross the sea and journey south to [[Гора Атафет|Mount Atafet]], where its weaponsmiths can forge him a special sword: [[Меч Де'лаим|De'laim]]. The blade grants finer control over lightning and serves as a conductor through which it may be summoned.

*98 NE*

[[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth]] arrives in the port of [[Город Солаис|Solais]] and takes passage aboard a [[Талассия|Thalassian]] ship named the *Lithei*. Here the song begins.

The captain, [[Рея Мелит|Rhea Melit]], welcomes a Highlander aboard, for such travellers commonly protect ships from foul weather. Rhea narrates this chapter.

The vessel sails from Solais towards [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]], but meets a storm along the way. Kenneth attempts to drive it off and only makes matters worse.

The ship sinks with all its crew, while the hero is cast ashore upon [[Остров Хтон|Chthon Island]].
