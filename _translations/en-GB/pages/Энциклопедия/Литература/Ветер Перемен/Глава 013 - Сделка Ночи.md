# Chapter 013 — Night's Bargain

*Qadir, 104 NE, winter*

Even after the volcano, the [[Пепельные земли|Ashlands]] did not release the travellers at once. They had found what they sought, but still had to carry the [[Сердце Огня|Heart of Fire]] out of a place where even the stones seemed burnt beyond memory. Victory had not yet become deliverance. It lay before them as a sphere of flame, too hot to hold and too important to abandon among the ashes.

[[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] carried the Heart towards the camp, its heat piercing even the ice-grip salve. Her skin burned, breath faltered, and the world narrowed to white light and pain. Mei tried to recall techniques from childhood: breath, inner stillness, the art of listening to fire rather than resisting it.

Meditation began as a desperate effort not to scream. Then Mei's awareness seemed to contract to the size of the artefact and turn inward. For an instant, she felt not only heat, but the meaning of flame. Her hands caught fire, yet the pain vanished and her skin remained whole. Fire climbed towards her shoulders, and panic nearly shattered the delicate balance, but Mei halted it and drew it back into her hands.

[[Данис ибн Синай|Danis's]] voice from the camp broke the state. Everything returned: the Heart burned once more, and the salve again became her only defence. Yet the experience could not be forgotten. Mei carefully placed the artefact upon a stone and withdrew, seeing both her task and her own path differently.

When [[Урист Китин|Urist]], [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] and Mei returned to camp, they told Danis of the cave, the [[Менада|maenads]], the [[Гнолл|gnolls]] and the dragon felled by [[Рувамба Мати|Ruwamba's]] poison. Danis listened with the hungry attention of a scholar confronted by the impossible. Touching the Heart through the salve, he felt a hard, rough surface—but that was not enough. The artefact demanded study.

The trouble was that carrying it proved nearly impossible. The salve was disappearing too quickly, and their supplies threatened to end before they reached safe country. Through the [[Чёрный Вестник|Black Harbinger]], the travellers contacted [[Хонсу-Намун|Khonsu]]. He asked where they were and fell silent for a long while after hearing the answer.

Almost at once, [[Мерката|Mercate]] appeared nearby.

Only Tom could see her. The Goddess of Night gestured for silence and watched him for a long time without intervening. Later, when the camp sank into nocturnal stillness, she spoke. Yet she called him not Thomas, but Matthew.

The conversation was unsettling. Mercate spoke as though she had known Tom before he could remember himself. She asked of his childhood, his parents, a forgotten name and a death amid the snows of Whitefield Plain. With every question, one thing became clearer: an old bond joined Tom to the Goddess of Night, severed or hidden even from him.

Once she understood that her power over him emerged only at the brink of death, Mercate offered a bargain. She would help the company with the Heart of Fire and give them the [[Корона Душ|Crown of Souls]]. In return, Tom would help her find [[Имитей|Imithei]] and secretly leave them Lunaar coins bearing the triskelion.

While Tom bargained with Night, Urist felt the change within him deepen. The exhilaration that had led him through the volcano gradually faded, but his broken arm seemed stronger than before. In the stillness he began to hear fragments of speech in an ancient language, unknown yet strangely intimate:

> Bi bol ukhliig avchiragch...
> Khamgiin khuchtei zevseg...
> namaig sulla...
> namaig sons...

The phrases grew clearer whenever he held the broken haft. Later, Danis or Khonsu might help him understand them, but one truth was already plain: the fragment of [[Топор Смертоносец|Deathbringer]] was no dead remnant of an ancient weapon.

[[Ошу Мати|Oshu's]] dream also changed that night. Instead of her customary nightmare, she saw a dark forest of withered trees and recognised the country around the temple of [[Руфу|Rufu]]. A vast winged creature circled above it, descending slowly towards the tomb where the God of Death rested.

When the creature landed, Oshu awoke to Danis's piercing screams. The road from the Ashlands continued, but now each of them carried not only their prize, but other voices, bargains and premonitions.
