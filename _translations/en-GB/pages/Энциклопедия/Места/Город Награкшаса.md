# Nagrakshasa

![[Assets/Images/Nagrakshasa_City.png]]

Nagrakshasa is a small town in [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]], closely associated with the [[Тхаги|Thuggee]].

Unlike most [[Раджати|Rajati]] towns, Nagrakshasa is known not for festivals, temple processions or spice markets, but for memory. Here, the Thuggee preserve records of earlier Cycles, gathering chronicles and testimonies that elsewhere in the country would be considered an intrusion upon the dead.

For those seeking knowledge of early [[Раджати|Rajati]] history, forgotten relics or the recurring calamities of [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]], Nagrakshasa can be a dangerous but invaluable destination. Thuggee sages can reveal what the official priesthood does not know, cannot remember or will not speak aloud.
