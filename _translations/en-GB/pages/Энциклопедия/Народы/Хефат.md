# Khefat

![[Assets/Images/Hefat.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Khefat** are an ancient people of central [[Архея|Archaea]] who created [[Амон-Астат|the Empire of the Sun]]. Old traditions name them among the continent's first peoples and trace many younger cultures of Archaea and [[Кельн|Keln]] to them. Across the millennia, the Khefat have survived wars, rivers running dry and changes of dynasty while preserving their language, rites and profound respect for the written memory.

Life amid the deserts taught the Khefat to regard water as the foundation of order. Wells, canals and the shade of fruit trees do not belong solely to the owner of the land; denying a traveller a cup of water is a grave dishonour. Hospitality does not prevent caution. A host will feed a stranger, but listen closely to where they came from and why they travel onwards.

Everyday clothing shields the body from sun and sand. Light fabrics are wrapped in several layers, while head and face are covered when a dry wind rises. Colour, embroidery and jewellery often reveal a native city, craft or temple service. On festival days, white and golden cloth reflects the sunlight until the streets seem an extension of the temple courts.

Faith in [[Аст|Ast]] binds the Khefat more strongly than the rule of any dynasty. The sun signifies divine power, but also clarity of purpose, the warmth of home and the responsibility borne by anyone who holds authority. Priests of [[Хат-Астат|Khat-Astat]] keep calendars, train scribes and aid those in need, while the finest among them become [[Светоносный|Lightbringers]].

The Great Drought and the uprising of the [[Жаждущие|Thirsty]] changed Khefat society painfully. Some saw the disaster as a trial of faith; others, as proof that even sacred institutions must answer for their failures. The Khefat of the present day therefore live between reverence for antiquity and a determination not to let tradition obscure the suffering of the living.
