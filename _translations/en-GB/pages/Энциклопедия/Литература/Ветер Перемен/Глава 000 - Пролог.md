# Chapter 000 — Prologue

[[Томас Робинсон|Thomas Robinson]], an ordinary hunter from [[Лунаар|Lunaar]], falls under suspicion of murdering King [[Бодивир Бомейн|Bodivir Beaumain]] through an absurd and tragic accident. His homeland, shrouded in grief and anger, becomes deadly. Hunted and broken but not yet defeated in spirit, Tom makes the only reasonable choice: he flees across the sea, leaving Lunaar's cold mists behind and sailing into the unknown.

Outwardly, he is calm, restrained, almost without emotion. His face seldom reveals the storm within: doubt, pain and bewilderment. Tom suffers from amnesia; his past slips from him like a dream at dawn. At times he loses consciousness and wakes in unfamiliar places with no memory of how he arrived.

Meanwhile, [[Урист Китин|Urist Kitin]], a young [[Авгарцы|Avgar]] from [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], a harsh realm, seeks a new life away from debt and poverty. He travels far east in search of his second cousin [[Арно Эклунд|Arno Eklund]], hoping to become a true smith. Fate brings him to [[Город Уасет|Waset]], from where he sets out for [[Город Тиза|Tiza]], city of knowledge. Crossing the merciless desert, he meets a northern hunter: Thomas Robinson. Their meeting proves fateful. Together they survive the road and reach Tiza's library.

There Urist asks scholar [[Хонсу-Намун|Khonsu-Namun]], a kind-hearted researcher obsessed with ancient [[Археи|Archaean]] knowledge, for help finding Arno. In return, Urist tells him of mysterious ruins that may conceal a great legacy. Khonsu sets out to find them and unexpectedly meets Tom again—the hunter has made a temporary refuge in the ancient temple.

They try to enter the temple together, but it begins to collapse. They narrowly escape, only to be captured by soldiers of [[Гилас|Gilas]] led by [[Персефона Раптис|Persephone Raptis]]. The travellers are taken to [[Город Ахей|Achaeus]] to be made gladiator slaves.
