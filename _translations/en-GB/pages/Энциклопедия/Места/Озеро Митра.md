# Lake Mitra

![[Assets/Images/Mitra_Lake.jpg]]

## Main text

**Mitra** is a great lake in western [[Архея|Archaea]] and the source of two of [[Гилас|Gilas's]] most important rivers, the [[Река Тавропос|Tavropos]] and the [[Река Неда|Neda]]. Its north-western shores belong to Gilas and its south-eastern shores to [[Амон-Астат|the Empire of the Sun]], so the water both joins and divides the two powers.

Cities, forts and fishing settlements encircle the lake. The harbours of [[Город Ахей|Achaeus]] and [[Город Мистрас|Mystras]] face the domains of [[Город Ксен-Аст|Ksen-Ast]] and [[Город Фибас|Phibas]], while boats cross the border far more often than official envoys do. Fishers recognise one another by their sails and frequently offer aid before remembering beneath which flag their neighbour travels.

Mitra feeds the surrounding lands with fish, tempers the dry climate and serves as a vital trade route. In spring, the rising water floods reed-filled lowlands where birds nest and small boats can hide. In the mist, shore lights are easily mistaken for a foreign harbour, a confusion exploited by smugglers, fugitives and scouts.

The lake has repeatedly become a cause of border conflict. Each side builds watchtowers, searches cargoes and accuses its neighbour of violating old treaties. Yet no one can seal Mitra altogether: too many families, markets and roads depend upon open water.

For a traveller, the lake offers dozens of paths. One may cross the border upon an official ferry, hire a boatman by night or seek the islets where remnants of ancient masonry have been found. Every choice demands a decision about whom to trust: the guards, the figure holding the oar, or the mist concealing the opposite shore.
