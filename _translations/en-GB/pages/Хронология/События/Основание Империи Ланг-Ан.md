# Foundation of the Lang-An Empire

> [!timeline] 2980 ChE
> [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Lang-An]] leads a part of [[Архея|Archaea's]] population east and names them the [[Джу|Dju]]. [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]] arises in [[Долина Гармонии|He-Dji-Gu]], with [[Город Джу-Суо|Dju-Suo]] as its capital.
