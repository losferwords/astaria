# Cassiopeia Athanatos

![[Assets/Images/Cassia.jpg]]

## Main text

**Cassiopeia Athanatos**, more often called **Cassia**, is a [[Профитис|Profitis]] of the [[Хтониды|Chthonid]] people. She was a child during the [[Падение Хтона|Fall of Chthon]] and took no part in the island's destruction. Having survived the catastrophe, Cassia remained among its ruins for many years. Her gift showed her the future with frightening clarity, while solitude gradually convinced her that every destiny had already been decided.

In 98 NE, the shipwrecked [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth mac Rayne]] was cast ashore upon Chthon. Cassia revived him and at first urged him to accept a future in which both were destined to remain upon the island. Yet a simple raft built by Kenneth altered many of her visions at once. To Cassia, it became proof of free will. She left Chthon with the Highlander and set out to meet the unknown.

Their path led through captivity in [[Гилас|Gilas]], an averted [[Танатомахия|Thanatomachia]], occupied [[Город Ахей|Achaeus]] and a desert they crossed with the aid of [[Шани-Хат-Су|Shani-Hat-Su]]. In [[Город Атафет|Atafet]], Cassia tended victims of the Great Drought and helped Kenneth raise the money required for a [[Меч Де'лаим|De'laim sword]]. Upon the mountain summit, she foresaw that calling the rain would end in his death. Unable either to stop the rite or condemn thousands of people, she removed her own presence from the vision and departed, hoping thereby to change its fatal course.

When Kenneth appeared in none of her later prophecies, Cassia believed him dead and travelled to [[Деревня Эрвин|Erwin]] to tell [[Лисандра мак Рейн|Lisandra mac Rayne]] of her son's sacrifice. She then vanished once more. These events became the heart of the saga [[Зов Бури|*Call of Thunder*]].

In 104 NE, Cassia appeared in [[Город Солаис|Solais]] during one of the darkest passages of her life. [[Эйдан мак Кинсли|Aidan mac Kinsley]] saved her from death at sea, after which the prophetess found herself aboard the *Atron*. During a storm she helped Aidan perceive another path, but [[Аристея Кронос|Aristea Kronos]] soon cast her overboard while taking the ship. Cassia survived and later met the travellers again in [[Город Бахара|Bahara]], where her visions helped them recognise a danger and find the creature that had stolen the [[Глаз Калипсо|Eye of Calypso]]. Her story thus continued in the chronicle [[Пока Боги Спят|*While Gods Sleep*]]: Cassia still beholds possible futures, but no longer considers any one of them inevitable.
