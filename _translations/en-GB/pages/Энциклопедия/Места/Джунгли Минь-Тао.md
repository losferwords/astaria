# Min-Tao Jungle

![[Assets/Images/Min-Tao_Jungle.jpg]]

**Min-Tao** is a dense jungle in the south of [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]], warm and humid throughout the year. Great rivers and lakes are rare here, but underground springs and moisture saturate the earth, making its vegetation exceptionally thick.

Tree branches form a dense green canopy that hides the sun, while constant mists lend the forest a mysterious air. Min-Tao is rich in rare plants: spices, medicinal roots and timber prized by [[Джу|Dju]] craftspeople. It is also a natural home to beasts respected and feared by nearby settlements. [[Деревня Тай-Линь|Tai-Lin]], on its edge, bases much of its trade and craft on the jungle's wealth.

There is no permanent road through Min-Tao. Trails vanish beneath growth in a season, and after rain an underground spring can rise and turn dry low ground into marsh. Guides mark the way with cuts in bark and woven ribbons, but a stranger struggles to distinguish a direction from a warning not to go farther.

Stone platforms and remnants of small shrines survive in the depths. Some belonged to early Dju settlements; others resemble no imperial architecture. Ceramics, metal masks and vessels still scented with unfamiliar herbs are found beneath the roots. Thus Min-Tao attracts not only gatherers, but those willing to risk themselves for a forgotten recipe or history.
