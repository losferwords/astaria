# Lightbringer

## Main text

Lightbringers, known in the Khefat language as the **Sau**, are the highest priests of [[Амон-Астат|the Empire of the Sun]] and chosen servants of [[Аст|Ast]]. The path opens only after many years of service, prayer, healing and discipline in the use of solar power. At initiation, light ceases to be merely an external force: it begins to radiate from the priest's own body, warming those overcome by cold and driving back the darkness.

## Training

The Sau excel above all in healing and protection. They close wounds with the warmth of their hands, sustain exhausted companions and create a radiance in which allies find it easier to withstand terror. Upon the battlefield, a Lightbringer seldom seeks the first blow. Their duty is to preserve the company and keep darkness or despair from destroying it within.

Mercy does not leave the Sau defenceless. Solar power blinds an enemy, scatters mist and fills the stroke of a heavy hammer with the heat of noon. To the people of Amon-Astat, it is vital that a Lightbringer distinguish protection from vengeance: anyone who bears the light of Ast must remember why that power is being used.

## Attributes

The ceremonial weapon of a Lightbringer is the [[Молот Са-Тар|Sa-Tar hammer]], fashioned for its bearer by the smiths of [[Гора Атафет|Mount Atafet]]. Its broad face catches the sunlight, while its weight demands the same discipline from the body that prayer requires of the spirit. In times of peace, the Sau are known by their pale robes, emblems of the sun and habit of appearing wherever sickness, hunger or fear has already led people to abandon hope.
