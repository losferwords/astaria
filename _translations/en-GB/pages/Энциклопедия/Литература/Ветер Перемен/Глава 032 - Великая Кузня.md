# Chapter 032 — The Great Forge

*[[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], 104 NE, autumn*

At the [[Великая Кузня|Great Forge]], the travellers encountered not a gate, but a riddle: “DISK YUGLE”, or “gyug e zhiksh”. Solving it, they entered and met [[Хангор|Khangor]].

The Smith-God spoke of [[Рея Мелит|Rhea]], who had brought him plans for a voltage stabiliser. The device might lessen the reactor's pain, but Rhea hoped for more: to awaken [[Калипсо|Calypso]]. The difficulty lay in an impossible vessel. Nothing could contain so much energy and carry it to [[Талассия|Thalassia]].

Rhea left in anger to seek a means of bringing Calypso herself to the Great Forge. Khangor could not abandon his place.

He then asked to examine the [[Сердце Огня|Heart of Fire]]. The artefact was set upon a pedestal, and the Forge began to pour power into it. The Heart opened not as a mechanism or vessel, but as an egg. A dragon hatched from within. Mei named him [[Мэйлун|Meilong]].

To [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]], this was more than a miracle. It was a new responsibility—living and fragile, with breath, eyes and a fate that others had already tried to exploit.

Khangor appointed [[Урист Китин|Urist]] his herald and gave him a buckle. They must now travel to Hamoa and seek a way to carry Calypso across the Dawn Sea instead of the Chthonic Sea.

The company returned to [[Город Даркхан|Darkhan]] to collect the gold and tell the khan of their meeting with Khangor. Then the road turned again towards [[Город Бахара|Bahara]], and from there farther south.
