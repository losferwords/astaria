# Chapter 087 — A New Shadow

*[[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]], 106 NE, spring*

While Shubhankari endured her vision within the pyramid, [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] and [[Селина Эллиот|Selina]] summoned [[Мерката|Mercate]]. The Goddess of Night heard them and ordered them to leave the cavern of the pyramid and never return. It was a temple of the [[Археи|Archaeans]], near-mythical ancient beings who had left behind their ruins—and, as it transpired, the gods themselves.

Selina asked to be made a [[Тень|Shadow]] once more. Mercate warned her that the old rite would not work as well upon one who had already died. Their bond would be weaker, and Selina would have to act upon her own judgement. She agreed. After the rite, Selina fell into a profound sleep. Tom believed his ability to walk through dreams might help and, with [[Мэйлун|Meilong's]] aid, drew her back. Thus Mercate gained a new Shadow.

Returning to the cavern, Tom witnessed the end of Shubhankari's fit. Now conscious, the goddess wept and concluded that her extra arms truly made her too much like the nagas. Her friends comforted her and even reattached the arm she had lost. Thereafter the Goddess of Blood cast away her raktakatars and declared that she had shed enough blood.

The nagas' striving for civilisation had affected her more deeply than she wished to admit. If they could become better, perhaps she could try as well.

Yet the Cycle of Destruction would not end by itself. Blood taken unlawfully had to be returned, and as many [[Ракша|Rakshas]] as possible must therefore gather at the [[Кузница Плоти|Flesh Forge]]. The goddess charged the heroes with persuading or compelling her priests, while she resolved to become better acquainted with her new, tailed congregation.

The travellers took the divine weapons to lend weight to their words and returned to [[Деревня Бадракали|Badrakali]] with [[Шаша|Shasha]] as an ambassador. The village was burning. Their reckonings had to wait while they fought the fire and saved [[Шанкар Марджари|Shankar Marjari's]] tavern.

After the blaze, the renegade priest appeared. He knew Shasha and begged her to flee before the villagers killed her. Their conversation revealed that he had not stolen the blood; rather, the “harvests” had served as cover for conveying people into the naga caves. The travellers decided that he would persuade the elder to receive Shasha as an ambassador.

After sleeping, the heroes discovered a new calamity: several villagers had died without any visible sign of violence.
