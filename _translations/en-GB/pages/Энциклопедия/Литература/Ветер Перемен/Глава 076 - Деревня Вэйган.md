# Chapter 076 — Veigan

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 105 NE, autumn*

The road divided in the [[Джунгли Минь-Тао|Min-Tao Jungle]]. A broad path led towards the sea, while a narrow and overgrown trail disappeared into the forest's depths. [[Урист Китин|Urist]] and [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] chose the broad road, but [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] felt a summons from the hills. Once again, he heard the cry of [[Мерката|Mercate's]] raven.

The group remained in camp while Tom went on alone. Along the way he found the bloodless body of a Rajati, but continued towards a solitary tree bathed in moonlight. Mercate awaited him there. She told him that a village farther along the trail must be saved. The night ended in the intimacy Tom had long desired.

At the camp, [[Велимир Сажин|Velimir]] told Urist and Gilja about the black marks upon their wrists. He could help free them from the curse, but in return asked for their aid in finding the black crystal. The difficulty was that removing the mark required them to find [[Вигдис Меткая|Vigdis the Unerring]], though Velimir hoped the crystal might offer another path.

Noticing movement in the jungle, Gilja hurled a stone and struck a predator. The creature fled along the narrow trail, and the travellers followed. On the way, Gilja found the drained body of a Rajati and recognised from his clothing one of the diplomats they had been pursuing. Amongst his possessions lay the mark of the [[Тхаги|Thuggee]] upon a scrap of human skin.

There was no life in [[Деревня Вэйган|Veigan]]. Beside a waterfall they found a cave whose entrance was blocked by the body of an old man, his back torn open. Two children hid within. They said unknown predators had carried away the villagers.

The bearers were sent back to Tai-Lin with the children, while the travellers followed the tracks. They found flayed bodies in the treetops. Gilja noticed notches and claw marks and urged everyone to leave.

The rustling in the undergrowth heralded not an attack, but the arrival of old acquaintances: [[Рон Эллиот|Ron Elliot]] and [[Бао Си|Bao Xi]]. Ron explained that Batar Sag had offered a bounty for the heads of the southern creatures and charged him with finding [[Айсан Баят|Aysan Bayat]]. Bao accompanied him because he was the only person Ron knew in the capital.
