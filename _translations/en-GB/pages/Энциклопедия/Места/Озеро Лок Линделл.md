# Loch Lindell

![[Assets/Images/Loch_Lindell_Lake.jpg]]

## Main text

**Loch Lindell** is a deep freshwater lake in western [[Лес Иомар|Iomar Forest]]. Its dark water appears almost black in rain, but on clear days turns transparent by the stony shores. Morning mist lingers longer here than in the surrounding valleys, hiding boats and low islands until noon.

Its banks support the fishing families of [[Деревня Каэлла|Kaella]] and the craftspeople of [[Город Грейлок|Greylok]]. Lindell is home to a long, silvery trout that can leap briefly from the water after swarms of midges. Its scales darken immediately after death, making truly fresh fish easy to distinguish from a catch merchants have carried for days.

Local boatmen do not cross the lake's middle during a storm. They say thunder returns from the water not as an echo, but as a stranger's reply, after which fog conceals even the best-known shores. Old sages connect this to the lake's stone bed, awakened by the voice of [[Тарун|Tarun]], though they do not present conjecture as truth. Before a long crossing, families tie a rowan twig to a boat's prow and leave the lake their first catch.

To a traveller, Loch Lindell seems a quiet route through western Iomar, but fog can turn a short crossing into wandering. Experienced guides read their course from waves, wind and birdcalls, for birds always return to land before a person can make it out. This knowledge passes by the hearth from one generation to the next, and no Goidair calls it less reliable merely because it was not written in a book.
