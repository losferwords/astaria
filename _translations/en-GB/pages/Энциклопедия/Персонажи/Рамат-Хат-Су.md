# Ramat-Hat-Su

![[Assets/Images/Ramat-Hat-Su.jpg]]

## Main text

**Ramat-Hat-Su** is the elder brother of [[Шани-Хат-Су|Shani-Hat-Su]] and a prominent leader of [[Жаждущие|the Thirsty]]. He grew up in [[Город Хесус|Khesus]], where the price of water and the consequences of decisions made in the distant capital were always felt more keenly than the splendour of the solar temples.

During the Great Drought, Ramat joined the uprising against [[Хат-Астат|Hat-Astat]]. To him, the Thirsty were not enemies of the light, but people demanding an answer for the hunger and deaths of those whom relief had failed to reach. The movement's radicalisation drew him into the [[Вторжение Амон-Астата в город Ахей|invasion of Achaeus]], where a struggle for justice rapidly became the occupation of another people's city.

In Achaeus, Ramat unexpectedly discovered his own sister among the captives. Defying his commanders, he helped Shani escape with [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth mac Rayne]] and [[Кассиопея Атанатос|Cassia Athanatos]]. The choice revealed the limit of his loyalty: no cause and no command could make him sacrifice those he loved for a convenient image of victory.

After the war, Ramat remained among the leaders of the Thirsty. He undertakes dangerous missions, protects vulnerable communities and strives to prevent the movement from becoming an army that speaks on behalf of the hungry without listening to them. His sincere care for freedom is inseparable from the heavy memory of what righteous anger can become when it is left without restraint.
