# Avgars

![[Assets/Images/Avgars.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Avgars** are a people of smiths, travellers and wandering clans inhabiting central [[Архея|Archaea]]. They descend from the northern [[Вактары|Vaktars]], but over the centuries mingled with the inhabitants of conquered lands and formed the distinct culture of [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]]. Neighbours who survived the early campaigns came to call them savages; the Avgars themselves see their people first and foremost as people of free will and personal responsibility.

In 678 ChE, [[Аксель Хан|Axel Khan]] travelled south in search of glory and new lands. His company's meeting with [[Хангор|Khangor]], god of craft, and the wars that followed against the [[Джу|Dju]] and [[Кадийцы|Qadians]] transformed the expedition into a campaign of conquest. Followers of the Iron God built the [[Великая Кузня|Great Forge]], raised settlements and welcomed people of many nations into their families. In this way, a former warband became a new society.

The clan remains the foundation of Avgar life. It may wander as an arbat, own an urban workshop or control a river landing, but it must support its members and answer for their deeds. A person is permitted much so long as they are prepared to name their motive openly, repair the harm they caused or accept a challenge from the injured party. Hiding one's guilt behind another's command is considered a sign of weakness.

The Avgars greatly value physical strength, but never reduce it to skill in battle. An enduring herder, a craftsperson able to repair a broken wheel upon the steppe, and a woman who keeps a household standing through a long campaign earn no less respect than a warrior. Horses serve for long journeys, carrying burdens and war, yet most Avgars are accustomed to travelling on foot. Children learn early to care for animals, pitch a camp and handle tools. Riding remains a useful skill rather than the centre of their culture.

Smithcraft is treated with something close to religious reverence. A good weapon must not merely look formidable, but outlive its owner; poor workmanship disgraces the maker's entire clan. Avgar markets therefore ring with arguments over metal, tempering and the balance of a blade, and a bargain often ends with the goods being tested before the eyes of the buyers.

The path to the [[Великая Кузня|Great Forge]] holds a special place in Avgar culture. Unrestricted entry is forbidden, yet the bravest and worthiest travel to its gates to face [[Хангор|Khangor's]] trials. One who reaches the interior, proves strength, endurance and craftsmanship, and earns the Smith-God's support may become a [[Мектиг|Mektig]]. Most return home before completing the path, but failure brings no shame if the seeker honestly acknowledged the limits of their strength.

A guest is met with hearty food and direct questions. The Avgars may seem blunt, but seldom conceal their feelings behind ceremony. Trust is won through deeds: sharing hard labour, keeping a promise and refusing to flee responsibility matter more here than an illustrious birth.
