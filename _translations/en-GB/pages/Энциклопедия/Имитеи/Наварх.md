# Navarch

## Main text

Navarchs are the greatest captains of [[Талассия|Thalassia]], chosen by [[Калипсо|Calypso]], goddess of adventure. They steer beyond the borders of trustworthy charts, decide a course in the heart of a storm and turn an unfamiliar current, a torn sail or an enemy's error into a path to victory.

No royal seal alone can bestow the title. A would-be Navarch must earn the confidence of a crew, prove themselves upon a distant voyage and devise an improvement of their own for a ship or weapon. Calypso values not reckless courage, but the mind that finds possibility where others perceive only danger.

## Training

A Navarch studies navigation, shipbuilding, command and the handling of gunpowder. They read the sea by stars and wind, yet may also employ a rare compass or an instrument of their own design. In battle, these captains rely upon movement, exact calculation and prepared stratagems: smoke, ropes, a sudden change of course and a blow against the weakest point of a structure.

Every Navarch searches for some trace of the vanished Calypso. To one it may be an ancient mechanism, to another an unknown island or the testimony of a sailor who survived the impossible. Service to the goddess thus makes explorers of them, and any voyage may become the beginning of a discovery capable of changing all Thalassia.

## Attributes

The traditional arms of a Navarch combine a kopis for boarding actions with a rare gunpowder pistol. Each captain adapts both weapons to a personal style of combat. Their surfaces often bear marks for seas crossed, ships commanded and the adventure upon which Calypso first acknowledged the bearer.
