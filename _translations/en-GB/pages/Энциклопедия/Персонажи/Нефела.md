# Nephele

![[Assets/Images/Nefela.jpg]]

## Main text

**Nephele** is the acting ruler of [[Город Бахара|Bahara]], having assumed the place of the city's khan after his unexplained disappearance. [[Батар Саг|Batar Sag]] had previously appointed her as an adviser to settle disputes between the [[Авгарцы|Avgars]] and the [[Талассийцы|Thalassians]] blockading the port.

Nephele quickly understood that Bahara could not be governed like an ordinary Avgar settlement. Maritime treaties, clan debts, the interests of smugglers and the habit of settling disputes by force all meet within the city. She prefers to bring her opponents to one table, knowing beforehand which concession each of them can accept without losing face.

In the spring of 104 NE, the khan vanished without trace, provoking panic and unrest. As his closest adviser, Nephele assumed command, kept the port from descending into an open struggle for power and promised to find those responsible. The word *acting* in her title reassures the old clans, but the longer the search continues, the more often the people address her as their true ruler.

Nephele's origins remain unknown. She gives no family name, speaks rarely of her childhood and appears equally at ease among Thalassian captains and Avgar warriors. These qualities have inspired dozens of rumours, from a secret kinship with a seafaring house to former service at the court of a distant land. Nephele does not dispute a theory when believing it might tempt another person to say too much.

Outwardly, she remains composed even when threatened, yet she possesses none of a Mektig's might and understands her own vulnerability well. Nephele therefore relies upon intelligence, agreements and carefully chosen agents. To Bahara, she has become a rare kind of ruler: one whose greatest weapon is the ability to convince people on opposing sides that peace still serves their interests.
