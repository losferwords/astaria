# Paragon

## Main text

Paragon is the highest military rank in [[Гилас|Gilas]] and the living embodiment of the [[Эллийцы|Ellian]] ideal of heroism. The road to the title begins long before a warrior's first true battle. Years of drill, competition and service teach mastery not only of weapons, but of fear. Few earn the right to be called a Paragon: only those who remain a pillar for their companions when the battle line itself is on the verge of flight.

## Training

Paragons build their style upon flawless defence, patiently compelling an opponent to expose a weakness. To them, a shield matters no less than a spear: it holds a passage, shelters a companion, breaks an enemy's balance and preserves the line against superior numbers. A Paragon's gifts heighten endurance and instinct, revealing the fault in a formation and allowing allies to be directed with a single terse command.

Most Paragons command armies, yet the most perilous duties often fall to them alone. Such a warrior must stand with equal assurance before a host of soldiers, upon an arena floor and in a narrow passage where several lives depend upon one decision.

## Structure

The final rite of initiation is the [[Танатомахия|Thanatomachia]], a mortal duel against a captive Imithei. The victor claims the power of the defeated warrior's [[Кристалл Души|Soul Crystal]] and leaves the arena no longer wholly human. To the Ellians, the rite proves that the greatest power must be earned face to face; to foreigners, it remains among the cruellest customs of Gilas.

## Attributes

A long spear and heavy shield are the unmistakable attributes of a Paragon. The shield commonly bears the signs of its owner's city, former unit and the battles through which the right to lead was won. To cast it aside through cowardice is considered a far greater disgrace than any wound.
