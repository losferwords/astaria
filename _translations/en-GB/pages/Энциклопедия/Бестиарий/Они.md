# Oni

## Main text

**Oni** (鬼) is an [[Эдзо|Edzo]] word commonly translated into the shared tongue as “demon”. It refers to people altered by a mysterious possession.

The possessed gradually lose the ability to distinguish their own memories and feelings from intrusive voices. They become anxious and suspicious, perceive threats in those close to them and may fall into uncontrollable rage. Their eyes often redden, while their behaviour grows so unpredictable that the people of a settlement begin to see a former neighbour as a true monster. Some Oni are consumed by a thirst for violence, others by paranoia or all-encompassing fear.

The people of [[Амато|Amato]] try not to kill the possessed, especially while they can still recognise a relative or friend. Instead they summon priests of [[Ицунэ|Itsune]] or an [[Онмёдзи|Onmyoji]] capable of performing a rite of cleansing. To some Edzo, the Onmyoji are saviours and demon hunters; to others, they stand dangerously close to the very beings they pursue.
