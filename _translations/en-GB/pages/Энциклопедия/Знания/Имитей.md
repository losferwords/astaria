# Imithei

![[Assets/Images/imithei.jpg]]

**Imithei** is the name given to the most extraordinary people of the many nations of [[Астария|Astaria]]. Every culture seeks in its own way to comprehend the order of the universe, anatomy and history, and each has shaped its own religions. The paths by which Imithei attain their power therefore differ greatly. Yet beneath every distinction of faith and culture, all Imithei share one trait: a [[Кристалл Души|Soul Crystal]] embedded within the brain. This is believed to be the source of their superhuman abilities.

The journey from an ordinary person to the full might of an Imithei may be divided into five ranks:

1. **Seeker** (rank 0) — a person may spontaneously display one of the gifts associated with the Imithei of their culture. They cannot control it, and it emerges only in extremity. Society does not yet regard such a person as an Imithei. A [[Гойдаир|Goidair]] born during a thunderstorm, for example, will grow and gather strength upon the road towards a remarkable future.

2. **Initiate** (rank 1) — a person has realised that they can call upon their potential when the need arises and has begun to use it deliberately. Their gifts commonly appear as feats of consummate skill, exceptional concentration or demanding combat techniques, performed without injury to themselves. Society expects such heroes to undergo the training and study required for a formal rite. Among the Goidair, all those born beneath thunder prepare to become the Named and face the [[Рунштоирм|Runstoirm]].

3. **Chosen** (rank 2) — a person who has passed through a rite of initiation as an Imithei, such as the [[Танатомахия|Thanatomachia]] or Runstoirm, and thereby gained greater power. The rite confirms their status before society, which in turn expects them to fulfil the duties of that status: taking part in conflicts, undertaking secret operations or conducting affairs of state. An Imithei of this rank can already perform acts most people would call miraculous—commanding the weather, moving with supernatural speed, closing wounds and much more. One example is a newly initiated [[Горец|Highlander]] of the Goidair, setting out upon a pilgrimage to obtain a [[Меч Де'лаим|De'laim Sword]].

4. **Hero** (rank 3) — an Imithei who has long played a part in the events that shape Astaria. Such figures commonly lead organisations, armies or settlements. Society obeys their words and deeds without question, and often fears them a little. A Hero can change the course of a battle alone through feats and spells of extraordinary difficulty: raising a sandstorm, calling a meteor from the heavens or loosing a rain of arrows. One example is a seasoned Highlander who has long held the [[Культ Меркаты|Cult of Mercate]] at bay in the [[Перевал Путь Забытых|Pass of the Forgotten]].

5. **Legend** (rank 4) — the name speaks for itself. These Imithei have entered history, and their names are known far beyond their native lands. They may rule a state or command its armies, and often commune directly with the gods of Astaria. Their wisdom, might and authority make them appear godlike in the eyes of ordinary people; their very presence alters those around them. A legendary Imithei can overcome an army alone, hold back the approach of old age, kindle a fire with a snap of the fingers or restore a mortally wounded companion to life. Examples include [[Индульф мак Торн|Indulf mac Thorn]], leader of the [[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]], and [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth mac Rayne]], the legendary Highlander of the saga [[Зов Бури|*Call of Thunder*]].
