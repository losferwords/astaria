# Foundation of Obitelj

> [!timeline] 1236 ChE
> The first Beregini made peace with [[Велисса|Velissa]] beside the [[Река Красивая|Krasivaya River]], after which the settlers founded [[Обитель|Obitelj]] and [[Город Мирград|Mirgrad]].

According to Wendish chronicles, Vaktar women came to take revenge upon the goddess for families broken by her unwitting enchantment. The encounter became the first conversation between Velissa and mortals. The women became her priestesses and intermediaries, while the goddess placed the settlers under her protection. Rich harvests, protective runes and a new devotion to love and fidelity marked the beginning of a new people: the [[Венды|Wends]].
