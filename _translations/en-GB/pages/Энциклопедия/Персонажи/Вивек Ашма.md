# Vivek Ashma

![[Assets/Images/Vivek_Ashma.png]]

Vivek Ashma is a young [[Раджати|Rajati]] from [[Город Ванпур|Vanpur]], son of the jeweller [[Джевахар Ашма|Djevahar Ashma]] and his wife, [[Шанта Ашма|Shanta]]. He grew up in the house behind the family shop, surrounded by the chime of small ornaments, talk of gemstone prices and the scent of spiced tea his mother served to visitors.

Vivek is slender, dark-haired and quick on his feet. In the familiar bustle of the market, he easily finds his way between the stalls and remembers which turns shoppers and messengers take. His knowledge of the trading rows makes him useful whenever a message needs delivering quickly or someone must be found from a few identifying details.

The family name ties him to his father's craft: many local people know the Ashma hallmark and the shop where his parents work. To Shanta and Djevahar, their son remains part of the household even after he stopped appearing at the counter. His absence disrupted the family's familiar routine and drove his father to search beyond his usual trading connections.

By the autumn of 106 NE, Vivek had left the family home. His exact whereabouts remain unknown.
