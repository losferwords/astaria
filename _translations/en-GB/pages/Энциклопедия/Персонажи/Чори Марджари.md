# Chori Marjari

![[Assets/Images/Chori_Mardjari.jpg]]

**Chori Marjari** is a young [[Раджати|Rajati]] woman from [[Деревня Бадракали|Badrakali]], the younger daughter of the local innkeeper [[Шанкар Марджари|Shankar Marjari]] and sister to [[Мерави Марджари|Meravi]]. From an early age, she understood that honest labour seldom saves the poor quickly enough. She therefore learnt to live between the marketplace, other people's pockets and those narrow passages of the city which guards enter only with the greatest reluctance.

Meravi is seven years older than Chori. As a child, the younger sister followed her in admiration, but with time began to envy her curiosity, easy confidence and many talents. Meravi charmed the inn's guests without effort and earned the right to become a [[Ракша|Raksha]], while Chori increasingly felt that their father saw her only as a child in need of protection. Her sister's departure for [[Город Танассар|Tanassar]] left an unspoken hurt between them: Chori is angry with Meravi precisely because she still longs for her love and recognition.

For many years, Chori lived in [[Город Ванпур|Vanpur]] and came to know its poorer quarters intimately: the laundries, porters' yards, rooftops above the market, illicit money-changers and child messengers who carry news faster than any city watch. To an outsider, she seems no more than a deft thief. Among the people of the streets, however, she is known as one of their own—bold, quick and far too stubborn to abandon someone she loves.

When Shankar fell gravely ill and a healer said that he had only months to live, Chori tried to buy him time at any cost. Hearing of the ‘black injections’ that could cast a body into a profound sleep without ageing, she stole a dangerous stone dagger from those distributing the treatment through Vanpur and the surrounding country. Chori used the dagger upon her father in the hope of saving him from imminent death, but in doing so made herself a target for the watch, the criminal underworld and everyone determined to conceal the trade in black injections.

Later, in Vanpur, she stole several of [[Мерката|Mercate's]] feathers from [[Томас Робинсон|Thomas Robinson]]. Chori did not intend to sell them as holy relics: she had heard that the nagas collected such feathers and hoped to exchange them for knowledge—either a way to help Shankar or some understanding of the sleep into which he had been cast. Both the city watch and the distributors of the black injections now hunt her, but her network of child messengers and command of the poorer quarters make Chori all but impossible to catch by ordinary pursuit.
