# Khalisat Desert

![[Assets/Images/Khalisat_Desert.jpg]]

## Main text

**Khalisat** is the vast desert of central [[Архея|Archaea]], stretching from [[Залив Фиальсахра|Fialsakhra Bay]] to the [[Горный хребет Шафар|Shafar Mountains]]. It makes up most of [[Кадир|Qadir]], yet it is misleading to picture it as an endless sea of identical sand.

Khalisat contains high dunes, stony plateaux, salt flats, dry riverbeds and grassy lowlands around water. Its sands vary in colour and density: the western wind carries pale dust, while reddish and almost black stretches appear nearer the mountains. The [[Кадийцы|Qadians]] know dozens of local names, but understand the desert as one living whole.

Within it lie the seven great lakes known as the Gardens of Qadir: [[Озеро Кхарим|Kharim]], [[Озеро Джамсар|Jamsar]], [[Озеро Шаради|Sharadi]], [[Озеро Зураиф|Zuraif]], [[Озеро Суэйд|Suwayd]], [[Озеро Рашах|Rashah]] and [[Озеро Альсалим|Alsalim]].

Caravan roads run between the Gardens. Stone pillars, wells and caravanserais mark them, but a storm may bury every landmark within hours. Experienced guides watch the stars, the wind, the shape of dunes and the behaviour of animals. They always carry more water than calculations demand: in Khalisat, a delay of one day may decide an entire party's fate.

[[Аль-Тахим|Al-Tahims]], [[Гарпия|harpies]], [[Гнолл|gnolls]] and [[Менада|maenads]] inhabit the desert. Most avoid busy roads, but hunger, heat or a change in watercourses can drive them towards settlements. Dry riverbeds are particularly dangerous, for a predator may remain unseen there until the final moment.

To the Qadians, Khalisat is neither hostile nor merciful. It punishes overconfidence, preserves traces of vanished cities and reminds its people each year that the Gardens survive only through common labour. A traveller may find rare minerals, ancient roads and a silence in which it is easy to hear one's own fears—yet not always to distinguish them from a true warning.
