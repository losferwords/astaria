# Tuanuku Peninsula

![[Assets/Images/Tuanuku_Peninsula.jpg]]

**Tuanuku** is a great green peninsula in eastern [[Архея|Archaea]], washed by the [[Рассветное море|Dawn Sea]]. [[Перешеек Мост Хранителей|Warden Bridge]] joins it to the mainland, while sleeping [[Вулкан Ротонуи|Rotonui Volcano]] rises above its central forests.

Ancient eruptions formed its stony spine and fertile slopes. Rivers flow from Rotonui towards Hamoa's gardens and villages; the coast alternates between sandy bays, black volcanic cliffs and mangroves where a boat can shelter from waves but easily lose its way among roots.

To the [[Манаи|Manai]], Tuanuku means ‘Mother Earth’: not a claim of ownership, but gratitude to the place that received the Wanderers after a long sea voyage. Before clearing a new garden, a family leaves gifts to water and earth and promises to restore the plot's fertility when it is exhausted.

Most travel is by water. Boat routes, river paths and forest crossings join the villages, though rain can erase the latter within hours. Local guides navigate by Rotonui's slopes, currents and birdsong. A foreigner attempting the forest alone often returns to the same stream from its opposite bank.

The western border near Warden Bridge remains uneasy because of [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]]. Deeper inland, danger takes another form: awakening hot springs, poisonous plants and old lava caves remind people that Hamoa's peaceful ground grew from a power that merely seems asleep.
