# Chapter 009 — Lament of the Night (Epilogue)

*Lament of the Night*

Spring 100 NE. The storm retreats from [[Гора Атафет|Mount Atafet]], and night settles over its snowbound slopes. In the silence comes the voice of [[Мерката|Mercate]], her lament addressed to [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth the Stormcaller]]. Below, rain restores life to the parched land, but the fate of the man who summons it remains hidden from the world.

Later, after the saga's publication, travellers from across [[Астария|Astaria]] journey to the summit to honour the [[Горец|Highlander]]. Yet none finds Kenneth's body or his sword. A few claim that at night they see an immense wandering shadow amongst the snowbound slopes, as though Night herself comes to mourn the great hero…
