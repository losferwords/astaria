# Theonia

![[Assets/Images/Theonia_Village.jpg]]

## Main text

**Theonia** is a fishing village on the north-eastern shore of [[Озеро Митра|Lake Mitra]]. Its boats put out before dawn, while its smokehouses and salting sheds work through most of the year. Much of the catch is sent to the nearby garrison guarding the eastern frontier of [[Гилас|Gilas]].

The fort is far larger than one would expect beside a peaceful settlement. A stone citadel, earthen rampart and palisade allow it to receive substantial reinforcements when the border is threatened. The village consequently lives by an unusual rhythm: drill commands, supply trains and the changing of the watch regularly disturb the quiet of the fishers' yards.

The people of Theonia know how to profit from the army's presence, but do not allow it to define their lives altogether. They weave exceptionally strong lake nets, repair light boats and dry herbs gathered from the shallows. Elder fishers know where the mist hides a passage between the rocks, and sometimes guide across the lake those who would rather not encounter an official patrol.
