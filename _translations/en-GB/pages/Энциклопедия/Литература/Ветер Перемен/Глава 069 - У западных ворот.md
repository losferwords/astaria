# Chapter 069 — At the Western Gate

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 105 NE, summer*

While the others recovered from their night at the [[Бордель Уй-Джан|Ui-Jan]], [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] cared for [[Нуа Си|Nuwa Xi]] and the newborn [[Чинь Си|Chin Xi]]. He somehow managed to cook rice and wash the swaddling cloths before setting out to find help. He found his friends at the gates of the [[Храм Дракона|Temple of the Dragon]], where [[Сунь Ляньши|Sun Lianshi]] and her retinue were already waiting.

[[Гиперион I|Hyperion]] arrived in the gleaming armour of [[Гилас|Gilas]]. His meeting with the empress passed peacefully. They spoke of diplomacy, trade and a southern route that might link [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]] with Gilas through the greatest nations of Archaea. The god of war then bowed and departed for his homeland.

The travellers tried to descend into the Halls of the Dragon, but the monks explained that without an [[Аватар|Avatar]], the temple's labyrinths were all but impassable. They therefore needed [[Бао Си|Bao Xi]]. Before leaving, Gilja looked through the archives and found amongst the temple's foreign visitors a Rajati recorded as a [[Тхаги|Thuggee]], an assassin from [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]].

The travellers found [[Ли Сан|Li San]] at the [[Таверна Красный Журавль|Red Crane Tavern]]. The young woman at the entrance refused to disclose anything about the guests, so [[Урист Китин|Urist]] simply stepped into the street and shouted loudly enough to shatter the windows on the ground floor. The damage was blamed upon an earthquake. Li San, her pregnancy now plainly visible, came down to him, and the couple spoke of the rescued San family.

[[Амрит Наир|Amrit Nair]], an employee of the inn, emerged at the noise. He spoke sharply of [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] and his eagerness to please Shubhankari.

Meanwhile, Bao Xi learnt of his niece's birth and became an exceedingly anxious nursemaid. Persuading him to leave Nuwa in the care of experienced Rajati women proved difficult, but the travellers eventually won his agreement. Together with Bao, they returned to the temple and prepared to descend.
