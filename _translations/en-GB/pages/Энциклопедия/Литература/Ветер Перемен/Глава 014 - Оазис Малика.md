# Chapter 014 — Malik's Oasis

*Qadir, 104 NE, winter*

The Ashlands lay behind them, but relief did not come. Ahead stretched the [[Пустыня Халисат|Khalisat Desert]]—not an emptiness, but an entire world of sand, heat, rare lakes and roads no traveller could afford to ignore. The company went north in hope of reaching the southern road, but the crossing proved merciless.

Their food ran out. Only a few flasks of water remained. The camels that had survived the Ashlands now failed one after another beneath Khalisat's sun. Dune followed dune, each new crest promising only another beyond it. Even [[Данис ибн Синай|Danis ibn Sinai]], who knew the desert better than the rest, grew steadily grimmer.

When an oasis appeared in the distance, hope flared too quickly. Danis stopped his companions. There were no empty oases in Khalisat, he told them. If water lay open and no people gathered around it, the place was either a trap, a mirage, or somewhere shunned for a reason best learnt in advance. He urged them to continue north towards the road.

The desert seldom grants a traveller a choice. At the oasis they met a man who named himself [[Малик Аль-Азвар|Malik al-Azwar]]. He allowed them to remain for the night. There was something curious in his composure: not the hospitality of a simple nomad, but the assurance of a man long acquainted with the price of roads, water and other people's secrets.

[[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] fulfilled part of his bargain with [[Мерката|Mercate]], secretly slipping Malik a Lunaar coin. The little disc vanished among his belongings so quietly that nothing seemed to have happened. Yet another's will already lived within that silence.

At night, Tom and [[Урист Китин|Urist]] decided to search the Qadians' supply crates. Whether from desperation, curiosity or the habit of surviving at any price, they sought anything that might help the company. Among the goods they found another [[Корона Душ|Crown of Souls]] and a [[Чёрный Вестник|Black Harbinger]].

By morning, Malik had disappeared.

The oasis fell behind them together with the sense that the meeting had not been chance. The travellers now possessed a letter and new artefacts, yet no greater clarity. In [[Кадир|Qadir]], even aid might be a trial, and a lone wanderer beside the water might see far more than he allowed others to know.
