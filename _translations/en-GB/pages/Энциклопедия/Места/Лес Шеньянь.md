# Shenyan

![[Assets/Images/Shenyang_Forest.jpg]]

## Main text

**Shenyan** (神焰) is a vast bamboo forest in western [[Долина Гармонии|He-Dji-Gu]], stretching between [[Озеро Шуан|Lake Shuan]] and [[Озеро Чанг|Lake Chang]]. Its name may be rendered as ‘divine flame’: at sunset, light falls between the stems in long bands of gold, and the forest appears to burn with a fire that consumes nothing.

Shenyan bamboo is used for building, vessels, paper, musical instruments and countless articles of daily life. Cutters divide the forest into plots and never take young shoots without need. Hasty felling can destroy an entire grove, so transgressors are watched not only by officials, but by the village communities themselves.

Deer, boar, brilliant birds and many small predators live beneath the green canopy. [[Менада|Maenads]] are encountered in the depths, and certain paths are marked with red ribbons on their account. The inhabitants do not regard them as ordinary beasts. They prefer to leave baskets of fruit at the edge of a plot rather than test the temper of those who dwell within the thicket.

Trails cross the forest towards hunting lodges, abandoned shrines and old watch platforms. After rain, the bamboo creaks and knocks from stem to stem as though someone were pacing beside the traveller. The noise hides approaching footsteps, making Shenyan equally hospitable to a hermit, a smuggler or a creature that does not wish to be found.

To the Empire, the forest remains an emblem of discipline: a single stem is easily broken, but a whole grove survives the storm by bending as one.
