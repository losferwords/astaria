# Ulyn-Gol

![[Assets/Images/Ulyn-Gol_River.jpg]]

The **Ulyn-Gol** is a great river of central [[Архея|Archaea]], flowing between the [[Горный хребет Шафар|Shafar Mountains]] and [[Горный хребет Зверя|Beast Range]]. It forms [[Долина Кровавого Цветения|Blood Blossom Valley]] and reaches the [[Хтоническое море|Chthonic Sea]] at [[Город Бахара|Bahara]].

Its banks long belonged to the [[Джу|Dju]], who built settlements and posts on the mountain road. After the [[Авгарцы|Avgars]] came, they entered [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], but [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]] kept its historical claim. Villages therefore carry mixed customs and old names beside new ones.

Upstream it is swift and cold; below, it broadens through red cedrela groves. Water feeds fields and small forges, while timber and ore float on its calm reaches. Floods destroy crossings but open fresh stone and metal deposits in fallen banks.

People make truces, exchange prisoners and negotiate caravan passage on this river. Its most dangerous reaches are near old forts, where underwater stakes and walls remain and abandoned tunnels can hide smugglers or mountain creatures.
