# Anvika Narai

![[Assets/Images/Anvika_Narai.jpg]]

**Anvika Narai** was born in [[Город Аштапал|Ashtapal]], a city where red dust mingles with the scent of the sea and every dawn begins with hymns in honour of [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari]].

Her family belonged to an old lineage of keepers of the temple gardens, where plants were cultivated for sacred rites and healing infusions. Her mother was renowned for her gentle voice, while her father was a healer revered for his ability to soothe the fire in both heart and body.

From childhood, Anvika was drawn to ancient words, rituals and the mysteries concealed within the rhythm of prayer. She loved listening to tales of the Great Cycle—the eternal breath of the world, in which destruction and rebirth walk hand in hand.

## The path of the Raksha

When the time came to choose her future, she set foot upon the path of the [[Ракша|Raksha]]. Her initiation took place on the longest night of the year, when the stars above the [[Джунгли Шанкари|Shankari Jungle]] hung motionless, as though the Goddess herself were watching from the heavens.

Her life has since become inseparable from the temple: morning hymns, rites of blood and the care of cultivated lands. Despite the severity of her calling, Anvika remains easy company. She knows how to inspire laughter, quieten anxiety and find the words that make another person feel truly heard.

She is regarded as one of Shubhankari's most devoted disciples. During the Great Harvest, she travels across Suraj Ka Ghar with the other priests, preparing fields and people alike for their passage into a new Cycle.

No one knows what truly moves her in those days: faith, duty or the simple desire to preserve balance in a world where the sun sometimes burns too fiercely.
