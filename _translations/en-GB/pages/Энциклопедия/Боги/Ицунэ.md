# Itsune

## Main text

Itsune is the goddess of wind and the spirit world, worshipped throughout the islands of [[Амато|Amato]]. She is said to guide the dead into the world beyond. Her mysterious lantern, Chochin-obake, illuminates the darkness, while a long blade cuts through the heavy mist upon the road to peace and eternal rest. Itsune also embodies air and the elemental power of wind.

In ancient times, sailors upon the [[Рассветное море|Dawn Sea]] told of a beautiful spirit of the fair wind who carried ships towards rich fishing grounds and fertile shores. Many heard a gentle whisper among the gusts—even words of comfort and counsel in moments of despair. As the years passed, the mariners of the eastern sea formed a cult around a Goddess of Winds who could not be seen, but could be heard and felt upon the skin.

In time the goddess's guidance brought the first settlers to the shores of [[Остров Ояширо|Oyashiro]]. They built a fishing village and the first shrine of the winds beside a sheltered bay; [[Город Кайто|Kaito]] would later rise upon the same ground. Tradition says that the unseen patron first whispered her name to humanity there: Itsune. Thus began the history of Amato.

Priests who choose to devote their entire lives to Itsune spend years preparing for sacred rebirth as [[Онмёдзи|Onmyoji]], hunters of demons. After the secret rite they receive new names, while the details of their initiation remain hidden even from other temple servants.

Their eyes turn red and are covered with a blindfold, though they do not lose the power of sight. Once initiated, the hunters can perceive traces of the spirit world and the presence of oni, but will not reveal the nature of that vision to outsiders. Itsune also grants them command of the sacred wind (神風), allowing them to direct the air, summon fierce gusts and veil the land in mist.

The Onmyoji describe their goddess as a slender, upright fox with nine tails. A ribbon covers Itsune's eyes, concealing the grief born from knowing every death in this world.

Tradition holds that, on rare occasions, Itsune refuses to accept the death of an especially worthy priest and returns them to life as an Onmyoji. What passes between the mortal and the goddess in such a miracle is known only to Itsune and the initiated.
