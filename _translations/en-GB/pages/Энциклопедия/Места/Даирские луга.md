# Dair Meadows

![[Assets/Images/dair_grasslands.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Dair Meadows** are the broad lands between [[Лес Иомар|Iomar Forest]] and the southern coast of central [[Кельн|Keln]]. In spring they are covered with tall grass, white umbellifers and small blue bells; by late summer, grain and dry wind from the [[Хтоническое море|Chthonic Sea]] turn them gold.

The first settlers from [[Архея|Archaea]] landed here and founded the communities of future [[Иомар|Iomar]], including [[Город Муир|Muir]]. Old stones on the coast still mark the sites of their first camps. Nadair families leave bowls of milk and honey there, thanking their forebears for surviving the first winter.

The meadows feed the country's south. People graze sheep and horses, grow grain, gather fragrant herbs and hold great seasonal fairs. Yet farmers do not plough the land into one unbroken field: strips of wildflowers remain between plots, giving birds places to nest and pollinating insects a home.

To a traveller, the Dair Meadows seem an easy road after forest trails, but their open space deceives. Sea fog can hide a village within a few hundred paces, and sudden rain turns low ground into torrents. Experienced guides watch gulls and the scent of grass: here weather is read not from instruments, but from the way the land itself prepares to meet the wind.
