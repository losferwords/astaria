# Archaeans

![[Assets/Images/Archaeans.jpg]]

The name **Archaeans** is given to an enigmatic and apparently advanced civilisation that existed long before the [[Хтониды|Chthonid Empire]]. Only scattered ruins, unusual objects and images remain, interpreted differently by every people that encounters them. Almost nothing reliable is known of the Archaeans' appearance, daily life or origin.

In the spring of 106 NE, a structure believed to be an Archaean sanctuary or repository was discovered in the caves of [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]]. Its reliefs depict majestic non-human figures beside the Archaeans. Whether these beings were gods, rulers, guardians or merely figures of myth remains unknown.
