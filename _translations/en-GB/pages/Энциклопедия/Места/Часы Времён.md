# Clock of Time

![[Assets/Images/Clock_of_Time.png]]

## Main text

The **Clock of Time** is a colossal hourglass in the principal underground chamber of the [[Храм Дар Мараат|Temple of Dar Maraat]], concealed beneath earth and sand at the centre of an island in [[Озеро Зураиф|Lake Zuraif]]. It towers above the ancient sanctuary's galleries; sand flows between two immense transparent vessels joined by a narrow neck.

For the [[Кадийцы|Qadians]], the clock is sacred to [[Альзаман|Alzaman]] and a visible symbol of time's continuity. According to their tradition, its first falling grain began all existence, and its last will announce the End of Time. The glass will then turn, and the course of being will begin anew. This belief shapes the sanctuary's veneration, but does not provide a verified account of its workings.

The clock stands in the oldest part of Dar Maraat, whose age is unknown. The university, library and accommodation on the island's surface appeared much later: the Qadians built them around an existing sanctuary. The route to the clock passes through towering corridors and partly ruined limestone halls, lit by torches and occasional shafts of sunlight.
