Poor thing. I think he knew before I did that this dream was not going to remain respectable.

And Losfy was there too — my sweet little goat-girl friend from dreams 🤍 She looked so calm, so kind, so caring… but also somehow far too knowledgeable for someone who looks so sweet and innocent. I do not know why my imagination keeps making her like that. Maybe because I feel safe with her. Maybe because she says naughty things in such a gentle voice that they stop sounding dangerous and start sounding like secrets wrapped in silk.

But Diary… why would my own imagination put her beside my bed while I was holding that carrot? 🥕The one from the garden. The one I have absolutely not been glancing at. Except perhaps once. Or twice. Or… enough times that I should probably apologize to vegetables as a species.

I was supposed to go downstairs for breakfast. A good bunny girl would have washed her face, brushed her ears, and eaten politely. Instead, I locked the door in my dream, curled my toes, and felt my whole body become warm in the strangest places. My paws were shaking. My tummy felt fluttery. Inside, everything felt soft and too warm, like someone had poured golden morning light under my skin.

I covered my eyes like a cowardly bunny and whispered sorry to absolutely nobody.
