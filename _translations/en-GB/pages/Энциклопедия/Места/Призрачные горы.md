# Ghost Mountains

![[Assets/Images/Ghost_Mountains.png]]

## Main text

The **Ghost Mountains** are a mountain range in the central part of [[Туманный остров|Mist Island]], surrounded by the [[Дымовая Чаща|Smoke Thicket]]. The mountains are home to [[Вервольф|werewolves]], which also inhabit the island's northern and western forests.

## Name and legends

The mountains take their name from the fog: ridges and summits emerge between white layers only to disappear again, making even familiar outlines difficult to recognise.

Local legends tell of silhouettes glimpsed in the mist and calls resembling the voices of missing people. These stories lend the mountains' name an unsettling meaning.

## Slopes and passes

Dense coniferous forests cover the northern and western foothills. Spruce woods and mossy lowlands give way to hidden valleys through which werewolf packs travel. Old animal trails connect these places to mountain crossings.

Higher up lie rocky ridges and exposed slopes with patches of snow. Narrow paths lead to passes that are difficult to distinguish in the fog. Strong crosswinds and sleet hinder travellers on exposed crossings, where shelter is scarce.

Werewolves inhabited these mountains long before humans arrived. The expansion of human activity in the forests forces the packs ever higher, where conditions are colder and prey is scarcer. Encounters with hungry predators are dangerous: they are stronger than ordinary wolves and can attack together. Their howls frighten even the island's coastal inhabitants.

Some former soldiers work as mountain guides. They remember the wartime paths and places where garrisons used to spend the night.

Since the war, the mountain paths have seen little use. The usual routes skirt the range; only occasional hunters and guides cross the passes.

## The abandoned stronghold

During the war, [[Амато|Amato]] maintained a border stronghold in the Ghost Mountains. Its garrison played an important part in the security of [[Город Касумито|Kasumito]] and [[Город Кири|Kiri]]. The soldiers abandoned the post after the conflict ended.

The harsh terrain and strong winds make life here difficult, and few people wish to settle in the stronghold. Some buildings have survived and offer shelter from bad weather.
