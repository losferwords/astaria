# Darkhan

![[Assets/Images/Darkhan_City.jpg]]

**Darkhan** is the largest city and capital of [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], on the [[Река Айлиз|Ayliz]] in [[Хангорская долина|Hangor Valley]]. The residence of Great Khan [[Батар Саг|Batar Sag]], it gathers clan representatives and roads from steppes, mountain mines and river ports.

It began as a supply camp for early [[Мектиг|Mektig]] campaigns. Smiths, traders, warriors' families and people from conquered lands joined it; tents became wooden yards, then stone stores, bridges and fortifications. Darkhan still resembles several camps grown together around one market.

Lower quarters line the Ayliz and receive ore, timber, wool and grain. The capital's power rests not on court ceremony but on councils, workshops and the Avgar rule that a person's word has weight only if they are ready to carry it upon their own shoulders.
