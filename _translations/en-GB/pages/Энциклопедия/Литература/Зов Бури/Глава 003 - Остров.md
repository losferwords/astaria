# Chapter 003 — The Island

*The Island*

[[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth]] survives, but lies unconscious upon the shore of [[Остров Хтон|Chthon Island]]. All the while, a [[Профитис|Profitis]] named [[Кассиопея Атанатос|Cassia]] watches him.

*98 NE*

Cassia reflects aloud upon the events of a century before: how the “chosen” were meant to bring glory to the [[Хтониды|Chthonids]], but instead became the [[Падение Хтона|ruin of Chthon]].

She then revives Kenneth. The astonished [[Горец|Highlander]] tries to understand the strange young woman living alone upon the cursed island. Their conversation leaves him with more questions than answers. He decides not to waste time and seeks a way to escape, for his road still leads south. Cassia merely watches and urges him to abandon the attempt, since she can clearly see his future: a slow death here beside her.

Everything changes when Kenneth succeeds in building a raft. At that instant, Cassia's visions transform entirely. She is delighted that one man has altered countless future events merely by constructing a raft. The [[Профитис|Profitis's]] certainty that all things are ordained collapses, and she decides to leave the cursed island and continue the journey beside him.
