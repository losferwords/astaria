# Chapter 006 — No Mercy for the Thirsty

*No Mercy for the Thirsty*

In [[Город Атафет|Atafet]], [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth]] sought a smith named [[Арно Эклунд|Arno Eklund]] and commissioned the [[Меч Де'лаим|De'laim sword]]. He found Arno's forge quickly, but the weapon's price lay far beyond the young [[Горец|Highlander's]] means. He had spent years wandering and imprisoned, while every treasure he possessed was lost in the shipwreck. Arno offered him a way to earn the money while aiding the city: work at the military academy, training soldiers and militia for a respectable wage. [[Кассиопея Атанатос|Cassia]] also helped raise the funds by tending victims of the Great Drought at the city refuge. Within several months, they had saved enough for Arno to begin forging De'laim.

*100 NE*

The Highlander's Sword was completed six months later. Yet on the very day Kenneth finally received his weapon, the event everyone had long expected came to pass.

Outraged by the [[Вторжение Амон-Астата в город Ахей|capture of Achaeus]], [[Гилас|Gilas]] declared [[Война Жаждущих|war]] upon [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]]. Within months, its army had not only reclaimed Achaeus but seized every western city of the Sun Empire: [[Город Фибас|Phibas]], [[Город Ксен-Аст|Ksen-Ast]], [[Город Сива|Siwa]], [[Город Натшепсут|Natshepsut]] and [[Город Акхетастон|Akhetaston]]. Now the fire of war reached Amon-Astat's centre of craft, Atafet.

The Ellian host was commanded by [[Персефона Раптис|Persephone]], polemarch of eastern [[Регион Элессия|Elessia]]. She had not yet become a [[Идеал|Paragon]], but desperately wished to return home victorious and earn the right to another [[Танатомахия|Thanatomachia]].

Atafet's rulers understood that the city could not withstand Gilas without the help of [[Имитей|Imithei]]. Unfortunately, no [[Светоносный|Lightbringers]] were present to lead the defence. The authorities sought any Imithei willing to aid the city for a worthy reward.

By a strange coincidence, only one other Imithei was in the city besides Kenneth and Cassia: [[Рея Мелит|Captain Rhea Melit]]. She had come seeking materials for a new ship and sailors for a new crew. When the council offered everything she required in exchange for aid against the Ellians, Rhea agreed without hesitation.

In this chapter of the saga, Rhea recounts the city's defence against the forces of Gilas.
