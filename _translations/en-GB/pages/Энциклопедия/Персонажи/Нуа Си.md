# Nuwa Xi

![[Assets/Images/Nuwa_Hsi.jpg]]

## Main text

**Nuwa Xi** is a young monk of the [[Храм Дракона|Temple of the Dragon]] and the younger sister of the [[Аватар|Avatar]] [[Бао Си|Bao Xi]].

Nuwa was orphaned at the age of five amid palace intrigues between two rival families. While wandering the streets of [[Город Джу-Суо|Dju-Suo]] with her elder brother, she approached a monk of the Temple of the Dragon and asked for help. He admitted the children within the temple walls, and thus began their path towards becoming Avatars.

Nuwa is still undergoing her trials as a junior Avatar of the fire god, [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Lang-An]]. Her pilgrimage became entangled with [[Мерката|Mercate's]] scythe: finding the weapon in a field, she mistook it for a sign that her journey was complete and carried it to the Temple, unaware that the relic would be turned against Lang-An himself.

For all her innocence and scant knowledge of life beyond the temple, Nuwa commands dragonfire and can conjure whirling flames that burst upon their target. Her gift shines brightest when someone dear to her is in danger. It was Nuwa who roused the gravely wounded Bao and helped him recover the will to live.

In Dju-Suo, Nuwa gave birth to a daughter, [[Чинь Си|Qin Xi]]. Motherhood did not end her spiritual path; it taught her to see service to Lang-An not merely as a trial of strength, but as a duty to protect a life that trusts her utterly.
