# Chapter 036 — Arisa's Shadow

*[[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], 104 NE, autumn*

The pursuit of [[Кирида Ариса|Arisa]] ended near [[Бордель Халык-Бындыр|Halyk-Byndir]]. Once she saw there was nowhere left to retreat, she committed seppuku and summoned [[Ицунэ|Itsune]].

The goddess told her the cold truth: [[Мэйлун|Meilong]] had already been born and could no longer serve as a source of power for Chochinbake. Arisa's sacrifice had been in vain, and that knowledge held more pain than the blade itself. Itsune invited [[Урист Китин|Urist]] to come before her in person to receive a gift, leaving Arisa's fate to the travellers.

[[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] proposed making Arisa a [[Тень|Shadow]]. [[Мерката|Mercate]] agreed reluctantly, but performed the rite. Thus Arisa, who had served one goddess to the limit of self-denial, became bound to another.

[[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] took the dragon to Blaine of Ruche, Bahara's herbalist, to have his wounds treated. The healer's staff bit Meilong, and Mei's patience—already worn thin by fear—nearly broke. She was furious with Urist and the others for allowing their decisions to endanger the dragon time and again.

Meanwhile, [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] experimented with blood and the [[Рактапатр|Raktapatra]]. The blade answered each donor with a different effect, as though every kind of blood bore not merely power, but its owner's brief signature.
