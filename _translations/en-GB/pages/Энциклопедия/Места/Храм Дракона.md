# Temple of the Dragon

![[Assets/Images/Dragon_Temple.jpg]]

The **Temple of the Dragon** is the foremost holy place of the [[Джу|Dju]] and the [[Империя Ланг-Ан|Empire of Fire]]. The complex is the dwelling of the [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Great Dragon]], the Empress's palace and home to hundreds of monks. Here the [[Аватар|Avatars]] are trained: Lang-An's chosen warriors, able to channel his power and see the world through their god's eyes.

Above ground are ceremonial courts, archives, audience halls, monastic quarters and palace floors. Beneath them lie the Dragon Halls, an ancient labyrinth closed to most capital residents. Deep within is the Well of Fire, a sea of incandescent Qi where Lang-An restores his strength and may speak with the Empress through a secret Hall of Audience.

[[Нуа Си|Nuwa Xi]] brought Mercate's scythe, found in a field, here. With [[Кион-Чи|Qiong-Qi's]] aid, [[Артур Бомейн|Arthur Beaumain]] entered the complex and turned the relic against Lang-An. The wounded god withdrew into the Well, and hundreds of Avatars sealed themselves in the Halls so that the predator could neither command them nor unleash their power against Dju-Suo.

The temple was freed only after the descent into the Well of Fire. [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] drew the scythe from the Dragon's body, while monks and [[Бао Си|Bao Xi]] helped break Qiong-Qi's hold over the Avatars. Since then, the temple has remained not only a symbol of unbroken imperial tradition, but a warning: even a divine fortress is vulnerable when an enemy knows its secrets better than its own keepers.
