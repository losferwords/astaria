# Seraph

![[Assets/Images/Seraph_City.jpg]]

## Main text

**Seraph** is the largest city of [[Кадир|Qadir]] and its acknowledged capital, standing upon the western shore of [[Озеро Зураиф|Lake Zuraif]]. Even after the country was divided among three rulers, it kept a special place: caravan roads converge here, common chronicles are preserved here and embassies addressing the whole kingdom are received here.

White and golden buildings rise in terraces from the water to the palace hill. The lower quarters hold quays, markets and workshops; above lie gardens, schools, scribes' houses and the court of [[Абу Али аль-Хассан|Abu Ali al-Hassan]]. Fabric awnings often cover the narrow streets, while inner courtyards are arranged around fountains and fruit trees.

The island [[Храм Дар Мараат|Temple of Dar Maraat]] can be seen from the lake. Pilgrim boats continually travel between it and the city, but movement falls still during the hours of temple rites. Seraph lives beside sacred time and is therefore unusually attentive to schedules: markets open to the chime of water bowls, courts begin after a shadow is measured and lateness for an important meeting is taken as disrespect for another person's life.

The city is famed for physicians, interpreters of the stars, manuscript merchants and makers of intricate mechanisms. One may find an answer here to almost any question—or someone who will persuasively prove that the question itself was wrongly framed.

Seraph is generous to an investigator, but demanding. Archives do not open to everyone, court schools guard their methods jealously and passages built before the present city plan survive beneath the ancient embankments. Some steps descend beneath the waters of Zuraif and appear only in years of rare shallows.
