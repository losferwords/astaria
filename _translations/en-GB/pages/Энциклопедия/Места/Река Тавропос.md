# Tavropos River

![[Assets/Images/Tauropos_River.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Tavropos** is one of the principal rivers of western [[Архея|Archaea]]. It flows out of [[Озеро Митра|Lake Mitra]], crosses the valleys of [[Регион Элессия|Elessia]] and reaches the sea beneath the walls of [[Город Аргос|Argos]]. Grain, timber, stone and reinforcements for the army all travel down its waters towards the capital.

In its upper reaches the Tavropos is broad and tranquil, but farther downstream it quickens between limestone banks. Spring raises the water over the low fields and leaves behind fertile silt. It also brings uprooted trees, wreckage from boats and, on occasion, possessions whose origins the river villagers prefer not to discuss with the city guard.

Watchtowers and inns stand at the crossings. The ferrymen know the schedules of military patrols as well as they know the current, while certain families have guided travellers between the lake and the capital's harbour for generations. For a traveller, the Tavropos may be the swiftest road through Gilas—provided one can reach an understanding with whoever controls the necessary boat.
