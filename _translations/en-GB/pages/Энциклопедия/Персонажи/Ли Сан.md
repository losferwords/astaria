# Li San

![[Assets/Images/Li_San.jpg]]

## Main text

**Li San** is a woman of the [[Джу|Dju]] people and the daughter of the elder [[Чани Сан|Chani San]].

While still young, Li was betrothed to the Kitin family of [[Деревня Улехей|Ulehey]] in the hope of strengthening kinship between the southern [[Авгарцы|Avgars]] and the Dju. Her intended bridegroom, [[Урист Китин|Urist Kitin]], left his home village before the wedding could take place. Li remained among the Avgars for almost all her adult life, gradually abandoning any hope that she would ever marry.

In 104 NE, Urist returned to his native village at the head of a nomad company, and his mother swiftly arranged the long-delayed wedding. The celebrations were marred by a horde of [[Вермин|Vermin]] attacking the village, although Li and her husband survived. The danger frightened Chani San so deeply that she took her daughter back to their native city of [[Город Найян|Nayan]].
