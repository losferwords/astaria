# Chapter 024 — Ungol's Villa

*[[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], 104 NE, spring*

[[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] tried to heal Nisa, but the treatment left her unable to move. There was something almost symbolic in it: the thief accustomed to slipping away was forced to speak.

She spoke with [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] of their homelands, and suddenly less enmity remained between them. Nisa saw in Mei not only a hunter pursuing a stolen artefact, but a young woman drawn homeward. A wanderer's longing for home often sounds quieter than steel, yet wounds no less deeply.

Nisa's refuge lay in the cellar of [[Унгол Баят|Ungol Bayat's]] villa. He was a wealthy merchant to whom [[Урист Китин|Urist]] already owed an uncomfortable sum. On the way, the travellers encountered [[Армар Саг|Armar Sag]], but the true trap awaited not in the market, but behind the villa's walls.

The box containing the [[Сердце Огня|Heart of Fire]] was not in the cellar. Mei's fury ignited before the building did: her hands burst into flame, and soon fire spread through the hideout. Urist rushed upstairs and collided with Ungol and his guards, while Mei searched for traces of the slender buyer who had come for the artefact.

She found the Heart of Fire in a secret room, but her relief died at once. The artefact was damaged.

Outside, Gilja and Nisa disabled two guards with a brutal trick of blood. Within, the floor gave way beneath the heat and Mei plunged into the burning cellar. She emerged unharmed, though the flames had taken her clothes. Struck by the sight, Nisa looked upon her for the first time not as an enemy, but as a marvel whose favour she desired.

Ungol's villa burned, Urist's debts grew worse, and the Heart of Fire—recovered at last—was no longer what it had been.
