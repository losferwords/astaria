# War of the Thirsty

![[Assets/Images/war_of_the_thirsty.jpg]]

Outraged by the [[Вторжение Амон-Астата в город Ахей|seizure of Achaeus]] by the rebellious [[Жаждущие|Thirsty]], [[Гилас|Gilas]] declared war upon [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]]. Its Ellian army was led by [[Персефона Раптис|Persephone Raptis]], polemarch of eastern [[Регион Элессия|Elessia]].

Within months, the forces of Gilas had not only retaken [[Город Ахей|Achaeus]], but captured every city in western Amon-Astat: [[Город Фибас|Phibas]], [[Город Ксен-Аст|Ksen-Ast]], [[Город Сива|Siwa]], [[Город Натшепсут|Natshepsut]] and [[Город Акхетастон|Akhetaston]].

The fire of war eventually reached [[Город Атафет|Atafet]], the great centre of craft in the Empire of the Sun. The city was besieged, but on the second day of the assault clouds gathered over the whole of Amon-Astat and a violent rain began. The storm turned the battle in favour of the defenders, forcing the armies of Gilas to retreat.

Two months later, Pharaoh [[Рамхотеп II|Ramhotep II]] came to Achaeus in person. He apologised for the rebels' invasion, paid tribute to Gilas and secured peace on the condition that the territories conquered by the Ellian army be restored.
