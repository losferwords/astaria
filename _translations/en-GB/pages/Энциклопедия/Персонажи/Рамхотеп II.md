# Ramhotep II

![[Assets/Images/Ramhotep_II.jpg]]

## Main text

**Ramhotep II** is the one hundred and thirty-third pharaoh of [[Амон-Астат|the Empire of the Sun]] and its present ruler. He inherited the throne upon his father's death at the age of twenty, and with the crown received responsibility for an ancient order of temples, cities and waterways.

Ramhotep's reign has encompassed the Great Drought, the [[Кризис веры в Амон-Астате|Crisis of Faith]], the [[Вторжение Амон-Астата в город Ахей|invasion of Achaeus]] by the Thirsty and the [[Война Жаждущих|War of the Thirsty]]. When the drying rivers turned sunlight from a blessing into a source of suffering, the pharaoh was forced to defend not only his borders, but the very idea of lawful rule.

Ramhotep preserved [[Хат-Астат|Hat-Astat]], yet he could not simply declare all doubt a crime: hunger had made the people's anger too fierce. He protected the priests from vengeance, thwarted the rebels' attempts to seize neighbouring lands and at the same time struggled to keep the cities from complete collapse. To some, this proved the pharaoh's fortitude; to others, it showed that the court had heard the suffering far too late.

After Achaeus was captured, Ramhotep refused to present the actions of [[Жаждущие|the Thirsty]] as an official campaign of the Empire. When the war brought the armies of [[Гилас|Gilas]] across his borders, he came to the negotiations in person, offered an apology and agreed to pay tribute for the return of the occupied cities. In warlike Astaria, such a step might appear humiliating, but the pharaoh valued the lives preserved and the integrity of his country above the flawless image of a victor.

Ramhotep now rules a realm that has survived its own division. His legacy has yet to be decided: he may be remembered as the pharaoh who held the Empire of the Sun together, or as the ruler beneath whom the ancient order was compelled, for the first time, to answer the questions of ordinary people.
