# Ulehey

![[Assets/Images/Ulehey_Village.jpg]]

**Ulehey** is a mining settlement in southern [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], on the [[Река Улын-Гол|Ulyn-Gol]]. It grew beside rich caves and retains a workers' camp character: close-set homes, warehouses joined to workshops and a mine bell governing the day.

Avgar clans once followed the river seeking ore for the [[Великая Кузня|Great Forge]], then found metal veins here. Their Kitin-Ulegey camp became Ulehey. Mining continued after the Forge's completion, supplying towns, weaponmakers and caravans.

Collapses, poisonous vapours and creatures in abandoned shafts make underground work dangerous. Before each shift, a master inspects lamps, ropes and supports, while miners' families leave bread at the entrance—as gift to earth, memory of the dead, or reminder that someone waits at home.

The settlement values labour above distant titles: respect belongs to the one who descends with others or stays to help after a collapse.
