# The Great Drought of Amon-Astat

> [!timeline] 94–100 NE
> A drought in the deserts of [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]] brought famine, destitution, a [[Кризис веры в Амон-Астате|crisis of faith]] in the Empire of the Sun and the [[Война Жаждущих|War of the Thirsty]]. Its cause remains unknown. It ended as abruptly as it had begun, when a storm front swept down from the north.
