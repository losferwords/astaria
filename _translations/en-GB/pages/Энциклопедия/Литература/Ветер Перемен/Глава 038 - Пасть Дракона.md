# Chapter 038 — Dragon's Maw

*[[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], 104 NE, autumn*

At night, [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] scattered [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari's]] spices, and a gentle wave of merriment washed over the camp. [[Урист Китин|Urist]] added alcohol, pushing caution still farther away.

[[Ошу Мати|Oshu Mati]] wanted to recover the [[Корона Душ|Crown of Souls]] and its stone from [[Пирей Никос|Piraeus's]] baggage. Piraeus regarded her with almost superstitious dread: at their previous meeting, he had ended tied to a post in her hut.

From [[Ишикути Макото|Makoto]], Oshu learnt that [[Ицунэ|Itsune]] might appear when an Imithei stood at the brink of death. This inspired a dangerous scheme: poison [[Эйдан мак Кинсли|Aidan]] with alcohol, await the goddess and ask how Rufu could be restored to life. The plan failed almost at once. Aidan truly began to die, and Oshu, Gilja and [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] found themselves fighting not for knowledge, but for his breath. They saved him, but Itsune never came.

[[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] went into the woods with Piraeus. A game of strip dice ended in intimacy beneath the night sky, far from the fires and other people's gods.

In the morning, a murdered Mektig was found in the camp. Oshu looked into his final moments: he had danced with a woman whose face was half-covered by a mask, before she bit his neck and drank his blood. Suspicion first fell upon Gilja, but he convinced the nomads that such a death bore no resemblance to Shubhankari's hunt.

The road brought the company to [[Гарнизон Пасть Дракона|Dragon's Maw]]. Urist, Tom and Gilja decided to test the man calling himself [[Аксель Хан|Axel Khan]]. Urist and Tom entered the tent while Gilja waited outside.

The pretender—if pretender he was—carried himself with confidence. He endured their questions, took up Urist's Deathbringer and displayed an axe of his own, matching the legends' descriptions. Even when Tom examined the weapon, he felt immense weight rather than magical inviolability.

Axel Khan offered Urist silver, glory and a campaign into [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]]. Urist agreed. He was entrusted with two arbats and sent south to his native [[Деревня Улехей|Ulehey]] to prepare its people for a possible attack.

While this unfolded, Mei observed the nomads' plans and noticed Oshu upon the field of a recent battle. Thousands of dead Dju from the Wu legion lay there. Oshu performed rites of repose, listened to hundreds of souls and found her father among them. He confessed that he had suffered the same illness as she and had gone east to place himself farther from Rufu. Oshu accepted his apology and received his skull as a remembrance of kinship that did not end with death.

When she awoke, the nomads seized her and brought her before Axel Khan. He said she must die and hurled a spear. Oshu lost consciousness. They abandoned her among the dead, and then [[Вермин|vermin]] dragged her body into the caves of the [[Горный хребет Шафар|Shafar Mountains]].
