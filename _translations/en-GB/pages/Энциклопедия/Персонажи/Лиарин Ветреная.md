# Liarin the Flighty

![[Assets/Images/Liarin_Windy.jpg]]

**Liarin the Flighty** is a young [[Надаир|Nadair]] woman of [[Иомар|Iomar]] and a [[Друид|Druid]] of [[Церунна|Cerunna]]. At only twenty-four, she has already received nature's gifts in rare abundance: Liarin readily earns the trust of animals, senses the breathing of the forest and can mend grievous wounds as though she were smiling through the act rather than enduring pain and exhaustion. She is naive, curious and quick to become enthralled, and does not always understand why the elder Druids urge caution when every instinct tells her to race towards the next mystery.

She never found a comfortable place within the [[Круг Друидов|Druid Circle]]. Her elder sisters regarded her as a thorn in their side—too loud and insufficiently solemn for important duties. It was this temperament that earned her the epithet *the Flighty*. Liarin remains undismayed. She is almost as proud of the name as she is of Cerunna's charge to seek the [[Реликты|Relics]] and recover knowledge of Astaria's ancient beings.

Some Druids considered the assignment less a sign of the goddess's trust than a convenient means of sending Liarin far from Iomar. Liarin chooses to think otherwise. To her, the journey means that Cerunna sees not a nuisance, but a living force that has yet to learn its proper course.

In [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]], Liarin was rescued from a naga cave. After her release, she joined travellers investigating the calamities of the south and began searching for elephants, elephant-folk and Relics who might preserve knowledge sought by Cerunna. She came to regard luminous cave fungi as a possible means of enticing elephants without harming them, although one such expedition nearly ended in an attack by a [[Ветал|Vetala]].
