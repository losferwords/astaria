# Rukh

![[Assets/Images/Rokh.jpg]]

## Main text

**Rukhs** are immense birds of prey found in the [[Горный хребет Зверя|Beast Range]] and above the [[Хангорская долина|Hangor Valley]]. An adult's wings may span ten metres, and its strength is sufficient to carry away a mountain beast — or a human being.

A rukh hunts from a great height. It circles almost motionless for long stretches, vanishing against the dark rock, then dives upon a creature that has strayed from its herd or caravan. The first impact is meant to stun; the bird then closes its talons around the prey and bears it back to the nest. Travellers are therefore advised to remain in a close group and seek cover whenever a familiar shadow suddenly disappears.

Rukh pairs remain together for life and defend a vast territory around their nest. They usually raise one or two chicks on an inaccessible ledge, building with branches, hides and objects carried away with earlier prey. Treasure-seekers sometimes attempt the climb in hopes of finding weapons, jewellery or ancient relics among the debris.

The birds readily recognise the habits of passing caravans. Brandishing a weapon may attract rather than frighten them, while ropes and bright cloth stretched above a camp make a safe dive difficult. Experienced Avgar guides also carry whistles that imitate an adult rukh's warning cry.

As people spread across [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], the species declined sharply. Rukhs grew warier and now seldom approach large cities, although a few pairs have learnt to follow herds and wagon trains. Killing one to protect human life is accepted; hunting merely for feathers is considered folly, for the surviving mate may pursue the culprit for many days.
