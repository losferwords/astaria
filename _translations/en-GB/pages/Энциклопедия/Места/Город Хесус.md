# Khesus

![[Assets/Images/Hiesus_City.jpg]]

## Main text

**Khesus** is a secluded city deep within the [[Пустыня Сехет|Sekhet Desert]] and the only dependable refuge for many days' journey. Caravans linking the north and south of [[Амон-Астат|the Empire of the Sun]] pass through it, so the condition of its wells concerns the pharaoh's officials no less than the security of a fortress.

The houses of Khesus stand close together, casting shade upon one another. Ancient cisterns lie beneath the streets, collecting rare rainwater and reserves delivered by caravan. The right to open them during a disaster belongs to the city council and temple keepers; here, a dispute over the distribution of water can become more dangerous than a dispute over silver.

The market sells dried dates, salt, remedies against the heat and cloth with which to cover the face. Guides mark their routes not only by the stars, but by the shape of dunes, gatherings of stones and the scent of the air before a sandstorm. To a stranger these signs appear indistinguishable, and a skilful guide is therefore never without work.

Khesus preserves many stories of caravans that never reached its gates. Sometimes the wind uncovers a chest, a weapon or part of an ancient road. Such discoveries quickly attract seekers—and people who would rather the past remained buried.
