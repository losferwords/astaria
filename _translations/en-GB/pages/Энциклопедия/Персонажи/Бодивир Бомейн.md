# Bodivir Beaumain

![[Assets/Images/Bodivir_Bomain.jpg]]

## Main text

**Bodivir Beaumain** was king of [[Лунаар|Lunaar]] from 75 to 98 NE. His contemporaries remembered him as a patient commander who preferred long preparation to a glorious offensive. During the war, he recovered the southern cities and provinces seized by [[Талассия|Thalassia]], then found the resolve to stop before victory became endless vengeance. The pact of non-aggression and trade alliance he concluded brought the borderlands several years of rare tranquillity.

Bodivir had three legitimate children, although only his firstborn, [[Роберт Бомейн|Robert]], was a son. The king paid close attention to the succession, but his relationships with his children remained difficult. The court expected them to become political figures long before they had the chance to become people in their own right.

In 98 NE, Bodivir went hunting in the forests above the [[Река Горсафон|Gorsafon]]. According to his companions, he suddenly saw something among the trees that no one else could perceive. The king ordered his retinue to remain where they were, entered the woodland alone and never returned. No body, weapon or sign of a struggle was found.

His disappearance gave rise to dozens of explanations. Some blame Thalassian enemies; others speak of an ancient debt owed by the Beaumains or a summons from Mercate. The [[Культ Меркаты|Cult of Mercate]] has confirmed none of these interpretations, and its silence has only strengthened the rumours. To Lunaar, Bodivir remains a king without a grave. To Robert, he is a father whose death was never established as fact.
