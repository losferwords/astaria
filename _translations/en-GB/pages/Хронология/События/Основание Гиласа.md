# Foundation of Gilas

> [!timeline] 854 ChE
> The [[Хтониды|Chthonid]] general [[Гиперион I|Hyperion]] establishes the colony of [[Гилас|Gilas]] in western [[Архея|Archaea]] and begins the construction of [[Город Аргос|Argos]]. Gilas will become an independent crowned republic only after the [[Падение Хтона|Fall of Chthon]] in 0 NE.
