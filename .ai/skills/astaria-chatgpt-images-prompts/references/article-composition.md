# Composition across encyclopedia categories

Read the requested category and, when relevant, the country-family or recurring-character section. English delivery and complete new-generation prompts remain governed by [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md). The compositions below adapt the author's Anima successes to ChatGPT Images briefs; they are not newly tested generation recipes.

## Characters and rulers

Choose one first read: face, gesture, action or silhouette. A portrait may be quiet; a specific task is useful when it expresses the person's role, not an obligation to make every character busy. Read appearance and occupation from the requested note, then select a few distinguishing anchors rather than a universal catalogue of beauty traits.

- A close or medium portrait suits a face, expression, identifying scar or headdress. Include a small amount of meaningful environment, and keep hands simple when visible.
- Full body suits distinctive anatomy, armor, clothing or equipment. Allow space for feet, ears, horns and defining objects; do not sacrifice the face to fit an unnecessarily large weapon.
- A ruler can be receiving a report, weighing a decision, observing their land, performing a rite or sitting in authority. The current Amatsu Rin and Hyperion show that a restrained portrait also works when face, clothing and place are specific enough.
- For mature men, select a few structural traits and age cues. Use the figure reference's skin and facial-hair treatment rather than stacking `rugged`, `perfect`, `handsome`, red eyes and mystical glamour.
- State one weapon state and a plausible grip. A spear held beside the body or a sword resting point-down is easier to read than unnecessary extreme foreshortening. Preserve an explicitly requested action rather than simplifying away its meaning.

## Gods

Begin with the deity's actual anatomy and one or two visible domain attributes. A human sovereign, a fox-headed goddess and a nature goddess need different silhouettes; divinity does not imply the same halo, armor, glowing eyes or lightning.

Let the environment echo the domain and the deity's relationship to it: a hand at a birch, a lantern among spirits, storm light around an armored figure, or a quiet place of authority when supported by the note. Tarun, Itsune, Velissa and Hyperion demonstrate this range. Show only powers established for the subject. Nudity, seduction, immense scale and supernatural glow are choices of the requested depiction, not generic divine requirements.

## Imitei

Make profession, service, characteristic equipment and the visible expression of power legible together. A Guardian's protective strength, an Onmyoji's ritual restraint and a Highlander's grounded sword stance should remain distinguishable without a caption.

For a requested standard pair, create separate male and female illustrations in portrait 9:16, following the article-format rule in SKILL.md. Share the cultural palette, materials, equipment function, background logic and intended clothing coverage; vary stance and face enough to make each individual convincing. Do not silently replace the woman's protective equipment with decorative exposure. Describe each prompt completely rather than referring to the other as `same as above`.

Keep effects subordinate to body and tools. One localized rune glow, current of mist or electrical path is often enough. Solve grip, blade/scabbard state and limb visibility before adding ornamental complexity.

## Organizations

Show coordinated work, distinct roles and an institutional setting. A scholar with an assistant sharing a manuscript communicates the Thuggee more precisely than generic robed people or an emblem. A negotiation, training exercise, diagnosis or ritual may serve another organization when supported by its note.

Usually two or three readable participants are sufficient; larger organizations do not require large foreground crowds. Assign each a position, action, partner or handled object, and plausible gaze. Preserve common insignia or materials without cloning faces, poses and costumes. Use exact heraldry only when supported by the note or supplied reference. An emblem-only design is appropriate when requested, not the default article cover.

In a working interior, allow architecture, equipment and background activity to explain the institution. `Ui-Djan_Brothel.jpg` is useful for spatial hierarchy; its intimate foreground scene is not a template for unrelated organizations.

## Peoples

Show culture through relationships or a shared activity as well as clothes. The Wends, Edzo and Ellians couples work through contact, expression and readable material traditions. Choose spouses, relatives, craft partners, travelers or an elder and apprentice according to the actual article; do not make every people a romantic man/woman pair.

Keep figures primary, with livelihood, terrain or settlement readable behind them. Describe visible facial variation, textile construction and practical objects rather than treating a real-world cultural analogy as a complete appearance specification. Do not give everyone identical faces, idealized bodies or warrior equipment unless that is the requested subject.

## Countries and coordinated families

For a country, show territory, livelihood and a coherent cultural material language. A wide landscape with a representative figure on one side is supported by Amato and Obitelj; the land must remain readable. A figure-free territorial composition is also valid when it better represents the note. Do not turn the country into a ruler portrait or the capital into a country cover with a giant foreground person.

For `country + people + Imitei + ruler + capital`, resolve the note's relevant links, such as `related_ethnicities`, `related_professions`, `deities`, `ruler` and `capital`. Build a compact shared family vocabulary:

- a recognizable cultural analogue refined by the actual lore;
- a small palette attached to cloth, wood, metal, stone or vegetation;
- recurring materials and ornament;
- geography and suitable light;
- a specific divine or civic motif where established.

Reuse these anchors across different visual jobs: territory for the country, lived relationships for the people, service and equipment for the Imitei, personal authority for the ruler, inhabited geography for the capital. Related images should look culturally connected without repeating one pose, one face or an identical background. Do not copy a deity's body or complete costume onto all followers.

## Bestiary and animal characters

Establish species, body plan, scale, posture and habitat before decorative detail. Include the distinctive silhouette: muzzle, beak, paws, wings, tail, scales or limb arrangement. Use landscape 16:9 for a bestiary article and portrait 9:16 for a named animal's character article, as required by SKILL.md. A creature image should not acquire a human beauty face merely to match the human portraits' style.

Morogrim is a useful positive reference for a bird's eye, beak and organized plumage. Musaka supplies useful pose/collar/habitat evidence but is a negative reference for the intended scruffy coat. For a furry subject, state the coat structure and uneven outline; for a snake-bodied or multi-limbed subject, separate the main body mass, limb origins and hand tasks in a readable pose.

Complex anatomy calls for deliberate staging, not endless exclusions. A close portrait can validly focus on a naga's face, as with Shasha, but does not demonstrate her full anatomy. If an article requires the complete body plan, choose a wider view and arrange limbs in separated groups; do not conceal the defining anatomy merely to avoid drawing it. Naga is an example of a compatible full creature silhouette, not a guarantee of correct future hands.

## Objects, flora, cuisine and concepts

| Article type | Main visual job |
| --- | --- |
| Objects and artifacts | Make shape, construction, material, damage and signature motif readable. Keep the object dominant; add a bearer or working hand only when use matters. Avoid a generic magical aura replacing specific construction. |
| Flora | Show growth habit, leaf/flower/fruit relationships, scale and habitat. Group foliage while keeping diagnostic parts readable. Use a field-guide layout or labels only when requested. |
| Cuisine | Show the recognizable dish, ingredients and culturally appropriate vessel or serving context. Keep food and pottery dimensional and illustrated; a human-face anime treatment is irrelevant. Do not invent ingredients as lore. |
| Knowledge, beliefs and phenomena | Translate the idea into a supported observable event, practice, artifact or relationship. If the concept cannot be depicted literally, identify a proposed symbolic interpretation in the Russian concept note rather than asserting new canon. |
| Events, chronology and literature | Select one actual moment, visible cast and spatial relationship from the named source. Preserve the passage's emotion, physical presence, clothing and available knowledge; a chapter illustration need not imitate an encyclopedia portrait. Read only the requested passage and context that changes the image. |

For places, cities and natural landscapes, use the composition guidance in SKILL.md and its environment references. A local map is a separate deliverable from an illustrative landscape; use its actual geographic requirements rather than inventing decorative cartography.

Read the shared context and authorial meta required by `.ai/AGENTS.md`, the relevant public character notes, and the identity/scene sections of [the existing trio reference](../../astaria-anima-prompts/references/losfy-losfer-misty.md). - Keep each subject's horns, ears, hair, eyes or blindfold, nails, clothing and weapon attached to that owner. In a shared scene, define stable viewer-left/center/right positions and one clear interaction. Scope exclusions to the affected person instead of suppressing another person's correct human ears or open eyes.
- Check current anatomy against both the requested note and the image. Misty's intended head has two long animal ears and no extra human pair; an old prompt asking for lowered ears does not prove that her current portrait depicts them lowered. Her English model vocabulary and Russian lore designation are explained in the trio reference.
- Ordinary present-day depictions are adults. Do not copy the historical sexualized `15yo` example or import an erotic pose, modern outfit, caption, companion or personal address from an unrelated example.
- Establish who is physically visible in the selected passage. A conversation or inner voice does not automatically add another body; an expressly requested dream or symbolic depiction can use a different arrangement with that interpretation stated briefly.

The author's persistent Anima ear problem is evidence about that workflow, not proof of an identical ChatGPT Images limitation or a solved problem. Inspect the new result before judging success. Do not read private notes or fill intentionally withheld biography merely to improve a portrait.
