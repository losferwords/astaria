# Beast Range

![[Assets/Images/Beast_Mountains.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Beast Range** is an ancient mountain chain in eastern [[Архея|Archaea]], named for its unusual resemblance to an animal's skull. Its northern and southern arms are known respectively as the Beast's upper and lower jaws.

Between those two jaws lies the deep [[Хангорская долина|Hangor Valley]], while water descending from the slopes feeds the [[Река Айлиз|Ayliz River]].
