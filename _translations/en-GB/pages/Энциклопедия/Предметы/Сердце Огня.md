# Heart of Fire

![[Assets/Images/Heart_of_Fire.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Heart of Fire** is a mysterious artefact: a sphere of living flame that radiates extraordinary heat. Such an object is almost impossible to find, and the archives presently preserve only one account of an encounter with a Heart. A company of adventurers—[[Урист Китин|Urist Kitin]], [[Томас Робинсон|Thomas Robinson]], [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] and [[Данис ибн Синай|Danis ibn Sinai]]—journeyed into [[Пепельные земли|the Ashlands]] in search of the artefact and, according to rumour, even succeeded in their quest.
