# Mangaia River

![[Assets/Images/Mangaia_River.jpg]]

The **Mangaia** is a river in eastern [[Полуостров Туануку|Tuanuku Peninsula]], rising at the foot of [[Вулкан Ротонуи|Rotonui Volcano]]. It passes forests and orchards before entering the [[Рассветное море|Dawn Sea]] beside capital [[Деревня Хамоа Хо|Hamoa Ho]].

For the [[Манаи|Manai]], it is road, water source and a living part of the tale of [[Икатерра|Ikaterra]]. Before their first long voyage, young helmspeople travel by boat from its source to the sea, learning how calm water gradually becomes tidal and salt.

Small gardens and communal plots of medicinal plants occupy its banks. Floodwater brings fertile silt but can carry away light buildings; homes stand on high ground and boats are tied to living trees with flexible ropes that can bear a sudden rise.

Warm springs and metallic-tasting water occur upstream. Healers use some deposits in ointments, but warn foreigners that similar colour does not mean identical properties: without a guide, one may draw water from a poisonous stream.

Mangaia's mouth is always busy with fishing boats and seagoing vessels. For Hamoa it is not merely a river by the capital, but a path joining volcano, forest, human home and open sea.
