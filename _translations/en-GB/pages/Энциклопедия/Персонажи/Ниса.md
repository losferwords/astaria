# Nisa

![[Assets/Images/Nisa_Nereid.jpg]]

## Main text

**Nisa** is a young [[Нереида|nereid]], able to alter her appearance and pass where a wall, a barred gate or an unfamiliar face would stop an ordinary human. In [[Город Бахара|Bahara]], she became notorious as a daring thief drawn into the hunt for the [[Сердце Огня|Heart of Fire]]. Her attempt to impersonate [[Мерката|Mercate]] failed, but meeting [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] changed her fate more profoundly than any theft.

After Bahara, Nisa travelled to [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]] and settled in the Wu family home near [[Город Чанг-Ша|Chang-Sha]]. For a time she assumed Mei's appearance, and the elder [[Шень Ву|Shen Wu]] allowed her to remain despite having understood the deception from the beginning. There Nisa raised her and Mei's daughter, [[Сао Ву|Sao Wu]], attempting for the first time to build a life in which she need not be forever running away.

Nisa is affectionate, impulsive and often fails to understand human boundaries, yet her actions cannot be dismissed as the whims of a predatory creature. Beneath her love of borrowed faces and dangerous games lies a powerful need to belong to someone—and to be accepted without having to wear yet another mask.
