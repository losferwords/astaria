# Arthur Beaumain

![[Assets/Images/Arthur_Bomain.jpg]]

## Main text

**Arthur Beaumain** is a young [[Лудаир|Ludair]] man from [[Город Эльданас|Eldanas]] and the illegitimate son of former king [[Бодивир Бомейн|Bodivir Beaumain]].

Despite his powerful father's protection, Arthur grew up in the shadow of his brother [[Роберт Бомейн|Robert]] and was kept away from affairs of the Crown. At court, he acquired the dubious reputation of a troublemaker and dissident.

Arthur's departure for the east did not grieve the nobility of Eldanas, but proved to be part of a dangerous design. With the aid of [[Кион-Чи|Qiong-Qi]], he infiltrated the [[Храм Дракона|Temple of the Dragon]] and sought to use [[Мерката|Mercate's]] scythe, which had been carried there, to kill [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Lang-An]]. His marksmanship, patience and talent for vanishing after an attack made him a formidable adversary even for the [[Имитей|Imithei]].

Arthur made several attempts to stop those seeking a way into the Dragon's Halls. At the Well of Fire, he seized the scythe and wounded mortals and the awakening god alike. After Lang-An was freed and Qiong-Qi's support was lost, Arthur laid down his arms. His path revealed how far an illegitimate son, condemned all his life to the shadow of the lawful heir, would go to claim a part of history for himself.
