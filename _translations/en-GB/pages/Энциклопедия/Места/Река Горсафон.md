# Gorsafon

![[Assets/Images/Gorsafon.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Gorsafon** is a major river of western [[Кельн|Keln]], gathering water from the [[Равнина Вайтфилд|Whitefield Plain]] and feeding the [[Болота Забвения|Marshes of Oblivion]]. [[Город Эльданас|Eldanas]], capital of [[Лунаар|Lunaar]], stands upon its banks; the river is therefore the chief road between the royal centre, northern settlements and the marsh frontier.

In its upper course the Gorsafon is swift and cold. In spring it carries ice fragments and uprooted shrubs, forcing boatmen to keep close to protected banks. Below Eldanas, the channel broadens, the current slows and its shores become damp and thick with dark reed.

The capital's river port follows a strict schedule of tides and changing watches. Timber, furs, grain, salt and people unwilling to test Whitefield's roads all travel on the Gorsafon. Every boat must carry a lantern: fog can descend within minutes, and without light oncoming vessels notice each other too late.

The disappearance of King [[Бодивир Бомейн|Bodivir Beaumain]] in woods upstream gave the Gorsafon a grim reputation. Hunters still argue about what he saw before entering the thicket. Camps on the northern bank therefore empty after sunset, and local people prefer not to follow a chance light between the trees.
