# Armar Sag

![[Assets/Images/Armar_Sag.jpg]]

## Main text

**Armar Sag** is the [[Мектиг|Mektig]] responsible for keeping order in [[Город Бахара|Bahara]] and the younger brother of the reigning Great Khan, [[Батар Саг|Batar Sag]].

From an early age, Armar drew inspiration from stories of [[Аксель Хан|Axel Khan's]] great deeds and regarded the ancient conqueror as the embodiment of Avgar honour. Life in his elder brother's shadow proved too quiet for the young warrior, however, and he devoted himself to imposing order upon the most lawless city in [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]].

Bahara taught Armar to distinguish a glorious legend from the daily exercise of power. He must deal with fires, feuds over debt, contraband and clashes between foreign fleets—problems for which a hammer alone is insufficient. He can be a stern judge and sends offenders to prison without hesitation, yet understands that fear cannot hold a harbour together by itself.

His relationship with Batar combines fraternal devotion with a concealed rivalry. Armar respects the Great Khan, but does not always share his commitment to peace and written law. Where Batar sees a state, his younger brother sometimes still sees an alliance of powerful clans obliged to defend their own honour. Their arguments can be bitter, but Armar has never permitted an outsider to turn those differences against the family.

As a Mektig, he favours heavy weapons and direct combat. City service has nevertheless taught him patience: it is often wiser to follow a criminal to their warehouse than defeat them in an alley, while closing a quay at the right moment may prevent a war more swiftly than a dozen duels. His subordinates value his readiness to appear at a disaster in person, although they remain wary of his temper.

Armar still reveres Axel Khan, but the longer he serves Bahara, the more difficult that image becomes. The ancient conqueror represents freedom and strength; the living city reveals each day the price ordinary people pay for another person's glory. That conflict makes Armar both a dangerous guardian of the present order and a man who may one day challenge it.
