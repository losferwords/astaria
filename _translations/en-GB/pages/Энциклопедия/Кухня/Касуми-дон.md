# Kasumi-don

![[Assets/Images/Kasumi-don.png]]

## The bowl of mist

**Kasumi-don** (霞丼, かすみどん) is a celebrated dish of the [[Река Кумокава|Kumokawa]] in southern [[Туманный остров|Mist Island]]: river fish grilled over charcoal and served on hot rice beneath a thick mushroom gravy. Its name means ‘bowl of mist’, recalling the steam rising from the rice and the island's mists.

The dish brings together familiar local ingredients: the river catch, rice from the fields of [[Луга Тамано|Tamano]] and mushrooms for the gravy. Visitors travel from elsewhere in Amato to taste Kasumi-don, and along the river it is prepared both as home-style fare and as a delicacy.

## Amanohama's home cooking

In [[Деревня Аманохама|Amanohama]], Kasumi-don is simple, home-style food. Fish from the charcoal grill is placed on hot rice and generously covered with mushroom gravy. Such bowls are served in local eating houses, including the tea house at the riverside yard, where boat crews and travellers eat while waiting for better weather or a boat travelling their way.

Those seeking especially skilful cooking and refined presentation are advised to travel north along the Kumokawa to Kasumito.

## The skill of Kasumito's cooks

[[Город Касумито|Kasumito]] is renowned for its refined preparation of Kasumi-don. Here, cooks compete in grilling the fish over charcoal, preserving its juiciness and making a thick gravy whose mushroom flavour complements the fish and rice.

Presentation receives as much attention as cooking: careful thought goes into arranging the fish and distributing the gravy over the hot rice. It is precisely this skill that draws visitors upriver. Each eating house has its admirers, ready to debate the best fish, gravy and presentation.

## Rivalry on the river

The cooks' rivalry remains friendly. Proprietors praise their dishes, while boat crews champion their favourite eating houses and recommend them to travellers.

Once a year, a river cooking competition is held with a communal tasting: residents and visitors sample Kasumi-don from different cooks and choose the best ‘bowl of mist’.
