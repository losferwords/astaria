# Axel Khan

![[Assets/Images/Axel_Khan.jpg]]

## Main text

**Axel Khan** was a [[Вактары|Vaktar]] who led an expedition to the southern continent of [[Архея|Archaea]] in search of glory and new conquests. After a succession of fateful events, he founded the realm known as [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]] upon the lands he had taken. He is remembered as the first Avgar.

Legend tells that Axel and his company encountered [[Хангор|Khangor]], god of craft, in the valley of the [[Река Айлиз|Ayliz River]]. The Vaktars attacked the unknown deity without hesitation, much to his astonishment. So deeply did the northern barbarians' courage and unity impress Khangor that, in the very heat of battle, he offered them immense power in return for their aid in building the [[Великая Кузня|Great Forge]]. Axel understood that his warriors could not overcome so mighty an adversary and accepted the god's bargain.

Thus Axel became Khangor's first disciple and, in time, an [[Имитей|Imithei]] of a new tradition which he himself named the [[Мектиг|Mektig]]. Several years later, Axel proclaimed himself ruler of the conquered country he called Wildlands. In the years that followed, the Khan continued his march south, now guided by his formidable mentor.

In the Vaktar tongue, *khan* means “strong man”. Axel's brothers-in-arms bestowed the title upon him because the word echoed the name of their new god. The warrior's true family name was lost across the centuries, and none of those close to him remained to tell of Axel's life before the expedition into the southern lands.

Countless legends and songs preserve the life and exploits of Axel Khan. He remains a defining figure in the making of the [[Авгарцы|Avgars]] and Wildlands themselves. The most widespread—and contradictory—tales concern his axe, [[Топор Смертоносец|Deathbringer]]. Rumour holds that Khangor forged the weapon as a gift to the Khan in honour of his great deeds and his service in raising the Great Forge.

No less mysterious was the mighty Khan's disappearance during a campaign through southern [[Кадир|Qadir]]. He set out for reasons unknown, taking only a small company of his closest warriors. None returned. They vanished without trace, leaving the new realm to the descendants of the Great Khan.
