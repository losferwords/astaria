# Thanatomachia

![[Assets/Images/Thanatomachia.jpg]]

## Main Text

The **Thanatomachia** is a ritual combat fought for the rank and powers of a [[Идеал|Paragon]].

When a soldier of [[Гилас|Gilas]] attains the rank of taxiarch—second only to a Paragon—they earn the right to enter a special duel known as the Thanatomachia. Their opponent is a captive [[Имитей|Imithei]], and the combat is fought to the death. If the soldier prevails, they claim the power of the defeated Imithei's [[Кристалл Души|Soul Crystal]] and are formally raised to the rank of Paragon. If the soldier dies, the captive Imithei is set free and must leave Gilas within a week.

The [[Эллийцы|Ellians]] are determined that the power of an Imithei should be won by honourable means. Captive Imithei are therefore treated with respect: they are well fed and allowed to walk, train, read and bathe. They nevertheless remain at all times under the strict guard of a large company of warders.

By attaining the rank of Paragon, a person becomes an Imithei in their own right. There are therefore cases in which Paragons themselves are taken prisoner by the state for various crimes.

The Thanatomachia is held in many of the great cities of Gilas.
