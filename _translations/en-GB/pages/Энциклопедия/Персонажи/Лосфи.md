# Losfy

![[Assets/Images/Losfy.jpg]]

Losfy is the name of a horned young woman spoken of by [[Онмёдзи|Onmyoji]] who have hunted with [[Лосфер|Losfer]] or shared a meal with him. The hunter himself is sparing with words, and retellings of his rare admissions leave more questions than answers. Some call Losfy his strange [[Они|Oni]]; others recall hearing of the [[Зеркальный Сон|Dream of Mirrors]].

She is described as young and long-haired, with dark eyes in which a red colour shows through, and a smile that reveals sharp teeth. In these stories an enormous two-handed sword, carefree laughter and unexpected tenderness appear side by side. They say she is certain of her own beauty, delights in teasing others and notices another person's sorrow before it is spoken aloud.

Among the Onmyoji, some joke that Losfer caught himself a “goat” instead of a serious Oni. Yet his good fortune in the most perilous hunts is often credited to her. The same accounts occasionally mention a shy young woman named [[Мисти|Misty]]. What these two are to one another, and how much of what is told should be taken literally, outsiders do not know.
