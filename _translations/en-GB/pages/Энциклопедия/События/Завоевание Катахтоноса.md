# Conquest of Katachthonos

![[Assets/Images/Conquest_of_Katachthonos.png]]

The conquest of [[Катахтонос|Katachthonos]] was the invasion by [[Талассия|Thalassia]] in 17 NE, culminating in the capture of [[Город Лефа|Lepha]] and its neighbouring settlements. It ended the independence of the community founded by surviving [[Хтониды|Chthonids]] after the [[Падение Хтона|Fall of Chthon]], turning their new refuge into a Thalassian colony.

## A Refuge After the Catastrophe

The first settlements of Katachthonos appeared on the [[Полуостров Таллас|Tallas Peninsula]] in 1 NE. People who had lost their island homeland built Lepha and attempted to begin again. Shared loss bound them together, but the young country had yet to become an established state.

Thalassia also descended from Chthonid civilisation. After Chthon's destruction, it declared independence and retained a maritime strength that the impoverished settlers could not match. Kinship did not protect Katachthonos from its more powerful neighbour's ambitions.

## The Capture of Lepha

In 17 NE, the Thalassians invaded Katachthonos. Lepha and the surrounding settlements offered little resistance and were occupied. Katachthonos was then declared a colony of Thalassia. The surviving chronology gives no detailed account of the fighting; its central outcome was the transfer of authority over the Chthonids' entire new homeland.

## The Legacy of Conquest

Dependence upon Thalassia shaped the country's future. Its fleet, commerce and protection sustained Katachthonos while limiting its independence. In later decades, Lepha became an administrative centre with its own court and laws, and dynastic ties strengthened the relationship between the two states.

Today, Katachthonos is ruled by [[Арета Лефийская|Areta of Lepha]], daughter of the Thalassian king [[Телемах II|Telemachus II]]. Their present relationship does not erase its beginnings: alliance and protection grew from the conquest of a people who had barely found a new refuge.
