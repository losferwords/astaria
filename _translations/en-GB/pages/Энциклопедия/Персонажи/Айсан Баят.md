# Aysan Bayat

![[Assets/Images/Aysan_Bayat.jpg]]

## Main text

**Aysan Bayat** is a [[Мектиг|Mektig]] woman from western [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]]. Among the [[Авгарцы|Avgars]], a female [[Имитей|Imithei]] is an extraordinary rarity.

Aysan was born into a wealthy family of craftspeople and grew up surrounded by comfort and the attentions of numerous relatives. Yet the young girl always longed for adventure and great deeds. Tales of [[Аксель Хан|Axel Khan's]] exploits inspired her so deeply that, at fifteen, she secretly left her family home and travelled to the [[Великая Кузня|Great Forge]] to meet [[Хангор|Khangor]] in person.

To her dismay, the doors of the Forge were sealed. For more than a year Aysan tried in vain to reach the deity, until chance compelled Khangor to take notice of the courageous young woman and her fierce will to live. Her conversation with the Smith-God gave her new strength and revealed a different path through life in Wildlands.

Aysan Bayat subsequently became known as a wandering mercenary who accepts the most difficult commissions—including contracts to kill.
