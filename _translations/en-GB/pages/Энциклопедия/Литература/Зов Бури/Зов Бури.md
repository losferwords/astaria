# Call of Thunder

![[Assets/Images/Thunder_Call_Saga.jpg]]

The saga recounts the life of a hero of the [[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]], the [[Горец|Highlander]] [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth the Stormcaller]]. Its story is told in the third person through the voices of several people who knew him in life.
