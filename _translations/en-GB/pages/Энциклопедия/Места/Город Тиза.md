# Tiza

![[Assets/Images/Tiza_City.jpg]]

## Main text

**Tiza** is the capital of [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]], standing upon the northern shore of [[Озеро Нехмет|Lake Nekhmet]]. It is regarded as the foremost seat of learning in the Empire of the Sun: archivists, copyists, antiquarians and scholars work here, and to them a dispute over the origin of a ruin may matter more than an intrigue at court.

Tiza owes much of its renown to its libraries and collections of maps. Here [[Хонсу-Намун|Khonsu-Namun]] and his mentor [[Мерес-Аран|Meres-Aran]] studied the inheritance of the [[Археи|Archaeans]], and accounts of forgotten temples spread across the continent in the hands of travellers. After the Great Drought, the city also became one of the places where the renewed state seeks to reconcile its scholarly tradition, the memory of rebellion and the authority of a reformed capital.

The pharaoh's palace, the chief sanctuary of [[Аст|Ast]] and the old archives form the ceremonial heart of Tiza. At dawn, light passes between the tall columns; soon afterwards the squares fill with scribes, petitioners and officials responsible for the distribution of water and grain. Imperial power appears majestic here, yet its daily work consists of thousands of tablets, measures and decisions.

The lower quarters descend towards the lake quays. Fishers, boatfolk and merchants live beside scholars, and a rare manuscript is sometimes found not in a library, but among forgotten cargo in a warehouse. For an antiquarian, Tiza may be the beginning of a great expedition; for a courtier, it is a place where one misread record can alter the fate of an entire province.
