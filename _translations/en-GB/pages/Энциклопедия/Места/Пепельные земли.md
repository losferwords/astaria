# Ashlands

![[Assets/Images/Ashlands.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Ashlands** are a region of southern [[Архея|Archaea]] rendered uninhabitable by violent terrain and extreme heat. Reaching them is difficult, for the [[Горный хребет Спина Археи|Spine of Archaea]] separates them from inhabited country.

Beyond the range lie grey screes, traces of ancient eruptions and volcanoes that seem dormant only until the first earthquake. Caves preserve rock art, rare minerals and the remains of expeditions that failed to survive heat, falling stone and local predators.

The Ashlands are considered one of the last refuges of [[Небесный дракон|sky dragons]]. The [[Сердце Огня|Heart of Fire]] once lay within a volcano's crater, surrounded by heaps of weapons and guarded by an adult dragon. A fragment of [[Топор Смертоносец|Deathbringer]] was found there as well, giving the barren land a place in several divine histories.
