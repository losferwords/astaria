# Chapter 001 — The Highlander's Soul

*The Highlander's Soul*

[[Лисандра мак Рейн|Lisandra]] names her son [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth]]. The saga's first chapter is narrated by the hero's mother. She tells him of his homeland, of the proud [[Гойдаир|Goidair]], and of how he will one day become a [[Горец|Highlander]], one of the mightiest of their people. Highlanders command the power of storms and possess extraordinary strength and longevity.

*79–97 NE*

Children born during thunderstorms are called the Named and spend their lives preparing for the rite known as the [[Рунштоирм|Runstoirm]]. Every spring, the Named gather upon the slopes of the [[Горный хребет Грань Тьмы|Edge of Darkness]], stand in a circle, join hands and await the storm. The trial begins when lightning strikes one of them. The future Highlanders must keep the bolt from reaching the earth and pass it through the chain formed by their bodies. The ordeal continues until the lightning's charge has been divided equally amongst them all. Despite years of preparation, the Named often lose consciousness or die. The fewer who remain conscious at the end, the greater the survivors' command of the elements. In Kenneth's trial, he alone remained awake to the end and received the lightning's entire power.
