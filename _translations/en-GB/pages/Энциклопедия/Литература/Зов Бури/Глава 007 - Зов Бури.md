# Chapter 007 — Call of Thunder

*Call of Thunder*

When [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth]] learnt that the armies of [[Гилас|Gilas]] would storm the city, he resolved to join its defence. Yet his long captivity amongst the [[Эллийцы|Ellians]] had taught him that Atafet's walls could not be held. Even with [[Кассиопея Атанатос|Cassia]], he could not turn the battle. The [[Горец|Highlander]] therefore decided to stop not the siege, but the war itself. He would use his new sword atop [[Гора Атафет|Mount Atafet]] to call a storm from the northern lands of [[Кельн|Keln]], so drought-stricken [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]] might feel rain once more. Cassia chose to help him climb.

The ascent was arduous. The [[Имитей|Imithei]] contended with sheer rock, wind, heat and cold, but reached the summit late on the second day. Everything now depended upon Kenneth. He had trained from birth to command the weather, yet calling a thunderstorm across the sea and into the desert would demand every measure of his strength and skill. Gathering his resolve, the Highlander stood upon the snowbound slope, raised his sword to the heavens and called to the storms of the north.

At that moment, Cassia received a vision and understood the summoning's price: Kenneth would die of exhaustion in her arms. Horrified, she knew it was already too late to change anything. Then she remembered the different future she had seen on [[Остров Хтон|Chthon Island]], before Kenneth built the raft and altered what had been ordained. To interrupt him would save his life, but rob thousands of hope for an end to drought and war. Cassia therefore chose to remove herself from the vision, hoping that this would change the fatal conclusion. With a heavy heart, the [[Профитис|Profitis]] turned her blind gaze upon her companion one final time and left the summit.

By dawn, clouds appeared upon the horizon and spread across Amon-Astat from [[Озеро Митра|Lake Mitra]] to [[Озеро Кафер|Lake Kafer]]. The battle for [[Город Атафет|Atafet]] stopped as both sides stared in wonder at the approaching storm. Within hours, the thunder front covered the empire. Then, as if upon command, lightning began striking Mount Atafet and rain fell.

After five years of agonising drought, the life-giving torrent restored hope to the defenders. No less astonished, the army of Gilas halted its assault to await the storm's passing and regroup. By the following evening, the army of Pharaoh [[Рамхотеп II|Ramhotep II]] arrived at Atafet demanding peace between Gilas and Amon-Astat. Persephone lacked authority to settle matters of diplomacy, and the siege was suspended pending negotiations.

A week later, the pharaoh came personally to Achaeus, apologised for the rebels' invasion, paid tribute to Gilas and secured peace in exchange for the return of the lands conquered by the Ellian army.

The [[Война Жаждущих|War of the Thirsty]] was over.
