# Soul Crystal

![[Assets/Images/Power_Crystal.jpg]]

**Soul Crystals**, also known as Soul Stones, are gemstones capable of greatly magnifying a human being's mental and physical abilities. It is believed that such a stone forms, or is already present, within the head of every animal in [[Астария|Astaria]], although large specimens are exceedingly rare. The crystal's development is somehow connected to the emergence of those known as the [[Имитей|Imithei]].

Soul Stones may be found in many colours, though shades of red are mentioned most often: claret, violet, crimson and dark orange. Their surface is invariably smooth and their shape either oval or round. A crystal's diameter may range from that of a grain of sand to that of a cherry.

Some scholars believe that the culture of the ancient [[Археи|Archaeans]] was intimately bound to these crystals, but no conclusive evidence has yet been found.
