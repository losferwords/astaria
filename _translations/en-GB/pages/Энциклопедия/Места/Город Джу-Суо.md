# Dju-Suo

![[Assets/Images/Dju-Suo.jpg]]

## Main text

**Dju-Suo**, one of the largest cities in [[Астария|Astaria]], stands on the northern shore of [[Озеро Чанг|Lake Chang]] and serves as the capital of [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]]. Its districts bring together palace ceremony, monastic discipline and markets supplied from every corner of the Empire — all beneath a rigorous system of permits capable of turning a simple crossing of the street into an interview with an official.

The city's name means ‘Home of the [[Джу|Dju]]’. According to tradition, this was the place to which [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Lang-An]] led his followers some three thousand years ago.

The capital's holiest site is the [[Храм Дракона|Temple of the Dragon]]: sanctuary of Lang-An, palace of the empress and school for warriors aspiring to become [[Аватар|Avatars]]. Around it grew the houses of the great families, archives, workshops and entire quarters whose lives depend upon the ceremonies of the court.

During the nomad advance, Dju-Suo received refugees from [[Город Найян|Nayan]] and [[Город Чанг-Ша|Chang-Sha]]. At the same time, the capital endured a curfew and a palace crisis. Empress [[Сунь Ляньши|Sun Lianshi]] became, in effect, a prisoner of her own guard, while the wounding of the Great Dragon deprived the city of its accustomed protection.

After Lang-An's deliverance, the capital began to open itself to the world once more. The adviser [[Чень Ляньши|Chen Lianshi]] urged the restoration of cultural and mercantile ties with neighbouring realms, while the empress's meeting with [[Гиперион I|Hyperion]] raised the prospect of a southern road reaching [[Гилас|Gilas]]. Dju-Suo remains cautious and exactingly bureaucratic, but can no longer afford its former seclusion.
