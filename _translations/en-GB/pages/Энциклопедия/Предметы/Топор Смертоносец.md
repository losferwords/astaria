# Deathbringer

![[Assets/Images/Deathbringer.jpg]]

## Main text

**Deathbringer** is the legendary battleaxe of [[Аксель Хан|Axel Khan]], forged by [[Хангор|Khangor]] as a reward for the warrior's aid in building [[Великая Кузня|the Great Forge]]. In Avgar song, its making marks the moment when the northern warlord ceased to lead a single retinue and became first among the Mighty.

The axe was immense, yet finely balanced, allowing blows no ordinary weapon of its weight could deliver. Its edge remained keen after striking armour and stone, while its long haft endured the full strength of its wielder. Later accounts claim that Khangor used [[Золотая сталь|golden steel]], though no surviving tale explains where he obtained so rare a material.

The back of the axe was said to bear signs resembling both runes and a smith's marks. Some singers call them a promise of strength; others, a reckoning of the debts Axel accepted with the gift. Depictions differ so greatly that craftsmen dispute even the shape of the blade, yet every Avgar child knows Deathbringer from the songs.

The artefact became renowned for more than its crushing power. To the first [[Мектиг|Mektigs]], it proved that a worthy warrior should bear a weapon shaped to their nature, not merely the costliest blade. That custom endures: a Mektig fashions their own axe and accepts responsibility for all that is done with it.

Deathbringer vanished with its master. From time to time, merchants offer shards of golden metal or axes supposedly unearthed at one of Axel's old encampments, but no discovery has been recognised as genuine. To some, the relic's return would herald the rebirth of ancient glory; to others, it would threaten the return of the old conquests as well.
