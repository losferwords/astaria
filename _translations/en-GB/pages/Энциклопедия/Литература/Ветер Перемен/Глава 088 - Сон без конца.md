# Chapter 088 — The Endless Sleep

*[[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]], 106 NE, spring*

As the travellers examined [[Шанкар Марджари|Shankar Marjari's]] body, a noise came from the kitchen. [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]], [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] and [[Мэй Ву|Mei]] rushed inside, but saw only a figure fleeing into the jungle. The pursuit failed: the woman was too well prepared and too fortunate.

[[Урист Китин|Urist]] found a stone dagger driven into the doorframe. The weapon broke when he tried to remove it. [[Велимир Сажин|Velimir]] sensed a dangerous power in the stone and sealed the fragment inside enchanted earth so it could harm no one else.

They decided to carry the body to the temple for the rites. Tom set [[Мусака|Musaka]] upon the trail, but the dog unexpectedly pointed to the dead man's neck, where a tiny puncture resembled the mark of the stone dagger's point.

Seven more bodies bearing the same marks waited in the temple. Gilja began the funerary rite, but blood from a severed artery flowed hot as though from a living person. The travellers staunched it and realised that the victims were not dead, but plunged into a profound sleep.

Tom entered one victim's dream and found not an ordinary vision, but an endless circle of daily life: waking, routine, an accidental sleep and waking once again. Then the trap closed around him as well. He escaped with difficulty and told his friends what he had seen.

They needed answers from the gods. Urist called upon [[Хангор|Khangor]]. The smith-god spoke of plans for the end of the world: he wished to leave Astaria aboard the [[Великая Кузня|Great Forge]], but needed [[Калипсо|Calypso]]; [[Аст|Ast]] wished to summon the Archaeans; Mercate intended to survive the end, though she did not yet fully understand how. Of the [[Кузница Плоти|Flesh Forge]], Khangor spoke cautiously: perhaps it only needed cleaning, washing and oiling, but entering such a mechanism without understanding it was dangerous.

Mei contacted [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Lang-An]]. He said the fugitive resembled a Dju, but every Avatar was accounted for. Apart from Mei and [[Бао Си|Bao Xi]], none should be outside the Empire of Fire. The dragon that had burnt the village might have come from the Ashlands or been one of [[Винтра|the Winter Goddess's]] consorts. He was also troubled by Meilong's illness and the sea monster now loose from its chains.

Tom contacted Mercate. She warned him of the dagger's danger and explained that such sleep usually afflicted native peoples, monsters and beastfolk. The victims had one consolation: they did not age in this state, so there was still time.
