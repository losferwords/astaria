# Chapter 057 — The House of Wu

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 105 NE, spring*

[[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] remained above in the Archaean temple with [[Кирида Ариса|Kirida Arisa]], while the others were trapped at the bottom of the well. Arisa tried to snatch the [[Чёрный Вестник|Black Harbinger]] from him, but failed. She claimed to know the way, and Tom led the refugees after her towards the exit.

The route across the bridges was riven with cracks and gaps. Tom helped people over them, but [[Кион-Чи|Qiong-Qi]] caught some of those fleeing, and not everyone escaped. The survivors ascended in a lift and emerged into the forbidden forest. [[Ошу Мати|Oshu]] had disappeared, and no one knew whether she had found her way out.

In Shenyan, the refugees heard their friends' voices. [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]], [[Мэй Ву|Mei]] and [[Урист Китин|Urist]] found them, and together they reached the forest's edge. Before them stretched the empty fields of [[Город Чанг-Ша|Chang-Sha]], where no one worked, as though the city had been holding its breath for a long while.

At the Wu family home, Mei was greeted as though she had never left. Aunt Wei asked why she had not brought cabbage back from the market and sent her to her room. It transpired that for the past six months [[Ниса|Nisa]] had been living in Chang-Sha in Mei's likeness.

Nisa explained how she had come to be there. The refugees were settled in guest rooms, while [[Мэйлун|Meilong]] was sent to the stables so that he would not frighten anyone. At breakfast, Urist told [[Шень Ву|Shen Wu]] of the fall of [[Город Найян|Nayan]] and the approaching nomads. Shen Wu was alarmed, but said he had already sent to the capital for reinforcements.

Mei saw her mother, [[Ао Ву|Ao Wu]]. She seemed to recognise her daughter, yet silently continued her embroidery. Meanwhile, Tom and Gilja prepared a ritual to find [[Артур Бомейн|Arthur Beaumain]], while Mei went to the kitchen for herbs and met a little girl who called her Mother.
