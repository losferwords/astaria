# Umar ibn La-Ahad

![[Assets/Images/Umar_Ibn_La-Ahad.jpg]]

## Main text

**Umar ibn La-Ahad** is one of the first [[Мститель|Avengers]], former vizier to [[Астиаг II|Astyages II]] and Eternal Prince of eastern [[Кадир|Qadir]]. Where Abu Ali has always trusted military discipline and Malik the art of the blade, Umar prefers negotiation, knowledge and a road prepared with care.

After the [[Раскол Кадира|Schism of Qadir]], it was he who persuaded nobles and common people alike to accept the rule of the three Eternal Princes. Umar assumed authority over the eastern [[Пустыня Халисат|Khalisat Desert]] and the cities around [[Озеро Рашах|Lake Rashah]] and [[Озеро Альсалим|Lake Alsalim]]. He also undertook negotiations with the Avgars to end the war which the former king had intended to continue.

In [[Город Кафат|Kafat]], Umar founded the Way of the Desert. His pupils study not only the command of sand, but geography, languages, the qualities of minerals, the night sky and the arts of survival. The prince believes that an Avenger must understand both land and people before attempting to bend either to their will.

Umar speaks softly, listens with care and seldom reveals the final purpose of a conversation. Over the centuries, he has turned patience into a political weapon: while the prince merely asks questions, his counterpart will often name their own price, concession and fear. Some regard him as the most merciful of the first Avengers. Others consider him the most dangerous precisely because his pressure is so difficult to perceive.

The isolation of Kafat sorely tested his principles. The desert his school had regarded as an ally nearly destroyed the city when its roads were cut off. Since that crisis, Umar has devoted greater attention to simple provisions, trusted guides and relations with neighbouring peoples, acknowledging that knowledge of the world is worthless when a ruler ceases to hear those who live within it.
