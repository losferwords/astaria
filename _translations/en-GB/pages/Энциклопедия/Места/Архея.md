# Archaea

![[Assets/Images/Amon-Astat_Desert_2.jpg]]

## Main text

**Archaea** is Astaria's southern continent, a land of dazzling sunlight, days-long downpours and roads that can disappear beneath the sand in a single night. Scorching deserts and highlands cover much of the continent, but the image is deceptive: [[Регион Элессия|Elessia]] lies green in the west, fertile country follows the river valleys, and forests and humid jungles cloak the eastern slopes.

Archaea's climate changes with startling speed. A caravan may leave the cold shadow of the mountains, find itself among sun-scorched stones by noon and hear the roar of a tropical river several days later. The continent's southernmost reaches are almost uninhabitable. In the [[Пепельные земли|Ashlands]], heat rises through cracks in the earth, volcanic ash veils the horizon and the few surviving springs can become the cause of desperate wars between travellers.

The great ranges of the [[Горный хребет Спина Археи|Archaean Spine]], the [[Горный хребет Шафар|Shafar Mountains]] and the [[Пламенные горы|Fiery Mountains]] divide the continent into natural regions and govern the courses of its rivers. Their passes connect the realms, but are never entirely safe: abandoned sanctuaries, deep caverns and trails first marked upon maps before the [[Падение Хтона|Fall of Chthon]] may all be encountered among the peaks.

The [[Хтониды|Chthonids]] named the continent after discovering a wealth of ruins left by the vanished [[Археи|Archaean]] civilisation. Some have long since become the foundations of new cities; others still lie far beyond any road. Archaean stone scarcely yields to time, and the halls beneath it draw explorers, adventurers and hunters of relics. The fortunate return with their discoveries to archivists, sages and students of antiquity. Others wish that the knowledge they uncovered had never seen the light.
