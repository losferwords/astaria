# Fialsakhra Bay

![[Assets/Images/Fialsakhra_Bay.jpg]]

## Main text

**Fialsakhra** is a deep bay of the [[Хтоническое море|Chthonic Sea]] in central [[Архея|Archaea]]. Its waters cut between two great deserts: [[Пустыня Сехет|Sekhet]] lies to the west and [[Пустыня Халисат|Khalisat]] to the east. The bay is therefore both a natural boundary and the shortest passage between the two dry lands.

The eastern shore belongs to [[Кадир|Qadir]]. There stands the port of [[Город Самаан|Samaan]], receiving seagoing vessels and caravans from the heart of the Seven Gardens. The western coast is drier and more sparsely settled; fishers, smugglers and captains unwilling to explain the origin of their cargo use its coves.

By day the water reflects the white sky and appears almost colourless, but by evening it turns coppery. The wind can suddenly carry hot dust from either shore, hiding the sea in a yellow haze. Seasoned sailors lower their sails and wait, for it becomes nearly impossible to judge the distance to the rocks.

The bay is rich in fish and shells used to make purple and rose dyes. Salt is gathered from the shallows, while the rocky coves yield fragments of old wrecks. Fialsakhra's currents often carry objects far from the site of a wreck, so a seal or weapon found upon one shore may lead an investigator to a story that took place on the other many years before.
