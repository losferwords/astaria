# Chapter 081 — Blood upon the Slab

*[[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]], 106 NE, winter*

The travellers slew a naga. Upon its body, [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] found a shard of black stone and a strange counterfeit Lunaar coin. The trail led them to an ancient temple of [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari]], where dark blood stained a stone slab.

Inside the temple, they discovered a concealed passage leading down. The chambers below had once housed acolytes: cells, silence, the scent of old prayers and blood. Sajar Viraan sat inside one cell. The merchant greeted the travellers gladly and asked for time to offer his morning prayer.

When Sajar returned, he asked Gilja to help perform a rite to summon Shubhankari. Gilja flew into a rage upon learning that the merchant meant to use blood disgorged by a vetal during the night. To a Raksha, this was ignorance bordering upon an insult to the goddess. His friends restrained him from murder and gave Sajar a chance to atone.

The moon favoured the rite. Shubhankari appeared and spoke with the travellers about vetals and their role in the Great Harvest. She commanded Gilja to find the vial of her blood, which someone had put to use through another's hand. Then a wave of blood swept from the forest into the temple, and the goddess absorbed it.

[[Урист Китин|Urist]] asked about the voices in [[Мэйлун|Meilong's]] mind, and Shubhankari told him of a sea monster. It offered no comfort, only further confirmation that even Mei's dragon was bound to a calamity growing far beyond the borders of a single country.

After the rite, Urist demanded that Sajar immediately show them the way to the cart bearing the sakura petals. The merchant resisted, and Urist used force, crippling him. Thus Sajar's piety ended not in forgiveness, but in the necessity of leading the travellers back to the site of his failure.
