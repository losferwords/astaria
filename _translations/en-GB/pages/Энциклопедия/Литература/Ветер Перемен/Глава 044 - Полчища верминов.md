# Chapter 044 — Hordes of Vermin

*[[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], 104 NE, autumn*

The San family's procession entered [[Деревня Улехей|Ulehey]]. [[Урист Китин|Urist]] met it with suspicion, but the truth soon became clear: they were indeed [[Ли Сан|Li San's]] kin, come for the wedding after seeing the lights cast by [[Мэй Ву|Mei]]. The feast began anew, as though the village wished to finish celebrating before night overtook it.

Meanwhile, [[Ошу Мати|Oshu Mati]] struggled down from the stone rise and reached the village. An Avgar guard noticed her and carried her into Ulehey. [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] once more found himself upon the boundary between life and death, now as healer rather than victim. He fought for Oshu and brought her back to consciousness.

Then the cloud of dust drew closer. From the watchtower, [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] discerned its source: hordes of [[Вермин|vermin]] were advancing upon the village. He raised the alarm, and in a single moment the celebration became a defence.

The people fought bravely. Barely conscious, Oshu tried to instil fear in the vermin that burst into the tent where Gilja treated her. She broke their will, and the creatures collapsed in terror. Then she took the [[Корона Душ|Crown of Souls]] and extracted an active crystal from one vermin's head.

Gilja scattered his spices, hoping to quell the enemy's fury as he had once altered human moods. They had no effect upon the vermin. Worse, the defenders' battle fervour faded, and before the subterranean creatures' savage assault, people began to lose heart.

Ulehey, which had lately celebrated a wedding, now faced a true siege. This time the enemy came neither beneath Dju banners nor with nomad threats, but from the very depths of Shafar.
