# Chapter 026 — The Harvest Has Stopped

*[[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], 104 NE, spring*

[[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] awoke after his friends had already vanished into the city's troubles. To find them, he used a hunting rite that had previously aided his preparations, though he had never seen it brought to completion. The world turned white and translucent, like cloth stretched between life and death. Only the quarry remained vivid, marked by the blood used in the rite.

His quarry was [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]].

The trail led Gilja to the upper quarter and the prison doors, but he could not open them. He could only wait until the prey began to move. When Mei emerged, an invisible presence attacked her. It drank her blood, drawing closer to the visible world with every drop.

Thus [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari]] appeared before the travellers. She could not be fought by ordinary means. They could only speak, plead and hope the goddess would listen. Gilja persuaded her to spare Mei.

Their exchange revealed a dreadful truth: the Harvest had stopped. For almost a year, no blood had reached the goddess. To [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]], this was more than the loss of a rite; it meant that the very order of worship had broken. Shubhankari restored some of Mei's strength and departed, leaving more questions than answers.

On the road to [[Бордель Халык-Бындыр|Halyk-Byndir]], dust rose above the harbour. [[Умар ибн Ла-Ахад|Umar ibn La-Ahad]] had come to [[Город Бахара|Bahara]] to lift the blockade. The explanations of [[Данис ибн Синай|Danis ibn Sinai]], who remained in [[Кадир|Qadir]] and was connected to [[Хонсу-Намун|Khonsu's]] commission, helped clarify the situation in [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]].

The travellers intervened, urging both sides to stop before the dispute became a war. [[Неарх Мелит|Nearch Melit]], Umar and the heroes continued their talks aboard the *Typhon*. The blockade might yet be lifted by words—but words had to arrive before the blade.
