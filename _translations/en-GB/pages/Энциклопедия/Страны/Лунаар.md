## The Kingdom of Night

Lunaar is the Kingdom of Night in north-western [[Кельн|Keln]], founded by the [[Лудаир|Ludair]] in 1457 ChE alongside the building of [[Город Эльданас|Eldanas]]. Their ancestors travelled ever farther west until they reached the cold [[Равнина Вайтфилд|Whitefield Plain]], dark forests and the shores of the Sunset Sea. This austere country taught them to prize strong walls, the long shot, a measured word and the light in a window that guides a traveller through the snow.

## A crown beneath the veil of night

The fortified city of Eldanas, upon the [[Река Горсафон|Gorsafon]], is the capital. From here, the Crown watches the river roads, northern outposts and lands Lunaar has contested with its neighbours for centuries. The Ludair seldom begin a war without long preparation, but their archers, trackers and night watches can outlast an enemy and strike just as safety seems assured.

Lunaar's history includes wars of conquest against [[Талассия|Thalassia]] and the [[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]]. Beyond its borders, Ludair composure is often mistaken for coldness and caution for concealed hostility. The people themselves see the matter differently: those who live beside marsh, icy wind and long night cannot afford to squander food, arrows or promises.

## Mercate and the Cult

[[Мерката|Mercate]], goddess of night, secrets and dreams, is the country's chief patron. The [[Культ Меркаты|Cult of Mercate]] predates the kingdom and has never been wholly subject to the Crown. Its priestesses serve as counsellors, keepers of tradition and intermediaries in matters that cannot be settled by daylight. The most enigmatic servants of the goddess are the [[Тень|Shadows]], who return from frigid [[Антра|Antra]] no longer quite the people they once were.

This coexistence creates a fragile balance. The king governs land and army but cannot command the Cult; the priestesses guide Lunaar's spiritual life but refrain from claiming the throne openly. After Bodivir vanished, power passed to the young [[Роберт Бомейн|Robert Beaumain]], and the question of where the Cult's care ends and its influence begins has become urgent once more.

## Daily life

Ludair houses are low and warm, built with heavy shutters and a common hearth. In the evening people mend bows, tell stories, embroider constellations in silver thread and listen to the wind for signs of weather moving in from Antra. A guest receives hot food and a place by the fire, but trust must be earned. In Lunaar, a person is judged not by the splendour of their name, but by whether they returned for a companion in the dark.

For an explorer, the kingdom is filled with quiet mysteries: old roads across Whitefield, abandoned watchtowers and sanctuaries where ravens gather before the priestesses arrive. Danger is seldom announced twice, yet an oath once given may outlive both war and death.
