# Chapter 007 — Persephone's Ship

*Gilas, 103 NE, autumn*

Argos prepared for its harvest festival. Music, food, flowers and traders filled the streets as the capital tried briefly to forget fear. For the fugitives, the crowds were blessing and threat: they hid faces, but drew more guards outdoors.

With Nikandros's plan ruined, [[Урист Китин|Urist]], [[Томас Робинсон|Thomas]], [[Хонсу-Намун|Khonsu]] and [[Мэй Ву|Helena]] again sought [[Данис ибн Синай|Danis ibn Sinai]], known locally as Philoctetes. They found him not at work but under blows. Guards questioned him about the troublemakers, each demand accompanied by violence. Former prisoners themselves, the travellers had little room for caution.

They intervened. The fight in the cramped room was brief and brutal. The guards fell, and the beaten, shaken Danis became part of their flight for good. His records, research and borrowed name were already threatened; staying would be fatal.

At night they descended to the [[Река Тавропос|Tavropos]], intending to steal aboard a merchant vessel bound for [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]]. Behind them the festival roared; beside the water remained only splashing, rigging and low sailors' voices.

Then a trireme reached the bank, carrying [[Персефона Раптис|Persephone Raptis]]. The woman they had escaped in Phibas again stood between them and freedom. She saw them and came ashore. In open combat they had no hope: the [[Идеал|Ideal]] scattered them with frightening ease.

Yet strength does not always keep pace with audacity. Amid pain and nocturnal confusion, the fugitives deceived Persephone. Instead of fleeing her ship, they took it. With Danis aboard, they sailed from Argos.

Arena, guards, Phibas, Mystras, Argos and Persephone's anger remained behind. Ahead lay Amon-Astat, land of light, sand and new debts. That night the river carried them out of Gilas, and for the first time the wind smelt not only of pursuit, but of change.
