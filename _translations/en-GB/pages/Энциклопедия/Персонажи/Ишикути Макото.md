# Ishikuchi Makoto

![[Assets/Images/Isikuti_Makoto.jpg]]

## Main text

**Ishikuchi Makoto** (石口 誠) grew up in the port of [[Город Осуми|Osumi]], within a family that had distrusted gods, the [[Имитей|Imithei]] and every manifestation of the unseen for generations. Makoto chose the opposite path: he devoted his life to [[Ицунэ|Itsune]], entered the priesthood and eventually assumed responsibility for a small coastal temple.

Makoto possesses none of an Onmyoji's power, but knows the funerary rites, temple archives and signs of the sea well. His congregation valued his patience: he could listen to a frightened person for hours without turning their fear into an accusation. He worked especially closely with the shrine's guardian, [[Кирида Ариса|Kirida Arisa]], although her absolute faith often clashed with his habit of seeking an earthly explanation for every miracle.

In 103 NE, Thalassian pirates plundered the temple. Makoto and Arisa were taken captive, and an Ishikuchi family relic vanished among the stolen treasures. For several months, the priest scrubbed the deck and waited upon the crew. He came to recognise that the sailors were not all alike, yet the humiliation he suffered proved stronger than reason and hardened fear into bitter hostility towards everything foreign.

Makoto escaped when ships of [[Гилас|Gilas]] attacked near the shores of [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]]. He found himself alone, without the relic and with no knowledge of Arisa's fate. Returning home was no longer a simple journey: first he meant to find his companion and recover what his family had entrusted to the temple.

Makoto's courtesy remains impeccable, but his wariness is easy to discern beneath it. He is too quick to mistake an unfamiliar custom for dishonour and reacts painfully to mockery of Amato. His travels may either entrench this prejudice or remind the priest of Itsune of the patience with which he once restored peace to others.
