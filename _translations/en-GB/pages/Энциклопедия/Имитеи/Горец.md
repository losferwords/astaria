# Highlander

## Main text

The [[Горный хребет Грань Тьмы|Edge of Darkness]] rises in the west of [[Кельн|Keln]]. East of its passes, among the [[Предгорья Битхорн|Bithorn Foothills]], the free [[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]] of the [[Гойдаир|Goidair]] have dwelt since time beyond memory. Here, a child born during a thunderstorm is believed to have been noticed by the sky itself. Such children are called the Named. They are honoured by their communities and prepared from youth for the arduous path of chieftain and protector.

## Training

Initiation takes place during the [[Рунштоирм|Runstoirm]], when the Named receive a true bolt of lightning into their own bodies and hold its force within a shared circle. One who endures the trial becomes a Highlander—an Imithei of [[Тарун|Tarun]], able to channel the storm through a weapon.

A Highlander fights with sudden, overwhelming force. A heavy blow can sunder a formation; lightning can be driven into an enemy's weapon; and upon open ground the sky itself becomes an ally. Yet a Highlander's foremost duty is not war. They lead clans, settle disputes, witness oaths and answer first for their people in times of famine, raid or cruel winter.

## Attributes

After initiation, a new Highlander crosses the [[Хтоническое море|Chthonic Sea]] to [[Гора Атафет|Mount Atafet]], there to commission a [[Меч Де'лаим|De'laim sword]] from the local smiths. The weapon conducts thunder and lightning and, by means no one fully understands, slows the ageing of its bearer. The journey is considered a continuation of the trial: the Highlander must return not only with a weapon, but with an understanding of those for whom it will be raised.
