# Chapter 058 — The Secret of Chang-Sha

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 105 NE, spring*

The girl's name was [[Сао Ву|Sao Wu]]. She was the daughter of [[Мэй Ву|Mei]] and [[Ниса|Nisa]]. Nisa had brought her to the Wu estate and raised her there, insisting that she meant no harm and wanted only a quiet life with her child.

[[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] heard a raven's cry and went into the forest. The bird was attacked by [[Менада|sheng-hu]], but Tom managed to escape. At the forest's edge he met [[Мерката|Mercate]], who told him something dreadful in its sobriety: there was no sense in fighting [[Аксель Хан|Axel Khan's]] army. [[Город Чанг-Ша|Chang-Sha]] would fall as [[Город Найян|Nayan]] had unless it was evacuated.

Tom returned with the warning. Shen Wu was speaking with Mei beneath a peach tree. He admitted that he had known at once that Nisa was an impostor. They remembered the past and spoke of nereids, the [[Мэйлун|dragon]] and Mei's mission in the west.

The travellers persuaded Shen Wu to begin an immediate evacuation. He acknowledged that his past decisions might have brought the nomads upon them and ordered the inhabitants to be sent by boat to [[Город Джу-Суо|Dju-Suo]].

Then an even more terrible truth emerged: the disappearance of Chang-Sha's people had been a conspiracy. A thousand farmers had been abducted in a single night and sent to [[Гарнизон Пасть Дракона|Dragon's Maw]]. The tale of the Wu Legion's heroic destruction proved false: the legion had vanished long ago, and the nomads had probably slaughtered ordinary people dressed as soldiers. The only witness was Mulondji Mati's mutilated son, found in the Temple of Qiong-Qi.

[[Урист Китин|Urist]] helped [[Ао Ву|Ao Wu]] carry an expensive carpet to the quay, but after judging its worth decided to sell it. Mei watched him in ominous silence. Meanwhile, Tom explained to [[Мусака|Musaka]] how they would take the horses across the lake, determined to save not only the people but the animals as well.
