# Bahara

![[Assets/Images/Bahara_City.jpg]]

**Bahara**, at the mouth of the [[Река Улын-Гол|Ulyn-Gol]], is the largest port of [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]]. Once [[Кадир|Qadir's]], it was taken by the [[Авгарцы|Avgars]] in 668 ChE and remains mixed in character: Avgar law lives beside Qadian trade custom, while sea contracts carry as much weight as a local khan's will.

When the [[Белый пролив|White Strait]] freezes, sailors bound for the [[Рассветное море|Dawn Sea]] wait in Wildlands ports. Bahara became their chief refuge, drawing merchants and pirates as well as contraband, slavery and entire quarters where law works only after prolonged bargaining.

A sea blockade once nearly brought war by cutting eastern Qadir supplies. A council aboard the Thalassian ship *Typhon* lifted it before hunger and accusations became open conflict. Bahara remains dangerous, but is learning to protect trade roads without surrendering the rough freedom that draws people from across the continent.
