# Ast

## Main text

Ast is the chief god of [[Амон-Астат|the Empire of the Sun]], embodying light, warmth and peaceful life. The Khefat regard him as the living incarnation of the star [[Аст|Ast]], which they call the Sun. Every dawn is the god's return to the world, and every sunbeam a reminder that light must do more than shine: it must also give warmth.

No reliable record tells of Ast ever assuming a mortal form. Every image of him is therefore an artist's interpretation. He is most often portrayed as an eagle or as a man with avian features, for flight signifies the height of heaven, far sight and the power to behold the whole country at once.

Ast's temples are built so that the first ray of morning reaches the innermost altar. His signs are the sun, the eagle and the ankh, symbolising life preserved by warmth. Prayers are offered at the birth of a child, before a long journey and after recovery from illness; rulers ask him less for victory than for clarity of judgement.

The worship of Ast is ordered through the faith of [[Хат-Астат|Hat-Astat]]. Its priests keep the calendar, tend the sick and train those who may one day become [[Светоносный|Lightbringers]]. Although Ast is the foremost god of Amon-Astat, small solar shrines stand throughout [[Астария|Astaria]], especially where life depends upon harvests, warmth and the safe return of spring.

The Great Drought sorely tested his cult. Beneath a cloudless sky and beside failing rivers, saving light began to seem merciless. Some blamed the god; others blamed priests and rulers who had failed in their duty. The schism did not destroy faith in Ast, but it changed for ever the question his people ask of him: what does it mean to be a light to those who suffer beneath the sun itself?
