# Chapter 012 — Heart of the Volcano

*Amon-Astat, 104 NE, winter*

The [[Пепельные земли|Ashlands]] greeted the travellers as they greet all living things: with heat, ash and indifferent menace. The land itself seemed diseased. Air trembled above the stones, grey dust coated clothes and skin, and every step demanded more strength than it ought. Provisions dwindled, water became dearer than silver, and even silence seemed scorched.

The ice-grip salve given to them by [[Рувамба Мати|Ruwamba Mati]] kept them from dying of the heat too quickly, but did not make the road easy. Weariness grew. The company's spirits wore thin as sun-bleached cloth. Only [[Урист Китин|Urist Kitin]] felt a strange exhilaration. The deeper they ventured into these lethal lands, the surer the Avgar became, as though an unseen hand drew him onward.

At last Urist noticed a volcano with a steep, ponderous slope. Nothing about it welcomed them: black stone, grey scree and the scars of ancient fire. Yet Urist scarcely doubted that their quarry lay somewhere within.

The travellers found countless bones upon its slopes. They lay among the stones like warnings no one had managed to read in time. Then the earth shuddered. A small earthquake loosed a landslide, sending boulders hurtling down. The company barely escaped, but the Ashlands had made their meaning plain: here, even motionless mountains might suddenly desire death.

A cave exposed by the fall led into the volcano. It was cooler within, as though the stone preserved not heat in its depths, but an ancient shadow. Rock paintings marked the walls. Their meaning was difficult to discern, yet they carried the memory of those who had come here long before the present peoples of [[Астария|Astaria]].

In that cave, Urist found the minerals required for [[Золотая сталь|Golden Steel]]: anatase and red pyrite. [[Арно Эклунд|Arno Eklund's]] trial ceased to be a distant smithing problem; it now rested in his palm as stones won at the edge of the world.

The respite did not last. [[Менада|Maenads]] attacked the travellers, followed by [[Гнолл|gnolls]] bursting into the fray. The cave became the site of a three-sided battle, cries echoing from the walls and shadows leaping in the torchlight. No alliances existed. Each side fought to survive, hold the passage and avoid being crushed between two foreign furies.

After the fighting, the company emerged into the volcano's crater. It lay dormant: a vast circle of hardened lava surrounded by stones fallen from the summit. No living flame remained, but the ground still remembered its shape.

Urist caught a metallic gleam and approached it, obeying the same instinct that had guided him from the beginning. A heap of weapons and armour lay before them, cast together as though the remnants of failed expeditions had been brought here for years. Yet the object that drew Urist most strongly seemed almost worthless: a plain wooden shaft lying apart from the rest.

The others could not prise it from the ground. Urist lifted it with ease.

Its weight and balance told him it was the haft of a long axe or poleaxe, its head roughly broken away. He could not identify the wood, while the counterweight appeared to be made of Golden Steel. In time, Urist would learn that it was a fragment of [[Топор Смертоносец|Deathbringer]], the legendary weapon of [[Хангор|Khangor]] and [[Аксель Хан|Axel Khan]]. In that moment, he merely held something that seemed to have waited for him.

[[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] noticed another light. In a shallow hollow of the crater lay a fiery sphere, brilliant and unbearably hot. It was the [[Сердце Огня|Heart of Fire]].

As they drew near, a thunderous roar sounded overhead. The Heart's guardian—a dragon—appeared not as legend, but as an immense living threat against which mortals are seldom granted an honest battle. The travellers used Ruwamba's poison. The dragon fell.

Even then, the Heart of Fire did not become easy plunder. It scorched the skin and radiated heat as though it contained not an object, but a portion of flame itself. The travellers had to consider not victory, but how they might carry it towards [[Кадир|Qadir]] without perishing by their own success.

The expedition into the Ashlands was over. The company turned north, away from the volcano, towards the [[Пустыня Халисат|Khalisat Desert]] and its seven gardens. Behind them lay bones, ash, a dead guardian and a heart for which too many powers had already begun to move their pieces.
