# Isgeluat

![[Assets/Images/Isgeluat_River.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Isgeluat** is a great cold river of western [[Кельн|Keln]], forming the eastern natural boundary of the [[Предгорья Битхорн|Bithorn Foothills]]. It begins as several swift streams among stony slopes, then broadens into a navigable river, gathering water from heather valleys and peat-fed brooks.

In spring, Isgeluat roars far beyond its banks. Snowmelt carries branches, ice fragments and dark mountain sand that makes the channel appear almost black. By midsummer the current calms and convenient fords emerge; then the clans drive their livestock, repair bridges and hold riverside fairs.

The river contains blue-finned pike, predatory fish whose fins have a metallic sheen. Before a storm they rise to the surface and hunt almost beside boats. Fishers trust their appearance more than any weather forecast and find shelter before the first lightning reaches Bithorn.

[[Город Солаис|Solais]] stands in Isgeluat's delta, the customary capital of the [[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]] and the region's largest river harbour. Sea-borne goods, ore, wool and news from distant valleys reach it by water. Only a local boatman can take a vessel upstream, however: stone teeth and whirlpools lie hidden between calm reaches, their names learned by the Goidair like lines of an old song.
