# Selina Elliot

![[Assets/Images/Selina_Elliot.jpg]]

**Selina Elliot** is a youthful priestess of [[Мерката|Mercate]] from the [[Лудаир|Ludair]] people.

She became a [[Тень|Shadow]] at a remarkably young age, and the transformation preserved her agility and stealth across the decades that followed. Selina is one of the finest scouts of the [[Культ Меркаты|Cult of Night]], entrusted with missions of particular importance in eastern [[Архея|Archaea]].

During Mercate's weakening, Selina was cut off from the goddess's will in caverns inhabited by [[Нага|Nagas]]. Sensing a remnant of divine power within their captive, the Nagas made her their queen and tutor, hoping she would bring their people closer to human civilisation. The honoured position remained a prison: Selina was forbidden to leave the underground city, while an ancient pyramid muffled her bond with the Goddess of Night.

After escaping, Selina asked to be made a [[Тень|Shadow]] once more. The second rite proved weaker than the first and cast her into a dangerous sleep, from which [[Томас Робинсон|Thomas Robinson]] and [[Мэйлун|Meilong]] helped awaken her. Selina now serves Mercate again, yet her bond with the goddess requires a greater measure of independent judgement than it once did.
