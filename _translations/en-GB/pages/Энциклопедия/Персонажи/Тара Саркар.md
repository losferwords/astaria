# Tara Sarkar

![[Assets/Images/Tara_Sarkar.png]]

Tara Sarkar is a young, dark-haired [[Раджати|Rajati]] woman from [[Город Ванпур|Vanpur]]. She appears to be about nineteen. She tries to remain composed in unfamiliar surroundings, speaks little and knows how to conceal her anxiety behind an unreadable expression.

Her reserve does not mean indifference. When Tara feels that someone is genuinely willing to help her, caution gives way to a desire to trust. It matters to her that she can choose for herself whom to stay with and which places to leave.
