# Lake Zhen

![[Assets/Images/Zhen_Lake.jpg]]

**Lake Zhen** (珍湖; Mandarin: Zhēn Hú) lies beneath the eastern foothills of the [[Горный хребет Шафар|Shafar Mountains]] and feeds an upper branch of the [[Река Тяо Хэ|Tiao He]]. Mountain streams keep it cold and clear; even in summer fishers go out at dawn in heavy cloaks until the sun warms the surface.

Its western edge is ringed by stone slopes and conifer groves, while the land eastward softens into fields and gardens. Canals from the lake irrigate [[Город Лан-Цзы|Lan-Zi]], so its water level is watched closely. After a winter of little snow, farmers and mountain settlements argue at length over who may first open the sluices.

The name is interpreted poetically as ‘Precious Lake’, evoking the value of its water to the fields and gardens. Local people say Zhen does not grant the earth wealth, but lets what already lies hidden in the soil emerge. Before the first sowing, they bring seeds here and dip them briefly in the water, asking not for abundance but for an honest harvest.

Old mountain trails and stone platforms above the water draw travellers. Some were clearly made by human hands, though they have neither inscriptions nor roads. In fog, ringing is sometimes heard from them although the nearest temple lies far below.
