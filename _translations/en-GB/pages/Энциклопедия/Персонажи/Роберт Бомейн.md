# Robert Beaumain

![[Assets/Images/Robert_Bomain.jpg]]

## Main text

**Robert Beaumain** is the reigning king of [[Лунаар|Lunaar]] and the eldest of [[Бодивир Бомейн|Bodivir Beaumain's]] three children. He grew up at a court where military reports were delivered in the silent presence of priestesses from the [[Культ Меркаты|Cult of Mercate]]. Robert therefore understood from an early age that a monarch of Lunaar never rules alone, and that not all power belongs to the Crown.

After his father disappeared in 98 NE, Robert inherited the throne while still a minor. Under the laws of Lunaar, a king younger than sixteen does not receive the full powers of the Crown, and a council of high priestesses temporarily performed the sovereign's duties. Those years taught the young man caution: a hasty word could be made into a decree, weakness into an opportunity for pressure and honest grief into an instrument of politics.

Upon assuming his full authority, Robert did not openly oppose the Cult, but began to draw a clearer distinction between counsel and command. He reveres Mercate and understands the importance of the Shadows to his realm, yet refuses to have Lunaar's fate decided entirely within closed temple halls.

Robert is reserved and seldom permits himself anger in public. His attendants observe that he listens most closely to reports from the [[Река Горсафон|Gorsafon]] and the northern hunting grounds where his father vanished. The king has never declared the search concluded, although with every passing year his hope of finding Bodivir resembles a private vow more than an affair of state.

To some, Robert remains a ruler too young and too deeply overshadowed by the priestesses. To others, he is the first Beaumain capable of turning the caution learnt through a long night into a genuine political virtue.
