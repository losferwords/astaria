# Whitefield Plain

![[Assets/Images/Whitefield_Plains.jpg]]

## Main text

**Whitefield Plain** is a cold expanse in north-western [[Кельн|Keln]]. To the south it becomes the [[Болота Забвения|Marshes of Oblivion]]; to the east it meets the [[Горный хребет Грань Тьмы|Edge of Darkness]]; to the west it falls away towards the [[Закатное море|Sunset Sea]]; and to the north it gradually dissolves into the icy breath of [[Антра|Antra]].

Most of Whitefield is tundra, with low scrub, tough grass and scatterings of pale stone. In summer the ground briefly flowers, but the first autumn winds press the blossoms down. Further south lie coniferous woods and marshy lowlands, where a dry road can disappear under water in a single night.

The [[Лудаир|Ludair]] build settlements beside rivers and on slopes sheltered from the wind. Watchtowers, stone markers and small refuges for travellers stand upon the open plain. To leave such shelter without firewood is considered shameful: the next person may need it before reaching a village.

Whitefield is known for deer, white foxes and great flocks of migratory birds. Yet [[Вермин|vermin]] also travel across it, especially after severe winters. Their burrows are easy to mistake for ordinary hollows in frozen ground, so an experienced tracker always examines a site before making camp.

For a traveller, Whitefield's greatest danger is not a monster but distance. One can see far, but things look closer than they are. A lone fire may beckon until dawn, and a black speck on the snow may prove to be either a watchtower or someone who has not moved for many hours.
