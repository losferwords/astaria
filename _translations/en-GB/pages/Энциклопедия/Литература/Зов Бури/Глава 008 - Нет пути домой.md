# Chapter 008 — No Way Home

*No Way Home*

After leaving the summit, [[Кассиопея Атанатос|Cassia]] sees the future change. But [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth]] no longer appears in her visions. Believing her beloved dead, she journeys north to tell the peoples of the [[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]] of his great deed. The search for his mother takes time.

Only in spring 101 NE, in [[Деревня Эрвин|Erwin]], does Cassia find [[Лисандра мак Рейн|Lisandra]] and tell her the wondrous story of Kenneth's journey and presumed death. His mother is seized at once by fierce pride and sorrow for her son. Cassia remains in the mac Rayne household for several more days, then departs for an unknown destination.

Lisandra carries word of [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth the Stormcaller]] throughout the Thunder Clans. From these tales, the saga *Call of Thunder* is composed in [[Город Солаис|Solais]].

In its final chapter, Lisandra recounts her son's deed and the great sacrifice that brought life and peace to distant southern lands.
