# Urist Kitin

![[Assets/Images/Urist_Kitin.jpg]]

**Urist Kitin** is a smith from [[Деревня Улехей|Ulehey]] and a distant cousin of the renowned master [[Арно Эклунд|Arno Eklund]]. Debt and a failed trade drove him from [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]] in search of another life. His journey ended in captivity in [[Гилас|Gilas]], where the Avgar was sent to the arena at [[Город Ахей|Achaeus]]. His escape from the gladiators' cage, however, became the beginning of a far longer road home.

In the deserts of [[Кадир|Qadir]], Urist found a fragment of [[Топор Смертоносец|Deathbringer]], the weapon bound to [[Хангор|Khangor]] and the legend of [[Аксель Хан|Axel Khan]]. By joining its ancient haft to his own hammer of [[Золотая сталь|golden steel]], he proved that he was ready to seek the Smith-God's recognition. Within the [[Великая Кузня|Great Forge]], Urist overcame its trials, met Khangor and earned the right to be called a [[Мектиг|Mektig]]. Urist has since been known not merely as a formidable warrior, but as a master capable of teaching metal to remember an oath.

His return to Wildlands drew him into the struggle for the future of the Avgars. Urist publicly questioned Axel Khan's infallibility, defended Ulehey from the Vermin and married [[Ли Сан|Li San]], binding his family to the [[Джу|Dju]] people. His fury saved those around him more than once, but during the fall of [[Город Найян|Nayan]] it also caused the death of [[Ксан Ляньши|Xan Lianshi]]. His strength has remained both his greatest gift and his most dangerous weakness ever since.

Death briefly parted Urist from the living, but [[Ицунэ|Itsune]] allowed him to return because his soul was bound to Deathbringer. That bond later came close to sundering his emotions until the goddess of souls restored the Avgar to wholeness. Urist now serves as Khangor's herald and knows that the Great Forge, the designs of the gods and the approaching end of the world are intertwined more closely than mortal smiths imagine.
