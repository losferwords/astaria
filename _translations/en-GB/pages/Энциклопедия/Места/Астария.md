# Astaria

![[Assets/Images/Astaria.jpg]]

## Beyond the next bend

Sails are already rising in the harbour. In a mountain village, a child born during a storm is being prepared to face the lightning. Behind the sealed gates of a divine forge, a rumble sounds that no one can explain. Here, you might set out in search of a rare blade, enter an argument with a god, or simply try to find your way home—and find yourself inside a future saga.

**Astaria is a mythological fantasy world of ancient civilisations.** Oaths, hospitality, faith and the memory of kin matter here. Wonders enter everyday life, yet even the greatest hero needs companions, shelter and a reason to keep going.

> [!tip] New to Astaria?
> Open the [[Карта Астарии|map]] to choose a shore, [[Имитей|discover the Imithei]] to imagine your own hero, or begin with [[Ветер Перемен|Wind of Change]]. You do not need to know the whole world before setting out.

## One world, different shores

In the tongue of the [[Хефат|Khefat]], ‘Astaria’ means ‘She Who Lies beneath the Sun’ or ‘Beneath the Shelter of the Star’. Sages believe the world to be an immense sphere of land and water, but only a small part of its surface is known. Around the [[Хтоническое море|Chthonic Sea]] lie [[Архея|Archaea]] to the south and [[Кельн|Keln]] to the north; beyond the settled shores, roads lead towards deserts and ice.

- **[[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]]:** mountain trails, woollen plaids and chieftains who must earn the right to lead free people.
- **[[Амато|Amato]]:** mist-veiled islands, tea before an important conversation, and lanterns for souls that have lost their way.
- **[[Обитель|Obitelj]]:** grain fields, birch groves and a realm learning to welcome a guest without fear after war.

## Power that changes fate

The [[Имитей|Imithei]] surpass human limits, but every people finds its own path. A [[Горец|Highlander]] receives the lightning and becomes a protector of the clan. An [[Аватар|Avatar]] turns bare hands into flame. A [[Мектиг|Mektig]] earns the Smith God's recognition through strength, will and craftsmanship.

Such a gift brings more than power. Others expect decisions they can follow. And [[Тарун|Tarun]], [[Ицунэ|Itsune]] or [[Хангор|Khangor]] are not distant names in prayers: meeting them may become part of your story.

## The past still has questions

Who were the [[Археи|Archaeans]], whose ruins and artefacts have outlived their meaning? Why did [[Хангор|Khangor]] restrict access to the [[Великая Кузня|Great Forge]], which his people spent centuries building? Astaria's inhabitants have legends, guesses and testimony—and these do not always agree.

It is now **106 of the New Era**. The [[Война Жаждущих|War of the Thirsty]] has ended, but recent years have brought fresh upheavals: the siege and naval blockade of [[Город Бахара|Bahara]], war between the nomads and the [[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], and troubling reports of a disease called Rakta-Dosha in [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj ka Ghar]]. For some, these are tidings from distant shores; for others, a closed road, a lost home or the hope of finding help.

The [[История Астарии|timeline]] draws the world's past together, while [[Ветер Перемен|Wind of Change]] lets you witness recent upheavals through the eyes of those caught in them. The next chapter has yet to be written.
