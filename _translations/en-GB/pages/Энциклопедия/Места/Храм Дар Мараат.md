# Temple of Dar Maraat

![[Assets/Images/Dar_Maraat_Temple.png]]

## Main text

**Dar Maraat**, or the **Abode of Time**, is a temple complex upon an island in the middle of [[Озеро Зураиф|Lake Zuraif]]. Its principal part lies deep beneath earth and sand at the island's centre. From the shore, only the later buildings of the [[Кадийцы|Qadians]] are visible: a university with a library, accommodation for scholars and pilgrims, courtyards and landings. The complex's foundation date, 2374 ChE, refers to the Qadian development of the island, not the origin of the ancient sanctuary.

## The underground sanctuary

Within the island opens an immense cavern with deliberately built walls, columns and galleries. Time and sand have not spared its ancient limestone masonry: steps are worn, carved ornament has crumbled, and drifts and fragments lie at the columns' bases. A succession of towering corridors leads to the principal chamber, where the colossal [[Часы Времён|Clock of Time]] stands. The underground temple's age is unknown; beside it, the buildings above seem only a recent addition.

According to Qadian tradition, time itself begins here. The first grain to fall in [[Альзаман|Alzaman's]] clock is associated with the beginning of all existence, and the last with the world's eventual end. Then the clock will turn, and the course of being will begin anew.

Most chambers remain dark even by day. Wall torches cast warm light that never reaches the upper vaults; only here and there does a narrow sunbeam enter through a fissure in the stone ceiling. A pilgrim gradually loses sight of the lake and ceases to understand how long they have spent within. The keepers do not explain the complex's design, maintaining that the path must be lived, not dismantled.

## The university and the island's inhabitants

The Qadians built a place of learning and hospitality around the ancient sanctuary. The university and library stand above ground beside accommodation for scholars and pilgrims. The island's life revolves around observations, conversations and care for those who come seeking knowledge or counsel.

A small settlement of boatfolk, keepers, sages and pilgrims has grown around the sanctuary. There is no noisy market here, and trade ceases during the chief rites. Visitors are given simple clothing and asked to leave their weapons at the outer landing, regardless of origin or rank. The inhabitants do not form a separate priesthood of Alzaman: they tend the place, make observations and assist those who have come for knowledge or counsel.

## Pilgrimage and closed passages

Dar Maraat is connected to the birth of the first [[Мститель|Avengers]] and the [[Раскол Кадира|Schism of Qadir]]. People therefore come not only to pray, but to seek a decision that can no longer be deferred. Alzaman is not believed to offer a ready answer: the temple merely strips a person of their familiar excuses.

Ancient catacombs extend beyond the open route to the sanctuary. Most are closed to pilgrims, and some passages change after the water shifts. The keepers admit that they do not know every chamber, but prohibit independent exploration—in the Abode of Time, one may lose much more than the way.
