# Ao Wu

![[Assets/Images/Ao_Wu.jpg]]

## Main text

**Ao Wu** is an enigmatic woman of the ancient Wu family who lives in [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]]. She is renowned for her embroidery and for creating costly tapestries, carpets and furnishings for noble houses. Patterns and symbols intertwine throughout her work, their meanings known to few—and, at times, to Ao alone. Every stitch seems to bind something of her soul into the cloth, filling each piece with secret significance. As a great-granddaughter of [[Шень Ву|Shen Wu]], the legendary [[Аватар|Avatar]] of [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Lang-An]], she carries a fragment of the [[Джу|Dju]] people's ancient inheritance.

Ao Wu does not speak. No one knows what caused her to relinquish words, and her silence remains veiled in mystery. Deep sadness can nevertheless be read in her eyes, while her every movement bears the trace of some unspoken grief. She seldom leaves her workshop, preferring to spend her days at the embroidery frame. Only occasionally is her shadow seen passing through moonlit [[Город Чанг-Ша|Chang-Sha]].

Alongside traditional designs, her workshop contains images of birds, clouds and mountains—motifs drawn from ancient legends. Rumour claims that her golden threads carry magic; some even believe that a person's fate may be read in her tapestries. Perhaps Ao knows far more than she appears to, but her secrets, like the meanings woven into her work, will remain hidden until their appointed hour.

Ao Wu is the mother of [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]], a young woman whose destiny carried her far beyond the Empire of Fire.
