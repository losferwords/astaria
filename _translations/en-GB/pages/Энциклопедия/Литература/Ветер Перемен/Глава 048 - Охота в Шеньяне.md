# Chapter 048 — The Hunt in Shenyan

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 104 NE, winter*

[[Ли Сан|Li San]] took [[Урист Китин|Urist]] to a healer to tend the dreadful wound in his side. The court physician used acupuncture to halt the infection and ease his pain. Meanwhile, [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] spoke with [[Чани Сан|Chani San]], who tried to trade knowledge of the young dragon for the secrets of the empress's commission. Mei disclosed nothing, but received a letter for her great-grandfather, [[Шень Ву|Shen Wu]].

The imperial hunt travelled north. Oto San, Chani's son, explained the rules of [[Лес Шеньянь|Shenyan]]: do not separate, do not move if lost, and prostrate oneself before a sheng-hu. In this forest, respect was not courtesy but a means of survival.

Among the bamboo, [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] shot a partridge that fell into a ravine. Below he saw [[Рон Эллиот|Ron]], and behind Ron a [[Менада|sheng-hu]] poised to spring. Tom cried a warning. Almost at once, a throwing axe struck the maenad: nomads had killed her, and a battle began.

The company defeated six Avgars, but other sheng-hu answered their kinswoman's cry, led by a matriarch. Finding an arrow in the body, she began searching for Ron. Tom hid the young man beneath a veil of darkness, and Ron fled into the forest.

Then the sheng-hu themselves suddenly scattered. [[Мэйлун|Meilong]] sprang from Mei's hood and raced north. Bamboo fell one stalk after another, throwing up dirt and dust. Mei pursued, only to be pinned beneath a tree. The young dragon forced one of the maenads to lift the trunk before continuing his flight from an unknown monster.

The trail ended at a frozen lake. Meilong had apparently taken to the air. Mei saw only a small winged shadow far to the north. The hunt ended in loss, and Nayan received the travellers no longer as a hospitable city, but as a place where everything was costly, anxious and far too many weapons were forged without true mastery.
