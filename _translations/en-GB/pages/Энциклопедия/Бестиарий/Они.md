# Oni

![[Assets/Images/Oni.jpg]]

## Main text

**Oni** (鬼) is an [[Эдзо|Edzo]] word commonly translated into the shared tongue as “demon”. It refers to people altered by a mysterious possession.

## Appearance and possession

Oni often retain a human appearance: a familiar face, everyday clothing and the movements of someone seen in the street only a short while ago. This makes the change in their gaze and expression all the more frightening. Red eyes and a strained smile can make recognisable features seem alien, while an ordinary work knife in the hand of the possessed can become a weapon. Blood on their clothes and hurried bandages speak of violence, but do not reveal what happened: behind that frightening appearance may be someone who inflicted pain, someone who suffered it, or both.

The possessed gradually lose the ability to distinguish their own memories and feelings from intrusive voices. They become anxious and suspicious, perceive threats in those close to them and may fall into uncontrollable rage. Their eyes often redden, while their behaviour grows so unpredictable that the people of a settlement begin to see a former neighbour as a true monster. Some Oni are consumed by a thirst for violence, others by paranoia or all-encompassing fear.

Red eyes alone do not prove possession: the eyes of the [[Онмёдзи|Onmyoji]] take on the same colour after their sacred initiation. The resemblance troubles those unable to recognise the signs of another will, making an encounter with a demon hunter almost as frightening as one with an Oni.

## Cleansing

The people of [[Амато|Amato]] try not to kill the possessed, especially while they can still recognise a relative or friend. Instead they summon priests of [[Ицунэ|Itsune]] or an [[Онмёдзи|Onmyoji]] capable of performing a rite of cleansing. To some Edzo, the Onmyoji are saviours and demon hunters; to others, they stand dangerously close to the very beings they pursue.

During a confrontation, an Onmyoji seeks to weaken the Oni and prepare the cleansing. For the hunter, the familiar human face behind those red eyes remains a reminder of someone who can still be freed from possession.
