# While Gods Sleep

![[Assets/Images/Eye_of_Calypso.jpg]]

The saga follows [[Пирей Никос|Piraeus Nikos]], [[Эйдан мак Кинсли|Aidan mac Kinsley]], [[Ишикути Макото|Ishikuchi Makoto]] and those gradually drawn into their journey by the mystery of the sleeping gods. The source material began as a campaign draft, so this text has been shaped into a chronicle: free of working branches, game-master notes and mechanical conventions.
