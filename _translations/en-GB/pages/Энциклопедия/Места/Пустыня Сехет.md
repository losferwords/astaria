# Sekhet Desert

![[Assets/Images/Amon-Astat_Desert_2.jpg]]

## Main text

**Sekhet** is the hottest desert in [[Астария|Astaria]], occupying much of eastern [[Архея|Archaea]]. It extends from the valley of the [[Река Тавропос|Tavropos]] in the west to [[Залив Фиальсахра|Fialsakhra Bay]] in the east. [[Амон-Астат|The Empire of the Sun]] arose among its sands, stone plateaux and scattered oases.

The desert is far from uniform. Pale dunes give way to black fields of scorched stone, dry riverbeds and hollows where flowers appear for a few days after rare rain. The air shimmers with heat by day, while the temperature falls sharply at night. Caravans try to move before sunrise and after sunset, steering by the stars and ancient stone markers.

Life clings to rivers, wells and guarded stations. Every road has customs of its own: in some places water is freely offered to all, while elsewhere a well belongs to a temple or to a family of guides. To destroy a source, or conceal it from a person in distress, is regarded as a crime against the entire realm.

[[Аль-Тахим|Al-Tahim]], [[Гнолл|gnolls]] and other creatures adapted to the heat inhabit Sekhet. A sandstorm can prove more dangerous than any predator: it erases tracks, reshapes dunes and uncovers ruins believed lost for decades. Every great storm therefore draws not only rescuers into the desert, but hunters of antiquities as well.

In 94 NE, the Great Drought began for reasons still unknown. Familiar springs and riverbeds failed, and years of hunger brought about the [[Кризис веры в Амон-Астате|Crisis of Faith]] and war. Even after the waters returned, the people of Sekhet watch every well carefully, as though the desert might demand its debt again at any moment.
