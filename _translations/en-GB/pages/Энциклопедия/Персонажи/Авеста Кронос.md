# Avesta Kronos

![[Assets/Images/Avesta_Kronos.jpg]]

## Main text

**Avesta Kronos** is a young [[Хтониды|Chthonid]] woman raised in the city of [[Город Лефа|Lepha]]. She is the daughter of [[Катахтонос|Katachthonos's]] former queen, [[Эвридика Лефийская|Eurydice of Lepha]]. Despite her royal birth, Avesta's childhood held little happiness. She was kept under the strictest watch within the palace and forbidden even to walk in its gardens. This cruelty arose from strange incidents in which she moved objects without touching them or heard the thoughts of others. Her mother feared that Avesta might one day become a [[Профитис|Profitis]], despite bearing no visible [[Хтонотема|Chthonotema]]. Were the nobility to discover the truth, the girl would inevitably be put to death.

Everything changed in 101 NE, immediately after her mother's death. At twenty-five, Avesta used the confusion within the palace to escape Katachthonos and sailed south in search of a new home and a purpose of her own.

Freedom did not rid Avesta of the habit of expecting punishment for every unusual thought. She treats her abilities with caution, avoids physical contact and watches people's reactions whenever she speaks of something she could not possibly have heard. Yet years of isolation did not extinguish her curiosity. Beyond the palace walls, she encounters ordinary life with the almost childlike wonder of someone discovering all that was once denied to her.

Her meeting with [[Рашаад аль-Сакхар|Rashaad al-Sakhar]], himself hiding from pursuers, led Avesta into the [[Пустыня Халисат|Khalisat Desert]]. Its ordeals and her own terrifying visions taught her an important lesson: prophecy seldom offers a ready answer, and recognising a repeated detail may matter more than interpreting every horror literally.

Avesta has no desire for a throne and regards royal blood as no advantage. Her path is bound instead to a more difficult question: can someone who bears the gift of a Profitis remain herself without sharing the fate of those whom an entire people fear? To some, meeting her will confirm an ancient terror. To others, it may reveal that Chthonic power is not a sentence, but a choice.
