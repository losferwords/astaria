# Trella

![[Assets/Images/Trella_City.jpg]]

## Main text

**Trella** is the largest city and capital of [[Вактар-Йорден|Vaktar-Jorden]], at the source of the [[Река Нид|Nid]], where [[Озеро Хорниндаль|Lake Hornindal's]] water begins its journey to the [[Белый пролив|White Strait]]. [[Храм Винтерхейм|Winterheim]] surpasses it in religious life, but Trella holds the royal court, chief winter harbour and councils of the seafaring houses.

The city grew around a crossing and anchorage. Canals and wooden walkways divide the lower quarters; above stand longhouses, forges, dried-fish stores and halls where crews spend the winter. Roofs are steep against snow, and doors face away from the prevailing wind.

Its mild climate and connection from lake to sea made Trella the capital. In winter the Nid partly freezes and its quays become markets upon the ice. Spring trade returns to the water before the current can destroy the temporary platforms.

The city honours Trella Holm, the navigator who found the route through the White Strait into the [[Рассветное море|Dawn Sea]]. Her steering oar is kept in the helmspeople's hall, where young captains swear never to abandon their crew to save themselves.

The court of [[Сигрид Дракендоттир|Sigrid Drakendottir]] preserves northern simplicity. There is no high throne in its main hall, but ample space for houses, warriors and sailors. Debate is loud, and rulers must endure direct objection: Vaktars consider silent agreement more dangerous than honest dispute.

Trella is a natural starting point for the north, but a clear winter day should never be mistaken for a promise of good weather. The city was built upon memories of people who noticed the wind's change too late.
