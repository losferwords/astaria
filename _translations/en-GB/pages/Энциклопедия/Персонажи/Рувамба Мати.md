# Ruwamba Mati

![[Assets/Images/Ruwamba_Mati.jpg]]

## Main text

**Ruwamba Mati** is a high [[Шаман|Shaman]] of the [[Ваку|Vaku]] people and one of [[Руфу|Rufu's]] closest priestesses. For decades, she has studied the effects of poisons, medicinal infusions and ritual substances upon ordinary people and the [[Имитей|Imithei]], turning herbalism into a frighteningly exact art.

Ruwamba carries herself with the dignity of one accustomed to being obeyed the first time she speaks. She adorns her long dark hair with beads of bone, each recalling a departed mentor or a completed rite. Her burgundy eyes seldom betray emotion, while her hands invariably smell of smoke, roots and bitter oils.

The Shaman's knowledge is valued far beyond the lands of [[Вакумара|Vakumara]]. Her preparations can plunge a mighty creature into sleep, halt the spread of poison or, conversely, turn a minor wound into a mortal one. To Ruwamba, such remedies are never mere commodities. Before she agrees to help, she learns the petitioner's intentions and sets a price that measures resolve rather than wealth.

Ruwamba's relationship with her daughter [[Ошу Мати|Oshu]] remains painful. She prepared Oshu for the priestly path, while her daughter rejected the role chosen for her and fled their homeland. Beneath Ruwamba's severity lies more than a desire for control: she truly believes that abandoning tradition can leave a person defenceless before powers the Vaku have revered for centuries.

Even so, the Shaman does not consider her daughter lost. She gathers rumours of Oshu's travels, challenges anyone who calls her a traitor and keeps a place for her beside the family fire. Ruwamba is too proud to speak her fear aloud, and Oshu too stubborn to return without a victory of her own. Their love reveals itself as so many Vaku rites do: sternly, painfully and with a hope that neither woman is willing to call hope.
