# Warden

## Main text

The Wardens, or **Hiku**, are seven chosen priests of [[Икатерра|Ikaterra]] who protect the [[Манаи|Manai]] and the eastern shores of [[Архея|Archaea]]. They heal the injured, calm dangerous waters and stand between the peaceful villages of [[Хамоа|Hamoa]] and threats that cannot be turned aside by ordinary words.

The seven follow a strict order founded upon experience and the strength of each priest's bond with the goddess. The eldest holds the title First Warden and answers not only for the decisions of the others, but for the consequences of every charge entrusted to them. The Hiku seldom gather in one place. If all seven are seen together, the people understand that the danger threatens more than a single village.

## Training

The Warden's path begins with healing, swimming and service to the community. Only one who has learnt to preserve life without divine power may ask for a greater gift. Ikaterra permits the Hiku to command currents, mend grievous wounds and seek the aid of creatures of the sea.

Those whose kinship with the ocean becomes especially profound gradually develop gills. To the Manai, this is a sign of union with nature, akin to the horns borne by [[Друид|Druids]] in the forests of Iomar. The gills allow a Hiku to remain beneath the water for long periods, but the deeper change is that the body no longer experiences the sea as something foreign.

Wardens shun violence, but take no vow of helplessness. In battle they seek to disarm, drive back or exhaust an opponent before dealing a mortal blow. Their purpose is to restore balance and preserve as many lives as possible—including that of a person whom fear or rage has made an enemy.

## Attributes

The mark of service is the Trident of the Abyss. Through it, a Warden directs water, sustains rites of healing and can draw moisture from an opponent at a distance. A tranquil Hiku is therefore a formidable adversary: the same gift that restores life to a withered body may sap an attacker's strength within moments.
