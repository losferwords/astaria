# Sangalla

![[Assets/Images/Sangalla_City.jpg]]

**Sangalla** is the capital of [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]], standing upon [[Багровый залив|Crimson Bay]] at the mouth of the [[Река Рави|Ravi River]]. From the sea, the city is known by its golden spires and red banners above the waterfront; from the land, by the scent of spice long before the first gate comes into view. It is one of the greatest centres of trade in [[Архея|Archaea]] and the spiritual heart of the [[Раджати|Rajati]].

Founded more than two thousand years ago, Sangalla has endured many Cycles of Creation and Destruction. Each left a layer of its own: a new palace stands upon the foundations of its predecessor, a festival square remembers executions, and stones from ancient temples can be found within the walls of ordinary homes. During Creation, processions, music and ritual dances fill the streets in honour of [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari]] in her radiant aspect.

During Destruction, the same squares become the ground of the Great Harvest, and the [[Ракша|Rakshas]] enter the streets to carry out the goddess's will. The people have learnt to recognise the approach of the rite in shuttered windows, emptying markets and the scent of particular incense. To a foreigner the change appears sudden; to a Rajati it belongs to the rhythm of the capital.

## The face of the city

Sangalla's architecture reflects this duality. Sumptuous palaces and sanctuaries with golden spires stand beside ancient stone arenas, dyers' markets and quiet courtyards concealed behind carved gates. The great temple of Shubhankari rises above the city, its walls adorned with images of the six-armed goddess, while the colour of its cloth and banners changes with the Cycle.

Spice, timber and medicinal plants are unloaded upon the quays. Farther up the slope lie jewellers' workshops, schools of dance and houses of healing, where the cure may seem no less frightening than the illness. Steps for ritual bathing, boat landings and canal-fed gardens line the Ravi.

## The court and the present crisis

Queen [[Индира Раштра|Indira Rashtra]] rules from a palace at the centre of the capital. Courtly families, priests and Rakshas contend for her attention while the prolonged Cycle of Destruction and [[Ракта-Доша|Rakta-Dosha]] exhaust the realm. The number of the sick grows, rumours travel faster than medicines, and caravans arrive under heavier guard because of the Vetals.

Yet Sangalla does not look defeated. Even in the hardest years, its people bargain, fall in love, garland their doors with flowers and teach their children to dance. The capital seems to defy its own goddess: while music still sounds in the streets, Creation has not been forgotten altogether.
