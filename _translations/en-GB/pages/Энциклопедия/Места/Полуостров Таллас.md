# Tallas Peninsula

![[Assets/Images/Tallas_Peninsula.jpg]]

**Tallas** is a great peninsula in south-western [[Кельн|Keln]], stretched between sea routes and warm coastal currents. Most belongs to [[Талассия|Thalassia]], while the eastern coast is held by young [[Катахтонос|Katachthonos]].

Its shoreline is deeply cut with bays suited to small harbours. Hills, forests, river valleys and marshy lowlands lie inland, so travelling by land between neighbouring ports can take longer than sailing the coast. Local captains have used this for centuries, joining settlements by a web of short sea routes.

Thalassians see the peninsula as a natural extension of their maritime realm; Katachthonian Chthonids see a land where their people found a new home after the [[Падение Хтона|Fall of Chthon]]. The difference is felt in bay names, old boundary stones and disputes over tolls. Borders seem clear on maps, yet fishing families and trade rarely obey a single line.

Wine, timber, grain and port-made goods travel Tallas's sea roads. Abandoned shrines and settlement remains are found inland, their ownership contested by both sides. To a traveller, Tallas is a convenient beginning to a voyage; to a diplomat, a conversation about an old quay can suddenly become an argument over two states' future.
