# Lake Kafer

![[Assets/Images/Kafer_Lake.jpg]]

## Main text

**Kafer** is a freshwater lake in eastern [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]] that feeds the [[Река Сатис|Satis River]]. Amid the dry country it appears almost impossible: a broad blue surface surrounded by reeds, date groves and pale limestone shores in which the wind has carved shallow caves.

The water level changes markedly with the seasons. After rare downpours, the lake leaves its banks and fills a web of natural pools where farmers cultivate moisture-loving herbs. During the dry months the water retreats, exposing bands of fertile silt and white mineral crust. The people of the neighbouring villages read these traces and know in advance how generous the next harvest is likely to be.

Great flocks of copper-winged birds visit Kafer. At dawn their feathers catch the light so brightly that the flock resembles a shower of sparks above the water. The birds are never hunted near their nesting grounds, for they are said to show lost caravans the safe direction through a dust storm.

The eastern coves are calm and hospitable to boats, while cold underwater springs rise close to the western shore. Divers gather dark shells around them to make dye for temple inscriptions. The work is dangerous: a sudden change of temperature cramps the limbs, and the clear water deceives the eye about its depth.
