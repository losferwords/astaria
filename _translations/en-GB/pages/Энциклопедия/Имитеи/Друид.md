# Druid

## Main text

The vast and ancient [[Лес Иомар|Iomar Forest]] stretches along the southern coast of [[Кельн|Keln]]. Beneath its canopy, the [[Надаир|Nadair]] have long worshipped [[Церунна|Cerunna]], goddess of life and fertility. Yet only the truest servants of the living world may become her disciples—the Druids. These guardians protect their people and the forests of Iomar with fierce wisdom, while tending the balance of nature far beyond their homeland. Their extraordinary bond with the living world allows them to close grievous wounds and summon roots, beasts and spirits of the forest to their aid.

## Training

Druids command the powers of nature in many different forms. Some wear an enemy down with spells that take root and grow stronger over time; others heal and fortify their companions or call forest spirits onto the battlefield. Their art is not transformation of the body, but a covenant with living creatures, soil and growing things.

## Attributes

A Druid's staff is carved from the wood of the [[Дерево Ларудан|larudan tree]]. This remarkably strong plant possesses a profound bond with the earth. Among the Nadair, an old tradition holds that the larudan was the first tree ever to take root in [[Астария|Astaria]].
