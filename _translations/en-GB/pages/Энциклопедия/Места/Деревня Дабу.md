# Dabu

![[Assets/Images/Dabu_Village.jpg]]

## Main text

**Dabu** is a large village upon the western frontier of [[Амон-Астат|the Empire of the Sun]], standing on the banks of the [[Река Мвиди|Mvidi River]]. The [[Ваку|Vaku]] founded the settlement, but in time it passed beneath the rule of the [[Хефат|Khefat]]. Traces of both cultures remain in its names, funerary customs and the patterns painted upon its boats.

The village is the last permanent border post before [[Закатная долина|Sunset Valley]]. The road beyond is dangerous because of the difficult terrain and the nearness of [[Вакумара|Vakumara]]. Caravans linger in Dabu to hire guides, replenish their water and learn which trails are presently considered safe.

The river provides fish and sustains small fields, but its level changes swiftly. Houses are raised upon high foundations, and a light boat is kept beside every yard. When the river floods, the inhabitants reinforce the banks together, making no distinction between the old Vaku families and the later Khefat settlers.

Dabu lies upon a frontier not only between states, but between ways of understanding the world. Solar amulets hang beside carved emblems of death, and the evening tales of travellers receive more attention than official tidings. For an explorer, it is among the finest places to find a road farther west—and to learn why so many guides refuse to return there.
