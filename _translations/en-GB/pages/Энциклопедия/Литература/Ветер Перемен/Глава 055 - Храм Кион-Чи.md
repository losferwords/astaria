# Chapter 055 — The Temple of Qiong-Qi

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 105 NE, winter*

[[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] and [[Мэй Ву|Mei]] told the others what had happened in the forest. [[Нуа Си|Nuwa Xi]] overheard them and grew frightened: she said they had encountered [[Кион-Чи|Qiong-Qi]], the ancient terror of [[Лес Шеньянь|Shenyan]]. The travellers decided not to venture deeper into the woods, but to follow the river overland.

After his night with Navchin, [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] developed a sharp pain low in his abdomen. His condition worsened until [[Ошу Мати|Oshu]], overcoming her revulsion, performed an unpleasant procedure and saved him from death.

The group made scores of torches to ward off the creature Nuwa had described. Yet the forest grew ever denser until it reached the water itself, forcing them to continue along the bank beneath the trees.

A mysterious voice spoke to Mei. It said she carried the scent of a hatchling, which meant it knew something of the missing [[Мэйлун|Meilong]]. The voice promised to show her the young dragon alive if she led the group to a certain place. Mei lied to her friends that treasure might be hidden there and guided everyone into a ravine.

There [[Урист Китин|Urist]] spotted a gleaming lever and pulled it. The ground opened, and the group and refugees plunged into a dark chamber with iron walls. Neither of its two doors had a handle or lock—only a slot shaped for the [[Чёрный Вестник|Black Harbinger]].

Oshu opened one of the doors. Beyond lay a circular hall with a well and twelve doors, each marked with the name of Qiong-Qi. In the first cell lay a man whose arms and legs had been severed. His blackened wounds resembled Oshu's marks. When Tom knocked upon another door, something inside knocked back.

Meanwhile, Urist and Gilja found a half-ruined bridge and a room filled with black glass rectangles, dust, cobwebs and a peculiar column. The Temple of Qiong-Qi was no ancient myth, but a mechanism that still knew how to wait.
