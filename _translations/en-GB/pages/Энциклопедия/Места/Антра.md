# Antra

![[Assets/Images/Antra.jpg]]

## Main text

**Antra** is an ice desert in the north of [[Кельн|Keln]], where winter does not retreat even in the warmest months. Snow lies here not as a gentle covering, but in hard waves polished by the wind. Black crags and ridges of blue ice rise between them, liable to split with a deafening crack in the midst of a perfectly clear day.

Antra has no permanent settlements. Hunters and explorers use stone shelters left by earlier expeditions and stack dry fuel beside the entrance for the next traveller. To violate this custom is considered little short of murder: without fire, a person seldom survives even a single night here.

Pale banners of light often shine above the desert. Beneath them, the snow turns green and violet, and distance becomes impossible to judge. Travellers tell of mirages in which they saw warm windows, solitary figures and tracks advancing towards their own. Experienced guides neither argue with such visions nor attempt to approach them. They simply bind the company together with a single rope and continue onwards.

For all its cold, Antra is not entirely lifeless. Coarse grey lichens grow within the fissures, drawing white-furred ungulates and the predators that follow them. During the brief summer, lakes of meltwater emerge in the lowlands. Tiny blue flowers bloom around them for a few short weeks, though their seeds may wait decades for the next thaw.
