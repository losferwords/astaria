# Temple of Dar Maraat

![[Assets/Images/Dar_Maraat_Temple.jpg]]

## Main text

**Dar Maraat**, or the **Abode of Time**, is a temple complex upon an island in the middle of [[Озеро Зураиф|Lake Zuraif]]. Pale terraces rise directly from the water, and the central building has no high tower: its keepers believe there is no reason for a person to try to seem taller before time.

According to Qadian tradition, time itself begins here. Deep within the temple stands the [[Часы Времён|Clock of Time]] of [[Альзаман|Alzaman]]: the first grain of sand to fall created all that exists, and the last will one day end the world. Then the clock will turn, and the course of being will begin anew.

A small settlement of boatfolk, keepers, sages and pilgrims has grown around the sanctuary. There is no noisy market here, and trade ceases during the chief rites. Visitors are given simple clothing and asked to leave their weapons at the outer landing, regardless of origin or rank. The inhabitants do not form a separate priesthood of Alzaman: they tend the place, make observations and assist those who have come for knowledge or counsel.

The inner halls are arranged as a succession of courtyards, galleries and stairs. A pilgrim gradually loses sight of the lake and ceases to understand how long they have spent within. Some rooms are lit by a motionless ray of sunlight, while others remain in half-light even by day. The keepers do not explain the complex's design, maintaining that the path must be lived, not dismantled.

Dar Maraat is connected to the birth of the first [[Мститель|Avengers]] and the [[Раскол Кадира|Schism of Qadir]]. People therefore come not only to pray, but to seek a decision that can no longer be deferred. Alzaman is not believed to offer a ready answer: the temple merely strips a person of their familiar excuses.

Ancient catacombs lie beneath the island. Most are closed to pilgrims, and some passages change after the water shifts. The keepers admit that they do not know every chamber, but prohibit independent exploration—in the Abode of Time, one may lose much more than the way.
