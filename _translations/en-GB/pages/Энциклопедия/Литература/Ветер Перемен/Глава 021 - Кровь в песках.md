# Chapter 021 — Blood in the Sands

*[[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], 104 NE, spring*

After [[Город Кафат|Kafat]], the road carried the travellers along the [[Горный хребет Шафар|Shafar Mountains]], where sand was no longer merely sand, but the ground itself might prove to be a maw. Amid the yellow emptiness they saw a lone figure. The man walked as though stubbornness were his last reserve of water.

He was [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja Kangarlavehotri]], an acolyte from [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]]. Behind him moved a creature spoken of in hushed voices: an enormous [[Аль-Тахим|Al-Tahim]], a sandworm capable of erasing not only a traveller from the road, but every memory of his passing.

The company helped Gilja escape death beneath the sands. In gratitude, he examined [[Томас Робинсон|Tom's]] wounded hand and healed it with an efficiency that a foreigner might find alarming. His price was stranger still: Gilja asked for a little blood. To a servant of [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari]], blood was more than a bodily fluid; it was a trace of fate, a key to life and death.

Thus the company gained a man whose prayers carried the scent of spices, medicines and sacrificial knives.

By evening, the travellers reached [[Город Бахара|Bahara]], surrendered their camel and found lodging in [[Бордель Халык-Бындыр|Halyk-Byndir]]. The city lived with harbour bustle, foreign rumours and an anxiety that had yet to be given a name.

Meanwhile, [[Мэй Ву|Mei]] resolved to find other members of the Thirsty. She was to seek Whistle and Bert in Bahara, but Wildlands had already shown that even a chance encounter could become the beginning of new bloodshed.
