# Dragon's Maw Garrison

![[Assets/Images/Dragon_Gates.jpg]]

**Dragon's Maw** is a vast border garrison in the narrow passage between the [[Горный хребет Шафар|Shafar Mountains]] and [[Горный хребет Зверя|Beast Range]]. Its walls block the main road through [[Долина Кровавого Цветения|Blood Blossom Valley]], so its holder controls caravans and armies between north and east.

[[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]] built the fortress against northern invasion. Its gates stood between two rock outcrops like an open dragon's jaws, and its terraces placed attackers under archers and stone-throwers even after the first wall fell.

In 670 ChE, [[Авгарцы|Avgars]] destroyed and took it while seeking material for the [[Великая Кузня|Great Forge]]. The Dju saw the fall as a bitter symbol. Wildlands now holds it, restoring some walls and laying its own marks over imperial reliefs; the Dju still seek its return.

Its tense border life includes changing horses, inspecting goods, ransoming captives and arranging brief truces. Smugglers use old drains and ruined galleries, though not every tunnel leads out of the mountain.
