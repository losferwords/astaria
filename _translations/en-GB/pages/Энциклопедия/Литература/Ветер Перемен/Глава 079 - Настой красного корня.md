# Chapter 079 — Red Root Infusion

*[[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]], 106 NE, winter*

The travellers tried to understand what had happened to [[Сао Ву|Naika]] and how to restore [[Топор Смертоносец|Deathbringer's]] power. [[Урист Китин|Urist]] decided that [[Кирида Ариса|Kirida Arisa]] might help, and Naika knew where to find her and [[Айсан Баят|Aysan Bayat]]. She led the group to a harbour tavern called the Last Berth, but the two women had left to hunt by night.

While awaiting them, Urist tried the strongest local drink, an infusion of red root. Its fiery flavour struck harder than ordinary spirits, and Urist heard [[Хангор|Khangor]]. The smith-god told him how to restore the hammer's ancestral bond: a charm must be spoken over the weapon and Naika's essence, which had absorbed its power. This would not restore the missing portion of Urist's soul, but Deathbringer would become itself once more.

Meanwhile, [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] broke into Arisa and Aysan's room, left a Lunaar coin amongst Aysan's possessions and lay down to rest, pretending he had simply exhausted himself. [[Мэй Ву|Mei]] went out to intercept the hunters before they could discover Tom.

Aysan returned carrying Arisa over her shoulder. She was unconscious, with a grave wound in her side. Mei cauterised it with dragon magic. It transpired that Aysan and Arisa hunted ordinary animals rather than vetals, for vetals vanished upon death and offered no trophy upon which to claim a bounty. Nagas had attacked them in the night.

Arisa needed a ship to return to her homeland. A boatbuilder in Tanassar had agreed to construct one, but the work required silver. That was why the women hunted.

While Arisa lay unconscious, Tom entered her dream and saw memories of the time when she had been a [[Тень|Shadow]], stealing power from [[Мерката|Mercate]] and passing it to [[Ицунэ|Itsune]]. Arisa awoke screaming in pain and confusion.

Urist asked her to perform a rite that would draw his soul out of Naika and return it to the hammer. Arisa feared calling upon the goddess of wind: she had not prayed in a long while and had avoided her duties. Yet the red root infusion did its work. Itsune appeared in wrath and rebuked her Onmyoji. Arisa agreed to help if she could find sakura petals.

In the morning, Urist and Naika went to a smithy. The young smith became distracted by the alluring nereid, and she nearly forgot their purpose, but Urist drew her back to the rite. Naika spoke a charm in ancient Avgar, Urist reforged the hammer, and its ancestral power returned. Mei could no longer lift Deathbringer, proving that part of the ritual had succeeded.

At the harbour, however, Meilong behaved strangely, shaking his head as though trying to dislodge alien thoughts. He spoke of a dreadful voice within his mind, proclaiming that it would consume the souls of the world. He felt better at the city gates, and the travellers decided to house the dragon in a food warehouse far from the sea.
