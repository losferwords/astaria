# Marshes of Oblivion

![[Assets/Images/Edge_of_Darkness_Mountains.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Marshes of Oblivion** fill a vast lowland west of the [[Горный хребет Грань Тьмы|Edge of Darkness]]. Five rivers divide the country into peat islands, flooded woods and long belts of sedge. After rain, a familiar path may vanish below the water, while a passage opens where impassable mire stood the day before.

Their name is linked not to sorcery but to treacherous sameness. Fog hides landmarks, peat muffles footsteps, and the thickets repeat so precisely that a traveller ceases to remember how often they have passed the same broken trunk. Local guides mark their way with bundles of dry reed: cuts in trees are unreliable, for half the forest drifts slowly with the floating islands.

The marshes yield medicinal roots, peat, bog iron and thick amber honey. At night, cold lights gleam above the water—flammable vapours that can ignite from an open flame. Herb gatherers use shuttered lanterns and never run towards a solitary light, however near it seems.

Beneath the moss, people sometimes find remnants of old boardwalks and stone foundations of settlements absent from every map. Some roads lead directly into deep water. Scholars believe the marshes were once a chain of lakes and fertile valleys, but local people prefer not to excavate places where the earth itself has chosen to bury something.
