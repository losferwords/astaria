# Indira Rashtra

![[Assets/Images/Indira_Rashtra.jpg]]

**Indira Rashtra**, Queen of [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]], is a woman whose name is spoken with both awe and apprehension. Her beauty is as regal and perilous as a panther of the [[Джунгли Сундари|Sundari Jungle]], and the heat of living blood gleams within her gaze.

Every year of her life conceals both tragedy and triumph. Born into poverty, Indira learnt early the price of men's desires—and the worth of her own wit. In her youth she was a concubine at the court of [[Город Сангалла|Sangalla]], yet became the beloved of King [[Ашрам Виджай|Ashram Vijay]] himself: a stern ruler who had rejected the institution of marriage until he met her.

She bore him a son, [[Кунал Виджай|Kunal]], an heir whose future remains uncertain. Under the laws of Suraj Ka Ghar, motherhood to the successor placed Indira herself upon the throne after the king's sudden death five years ago. Rumours still whisper that she may have had a hand in it, for passion is easily mistaken for poison.

## Character

Her moods shift like the seasonal winds above the plains of [[Ладвакхара|Ladvakhara]]. At one moment she may be wilful and implacable; at the next, filled with captivating tenderness, vulnerable and even sincere.

She loves her son beyond measure and hides how deeply the frailty of his position frightens her: he is a bastard, and at seven years old far too young to rule. Until he comes of age, Indira reigns alone, every step she takes in Sangalla attended by the serpentine shadow of court gossip and intrigue.

Her destiny is one of struggle: for the throne, for love and for survival in a society that does not forgive a woman who seizes power without the right of blood.

## The illness and the northern mission

New storm clouds now gather over Suraj Ka Ghar. An unknown illness is claiming lives, and even the throne offers no protection. Concealing the signs of sickness from her subjects, Indira asked her court acolyte, [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja Kangarlavehotri]], to travel into the distant lands of the north.

She needs rare blood—her final hope of a cure. She fights not only the poison in her veins, but also the solitude of a palace where every step may be her last. And still she dreams of more than survival: of being loved not as a prize, but as a woman.
