# Persephone Raptis

![[Assets/Images/Persephone.jpg]]

## Main text

**Persephone Raptis** is a warrior of [[Гилас|Gilas]] who became a [[Идеал|Paragon]] and commander of the realm's south-eastern forces. Her ascent was bound to the events of [[Зов Бури|*Call of Thunder*]] and to one of the gravest defeats in the history of [[Город Ахей|Achaeus]].

At the age of twenty-seven, Persephone earned the right to undergo the [[Танатомахия|Thanatomachia]]. She chose the captive [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth mac Rayne]] as her opponent, believing his power promised a worthy trial. After a gruelling fight, Persephone overcame the Highlander and was preparing to deliver the final blow when an alarm announced an [[Вторжение Амон-Астата в город Ахей|attack upon the city]]. Enraged that the duel had been interrupted, she immediately assumed command of the defence.

Achaeus had been taken by surprise, and the rebels from [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]] outnumbered its defenders beyond hope. Persephone chose to preserve the surviving warriors and ordered a retreat, yielding the city within hours. Defeat did not break her. During the [[Война Жаждущих|War of the Thirsty]], she led Gilas's eastern army, reclaimed the occupied lands and marched as far as the walls of [[Город Атафет|Atafet]]. The siege ended only when Kenneth summoned the first rain in five years and both sides agreed to negotiate.

At twenty-eight, Persephone fought a second Thanatomachia and at last attained the rank of Paragon—an exceptionally young age even among the celebrated warriors of Gilas. Her experience taught her that a commander's courage is measured by more than victory. At times it is revealed in the strength to withdraw, save lives and return to an unfinished battle when the hour is right.
