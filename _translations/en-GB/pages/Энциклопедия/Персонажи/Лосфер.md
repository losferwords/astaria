# Losfer

![[Assets/Images/Losfer.jpg]]

Losfer is a taciturn [[Онмёдзи|Onmyoji]] of [[Амато|Amato]]. He wears layered black travelling clothes, a broad hood, a blindfold and a katana with dark-red binding; small books of notes hang at his belt. To outsiders, the blindfold marks his calling and conceals the red eyes with which an Onmyoji does not wish to frighten ordinary people. He speaks rarely and in a low voice, preferring a few precise words to lengthy explanations.

When he encounters the possessed, he sees people in need of help before anything else. Losfer patiently gathers accounts, listens to those involved and tries to understand what happened before drawing his blade. His reserve can easily be mistaken for coldness, although the words “You are not alone” mean more to him than a promise of swift victory.

Neither respect, material comfort nor the closeness of another soul can free Losfer from his unbidden melancholy.

Years of wandering have earned Losfer a name as one of the finest hunters. Other Onmyoji regard him as a recluse, a loner and a gloomy eccentric, yet acknowledge his mastery when a hunt turns dire. Wind and mist accompany his service to [[Ицунэ|Itsune]]; of his own past, he says almost nothing.

Those who have hunted beside him or shared a meal with him repeat his rare mentions of [[Лосфи|Losfy]] and the [[Зеркальный Сон|Dream of Mirrors]]. Losfer's strange companion is commonly taken for his [[Они|Oni]], and some joke that he caught himself a “goat” instead of a fearsome spirit. Many credit her with the hunter's unusual successes. Losfer himself offers no explanation of how close these guesses come to the truth.
