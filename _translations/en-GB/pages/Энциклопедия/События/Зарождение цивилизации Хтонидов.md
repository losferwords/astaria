# Dawn of Chthonid Civilisation

![[Assets/Images/Orion_City.png]]

Ancient tradition places the beginnings of [[Хтониды|Chthonid]] civilisation on [[Остров Хтон|Chthon Island]] in 4950 ChE. Its emergence is associated with [[Тиресий|Tiresias]], god of wisdom, prophecy and law. Later generations regarded this as the starting point of a people whose cities and temples would remain centres of learning for millennia.

## The Teachings of Tiresias

According to tradition, Tiresias appeared to the first island communities and imparted his teachings. No reliable testimony survives, however, of anyone seeing the god in person. Ancient history can no longer be separated from later religious accounts; the date is an accepted milestone in the chronicles, rather than a precisely established day upon which a state was founded.

Chthonid tradition bound wisdom to restraint and law. Tiresias was depicted with a book and scales: knowledge carried responsibility towards those whose lives it changed. Such ideas shaped a civilisation that sought order both in society and in the movements of the stars.

## An Island of Learning

In later ages, magnificent cities arose on Chthon; [[Город Орион|Orion]] was built after the earliest communities. Temples housed archives and schools devoted to the interpretation of law, while scholars observed the heavens and investigated time. The study of the world became a foundation of Chthonid culture.

The precise path from early settlements to a powerful empire is only partly known. Accounts of its beginnings passed through many generations, and the [[Падение Хтона|Fall of Chthon]] destroyed the island homeland itself, breaking many chains of memory. Any account of the civilisation's birth must therefore combine surviving history with a tradition whose significance to its descendants is clearer than its literal accuracy.
