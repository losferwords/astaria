# Chapter 020 — The Isolation of Kafat

*Qadir, 104 NE, spring*

After meeting [[Альзаман|Alzaman]], [[Данис ибн Синай|Danis ibn Sinai]] spoke with [[Малик Аль-Азвар|Malik]] and [[Абу Али аль-Хассан|Abu Ali]] about his past. In [[Кадир|Qadir]], the past seldom remains behind: it may wait within a clock, the sand, a letter, or a choice made many years ago.

The Eternal Princes asked Danis to deliver an invitation to council to [[Умар ибн Ла-Ахад|Umar ibn La-Ahad]]. For six months, no messenger sent to the eastern prince had returned with an answer. In a country whose three realms depended upon the balance between old allies, such silence was an ill omen.

The eastern road across [[Пустыня Халисат|Khalisat]] proved perilous, but the travellers reached [[Город Кафат|Kafat]] on the shore of [[Озеро Альсалим|Lake Alsalim]]. Even its outskirts seemed unnervingly empty. There were no beggars, no farmers beside the water, no guards beyond the gates. The city did not greet them with life. It seemed to hold its breath.

The gate guards were genuinely astonished by the strangers' arrival. Learning who had come, they escorted the guests to the prince's palace. Within Kafat, the scene was graver still: the people starved and thirsted, inns were overcrowded, and queues stretched everywhere towards healers and volunteers distributing food.

Umar had long expected the travellers. He too possessed a black artefact akin to those already found by the company. Before speaking of the [[Сердце Огня|Heart of Fire]], however, he explained the city's plight.

Kafat was isolated. Caravans could neither leave nor approach overland because the [[Аль-Тахим|Al-Tahim]], the sandworms of Khalisat, had become a threat to the roads once more. The sea route to [[Город Бахара|Bahara]] was blocked as well. For eastern Qadir, this was not merely a crisis of supply, but a political danger: relations with the [[Авгарцы|Avgars]] were already strained, and broken trade could easily be mistaken for a breach of treaty.

The last messenger sent by sea into [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]] had been beheaded.

Umar agreed to help study the Heart of Fire, but asked for aid in return. By any means necessary, the travellers had to reach [[Батар Саг|Batar Sag]] and convince him that disaster in Qadir—not treachery—was responsible for the failed deliveries.

The only road to Bahara ran along the foothills of the [[Горный хребет Шафар|Shafar Mountains]]. It was dangerous, but little choice remained.

[[Гарпия|Harpies]] attacked along the way, and then the sand beneath them came alive. A colossal [[Аль-Тахим|Al-Tahim]] rose from the depths, turning the desert itself into an enemy. The Qadian crisis ceased to be Umar's tale. It burst from the ground with a maw capable of swallowing a caravan.

To reach Wildlands, the travellers would first have to survive where roads were the first things to vanish.
