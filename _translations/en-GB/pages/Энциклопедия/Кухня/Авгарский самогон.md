# Avgar Moonshine

## The drink of road and forge

**Avgar moonshine** is the name given to many strong spirits of [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], united by an uncompromising flavour and an utter lack of pretence. It begins with whatever grain is at hand—millet, barley or rye. The grain is malted, dried beside the stove, fermented with honey and distilled in small copper vessels whose design every family guards as its own secret.

Good moonshine smells of bread crust, smoke and the faintest trace of metal. Bad moonshine merely burns the throat, yet an Avgar can always be found to insist that this is precisely how the drink's true character ought to speak. The first runnings are cast into the fire, both to test the spirit and to honour [[Хангор|Khangor]]. A blue flame promises a successful batch; a sooty one, a long argument between host and distiller.

Moonshine is set upon the table when peace is made, when travellers return from a campaign and after arduous work shared by many hands. It is drunk from small cups and always accompanied by hot meat, flatbread or salted roots. Refusal causes no offence if a guest gives their reason plainly. Far worse is taking a ceremonial sip, only to pour the drink away in secret.

In [[Город Бахара|Bahara]], moonshine has become part of port life. There it is infused with dried fruit, herbs and even charred wood, while tavern keepers bestow grand names upon their mixtures. Avgars from the steppe arbats laugh at the fashion, but gladly carry a bottle home—provided none of their kin saw them buy it.
