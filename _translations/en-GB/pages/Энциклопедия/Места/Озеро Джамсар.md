# Lake Jamsar

![[Assets/Images/Jamsar_Lake.jpg]]

## Main text

**Jamsar** is the western Garden of [[Кадир|Qadir]], formed where the [[Река Макаба|Makaba River]] broadens. Unlike many lakes in the [[Пустыня Халисат|Khalisat Desert]], its water is always in motion: a broad branch enters from the south, while in the north it gathers again into the river and flows towards the sea.

Reeds, gardens and fields of aromatic herbs cover Jamsar's shores. At [[Город Фардан|Fardan]], people build riverboats and weave great baskets for transporting fruit. The local inhabitants are famed for reading the current by the colour of water: a muddy band warns of distant rain, while a build-up of foam signals a broken dam farther up the Makaba.

Jamsar belongs to western Qadir and is closely tied to the port of [[Город Самаан|Samaan]]. Oil, dried fruit, dyes and wine travel downriver to the bay; salt, sea fish and foreign wares come back. Foreign languages are therefore heard here more often than in the heart of the desert.

The lake is called the Garden of Motion. Qadian teachers bring pupils here to show that time resembles not still water, but a current that keeps its name while continually changing. For a traveller, this metaphor is practical: Jamsar's quays must be moved each year to follow the channel.

Submerged steps and stone rings of forgotten purpose lie in the reeds. Fishers avoid setting their nets nearby, saying that the current returns things left there, though not always to the same owners.
