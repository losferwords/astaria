# Chapter 031 — Deathbringer's Duel

*[[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], 104 NE, autumn*

The duel with [[Сатор Саг|Sator Kitin]] tested even [[Урист Китин|Urist]]. In his hands, Deathbringer was not merely a weapon, but the voice of ancestors, wrath and a hunger to prove his right to stand before the khan. Urist prevailed, but only narrowly, at the very edge of breath.

While he fought, the others questioned the mysterious stranger. He named himself Matthew Robinson, a [[Тень|Shadow]] of [[Мерката|Mercate]], and warned them not to trust Selina: she had deceived him. His claim held too little evidence, yet too much unease to be readily forgotten.

After the battle, the company's road turned towards the [[Великая Кузня|Great Forge]]. Wildlands revealed their heart ever more clearly: faith in the gods, ancient implements and private reckonings did not follow separate roads here. They converged where metal remembers more than people do.
