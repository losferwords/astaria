# Siwa

![[Assets/Images/Siwa.jpg]]

## Main text

**Siwa** is a trading city in the east of [[Амон-Астат|the Empire of the Sun]], standing upon the road from [[Озеро Митра|Lake Mitra]] to [[Город Атафет|Atafet]] and [[Город Тиза|Tiza]]. Caravans from [[Гилас|Gilas]], metal trains, pilgrims and envoys all pass through its gates, and its inns are seldom empty.

Siwa's chief wealth is not any single commodity, but the chance to continue one's journey. Weary animals are exchanged, wagons repaired, guards hired and the condition of the wells ahead discovered here. City scribes maintain boards of road notices, marking closed tracks, sighted companies and stretches where sand has swallowed the old landmarks.

Border trade has made the inhabitants tolerant of foreign customs and watchful of foreign intentions. In the tea houses an Ellian veteran, a Khefat priest and a guide from Atafet may sit together. Conversation sounds friendly, yet everyone understands that in wartime these streets will be the first to fill with spies, refugees and military carts.

Siwa comes most fully alive before sunset, when the heat eases and caravans prepare for the night's march. To those departing into the desert, the lights beside the eastern gate are the city's final farewell.
