# Phibas

![[Assets/Images/Fibas.jpg]]

## Main text

**Phibas** is a small city of [[Амон-Астат|the Empire of the Sun]] upon the eastern shore of [[Озеро Митра|Lake Mitra]], close to the border with [[Гилас|Gilas]].

Its position has caused it to pass frequently between the control of Amon-Astat and Gilas. The population is consequently mixed, with [[Эллийцы|Ellians]] and [[Хефат|Khefat]] living side by side.

Changing frontiers have taught Phibas to place less faith in official maps. Both languages are heard in its markets, families preserve ties across the border, and attitudes towards fugitive slaves or military patrols depend more upon private memory than upon the flag above the gates.

The lake makes the city a convenient crossing point, but also leaves it open to pursuit from both water and land. It is easy to find a fisher willing to carry someone secretly across Mitra—and just as easy to meet a soldier who remembers the face of a wanted gladiator.
