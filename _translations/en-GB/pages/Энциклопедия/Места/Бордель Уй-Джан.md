# Ui-Djan

![[Assets/Images/Ui-Djan_Brothel.jpg]]

## Main text

**Ui-Djan**, also known as the ‘Jade Rod’, is the largest brothel in [[Город Джу-Суо|Dju-Suo]] and one of the best-known establishments on the capital's Street of Pleasures. Its reputation rests not only upon luxury, but upon an unusual combination of order, safety and careful discretion. Wealthy merchants, soldiers, officials, monks and foreign guests meet here, preferring to conduct their conversations far from palace reception rooms and unwelcome ears.

The house's name is at once a bawdy joke and a mark of metropolitan refinement. Jade dragons, dark lacquered wood and red silk curtains recur throughout its rooms, while the tall dragon beside the main staircase has become the establishment's unofficial emblem.

## Spaces and services

The two-storey building is arranged around a spacious common hall. The ground floor contains the reception, the manager's counter, small tables, a musicians' stage and parlours divided by screens. A visitor may spend the evening over wine, tea, food, music or conversation without ever entering a private room. People therefore come to Ui-Djan not only for physical pleasure, but also to make acquaintances, rest in comfort or encounter someone important in a less formal setting.

The upper galleries lead to rooms intended for seclusion. Their size and furnishings vary, from modest rooms for regular patrons to costly chambers with carved screens, silk canopies and private wine tables. Several small parlours are reserved for closed dinners and negotiations in which privacy matters more than the entertainments of the common hall.

A separate wing houses Ui-Djan's celebrated sauna. It may be booked independently of the house's other services, allowing an official or merchant to visit under an entirely respectable pretext. Warm bathing rooms, massage, incense and rest after a journey have made this wing famous far beyond the Street of Pleasures.

The house's courtesans are valued for more than beauty. Some are musicians, dancers or accomplished companions familiar with court etiquette, mercantile customs and the manners of foreign guests. Ui-Djan makes no promise that every tale heard within its walls is true, yet few places in the capital gather rumours of new appointments, arriving caravans and private debts so swiftly.

## Order and safety

Ui-Djan maintains a permanent staff and keeps strict records of rooms, payments and supplies. Guests' names are not disclosed to outsiders, yet within the house there is a precise memory of who paid, who owes a debt and who broke the rules. Weapons are left under guard at the entrance, while threats, violence and coercion are grounds for immediate expulsion. These requirements apply equally to a common merchant, an officer and an imperial official.

Workers may refuse a guest, and disputes are referred to the senior attendant or the house guards. Such discipline does not make Ui-Djan a charitable institution, but it distinguishes the house from places where a patron's wealth excuses any behaviour. Scandals are contained before they reach the street whenever possible: in the capital, a careless quarrel can damage not only the house's income, but relations between influential families.

## Management and connections

[[Сяо Ша|Xiao Sha]] is Ui-Djan's effective owner and manager. Under her, the pleasure house became a stable enterprise with permanent staff, schedules, trusted suppliers and rules binding even upon eminent guests. If the ordinary guards cannot end a disturbance with words, Xiao's presence is usually enough.

Ui-Djan shares a supply and exchange network with [[Бордель Халык-Бындыр|Halyk-Byndir]] in the port city of [[Город Бахара|Bahara]]. Foreign wines, fabrics, incense and rare goods reach the capital through this connection, while Halyk-Byndir's owners have adopted Ui-Djan's safer methods of organising work. The two establishments nevertheless retain very different characters: Halyk-Byndir remains a noisy harbour refuge, whereas Ui-Djan is a carefully ordered house of pleasure, introductions and unspoken negotiations at the heart of [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]].
