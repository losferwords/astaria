# Chapter 033 — Bahara's New Khan

*[[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], 104 NE, autumn*

On the return road, the travellers encountered [[Бао Си|Bao Xi]]. Believing they had harmed his sister [[Нуа Си|Nuwa Xi]], he swiftly turned the meeting into something resembling a battle. Only after the clash could they persuade him that Nuwa lived and a brother's fear had outrun the truth.

In [[Город Бахара|Bahara]], the heroes received fresh news: the khan was dead. The city, only lately freed from blockade, again stood upon the threshold of change. The travellers confronted [[Нефела|Nephele]] and exposed her game. After the revelation, the captain of the guard, [[Армар Саг|Armar Sag]], became Bahara's new khan.

It was a decision in the spirit of Wildlands: power passed to the man who stood closest to order, weapons and the streets.

The heroes then bought [[Бордель Халык-Бындыр|Halyk-Byndir]]. To any other company, the purchase might have seemed a strange accident, but to them the place had already become a harbour between roads, prisons, gods and debts. The celebration was loud and generously supplied with Avgar moonshine.

Bahara gained a new khan. The travellers gained a home, however temporary. And night, as it so often did, chose to exact payment in the morning.
