# Diomedes the Achaean

![[Assets/Images/Diomed_Achaean.jpg]]

## Main text

**Diomedes the Achaean** is a former [[Идеал|Paragon]] and long-serving master of gladiators in [[Город Ахей|Achaeus]]. In his youth, he served [[Гиперион I|Hyperion I]], marched to war and earned the right to lead other warriors. Age has taken his former speed, but not his ability to see a flaw in a stance—or an opponent's intent—before the first blow is struck.

In the arena, Diomedes trained fighters, many of whom had been brought there against their will. He demanded discipline and honourable combat, yet treated his pupils as people rather than expendable entertainment. To some gladiators, the old man was a stern overseer; to others, he was the only person in Achaeus willing to bind a wound, listen to their fears and teach them to survive without surrendering their dignity.

In 103 NE, Diomedes was arrested on suspicion of arranging the gladiators' escape and taken to [[Город Аргос|Argos]] for trial. By a turn of fortune, however, he escaped into [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]], where he remains in hiding.

The escape transformed an honoured Paragon into a criminal in the eyes of his own state. Diomedes has not renounced Gilas and still reveres many Ellian ideals, but he can no longer ignore cruelty justified by law and tradition. This makes him a dangerous witness: he knows too much of the army and the arenas for the authorities simply to forget him.

Even far from Achaeus, Diomedes remains a teacher. He speaks little of glory, instructing others instead to preserve their breath, guard the person beside them and understand the price of every battle. For a young warrior, meeting him may offer a rare lesson in what separates a hero from someone who merely knows how to win.
