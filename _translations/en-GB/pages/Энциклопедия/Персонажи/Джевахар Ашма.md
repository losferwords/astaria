# Djevahar Ashma

![[Assets/Images/Djevahar_Ashma.jpg]]

**Djevahar Ashma** is a [[Раджати|Rajati]] merchant and jeweller from [[Город Ванпур|Vanpur]]. His family keeps a small shop in the city market: his wife, [[Шанта Ашма|Shanta]], handles the trade and bargains with customers, while Djevahar prefers to work in the workshop behind the counter. There he receives established clients, appraises stones and repairs jewellery whose owners are unwilling to entrust it to street craftsmen.

Djevahar works in gold and silver, making earrings, signet rings, fine chains and small ritual vessels. He pays particular attention to the weight of the metal and to flaws hidden within a piece. The jeweller charges well for faultless work, but values returning customers and can keep the origin of an object or the name of its patron private for years.

Djevahar is cautious with strangers and rarely leaves the western quarters without good reason. He dislikes naming the people for whom he works and regards silence about another person's purchases as part of a craftsperson's honour. Wealthy visitors appreciate such discretion, but it also feeds market rumours about commissions delivered to him through the back door rather than across the counter.

Djevahar and Shanta have a son, [[Вивек Ашма|Vivek]]. After he disappeared, the jeweller went looking for the boy, and concern for his family came to govern many of his decisions. Shanta continued to handle the trade, but increasingly faced the customers alone while her husband tried to learn where their son had gone.

In the autumn of 106 NE, Djevahar's shop was damaged by fire. At about the same time, the city watch began to take an interest in certain pieces bearing his hallmark. That alone proved nothing: hundreds of Vanpur's residents wore Ashma's work. The jeweller was nevertheless detained for questioning, and both his shop and workshop were sealed.
