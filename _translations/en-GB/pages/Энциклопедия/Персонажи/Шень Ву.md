# Shen Wu

![[Assets/Images/Shen_Wu.jpg]]

## Main text

**Shen Wu** is a wise elder of the [[Джу|Dju]] people and a former high [[Аватар|Avatar]] of [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Lang-An]].

Shen fought in the battle for the [[Гарнизон Пасть Дракона|Dragon's Maw]] and was defeated by the army of [[Аксель Хан|Axel Khan]]. Official chronicles later credited the newly formed Wu Legion with defending [[Долина Гармонии|He-Dji-Gu]]. In truth, the unit had vanished long before. A thousand farmers were secretly taken from [[Город Чанг-Ша|Chang-Sha]], dressed as soldiers and sent to their deaths so that the legend of a living legion might be preserved.

As elder of Chang-Sha, Shen lives among ordinary farmers and works beside them in the fields, refusing to separate authority from common labour. When a nomad invasion threatened the city, he acknowledged the burden of his former choices and ordered the people evacuated across the lake to [[Город Джу-Суо|Dju-Suo]]. He would not repeat a beautiful lie at the cost of more lives.

Shen Wu is an ancestor of the young [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]], separated from her by fourteen generations.
