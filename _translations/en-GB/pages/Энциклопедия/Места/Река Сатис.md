# Satis River

![[Assets/Images/Satis_River.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Satis** is a river in eastern [[Амон-Астат|the Empire of the Sun]], carrying water from [[Озеро Кафер|Lake Kafer]] into the [[Хтоническое море|Chthonic Sea]]. It is modest beside the country's great waterways, yet the gardens, pastures and settlements along the eastern road depend upon it.

In its upper reaches, the Satis runs between stony banks and stays cool even at the height of day. Farther downriver it slows, divides into branches and leaves bands of fertile silt. Local farmers build small dams from stone and reeds, restoring them after every flood.

The waters of the Satis are known for their clarity, although storms in the mountains stain them red with clay. Old guides regard such a change as a warning: turbid water is often followed by flooding, a landslide or packs of animals driven from their accustomed ground.

Riverboats and seagoing vessels meet at the mouth. The small harbours offer a convenient road for lawful trade—and for cargo whose owners would rather avoid the great imperial ports. Along the Satis, one might therefore find a quiet settlement, a rare plant from the damp banks or the traces of a bargain that no one will discuss before a temple scribe.
