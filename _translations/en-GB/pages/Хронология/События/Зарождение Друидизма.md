# Birth of Druidism

> [!timeline] 1797 ChE
> The [[Надаир|Nadair]] encounter the fertility goddess [[Церунна|Cerunna]] upon the shores of [[Озеро Сильвиан|Lake Silvian]], and she reveals to them the path of the [[Круг Друидов|Druid Circle]].
