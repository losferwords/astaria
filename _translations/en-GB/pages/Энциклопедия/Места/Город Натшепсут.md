# Natshepsut

![[Assets/Images/Natshepsut.jpg]]

## Main text

**Natshepsut** is the centre of worship of [[Мерката|Mercate, Goddess of Night]], in [[Амон-Астат|the Empire of the Sun]]. The city's name is also Mercate's name in the language of the [[Хефат|Khefat]]. In a country where almost every great temple faces the dawn, Natshepsut appears a deliberate exception.

The principal sanctuary opens its doors after sunset. High walls shelter its inner courtyards from the heat of the day, and the dark stone floor swiftly cools beneath the night sky. Those troubled by dreams, bereavement or fear of what cannot be seen in daylight come here.

Worshippers of Ast regard the city with suspicion, yet the very existence of Natshepsut is a reminder that ancient Khefat culture was never entirely uniform. The local priests do not openly dispute the solar faith; instead, they speak of balance: humankind needs light to find the way and darkness in which to rest.

Travellers remember Natshepsut for its quiet night markets, cool gardens and the veiled faces of its pilgrims. It is easy to find an interpreter of dreams or safe lodging in the desert here—and just as easy to attract the notice of those who consider any cult of Mercate suspect.
