# Chapter 053 — The Death of an Avatar

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 105 NE, winter*

[[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] freed herself from beneath [[Аксель Хан|Axel Khan's]] poleaxe and escaped to the far side of the wall. Axel saw that [[Урист Китин|Urist]] was consumed by fury and exploited it, throwing [[Ксан Ляньши|Xian Lianshi]] directly into the path of his blow.

Urist could not stop himself. One mighty swing took the senior [[Аватар|Avatar's]] head. The Dju soldiers froze, and the defenders' morale collapsed. Pleased with the outcome, Axel called Mei “Helena” and went in search of the second Avatar—[[Нуа Си|Nuwa Xi]].

Mei raced towards the eastern gate to reach it first. Left upon the battlefield, Urist's rage gradually ebbed, and he fell exhausted.

At the eastern gate, [[Ошу Мати|Oshu]] led the blinded [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] and the woman she had saved. The defenders held back a battering ram while [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] tried to calm the crowd of refugees. Gilja carved stigmata with his raktakatars, regained his sight and joined the defence.

When the gate was breached, Oshu cast visions of death upon the nomads. Mei arrived with a warning about Axel, seized Nuwa, disguised her and tried to lead her away.

[[Ван Сан|Wan San]] brought Urist back to consciousness. Neither knew what had happened at the western gate. Axel hurled a spear at the only Imithei by the eastern gate—Oshu—but Tom caught it in flight and saved her life. He then covered the refugees with a veil of darkness and led them towards the western gate, where the paths of those fleeing the city finally converged.
