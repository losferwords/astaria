# Aidan mac Kinsley

![[Assets/Images/Aidan_mac_Kinsley.jpg]]

## Main text

**Aidan mac Kinsley** is a young [[Горец|Highlander]] of the [[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]].

Like other Highlanders, Aidan was born during a thunderstorm. From childhood, however, he seemed to lack the qualities needed to endure the [[Рунштоирм|Runstoirm]]. His clan's chieftain advised the young man to venture into the wider world and find the strength within himself. Aidan suspects that the elders truly sent him away because they were convinced he would only bring disgrace upon them all.

Aidan thereafter wandered the [[Хтоническое море|Chthonic Sea]] in search of courage and resolve. Sailors he met upon his travels gave the young [[Гойдаир|Goidair]] the nickname ‘Faulty’, and it has clung to him ever since.

In 104 NE, Aidan found himself at the heart of a storm aboard the *Atron*. Desperate to save his friends, he called upon the tempest for aid. By some unknown means, he succeeded in mastering the thunder and lightning. When the storm subsided, Aidan had gained the powers of an [[Имитей|Imithei]] and become a Highlander.
