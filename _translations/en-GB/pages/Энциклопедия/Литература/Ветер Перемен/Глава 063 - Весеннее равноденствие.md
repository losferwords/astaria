# Chapter 063 — The Spring Equinox

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 105 NE, spring*

As [[Мэй Ву|Mei]] waited for the food in the tavern, an unusual man named [[Чень Ляньши|Chen Lianshi]] joined her. He introduced himself as a monk and the empress's brother, though he said that at present he was occupied with enlightening the townspeople. His tea proved to be baijiu. Mei nearly drank herself senseless, but Chen persuaded the landlady to let her take the food away.

After breakfast, the group discussed how to enter the palace. There had been no word from [[Бао Си|Bao Xi]]. Mei and [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] went to reconnoitre and separated. Gilja caught the scent of familiar spices and came to a house where local [[Раджати|Rajati]] were secretly celebrating the spring equinox in honour of [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari]].

He danced, ate and listened to troubling news: something strange and terrible was happening in his homeland, and ever more Rajati were arriving in the capital. When his hosts realised that a true [[Ракша|Raksha]] stood before them, they knelt and promised to aid him in anything save dealings with the [[Аватар|Avatars]] and the [[Храм Дракона|Temple of the Dragon]].

Mei discovered that the temple gate guards changed shifts, leaving a brief interval when the entrance was less well protected. Then two guards seized her. She burst into flame and fled through the city by night until Chen pulled her into an alley and helped her hide. He was drinking again, this time with other people, and once more appeared almost sober. As they talked, Chen told her more about the temple and gave Mei a map of the palace.

Meanwhile, Bao Xi stumbled into the refuge where [[Урист Китин|Urist]] and [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] waited, bearing a dreadful dark wound across his back. Tom managed to dispel the dark aura and staunch the bleeding, but Bao remained unconscious.
