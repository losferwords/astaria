# Musaka

![[Assets/Images/Musaka.jpg]]

**Musaka** is a hunting dog and the constant companion of [[Томас Робинсон|Thomas Robinson]]. She is large, sturdy and enduring, with a short tricolour coat: black across her back, russet around her muzzle and legs, and white upon her chest. Her upright ears lend her a watchful air even at rest, while her attentive dark eyes rarely miss any movement near her master. On her broad leather collar, Musaka wears a small crescent pendant—a mark of the road she shares with the [[Мерката|Shadow of Mercate]].

Musaka once belonged to a huntsman from [[Регион Элессия|Elessia]] who had been sent to track fugitives from [[Гилас|Gilas]]. The pursuit ended otherwise than her former master had expected: the dog chose to remain with Thomas, as though recognising in the young hunter a man worth following. He gave her a new name, and from that day onward they were seldom apart.

Musaka understands brief gestures, whistles and the slightest movement of a hand. She can follow an old trail through a crowded market, find a path through an unfamiliar forest, guard her companions as they sleep and hold back until Thomas gives leave to advance. When danger cannot be scented, she watches her master instead: his tension speaks to her as clearly as the smell of blood or smoke.

In [[Город Натшепсут|Natshepsut]], Musaka helped Thomas hunt and shared his first peaceful home after their long flight. Later, she travelled with the wanderers across the lakes of the [[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], through clamorous cities and into the humid jungles of [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]]. Her nose has led the companions to abducted people, secret messengers and all but invisible clues; on one occasion, it was Musaka who found the tiny puncture upon a dead man's neck which no human had noticed.

To Thomas, she is far more than a useful tracker. Musaka is a living reminder that even in the midst of flight, someone may choose you of their own free will and remain by your side without oaths, rewards or questions about the past.
