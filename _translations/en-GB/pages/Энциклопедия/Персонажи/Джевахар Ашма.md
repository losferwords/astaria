# Djevahar Ashma

**Djevahar Ashma** is a [[Раджати|Rajati]] jeweller from [[Город Ванпур|Vanpur]]. His family keeps a market shop: his wife tends the stall, while Djevahar prefers to work and receive trusted visitors in the house behind it. He knows the western quarters of the city well and seldom ventures outside without good reason.

In the summer of 106 NE, heroes investigating the trade in ‘black injections’ learnt that Djevahar attended meetings of the [[Чёрный Лотос|Black Lotus]]. [[Урист Китин|Urist]] entered his shop disguised as a wealthy patron, accompanied by his companions. A failed deception caused a fire, and [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] saved Djevahar when the jeweller's clothes caught alight.

Fearing for his wife and son, the jeweller revealed everything he knew. According to Djevahar, the Black Lotus grew from a band of poachers: within the bodies of slain elephants they found black, vitrified blood vessels from which they obtained the needles of unwaking sleep. The Vanpur cell answered to [[Киран Варма|Kiran Varma]], while Djevahar himself was an intermediary able to pass messages through a network of city informers.

In exchange for his family's safety, Djevahar agreed to arrange a meeting between the heroes and Kiran. He also gave Urist an antique brooch from the Sunset Valley of Amon-Astat, its green inlays forming a cactus—the meaning carried by the Avgar's surname.
