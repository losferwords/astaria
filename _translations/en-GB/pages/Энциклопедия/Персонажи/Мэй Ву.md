# Mei Wu

![[Assets/Images/Mei-Wu.jpg]]

**Mei Wu** is a wanderer of the [[Джу|Dju]] people whose origins long remained a mystery even to herself. After leaving [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]], she travelled through [[Кадир|Qadir]] and [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]], joined [[Жаждущие|the Thirsty]] and took part in the struggle against slavery. In the west, Mei lived under the name Elena, concealing both her homeland and her unusual dominion over fire.

Her search led her to the [[Сердце Огня|Heart of Fire]]. The artefact did more than obey her: it awakened an ability to hear flame as a living force. At her side grew the white dragon [[Мэйлун|Meilong]]—not a pet, but an independent companion and the last known [[Морской дракон|sea dragon]]. Their bond drew the notice of gods, hunters and creatures who believed his kind had long since vanished.

Upon returning east, Mei found herself caught between her native Empire and the nomad advance. She helped lead the inhabitants out of besieged [[Город Найян|Nayan]], uncovered the secrets of the Wu family and learnt that her parents were [[Ао Ву|Ao Wu]] and the senior Avatar [[Ксан Ляньши|Xan Lianshi]]. The truth came too late: Xan died without ever knowing his daughter.

At the [[Храм Дракона|Temple of the Dragon]], Mei descended into the Well of Fire, pulled a scythe from the wounded body of [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Lang-An]] and helped free his servants from the power of [[Кион-Чи|Qiong-Qi]]. After the Great Dragon's deliverance, she became an [[Аватар|Avatar]] herself and received the [[Наручи Иджи|Idji Bracers]]. Mei now carries within her the experience of an exile, a rebel and one chosen by Lang-An, while continuing to seek a cure for the sickness and alien voices that torment Meilong.
