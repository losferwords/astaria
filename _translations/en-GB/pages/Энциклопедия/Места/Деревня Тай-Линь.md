# Tai-Lin

![[Assets/Images/Tai_Lin_City.jpg]]

**Tai-Lin** (太林) is a village in the south of [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]], deep within the [[Джунгли Минь-Тао|Min-Tao Jungle]]. A trade route to [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]] passes through it.

It is an important stopping place for caravans carrying spices, paper, cloth, jewellery and rare medicines. Dense jungle, humid air and fertile soil allow its people to grow rice, medicinal herbs and exotic fruit. Houses stand on stilts, their roofs often carved with birds and dragons, symbols of luck and protection; markets keep its streets lively.

Tai-Lin is also known for a temple of the [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Great Dragon]] and annual water-and-fire festivals that draw merchants and pilgrims from many lands. Beyond the market street, however, the settlement rapidly gives way to jungle. Local gatherers know which lianas yield clean water, which flowers must not be touched after rain and where predators reach the caravan road. Their guidance is expensive, but travelling without it often costs cargo or days.

The village is especially valuable to seekers of rare medicines and news from the southern border. Merchant yards collect rumours faster than official messengers, and ordinary bales sometimes conceal seeds, relics or living creatures forbidden by imperial law.
