# Sun Lianshi

![[Assets/Images/Sun_Lianshi.jpg]]

## Main text

**Sun Lianshi** is the reigning ruler of [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]] and the mortal voice of the Great [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Dragon]]. At court, she is known for her immaculate bearing, measured speech and habit of preparing meticulously for every audience. Even her enemies concede that Sun is rarely caught unawares.

Sun belongs to the noble Lianshi lineage, whose origins reach back to the first empresses of the Empire of Fire. One of her great-grandmother's cousins was likewise a wife of the Great Dragon.

At fourteen, Sun succeeded the previous empress, who had died suddenly during a feast. The girl entered her audience at the [[Храм Дракона|Temple of the Dragon]] utterly resolved to win the crown. Her kin recalled that she had prepared from childhood to become Lang-An's consort, studying the history of the great houses, court etiquette, the governance of the provinces and the art of negotiation. Of all the candidates, the Dragon chose her.

Her early accession made Sun powerful, yet also dependent upon those around her. Her advisers are inclined to regard the young empress as a convenient symbol, the Lianshi family as a continuation of its own glory, and the Temple as the keeper of the deity's will. Sun answers by striving to know more than anyone who stands before her and by ensuring that no single person remains indispensable for long.

Her reign rests upon an outward harmony beneath which lies an unyielding will. The empress can be generous, but seldom forgets an insult. She will hear an objection if it is expressed with proper dignity, yet will not tolerate public disrespect towards the throne. To the people, she remains the Dragon's chosen bride; to the court, a dangerous player; and to Sun herself, the crown is the childhood dream she must now defend every day.
