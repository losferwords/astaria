# Halyk-Byndir

![[Assets/Images/Halyk-Byndir.jpg]]

## Main text

**Halyk-Byndir** is one of [[Город Бахара|Bahara's]] largest brothels, drawing sailors from every corner of [[Астария|Astaria]]. Alongside its principal services, it offers rooms and meals to weary travellers, making it a tavern as well.

The two-storey building holds a kitchen, bar, reception and common dining hall below, with rooms for sleep and private rest above. Like many Baharan brothels, it long had no permanent staff. Competition for work was fierce, and the quality of service unpredictable.

Its harbour setting defines the house. Sailors, mercenaries, merchants and fugitives come seeking less refinement than shelter, food, warmth and a night survived. Halyk-Byndir is rougher, louder and freer than the Lang-An Empire's elegant pleasure houses, such as [[Бордель Уй-Джан|Ui-Djan]]; fights start more easily here, but so does aid for someone just off the road.

After Bahara changed hands, [[Урист Китин|Urist Kitin]] and his companions acquired the establishment. Under them it became a stable base for travellers, merchants and people needing help without questions. Former worker [[Навчин Урт|Navchin Urt]] later left to seek adventure with the nomads.

Shared supplies and experience connect Halyk-Byndir with Ui-Djan in [[Город Джу-Суо|Dju-Suo]]. The eastern house inspired staff training, careful recruitment and plans for a bathhouse, yet Halyk-Byndir remains Baharan to the bone: every rule must coexist with harbour noise, Avgar spirits and guests who settle arguments loudly.

After thefts, interrupted supplies and nomad attacks on the road, its priority is no longer growth at any cost, but a durable refuge where the road's roughness does not erase human warmth or workers' safety.
