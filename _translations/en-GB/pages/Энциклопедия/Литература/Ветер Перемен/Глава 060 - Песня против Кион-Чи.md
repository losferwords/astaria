# Chapter 060 — A Song against Qiong-Qi

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 105 NE, spring*

After hearing [[Рон Эллиот|Ron's]] report, the group decided to travel to [[Город Джу-Суо|the capital]] with the Avgars. To lead an army through [[Лес Шеньянь|Shenyan]], they needed a large inscribed tablet for the sheng-hu. Materials were scarce, and [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] suggested dismantling a small shrine to [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Lang-An]]. [[Шень Ву|Shen Wu]] warned them that the shrine bore a ward which only an [[Аватар|Avatar]] could remove.

[[Мэй Ву|Mei]] made the attempt, although she did not formally consider herself an Avatar. To everyone's surprise, the ward obeyed her. More than that, she transferred its protection to [[Ао Ву|her mother's]] carpets hidden in a warehouse.

[[Навчин Урт|Navchin]] appeared in the morning and asked whether [[Айсан Баят|Aysan Bayat]] had left the city. Gilja discovered the cause of her fear: in her youth, Navchin had stolen Aysan's sweetheart, and Aysan still wanted to settle the score.

The army advanced through the forest. The sheng-hu left it alone, but soon the sounds of battle came from ahead. Monstrous creatures attacked the travellers and the Avgars. They resembled [[Раджати|Rajati]], but possessed row upon row of sharp teeth and a blood-mad fury in their eyes. The attackers were driven off with difficulty, and then it emerged that the sheng-hu themselves had also been assailed.

By evening the host reached the river. Mei heard the voice of [[Кион-Чи|Qiong-Qi]] once more. Urist resolved to challenge the ancient predator to honourable combat. Qiong-Qi refused and devoured the Avgar warriors one by one, until Urist asked [[Батар Саг|Batar Sag]] to perform the legendary Avgar rite of challenge.

The song of thousands of Avgars proved stronger than guile. Qiong-Qi stepped from the forest in all his terrible splendour.
