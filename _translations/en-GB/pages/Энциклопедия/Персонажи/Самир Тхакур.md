# Samir Thakur

![[Assets/Images/Samir_Thakur.jpg]]

**Samir Thakur** is a physician from [[Город Ванпур|Vanpur]], a [[Ракша|Raksha]] and the head of the city's hospital. The people there know him not as a fearsome servant of the Harvest, but as a weary doctor who receives the poor, argues with priests, goes without sleep and too often remains beside those whom everyone else has abandoned.

From without, Samir appears gentle and almost quiet. He speaks softly, studies a patient at length before prescribing treatment and has little patience for temple pomp at the bedside of the dying. Beneath that restraint, however, lies a dangerous inner fracture. He has not renounced [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari]], but has become too absorbed in studying the new disease and helping those who suffer to obey the duties of a Raksha without question.

Within his overcrowded hospital, Samir has assembled the most comprehensive observations of the [[Ракта-Доша|Rakta-Dosha]]. The illness begins with mounting weakness and fever towards evening, then wastes the internal organs and draws the sufferer into a sleep from which they never wake. A substantial transfusion of compatible blood can reverse its course, but no universal donor exists.

For the sake of this research, Samir ceased to take part in the Harvests, hoping that the absence of a single Raksha would go unnoticed. His choice bought the sick precious time, but brought the physician before the judgement of his own faith. Samir does not hide his findings from those who truly heal others, yet fears that temple dogma will turn knowledge of blood into a pretext for hasty sacrifice.
