# Chapter 004 — The New Paragon

*The New Paragon*

[[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth]] and [[Кассиопея Атанатос|Cassia]] leave [[Остров Хтон|Chthon Island]] upon their raft and reach the shores of [[Архея|Archaea]], only to be captured by the [[Эллийцы|Ellians]]. They are examined in [[Город Аргос|Argos]] and, once recognised as [[Имитей|Imithei]], sent to [[Город Ахей|Achaeus]] for the [[Танатомахия|Thanatomachia]], a ritual battle against the finest warriors of [[Гилас|Gilas]].

*99 NE*

Kenneth and Cassia spend six months as prisoners of Gilas before a young claimant to the mantle of [[Идеал|Paragon]] appears: [[Персефона Раптис|Persephone Raptis]]. She chooses Kenneth as her opponent. This chapter of the saga recounts Persephone's Thanatomachia against the Highlander.

After an arduous battle, Persephone prevails. Kenneth lies upon the sand awaiting the final blow that will grant her the Paragon's power. At that very moment, an alarm sounds: the city is under attack, and every warrior must immediately defend the walls. The wounded Kenneth is carried from the arena, while Persephone, furious at the unfinished duel, assumes command of the city's defence.
