# Badrakali

![[Assets/Images/Badrakali_Village.jpg]]

**Badrakali** stands between a fertile plain and stony hills, where the earth is red as dried blood and the air smells of hearth smoke, turmeric and wet leaves. The road to Suraj Ka Ghar's interior passes through it, keeping its small square crowded with carts, pilgrims and sellers of medicinal roots.

Low clay houses have ochre walls, inner yards and monsoon-tested palm-leaf or old-tile roofs. A market and inn occupy the centre; a modest temple of [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari]] at the edge is known for its strict [[Ракша|Raksha]] school.

South lies an old stepwell cut deep into red rock. After heavy rain its water turns dark scarlet. Elders call it a trace of an earlier Harvest; practical people blame clay. Flowers are left on its steps in any case.

The village raised [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilya Kangarlavehotri]] and the Marjari sisters [[Мерави Марджари|Meravi]] and [[Чори Марджари|Chori]]. It is a quiet stop that remembers even a household hearth lives under a goddess of two faces.
