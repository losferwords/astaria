# Chapter 018 — The Spider Queen's Crown

*Qadir, 104 NE, spring*

A curious respite settled over Samaan. The travellers found themselves in a tavern where hookah smoke rose in languid rings, softening their anxiety without dispelling it. Beyond the walls, the laws of [[Кадир|Qadir]] remained in force; dangerous artefacts rested in their pockets; and somewhere nearby, [[Данис ибн Синай|Danis's]] fate was being decided.

[[Ошу Мати|Oshu Mati]] performed a rite to speak with [[Руфу|Rufu]]. The God of Death answered not in majestic splendour, but in a manner almost absurd and therefore more disturbing: his presence entered a roasted stork. In Astaria, gods often address the world as they please, leaving mortals to grasp the meaning without becoming ensnared by its form.

Danis, meanwhile, was brought before [[Амира Аль-Азвар|Amira al-Azwar]]. The Eternal Princess of Samaan was speaking with [[Персефона Раптис|Persephone Raptis]] and promised her several [[Мститель|Avengers]] to settle the trouble with [[Жаждущие|the Thirsty]]. Thus, for an instant, the former pursuit from Gilas and the present affairs of Qadir met within a single hall.

While Danis drew ever closer to the power of western Qadir, [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] found the Thirsty's refuge. A guide named [[Рафаил Чалак|Rafael]] led the travellers through the sewers. Samaan's drains were no less foul than any underground passage: damp, stench, dark water and narrow tunnels where every sound returned as another's whisper.

Below, [[Урист Китин|Urist]] suddenly fell ill, and his weakness came at the worst possible moment. Spiders attacked from the darkness. At first they seemed an ordinary subterranean danger, but soon the guardian of the nest appeared—a gigantic spider, a true relic of ancient nature.

The battle in the sewers was filthy and arduous. Webbing clung to clothes, legs scraped upon stone, darkness obscured sight, and the cramped space allowed no freedom of movement. Yet Oshu saw more than an enemy in the creature. She felt drawn to its [[Кристалл Души|Soul Crystal]] and resolved to capture it in the [[Корона Душ|Crown of Souls]] at any cost.

She succeeded.

The Crown of Souls ceased to be merely an enigmatic Archaean object of unknown purpose. In Oshu's hands, it revealed for the first time that it could contain something living, dangerous and terribly precious. What had been won beneath Samaan might become a weapon, a mistake, or the beginning of a new understanding of ancient artefacts.
