# Chapter 009 — Lament of The Night (Epilogue)

*Lament of The Night*

Spring 100 NE. The storm retreats from [[Гора Атафет|Atafet]]. Below, on the parched earth of [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]], people lift their faces and open hands to the rain. Water fills empty vessels; the dust at their thresholds darkens. After five years of drought, even its scent seems to promise life.

There is still snow above. Night slowly hides the path to the summit. Far below, people keep looking towards the mountain. A dark slope stands between them and the sky; through the sound of rain, they can hear nothing.

Later, as the [[Горец|Highlander]]'s saga spreads across [[Астария|Astaria]], travellers come to Atafet. They carry [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth]]'s name and the story of a sword raised to meet the lightning. On the summit, they hope to find something before which they can kneel. But his body is not there. No one finds his sword either. They bring back only accounts of snow, cold and the long descent.

Yet another rumour travels the roads. They say that someone weeps on Atafet at night. Very softly, almost soothingly. In some tellings, the voice comes from beside the path; in others, from far above it, beyond a ridge of snow. Some speak of grief so deep that they are afraid to listen. Others recall only a strange tenderness.

Below, people argue: the wind in the clefts can make stranger sounds. Not everyone agrees, but no one can repeat the words of the lament. Ask who hears it, and the teller refers to a friend, who refers to someone else. Somehow, the person who can give a first-hand account always remains out of reach.
