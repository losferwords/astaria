# Telemachus II

![[Assets/Images/Telemachus II.jpg]]

## Main text

**Telemachus II** is the reigning king of [[Талассия|Thalassia]], a mariner and diplomat. During his reign he has strengthened his country's influence upon the [[Полуостров Таллас|Tallas Peninsula]] and supported the opening of routes to the eastern realms. Thalassian ships now appear more frequently in distant ports, while agreements for pilotage and trade have sometimes brought the country more than military victories.

Telemachus's calm disposition should not be mistaken for indecision. He prefers to understand an adversary's desires in advance and offer an agreement before a dispute becomes a war. Relations with [[Гилас|Gilas]] and [[Лунаар|Lunaar]] nevertheless remain strained. Control of sea routes, ancient borders and mutual suspicion weigh too heavily to be dispelled by a single accomplished speech.

Telemachus's marriage to [[Эвридика Лефийская|Eurydice of Lepha]] in 79 NE bound Thalassia to [[Катахтонос|Katachthonos]]. The union was personal as well as political, and Eurydice's murder upon their wedding anniversary became a grievous loss to the king. Suspicions that a Lunaar [[Тень|Shadow]] was involved further embittered relations between the two realms.

Telemachus's only acknowledged daughter, [[Арета Лефийская|Areta of Lepha]], has inherited the throne of Katachthonos and remains heir to Thalassia. To the king she is a beloved child; to his advisers, the future of two countries; and to their enemies, a vulnerable link. Telemachus tries to protect his daughter without turning that care into the distant rule of her kingdom, yet the boundary grows ever harder to maintain.

In the later years of his reign, Telemachus must consider not merely new routes but his legacy. His decisions will determine whether the bond between Thalassia and Katachthonos becomes an alliance of equals or a conflict Areta must resolve without him.
