# Chapter 009 — Lament of the Night (Epilogue)

*Lament of the Night*

After the saga's publication, travellers from across [[Астария|Astaria]] journeyed to the summit of [[Гора Атафет|Mount Atafet]] to honour the fallen [[Горец|Highlander]]. Yet none has ever found [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth's]] body or his sword. A few claim that at night they have seen an immense wandering shadow amongst the snowbound slopes, as though Night herself had come to mourn the great hero—Kenneth the Stormcaller.
