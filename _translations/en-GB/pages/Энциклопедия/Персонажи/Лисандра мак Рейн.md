# Lisandra mac Rayne

![[Assets/Images/Lisandra Macrayne.jpg]]

## Main text

**Lisandra mac Rayne** is a [[Гойдаир|Goidair]] woman, a former warrior and the mother of [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth mac Rayne]]. From the age of seventeen, she guarded the [[Перевал Путь Забытых|Pass of the Forgotten]], keeping [[Тень|Shadows]] from entering the lands of the [[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]]. When her years of service were behind her, Lisandra settled in [[Деревня Эрвин|Erwin]], yet retained the resolve of one accustomed to meeting danger face to face.

In 79 NE, during a fierce thunderstorm, Lisandra gave birth to a son and named him Kenneth. The child born beneath the thunder was recognised as one of the Named and prepared from boyhood for the [[Рунштоирм|Runstoirm]]. The saga [[Зов Бури|*Call of Thunder*]] opens in Lisandra's voice: she tells her son of his homeland, the memory of the clans and the destiny awaiting a future [[Горец|Highlander]].

Many years later, [[Кассиопея Атанатос|Cassia]] came to Erwin and told Lisandra of Kenneth's wanderings, of the rain that saved [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]] and of his presumed death upon [[Гора Атафет|Mount Atafet]]. Pride in her son became inseparable from grief, but Lisandra would not allow his sacrifice to vanish from memory. She carried the tale of the Stormcaller throughout the Thunder Clans; in time, those accounts became the saga *Call of Thunder*.
