# Chapter 070 — The Halls of the Dragon

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 105 NE, summer*

The monks admitted the group into the depths of the [[Храм Дракона|Temple of the Dragon]]. [[Бао Си|Bao Xi]] led them to the gates of the Halls, but they were closed. He sat down to meditate and fill the doors with Qi. [[Урист Китин|Urist]] and [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] had no wish to wait: the gate bore a slot identical to the one in the Temple of Qiong-Qi. Urist placed the [[Чёрный Вестник|Black Harbinger]] against it, and an explosion of energy flung the doors open and knocked Bao unconscious.

A host of [[Аватар|Avatars]] stood beyond. Their senior attendant explained that [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Lang-An]] had been wounded by [[Мерката|Mercate's Scythe]] and lay at the bottom of the Well of Fire, preparing to die. The Avatars had become puppets of [[Кион-Чи|Qiong-Qi]], who had learnt to use the same bond as the Dragon. They had sealed themselves inside to prevent the predator from harming the capital.

Gilja could bear no more and rushed into the Halls in search of anything useful. In the Great Hall he saw a well of unbearable heat and tried to locate the artefact vault. One of the Avatars under Qiong-Qi's control stopped him, yet did not stand in his way. Instead, he helped Gilja take the blood of [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari]] from the vault.

Urist and Tom tried to determine how Lang-An might be saved. Remembering that Bao's wound from the Scythe had been healed with gold and powerful objects, they decided to gather items tied to the Dragon. The empress gave them a hairpin that served as the treasury key, and the travellers took the gifts Lang-An had once bestowed upon his wives.

One problem remained: returning to the Halls while Bao lay unconscious. Sun Lianshi remembered a rumour about a seamstress who had once found a way below.

As the travellers discussed their plan, [[Артур Бомейн|Arthur Beaumain]] attacked. He lunged at Tom and prevented him from using Mercate's necklace. In the struggle, Tom fell from a palace window and crashed onto a balcony several floors below.
