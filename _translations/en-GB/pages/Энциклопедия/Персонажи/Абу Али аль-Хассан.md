# Abu Ali al-Hassan

![[Assets/Images/Abu_Ali_Al-Hassan.jpg]]

## Main text

**Abu Ali al-Hassan** is one of the first [[Мститель|Avengers]], Eternal Prince of central [[Кадир|Qadir]] and the country's longest-reigning active ruler. Before the [[Раскол Кадира|Schism of Qadir]], he served as the general of King [[Астиаг II|Astyages II]] and was known as a man to whom a soldier's oath mattered more than personal advantage.

It was Abu Ali who led the uprising against the king and beheaded him with his own hand after Astyages broke his compact with [[Альзаман|Alzaman]]. The first three Avengers then divided the realm so that Qadir would never again depend upon one person's will. Abu Ali took the central lands of the [[Пустыня Халисат|Khalisat Desert]], [[Город Сераф|Seraph]] and the cities around [[Озеро Зураиф|Zuraif]] and [[Озеро Суэйд|Suwayd]].

Long centuries have made him cautious and almost unreadable. He seldom raises his voice, has little patience for the splendour of court and prefers a counsellor to bring precise intelligence rather than a pleasing answer. His subjects honour him as the guardian of unity, yet fear the unchanging gaze of a man to whom several human generations may seem no more than a brief political season.

Abu Ali personally oversees the training of Avengers who follow the Way of Times. He demands discipline, not haste: a pupil must understand the cost of every arrested moment and never wield the gift merely for convenience. To the prince, mastery of time is a responsibility towards those who cannot defend themselves against the consequences of such power.

In recent years, courtiers have noticed a change in him. The prince visits the [[Храм Дар Мараат|Temple of Dar Maraat]] more often, cancels certain audiences and sometimes answers a question as though the conversation had already taken place. Some consider it a sign of weariness. Others believe the eldest Avenger has glimpsed the approach of an event of which he does not yet dare to speak.
