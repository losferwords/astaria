# Chapter 027 — Council of Admirals

*[[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], 104 NE, spring*

[[Мэй Ву|Mei]] awoke in [[Неарх Мелит|Nearch Melit's]] cabin amid the scents of sea, timber and shipboard spirits. Negotiations aboard the *Typhon* lacked the ceremony of a palace hall, but it was there that the fate of Bahara's blockade would be decided.

[[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] tried to slip a coin into Nearch's clothes, only for it to end up upon Mei. Even the smallest deceptions in Bahara seemed determined to change their intended recipient.

The admirals' council debated lifting the blockade. [[Айсан Баят|Aysan Bayat]] interrupted them, storming in and casting severed nereid heads at Nearch's feet. It was no argument, but a blow upon the table delivered in blood. The council resolved to end the blockade by evening and grant the travellers a reward.

In the harbour tavern called the Salted Crab, the heroes found [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari]]. The goddess drank blood from several drunken patrons and fell asleep like a weary beast after the hunt. The travellers covered her with a cloth, for sometimes even a deity who has brought terror looks defenceless in sleep.

After receiving their reward, the company prepared for the road. [[Урист Китин|Urist]] tried to sell one of [[Мерката|Mercate's]] feathers, but Tom prevented it by knocking him unconscious. [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] saw everything, and the new company understood ever more clearly that loyalty and recklessness often walked side by side among these people.

Having bought two camels and a donkey, the travellers set out for [[Хангорская долина|Hangor Valley]]. Along the way, they drove off nomads attacking [[Унгол Баят|Ungol Bayat]]. Night nevertheless claimed its due: during a halt, a gigantic [[Рух|Rukh]] carried Tom away.
