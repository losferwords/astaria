# Xiao Sha

![[Assets/Images/Xiao_Sha.jpg]]

## Main text

**Xiao Sha** is an [[Аватар|Avatar]] of [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]] and the daughter of [[Сунь Ляньши|Empress Sun Lianshi's]] adviser, [[Цинь Ша|Qin Sha]]. Born and raised in the capital of [[Город Джу-Суо|Dju-Suo]], her fate was decided in childhood. Driven by political ambition, her father sent her to train at the [[Храм Дракона|Temple of the Dragon]], hoping to cultivate an instrument of his own influence within the Avatars' spiritual order. Xiao displayed exceptional ability from an early age, but the harsh monastic discipline and relentless pressure of her mentors hardened her character and bred a concealed contempt for service without freedom.

After attaining the rank of senior Avatar, she discovered her father's treacherous intrigues and learnt how he had used her as a pawn. Xiao left the Temple, claiming that she was needed to “defend Dju-Suo from disorder and unrest”, although in truth she was fleeing the role imposed upon her.

In the years that followed, Xiao immersed herself in the life of the lower city. Drunken nights, street brawls and her unrestrained use of the Dragon's power made her a scandalous urban legend. Her fiery temper was matched by a rare ability to summon flame without meditation, frightening even hardened warriors. After several years of disorder, she settled in the red-lantern quarter and became a door guard at the [[Бордель Уй-Джан|Ui-Djan]] brothel. Following the mysterious poisoning of its former owner, she took the establishment for herself.

Rumour holds that Xiao still maintains ties with the Temple, though no longer as its servant. Instead, she acts as a patron to exiles and criminals. Beneath her defiant manner, a measure of nobility endures: she secretly aids orphans and women who have fled the tyranny of palace or family.
