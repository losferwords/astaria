# Chapter 000 — Before the Storm (Prologue)

*Before the Storm*

The [[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]] of the [[Гойдаир|Goidair]] live in the north of the continent of [[Кельн|Keln]]. Their tradition holds that a child born during a thunderstorm is marked for greatness.

*79 NE*

The saga begins in a small village called [[Деревня Эрвин|Erwin]], in the foothills of the [[Горный хребет Грань Тьмы|Edge of Darkness]]. A storm is gathering, and a young woman named [[Лисандра мак Рейн|Lisandra]] prepares to bear her first child. Though frightened by her first labour, she is immeasurably happy that her child may enter the world in a thunderstorm and grow to greatness.

Lisandra sings a lullaby called “Il la ba far noelly” in the Dair tongue.
