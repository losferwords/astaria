# Nearch Melit

![[Assets/Images/Nearch_Melit.jpg]]

## Main text

**Nearch Melit** is a [[Наварх|Navarch]], commander of [[Талассия|Thalassia's]] southern fleet and captain of the trireme *Typhon*. He is equally assured in a sea battle, across a negotiating table and within a bargain best omitted from the harbour ledgers. Nearch is particularly willing to carry contraband when it profits Thalassia while simultaneously vexing [[Гилас|Gilas]].

During the blockade of [[Город Бахара|Bahara]], it was aboard the *Typhon* that a council found the means to halt a confrontation between the Thalassian fleet, [[Кадир|Qadir]] and the Avgars before a dispute over trade became a war. Nearch can accept a peaceful solution, but only when it does not resemble weakness and leaves the sea free.

Only his sole daughter, [[Рея Мелит|Rhea]], matters to him more than authority and reputation. He takes pride in her becoming a Navarch in her own right, although their shared stubbornness often turns concern into an argument between two captains, each certain that they know the better course through a storm.
