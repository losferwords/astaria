# Black Harbinger

![[Assets/Images/Black_Harbinger.jpg]]

The **Black Harbinger** is an enigmatic object sometimes found in [[Археи|Archaean]] temples and ancient ruins. It plainly belongs to a remote, vanished age, though most scholars consider it worthless.

The artefact is a perfectly regular black rectangle, slightly smaller than the palm of a hand. Its surface and proportions are so exact that they seem almost unnatural.

Some owners claim to hear voices and strange sounds in its presence; others insist that the object is wholly inert. The material from which it was made remains unidentified and resists both smelting and every known form of working.
