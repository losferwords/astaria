# Styx

![[Assets/Images/Styx.jpg]]

## Main text

**Styx** is a [[Менада|maenad]] from the island of [[Остров Рёст|Ryost]]. Her kind ordinarily keep apart from human settlements, but Styx appears to harbour no fear of human company. The wild cat was last seen in [[Город Бахара|Bahara]] beside [[Аристея Кронос|Aristea Kronos]]; the reasons for so unlikely an alliance remain a mystery.
