# Chapter 003 — Voice of the Dead God

*[[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], 104 NE, spring*

After the attack by the *Proteus*, the captives awoke in a mysterious hut near a river delta. The air smelt of rotting herbs, smoke and death. [[Ошу Мати|Oshu Mati]], a priestess of the [[Ваку|Vaku]], was preparing a rite for [[Руфу|Rufu]], god of the dead. Even here, across the sea, her bond with her distant homeland, her mother and the spirit world remained strong, though black scars reminded her that all power exacted a price.

The body of a powerfully built man lay within the hut, chosen for the rite. When acrid, sweet smoke filled the room, the dead man seemed to rise and spoke in a voice Oshu could understand. Rufu thanked her for showing respect to a worthy sacrifice, but gently reproached her for the lives newly offered. He reminded her of her mother, the value of life and [[Ицунэ|Itsune]], whom old familiarity led him to call his sister—the goddess who grants peace to souls. Finding Itsune directly was almost impossible, but Rufu showed the path: seek her priests, the [[Онмёдзи|Onmyoji]], called demon hunters in the common tongue. They resembled the Dju and often concealed their eyes beneath a blindfold.

Makoto, hearing the God of the Dead through smoke and terror, understood how dangerous their plight had become. Itsune alone decided whom to take and whom to return from the realm of death, and disturbing that order might bring calamity even when another god attempted it. The captives escaped, Oshu disappeared, and the [[Корона Душ|Crown of Souls]] was found in the hut with a crystal set inside. The [[Глаз Калипсо|Eye of Calypso]] answered the crown with light, as though recognising part of a greater mystery.

Their road led to Bahara, a city made uneasy by news of the khan's death. The guards watched every stranger. Piraeus learnt that the *Atron's* cargo had been transferred to the *Proteus*, but Aristea was no longer aboard: her crew had betrayed their captain and thrown her overboard, then turned to the slave trade. Amongst their captives was [[Кирида Ариса|Kirida Arisa]], an Onmyoji bound to Makoto's past. The travellers bought her freedom together with her katana.

Arisa knelt before Makoto and begged forgiveness. She had failed to protect the temple, save the people of Osaka or spare him long months of slavery. Seeking to wash away her shame, she had committed seppuku, but Itsune refused to take her and restored her to life as an Onmyoji. Now the pain of the dead overflowed within her heart, and she hoped one day to repay Makoto for her freedom.

In Bahara, the threads of fate drew tighter. Arisa told Makoto that a woman stood behind him and wished to speak. The rite of *Seishin to Yugo Suru* would require a quiet place, a mirror or vessel of water, higanbana flowers and a guide. As they prepared, Aidan's scars flared with pain. Screams like those he had heard when the lightning struck sounded within his mind. A thunderous voice begged for mercy and pleaded for the torment to end. The guards intervened and brought the heroes before [[Нефела|Nephele]], who told them of the khan's disappearance and promised a reward for evidence. Aristea's blade became their first gift to the city.

That evening, outside Halyk-Byndir, Piraeus returned to the gaming table. A beautiful Thalassian calling herself Rhea lost a considerable sum to him and offered another form of payment. She was soon revealed as a nereid. She stole the Eye of Calypso and drew the heroes towards her lair. Cassia helped them find her despite trying to dissuade Piraeus from the search.

In a warehouse filled with crates of Soul Crystals, Piraeus found his father, Pelemeus Nikos, near death. At first Pelemeus mistook his son for the nereid, but then recognised him, initiated him amongst the Navarchs, revealed the secret of gunpowder and entrusted him with an unusual weapon called *Scylla*. Touching the Eye of Calypso, Pelemeus granted his son access to knowledge concerning the goddess of fortune's sleep.

When Piraeus's father died, Makoto saw Itsune come for his soul. The goddess said that freeing the spirit bound to Makoto's family relic required the [[Сердце Огня|Heart of Fire]]. Without the lantern Chochin-obake, the road into the realm of death was perilous, but an object filled with living energy—such as an active Soul Crystal—might suffice for a single crossing. The heroes spent the crystal from the Crown of Souls, only for Itsune to reveal a bitter truth: the relic was incomplete, and its spirit could find no peace until Makoto recovered the second half.

A commotion then erupted in the next room. Two Rheas stood opposed, each claiming the other was an impostor. Arisa helped Makoto see through an Onmyoji's eyes and recognise the nereid. The true [[Рея Мелит|Rhea Melit]] revealed her design to Piraeus: she had stolen a plan from Calypso's archives and taken it to [[Хангор|Khangor]], hoping the smith-god could wake the sleeping goddess. Khangor said this would require either bringing Calypso to the [[Великая Кузня|Great Forge]] or freeing [[Тарун|Tarun]] from captivity in exchange for his promise to awaken the Goddess of Fortune. At Tarun's name, Aidan understood that his own pain was bound to another chain yet to be broken.
