# Eirafon River

![[Assets/Images/Eirafon_River.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Eirafon** is a cold river of north-western [[Кельн|Keln]], running through the hilly [[Равнина Вайтфилд|Whitefield Plain]]. Its channel is shallow but fast: water tumbles over flat black stones and remains cold enough to numb fingers even in summer.

The [[Храм Меркаты|Temple of Mercate]] stands at its source. At night, water near the temple reflects the stars more brightly than the sky around it, so pilgrims come before a long journey, draw it into dark vessels and ask the goddess to guard their dreams. The attendants do not call the river sacred—by their account, only the night it knows how to keep is sacred.

Further downstream, white grasses cover the banks. Their fine stems open after sunset and hold drops of dew until noon. Dried flowers make a mild calming remedy, though too strong an infusion causes heavy and remarkably vivid dreams.

In spring, the Eirafon floods the meadows and leaves small fish-rich coves. Then the people of [[Деревня Гримсайд|Grimside]] fish by night with shuttered lanterns: bright light, local belief says, distracts the river and makes it carry good fortune downstream.
