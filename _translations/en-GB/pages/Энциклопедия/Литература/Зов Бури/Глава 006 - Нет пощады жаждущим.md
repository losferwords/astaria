# Chapter 006 — No Mercy for Thirsty

*No Mercy for Thirsty*

In [[Город Атафет|Atafet]], the sound of hammers carries beneath stone vaults. The city lies inside the [[Гора Атафет|mountain]], among forges, foundries and bridges above vast halls. Here [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth]] finds [[Арно Эклунд|Arno Eklund]], a smith who can forge his [[Меч Де'лаим|De'laim]]. Only the price of the blade stands between the Highlander and his journey's goal. He has nothing to pay it with: everything of value goes down with the Litei.

Arno offers him work at the city's military academy. Kenneth trains soldiers and militia, while [[Кассиопея Атанатос|Cassia]] cares for drought victims at the refuge. Over several months, they save the money. Another six months pass as a sword capable of conducting the storm's power takes shape in the forge. While the blade is made, the people among whom the travellers live speak ever more often of approaching war.

In spring 100 NE, Kenneth finally receives his weapon. The long road brings him to his sword—and to a city that will soon need it for its defence.

[[Гилас|Gilas]] answers the [[Вторжение Амон-Астата в город Ахей|invasion of Achaeus]] with a campaign into [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]]. The Ellian army retakes its city and advances, seizing the western lands of the Sun Empire. Now the [[Война Жаждущих|War of the Thirsty]] reaches Atafet. [[Персефона Раптис|Persephone Raptis]] leads the host: the warrior who comes within a blow of killing Kenneth in the arena. She still awaits another chance to enter the [[Танатомахия|Thanatomachia]] and wants to return home victorious.

There are no [[Светоносный|Lightbringers]] in the city to lead its defence. The council seeks any [[Имитей|Imithei]] willing to help for a reward. Kenneth and Cassia are already here. One more woman answers the call.

[[Рея Мелит|Rhea Melit]].

After the roar of the waves off Chthon, her voice returns among stone and clashing weapons. The captain is alive. Her ship and crew remain at sea, while she must find money, materials and people to sail again. Atafet's council promises to help with a new vessel in return for defending the city. Rhea accepts. She has a chance to reclaim her future, if these fortifications hold.

No Mercy for Thirsty carries her voice. In its opening lines she briefly names everything she loses in the wreck, as though testing the last thing left to her: she is still alive. Now a Navarch without a deck beneath her feet takes command again. Before her stand people she must hold together under the enemy's blows.

The din at the entrance fortifications drowns out the familiar sound of the forges. Stone shakes. Atafet is full of workshops, homes, wounded people and those the drought already drives from their land. Behind every stretch of the defences are people who fear the city's fall. No help is visible beyond the walls. Rhea calls the defenders to stand beside her, even when pain and fear drive them back.

In the first pre-chorus, she addresses the wounded. She sees their blood and still demands that they hold: the battle is only beginning, and they can endure together. Her voice carries a captain's habit of making a crew act as one amid disaster. Now those familiar orders ring out at the foot of a mountain.

The short chorus beats out a stubborn rhythm of resistance. Its few words can be repeated when breath runs too short for a speech. Endure another assault. Stay on your feet a little longer. Beyond the city's walls, the army is still there, and there is no respite.

Then, in the midst of battle, Rhea resolves to reach Persephone.

She breaks away from the people she commands. The decision comes within the fighting, without a plan prepared in advance: strike the enemy commander and cut the head from the advancing host. In place of a long siege, Rhea tries to force the war into a single duel she believes she might still win.

In her hands are a kopis and a rare Thalassian gunpowder pistol. Her voice takes on the bravado of someone about to put her weapons to work. Steel, a shot, smoke: the distance to Persephone closes, and Rhea's whole world narrows with it.

She clings to the thought that her opponent is still human, without the power of a [[Идеал|Paragon]]. That final blow in the arena never falls. But Persephone knows how to win without initiation, and guards stand beside her. Beyond the separate figures, the full scale of what Rhea plunges into becomes clear: an entire army of [[Гилас|Gilas]].

Now weapons surround her on every side. There is no way back. Rhea is wounded, and in the second pre-chorus the appeal changes: the blood is her own. The resolve she asks of others must now carry her through. She can still see the commander before her and insists to herself that the fight is not decided.

The final chorus repeats. Rhea tries to stand within the enemy ring; pain hampers her movements, but the blade remains in her hand. She tightens her grip on the kopis and advances once more towards Persephone's spear.
