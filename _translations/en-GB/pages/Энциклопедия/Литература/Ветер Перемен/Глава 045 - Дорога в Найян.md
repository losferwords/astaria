# Chapter 045 — The Road to Nayan

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 104 NE, autumn*

After the night's feast, [[Деревня Улехей|Ulehey]] greeted not the dawn, but hordes of [[Вермин|vermin]]. [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja's]] spices still clouded the defenders' minds, forcing them to fight through a compulsive desire to laugh, dance and forget their fear. [[Вигдис Меткая|Vigdis]] and [[Урист Китин|Urist]] held firmest, though even their strength had limits.

Vigdis fell, ready for a glorious death and ascension. [[Винтра|Wintra's]] roar sounded in the distance, and soon the goddess of winter covered the village with her immense body. She took Vigdis in her claws and carried her to Winterheim, while the vermin scattered in mindless terror.

The battle ended with Urist's cry. His fury struck the creatures so fiercely that the remnants of the horde retreated, but he collapsed exhausted, a dagger in his side. Delirious at the brink of death, Urist insisted that [[Аксель Хан|Axel Khan]] was a liar. The Avgars argued while Gilja drew the dagger and bound the dreadful wound. To prove the truth, Urist ordered the strongest warriors to bring him Deathbringer. They could not. His mother lifted the hammer with ease, making their doubt in the nomads' faith plain.

Many Avgars remained to rebuild the village. [[Чани Сан|Chani San]], however, declared that she would take [[Ли Сан|Li San]] to [[Город Найян|Nayan]] for safety. Urist argued, but the threat of losing the dowry carried almost as much force as military necessity.

When the company travelled east, the San caravan's trail divided. The travellers chose the road towards Harmony Valley and soon saw Nayan, a beautiful city of forges beneath a veil of smoke. The guard received them warily, but [[Мэй Ву|Mei]] persuaded a guide to escort them to the palace.

Li San pined for Ulehey within the palace. Her mother had left for several days, and Li herself was forbidden to leave her chambers. Urist promised to bring her meat, and the company went to a local tavern, where tea, mahjong and costly spiced dishes seemed almost a foreign rite to the Avgars.

When [[Мэйлун|Meilong]] fell from Mei's hood, the tavern froze between terror and worship. Urist swiftly turned the miracle into a source of coin, while an [[Аватар|Avatar]] advised Mei to show the young dragon at a temple. Later, at the market, [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] neatly shot a runaway pig, and the butcher invited him to an imperial hunt in [[Лес Шеньянь|Shenyan]].
