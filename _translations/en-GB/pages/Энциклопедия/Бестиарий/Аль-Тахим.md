# Al-Tahim

![[Assets/Images/Al-Tahim.jpg]]

## Main text

**Al-Tahims** are rare subterranean predators found in the deserts of [[Архея|Archaea]]. An adult may reach fifteen metres in length, while caravan tales speak of monsters larger still. Despite its size, the worm moves swiftly beneath the sand and can erupt under its prey before a traveller has understood why the ground is trembling.

The [[Кадийцы|Qadians]] gave the creature its name, meaning ‘the Wanderer’. Al-Tahims make no permanent lairs, but follow caravan roads, herds and the sparse veins of water beneath the desert. A route considered safe for decades may therefore become a monster's hunting ground overnight.

The predator senses impacts and vibrations upon the surface. Seasoned guides wrap their animals' hooves in cloth, forbid travellers to strike hard ground with their staffs and lead their company from one patch of stone to the next. If the sand begins to slip against the wind, a caravan makes for rock: an Al-Tahim cannot break through solid stone, though it may wait nearby for hours.

An adult worm's armoured hide turns most ordinary weapons, so only desperate hunters and [[Мститель|Avengers]] willingly face one in battle. More perilous still is any attempt to recover swallowed cargo. An Al-Tahim devours its prey whole, and tales of treasure lying inside ancient specimens have already claimed many treasure-seekers.

For many years the creatures were thought almost extinct, yet attacks have lately grown more frequent. Travellers report not only old giants, but juveniles. To Qadir's rulers, this is an ominous sign: either the desert has become more favourable to the predators' breeding, or something in the depths is driving them towards the Seven Gardens.
