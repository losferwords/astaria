# Foundation of Amato

> [!timeline] 2695 ChE
> The ancestors of the [[Эдзо|Edzo]] reached [[Остров Ояширо|Oyashiro]], founded [[Амато|Amato]] and established [[Город Кайто|Kaito]] beside the first shrine of [[Ицунэ|Itsune]].

Amato tradition holds that the goddess herself guided the refugees from their vanished homeland across the sea: her voice sounded in the sails and the wind led their ships through the fog. Their first settlement grew into the capital of the archipelago and marked the beginning of the Amatsu dynasty.
