# Iomar Forest

![[Assets/Images/Iomar_Forest_Summer.jpg]]

## Main text

**Iomar Forest** occupies the heart of [[Кельн|Keln]] and is considered the largest continuous forest in [[Астария|Astaria]]. Villages, apiaries and hunting outposts stand at its edges, but after a few hours' travel its crowns close so densely that noon resembles twilight. The air smells of wet bark, mushrooms and the cold water of countless streams.

Iomar is not regarded as a silent storehouse of timber. To the [[Надаир|Nadair]], it is a living realm with memory and will; to [[Друид|Druids]], it is temple, home and state at once. They permit only marked trees to be felled, move trails during nesting season and can close an entire valley if the forest needs rest. Foreigners may see such choices as caprice until they notice how swiftly beasts and plants return where people have learned to step back.

In its depths stand giant [[Дерево Ларудан|larudan trees]], groves of silvery fern and clearings where luminous night midges gather over flowers in animal shapes. Bitter Iomaran sweet flag grows by the water: its smoke drives away biting insects but draws curious [[Сатир|satyrs]]. Old paths may bring a traveller to [[Дриада|dryads]] and beings for which no human language has a name.

Iomar's roads change with the seasons. Spring streams cut off familiar fords, summer fern hides trails, and autumn fog thickens above the lakes. Local people therefore advise against cutting marks in the bark: if the forest does not wish to release a traveller, repeated signs will only persuade them that they are walking in circles.
