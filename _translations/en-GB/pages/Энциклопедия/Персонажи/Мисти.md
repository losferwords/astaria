# Misty

![[Assets/Images/Misty.jpg]]

Misty's name occasionally surfaces among [[Онмёдзи|Onmyoji]] who have met [[Лосфер|Losfer]] on a hunt or over a shared meal. According to their retellings, [[Лосфи|Losfy]] encounters her in a dream: a young adult woman with white hair, blue eyes and long hare ears. The storytellers can name neither her homeland nor her people.

In these accounts Misty is kind, cautious and easily embarrassed beside her direct and mischievous friend. Losfy is said to speak of her with great tenderness. Yet too many retellings lie between their meetings and a stranger's dinner table for anyone to judge Misty with certainty.

Among stories of the [[Зеркальный Сон|Dream of Mirrors]], she remains a quiet, almost domestic presence. Whether there is a life behind this image of which the hunters have heard nothing, none of them ventures to say.
