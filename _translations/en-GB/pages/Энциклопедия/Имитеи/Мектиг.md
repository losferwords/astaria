# Mektig

## Main text

No people in [[Астария|Astaria]] surpass the courageous [[Авгарцы|Avgars]] in strength or craft. Several centuries ago they struck a bargain with the Iron God [[Хангор|Khangor]], who entrusted them with the secret of attaining extraordinary might, an iron will and an unyielding spirit. Warriors forged by this new teaching took the name **Mektig**—the Mighty.

The gift carried a price. In exchange for their strength, the Avgars promised to help the god raise a vast construction requiring resources and materials beyond counting. These could be obtained only from neighbouring lands. Thus began the age of conquest in [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], and the peoples shaken by the fury of its new warriors named them Barbarians. To the Mektig themselves, this is a foreign and often insulting name, recalling an age when outsiders saw in them nothing but savage invaders.

## Training

Khangor favours only those who temper body and spirit without rest through hard labour and worthy battle. No Imithei upon the battlefield is therefore stronger or more enduring than a Mektig. Crushing assaults and cunning techniques can break an enemy with ease, while thunderous songs and fierce war cries hearten every comrade who fights beside them.

## Attributes

The indispensable symbol and faithful companion of every Mektig is a colossal axe, forged by the warrior's own hands according to the secret designs of [[Хангор|the Smith-God]] in the depths of the [[Великая Кузня|Great Forge]].
