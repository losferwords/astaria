# Lake Kharim

![[Assets/Images/Kharim_Lake.jpg]]

## Main text

**Kharim** is the north-western Garden of [[Кадир|Qadir]], closer than the other western lakes to the dry foothills of the [[Горный хребет Шафар|Shafar Mountains]]. A hot wind blows across the water by day, while cold descends swiftly from the mountains at night, forcing travellers to keep both a light wrap and a warm cloak close at hand.

The lake is deep and its shores stony. Gardens do not form an unbroken ring, but occupy sheltered coves where nuts, grapes and bitter herbs are grown. [[Город Хафримеш|Khafrimesh]] is the gateway to the mountain roads; miners, herders and caravans avoiding the central sands pass through it.

Kharim is called the Garden of Patience. Local farmers spend years strengthening thin layers of soil upon the slopes and guiding water to them through narrow stone channels. One landslide can destroy a family's labour, so neighbours help rebuild terraces regardless of old quarrels.

The lake is known for sudden gusts. Its smooth surface can become covered in short, steep waves within minutes, dangerous to overloaded boats. Fishers watch the dust on the mountain slopes: if it rises before noon, they do not cross to the far shore.

Abandoned mine galleries survive in the surrounding gorges. Some were closed by order of the western rulers, yet lights are still seen beside them at night, belonging to those who seek ore, ancient weapons or a short road beneath the mountains.
