# Kiri

![[Assets/Images/Kiri_City.png]]

## Main text

Kiri is a town of fishers, salt workers and potters on the eastern coast of [[Туманный остров|Mist Island]], in the northern part of the [[Луга Тамано|Tamano Meadows]]. It belongs to [[Амато|Amato]] and stands beside dry lands where meadow vegetation thins out, giving way to cold barrens. [[Город Рэйкацу|Reikatsu]] lies roughly thirty kilometres to the south-west in a straight line.

Nets dry beside the fishing quays, and the air smells of salt and the fresh catch. Boats return to shore while those on land prepare fish for sale and salting. Fishing and salt production sustain the town and connect it with Tamano's villages and farming towns.

## Fish, salt and clay

The catch is sold fresh or salted for storage and transport. Salt is also bought for the surrounding farms. Rice and other produce arrive in Kiri from Tamano's cultivated lands, and buyers take fish, salt and local pottery back with them.

Potters make bowls, jugs and large vessels for household stores. Their wares serve fishers, farmers and merchants, as well as Reikatsu's sake and rice vinegar workshops. The potters' yards smell of wet clay, and pieces awaiting firing stand beside the kilns. Some finished wares go to neighbouring settlements; others are taken to the southern port for further trade.

## Town life

Daily life in Kiri follows the departure of fishing boats, the return of the catch, and work at the salt works and pottery kilns. Weather and fish prices are discussed at the quays, while pottery orders are taken at the workshops. To the people of Tamano, the town is known chiefly for these trades and goods.

Kiri is governed by a daimyo of the samurai class, subject to Empress and Shogun [[Аматсу Рин|Amatsu Rin]]. Order in the coastal town forms part of the same strict system of rule that binds Amato's island domains to the capital.
