# Conquest of Katachthonos

> [!timeline] 17 NE
> [[Талассия|Thalassia]] invades [[Катахтонос|Katachthonos]], taking [[Город Лефа|Lepha]] and the neighbouring settlements with little resistance. Katachthonos is declared a Thalassian colony.
