# Vetala

![[Assets/Images/Vetal.jpg]]

## Main text

A **vetala** is a bloodthirsty creature of the jungles of [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]]. Until recently, an encounter with one was considered a rare misfortune confined to remote villages, but the calamity has spread with alarming speed. Even seasoned hunters no longer venture out alone, and some roads have become perilous to armed companies and [[Имитей|Imithei]].

While hungry, a vetala resembles an unsteady shadow cast in human shape. It is almost impossible to distinguish among rain and foliage: the creature breaks no branches, leaves no ordinary trail and attacks without warning. After it bites, stolen blood floods its body. Dark veins rise beneath the surface and its eyes kindle scarlet. A sated vetala becomes visible and, for a short while, assumes a horrifying solidity.

Its hunt seldom ends with the first attack. Once it has scented blood, a vetala stalks wounded prey from among the shadows, waiting for fear and exhaustion to separate the victim from their companions. A bright flame may reveal its movement, but will not always halt the predator. The surest protections are a close formation, covered skin and a guide who recognises when the jungle has fallen suddenly silent.

The [[Раджати|Rajati]] regard vetalas as punishment for frail faith, desecrated temples and forgotten songs of [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari]]. They are not revered as children of the goddess; to mention one at a feast is itself an ill omen. In afflicted lands, doors are marked with red thread and a bowl of bitter, smoking infusion is left upon the threshold, its scent believed to throw the hunter from the trail.
