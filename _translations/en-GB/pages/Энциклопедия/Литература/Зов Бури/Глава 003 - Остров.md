# Chapter 003 — The Island

*The Island*

*98 NE*

The surf approaches the rocks, touches the shore and withdraws, leaving a man upon it. [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth]] lies unconscious; the ship and the voices of its crew are somewhere beyond the roar of the water. Life still warms his chest. Beside him stands a young woman. To her, this traveller is a long-awaited guest.

This is [[Остров Хтон|Chthon]]. Only a tiny remnant of its former land rises above the sea, while steps and broken roads show through the clear water. The remains of human dwellings lie beneath the currents. Sailors give these waters a wide berth, and only the waves freely cross the boundary between the ruins and the abyss.

The young woman's name is [[Кассиопея Атанатос|Cassia]]. Her white eyes have no pupils. She revives Kenneth, and little by little he makes out the stones, an unfamiliar face and the sea that almost swallowed him. The [[Горец|Highlander]] tries to understand where he is and who is meeting him on the shore. But Cassia already knows more about him than he has time to say.

The Island speaks in her voice. Its opening lines hold tenderness for a man who survives the storm, and a calm certainty about what awaits him next. She offers him her hand and a place beside her. To abandon his dreams of the road, to remain on the island until death: to Cassia, that future is as plain as the stone beneath Kenneth's palm.

She is a [[Профитис|Profitis]], a seer of fallen Chthon. Her blindness does not prevent her from seeing life, thoughts and the threads of what is to come. Cassia has no need to look towards the horizon to know where a human path leads. Almost a century of solitude among the ruins teaches her to trust her visions so completely that hope of another outcome seems meaningless.

Cassia begins her story, and another land seems to emerge behind the fragments on the shore. Her words bring to life a vast [[Хтониды|Chthonid]] empire: cities, temples, sages seeking the mysteries of existence as hungrily as others seek gold. More and more children are born with the “white-eye disease”. Ordinary sight fades, but the future opens to them. The affliction is taken for a gift, and the blind seers are raised above those who see the world with ordinary eyes.

Then, in her account, the prophecy itself changes. The Profitis declare that their people must perish and the island vanish beneath the sea. They unite against their own homeland and fulfil the prediction. The world to which they promise glory dies; the [[Падение Хтона|Fall of Chthon]] marks the beginning of the New Era. Kenneth hears of this catastrophe in the very place where the sea still conceals its traces.

In the second chorus, Cassia's voice changes. Behind the seer's certainty emerges a child of the catastrophe, who cannot be blamed for causing it. She takes no part in the island's destruction. Yet almost her entire life passes among its ruins, and the memory of Chthon's fall accompanies every new vision. Now a man arrives on the shore with whom, she believes, she can share that solitude.

Kenneth listens, but his questions multiply. He still needs a sword; beyond the sea lies the road to [[Гора Атафет|Mount Atafet]]. He begins to seek a way off the island. Cassia watches and urges him to abandon the attempt: she sees a slow death upon Chthon, here beside her. Her words carry a familiar submission to what, for her, is already decided.

And Kenneth builds a raft.

He sets to simple work: finds what will float, gathers it and binds the pieces of his raft together. Each new knot becomes his answer to Cassia's assurances. The sea before him remains dangerous, yet beside the shore a way to leave gradually takes shape.

And at that moment, Cassia's visions change.

Events that seem inevitable only a moment earlier acquire a different course. Beyond this raft opens a road, and with it countless other destinies change. For the first time, Cassia can set one thing beside another: here is a man who refuses to submit, here is his work — and here is a future that no longer matches the one she knows.

Disbelief gives way to delight. Now she urges Kenneth to launch the raft and asks him to take her along. Impatience enters the voice that so confidently foretells his life among the ruins: Cassia wants to leave too. The very possibility of the unknown is enough to make her step towards it.

They set sail together. The final refrain returns to waves, a shore and an offered hand, but now ahead lies the land Kenneth seeks. Cassia offers to guide him and stay beside him. She is still blind, still carries the memory of Chthon — and even so, allows herself to hope for another path.

The raft draws away from the shore. Cassia holds Kenneth's hand, and in the song's last words a quiet question lingers: is the island truly behind them now?
