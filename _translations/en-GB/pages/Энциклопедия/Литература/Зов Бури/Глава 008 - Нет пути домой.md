# Chapter 008 — No Way Home

*No Way Home*

Wind from the sea stirs the branches above the mountain road. In [[Деревня Эрвин|Erwin]], rain runs down stone walls, darkens doorsteps and gathers in the bends of narrow streets. These are the sounds [[Лисандра мак Рейн|Lisandra]] has known all her life. Now she listens as though she might still catch familiar footsteps beneath the wind.

In spring 101 NE, [[Кассиопея Атанатос|Cassia]] reaches her home. The journey from the south and the search for [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth's]] mother take time. After she leaves [[Гора Атафет|Atafet]], the prophetess's visions change, but her beloved no longer appears in them. Believing him dead, she carries north the story of rain that saves a distant country.

Lisandra lets her into the house. Before her stands a woman who has shared the part of her son's journey his mother scarcely knows. Now she can hear who he becomes far from Erwin. How he builds a raft beside a fallen island. How he survives captivity and wounds. How he helps people who lack the strength to endure another day of drought.

Cassia comes to the ascent and the summoning of the storm. She tells of the death she sees and her attempt to change the future. Of leaving the summit while Kenneth is still alive. To her, his absence from her new visions becomes the answer she dreads. She brings that answer to his mother.

Lisandra listens. Behind the account of [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat's]] salvation, she can still hear the breathing of the child she first holds during a thunderstorm. Once, rain beyond this threshold accompanies the beginning of his life. Now it falls as someone tells her of the end of his road.

No Way Home returns to Lisandra's voice. In the opening song she sings to Kenneth of home, his homeland and the courage with which he must face his trial. Now she wants only her son beside her. Every word that can honour his deed must be spoken in the house to which he does not return.

Pride in Kenneth does little to ease that emptiness. Lisandra learns how many people he saves and how far his power reaches beyond their mountains. She can be proud of every step and still wish to hear the door open and his voice calling for her. That simple possibility becomes almost unbearable precisely because it is so easy to imagine.

For several days, Cassia stays with the mac Raynes. Beneath the same roof, Lisandra can return to her account, ask again and hear a little more of her son. Cassia loves him too. In the pauses between words, both women must remain with what neither knows how to change.

Then the prophetess leaves. Lisandra does not know where the road takes her. The story remains with his mother and slowly enters the familiar sounds of her life. Wind in the branches, rain and the silence after it seem to bring the same message home: there is no way back.

In the chorus, hope keeps trying to rise. Lisandra wants to believe; for a moment, the thought that her son might be somewhere better gives her something to hold. Then another thought comes: perhaps his journey still continues, perhaps he is still wandering far from her. Neither thought brings him to her threshold. The repeated answer cuts short another attempt to imagine his return.

Life goes on. Morning comes, then evening. That very ordinariness makes solitude so heavy: another day begins when she still needs the one in which her son can come home. Lisandra remains among familiar walls and sounds, and every day adds to the waiting.

In the second verse, the prophetess comes by night. Cassia returns in Lisandra's dreams with the story that cannot be set aside until morning. There, too, the thought of a closed road home appears. Sleep offers his mother no different answer; waking leaves her with the same absence.

Yet she feels she must tell the story of Kenneth's path. Write down how he leaves home, the trials he endures and the people for whom he raises his sword. Reaching the final line is hardest. The story demands an ending where his mother keeps waiting.

Lisandra carries word of [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth the Stormcaller]] through the [[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]]. From her words and Cassia's account, the saga *Call of Thunder* takes shape in [[Город Солаис|Solais]]. People learn Kenneth's name and pass on the story of his deed. Lisandra gains words with which to preserve his journey. Her son is still not beside her.

The final choruses return again and again to loneliness. The full account gives way to a few lines, then only the brief refrain of being unable to return remains. It carries the weariness of words his mother knows by heart and still cannot accept.

The song falls quiet. Lisandra listens to water running beyond the threshold. The rain goes on.
