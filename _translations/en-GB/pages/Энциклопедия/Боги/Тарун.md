# Tarun

## Main text

Tarun is the chief god of the [[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]] and one of the great triad of the Dair pantheon, beside [[Мерката|Mercate]] and [[Церунна|Cerunna]].

Goidair legend tells that, when the ancient Dair tribes spread westward from [[Иомар|Iomar]], they encountered giants of stone. The strength of the [[Друид|Druids]] was not enough to overcome the immense figures. Then a mysterious warrior clad in iron armour came to the Dair. He promised to aid the wanderers, but demanded in return that they defend those lands from ‘the creatures of night’. The Dair did not understand his meaning; desperate to be rid of the giants, they agreed all the same.

The stranger raised his blade to the sky. Lightning struck him squarely, yet he did not die: instead he seemed to catch the bolt upon his sword. He went alone onto the plain where the stone figures dwelt. A terrible battle followed. From the village, the Dair heard thunder rolling and saw lightning flare behind black clouds. When the clamour ceased and the clouds drew apart, mountains stood where the plain had been. The Dair believed them to be the bodies of the stone giants, defeated by the stranger and heaped one upon another. Thus, the story says, the [[Горный хребет Грань Тьмы|Edge of Darkness]] arose, and the valiant warrior who had given the people those lands became known as Tarun.

Tarun does not show himself to mortals. The [[Гойдаир|Goidair]] believe that their god was so wearied by the battle with the giants that he rests for an age high in the heavens. Only when a storm gathers does he briefly wake and speak with his people.

For many years the Thunder Clans studied the human power to command weather, hoping to repeat Tarun's wonder. Their long search at last gave rise to the rite of [[Рунштоирм|Runstoirm]] and the creation of the [[Горец|Highlanders]].

Tarun's blade is said to have shattered during the battle with the giants. The Highlanders therefore treasure their [[Меч Де'лаим|De'laim swords]] so dearly: when Tarun at last appears before them, each warrior wishes to be ready to offer a blade to the god.
