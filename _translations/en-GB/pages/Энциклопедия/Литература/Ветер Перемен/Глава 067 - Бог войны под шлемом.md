# Chapter 067 — The God of War beneath the Helm

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 105 NE, summer*

[[Сунь Ляньши|Sun Lianshi]] ordered her advisers placed under guard and sent [[Мэй Ву|Mei]] to General Li's field camp. Before departing, Mei took daggers, [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] received a new cypress bow, and [[Урист Китин|Urist]] carried the imperial standard so the guards would not hinder them in the streets.

On the way to the eastern gate, Mei remembered [[Мэйлун|Meilong]], and the group spent a day searching for him. The tracks showed that the young dragon had fought [[Кион-Чи|Qiong-Qi]] before heading south. They had no choice but to return to the empress's mission.

Beyond a hill, the travellers saw fire and battle. The Dju and the nomads had clashed without [[Аксель Хан|Axel Khan]] at their head. The travellers joined the fighting, and the army of [[Батар Саг|Batar Sag]] soon came to their aid. Without their leader, the nomads surrendered or fled. Batar gave Urist the Avgar moonshine he had promised for his mother-in-law.

Upon the hill, the travellers saw a warrior in imperial armour finishing off the remaining enemies. Within his tent the truth emerged: General Guan Li and Axel Khan were one and the same being—or, more precisely, [[Гиперион I|Hyperion]], god of war.

He told them of a charge laid upon him by [[Альзаман|Alzaman]]: to restore the power of the Sands of Time stolen by [[Шень Ву|Shen Wu]] and reduce the number of Avatars in the Empire. He had exploited the conflict between the Dju and the nomads, but reached an impasse when he learnt that most Avatars were hidden in the depths of the [[Храм Дракона|Temple of the Dragon]], protected by a draconic barrier. Now he asked the travellers to descend into those depths and discover what was happening.

In return, Hyperion handed over [[Дзин Сан|Dzin San]], the younger son of [[Чани Сан|Chani San]], and [[Цинь Ша|Qin Sha]], the man responsible for the abductions and the murder of Chang-Sha's farmers. He also warned Urist about the black mark and advised him to seek [[Велисса|Velissa]]. Wan San, it transpired, was alive and rebuilding Nayan.

The group returned to the capital with the prisoners and the god of war. Hyperion could not enter the temple courtyard and went to find [[Чень Ляньши|Chen Lianshi]], despite warnings not to drink with him.
