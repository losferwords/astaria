# Profitis

The Profitis are seers of the [[Хтониды|Chthonid]] people, better known in the common tongue as Oracles. Once they served [[Тиресий|Tiresias]], god of wisdom, interpreted the law and held an honoured place at the court of Chthon. Then every one of them turned against their own people and carried out the prophecy that culminated in the [[Падение Хтона|Fall of Chthon]]. To the rest of [[Астария|Astaria]], the reasons for that choice remain unfathomable.

The surviving Profitis wander the lands of [[Кельн|Keln]] and seldom linger near a settlement. They are met with fear and hatred: their white eyes recall the catastrophe from which the New Era is reckoned. An Oracle generally knows more of a person than they are willing to say, yet avoids a direct answer. The Profitis believe that a future revealed carelessly may alter the course of events more dangerously than silence ever could.

## Abilities

The Profitis command telekinesis, telepathy and the art of illusion. They sense intent before an opponent completes a movement, displace objects through force of thought and turn the battlefield into a chain of outcomes prepared in advance. Their blindness is deceptive. A Profitis possesses no ordinary sight, but perceives thoughts, energy and the threads of possible futures.

An Oracle's greatest danger lies not in raw power, but in knowledge. They may withdraw from a battle already won or permit an enemy to escape if that choice belongs to some more distant event. A companion can seldom tell whether today's decision was an act of mercy, calculation or inevitability.

## Attributes

Every Profitis carries the [[Книга Анарабет|Anarabet]], the Seer's Almanac. Its pages appear blank to an ordinary reader, but an Oracle finds within them accounts of events both past and yet to come. To lose the book is to surrender not merely a tool, but part of the path by which a Profitis finds their way among innumerable possible destinies.
