# Temple of Mercate

![[Assets/Images/Mercate_Temple.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Temple of Mercate** is the religious centre of [[Лунаар|Lunaar]] and the chief refuge of the [[Культ Меркаты|Cult of Mercate]]. At first, only a stone shrine of the earliest priests stood on the hill at the source of the [[Река Эйрафон|Eirafon]], but pilgrims, keepers and craftspeople gradually surrounded it with an entire city.

The settlement's upper part belongs to the temple. Narrow stairs lead to an inner court where no bright fire is ever lit, and its galleries are oriented so that each offers a view of a particular portion of the sky on a clear night. Below lie attendants' homes, pilgrim inns, bowyers' workshops and shops selling dark candles, silver thread and paper for recording dreams.

The Cult is older than Lunaar itself and does not consider the city the Crown's possession. Royal guards may escort an envoy as far as the gates, but its own keepers maintain order within. Important visitors wait for an audience as long as priestesses judge necessary; high birth does not aid a person whose request violates an ancient vow.

The halls of return hold a special place. People who have survived an encounter with Antra, lost their memory or heard Mercate's call in a dream are brought here. Not all will become [[Тень|Shadows]], but each is given several nights of quiet before anyone asks what has happened.

Travellers consider the temple city sombre, yet it is safe for those who respect others' peace. The most unsettling stories begin not inside its walls, but on trails around the source, where ravens sometimes fly against the wind and vanish above places absent from every map.
