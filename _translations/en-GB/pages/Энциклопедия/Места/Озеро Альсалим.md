# Lake Alsalim

![[Assets/Images/Alsalim_Lake.jpg]]

## Main text

**Alsalim** is the south-easternmost of the Seven Gardens of [[Кадир|Qadir]], a great lake amid the pale sands of the [[Пустыня Халисат|Khalisat Desert]]. [[Город Кафат|Kafat]] and [[Город Тасар|Tasar]] stand upon its shores, while caravan roads continue towards the eastern passes and the lands of neighbouring realms.

The waters of Alsalim have a greenish hue from fine algae and mineral springs. Date palms, tart pomegranates and tough reeds grow beside the shore; the reeds are woven into household mats. Fields lie in narrow strips along irrigation channels, beyond which the dry land begins again.

The lake belongs to eastern Qadir and is governed by [[Умар ибн Ла-Ахад|Umar ibn La-Ahad]]. Pupils of the Way of the Desert use the surrounding country for their trials. They must reach the water from distant dunes by relying on the stars, the form of sand ridges and the ability to preserve their strength—not on a marked road.

Alsalim is called the Garden of Return. A traveller who reaches it after a long crossing receives water and rest before all else, even if they arrive from hostile land. Hospitality does not abolish caution, however: the markets of Kafat watch unfamiliar goods closely and ask about every road by which a person has come.

After sandstorms, remnants of old stone canals emerge along the eastern shore. No one knows whether they belonged to the first Qadian settlers or to an older people.
