# Nadair

![[Assets/Images/Nadair.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Nadair** are a people of central [[Кельн|Keln]], descended from the first settlers who made their home beside [[Лес Иомар|Iomar Forest]] more than two millennia ago. Their ancestors arrived as conquerors of a new land, but meeting [[Церунна|Cerunna]] changed the very meaning of possession: the forest could not be claimed, only joined in a long alliance.

Of all Dair peoples, the Nadair have best preserved their ancestral culture. Families remember ancient paths, the wintering grounds of animals and the trees beneath which important oaths were sworn. History passes through song and story, and a fine storyteller commands no less respect than a skilled hunter. Their clothing favours natural colours, wool, leather and embroidery of branches, antlers and animal tracks.

Most Nadair follow the [[Круг Друидов|Druid Circle]]. Every settlement has keepers responsible for its water, woodland and relations with animals, while the most gifted enter Cerunna's service. A [[Друид|Druid]] may restrict a hunt, postpone the felling of trees or leave a field unploughed when the earth is exhausted. This is not regarded as priestly caprice: the Nadair know how easily one year's abundance becomes the next year's hunger.

Their hospitality is simple and sincere. A traveller receives a place by the fire and an infusion of herbs, but is asked to recount what they saw upon the road. News thus moves through Iomar more swiftly than official messages. The Nadair seldom seek wealth for its own sake. A worthy life is one that leaves children healthy land, a good name and a song they need not be ashamed to continue.
