# Shasha

![[Assets/Images/Shasha.jpg]]

**Shasha** is a young [[Нага|naga]], hatched in 83 NE, whose curiosity about humans has always exceeded her caution. She is considered beautiful by both naga and human standards, though her sisters envy her exceptional intelligence even more. Shasha mastered several human languages with ease and speaks them almost without an accent—a rare accomplishment among a people who lived in isolation for centuries.

Her green skin, thick black hair and yellow serpentine eyes make her origins plain even to those who have never encountered a naga before. Shasha knows the impression she makes upon humans and has learnt to turn their first alarm into an opportunity for conversation. She is observant, quick to notice a veiled insult and answers with such courtesy that the speaker does not immediately realise how neatly they have been put in their place.

After meeting foreigners in the subterranean city, Shasha agreed to become an ambassador and travelled to [[Деревня Бадракали|Badrakali]]. Her presence was intended to prove that the nagas could speak for themselves, enter into agreements and accept responsibility for their own decisions. For the young emissary, it became both her first long stay beneath the open sky and her first experience of human domestic life, which fascinates and perplexes her in equal measure.

Shasha helped reveal that certain priests had secretly been conveying people into the naga caves under the guise of the Great Harvest. Thereafter, the negotiations ceased to be a mere ceremony: the fate of the abducted, the safety of Badrakali and the chance to end a feud founded upon fear all hung in the balance. To the Rajati, Shasha remains a test of their ability to see a thinking neighbour rather than a monster in a six-armed being. To the nagas, she has become the first of their sisters charged with explaining why trust in humanity might be worth the risk at all.
