# Velimir Sazhin

![[Assets/Images/Velimir_Sajin.jpg]]

**Velimir Sazhin** is a [[Венды|Wend]] and one of the [[Страж|Guardians]] of the goddess [[Велисса|Velissa]]. He was born near [[Город Мирград|Mirgrad]] into a family of craftspeople and helped his father at the forge from childhood. His strength revealed itself early, yet the villagers remembered Velimir not for fighting, but for a rare gift for reconciling people: he could hear out two stubborn neighbours, mend what had been broken and restore their friendship before the hearth grew cold.

After several lean years in the [[Хвойничье предгорье|Pinewood Foothills]], Velimir joined a warband. He guarded caravans, searched for travellers lost in blizzards and protected villages from brigands, remaining a man who took up arms only after every word had failed. At the age of thirty-three, Velimir underwent initiation in the waters of the [[Река Красивая|Krasivaya River]], where Wend belief holds that the earth opens its own heart to the chosen.

As a Guardian, he gained command of stone and the hidden depths of the earth. Velimir can strengthen a crumbling slope, sense a hollow beneath the ground and seal a dangerous object within a shell of rock. Yet he considers patience, rather than power, to be the foremost gift: the earth bears an immense weight precisely because it does not answer every blow with an avalanche.

Velimir is tall and broad-shouldered, with long dark fair-brown hair and plain clothes suited to the road. His stern expression quickly softens around children. His wife Darina and their three little ones await him at home; in his travelling pack, he keeps their uneven drawings beside his weapons and a ribbon of prayer.

After many years of service, Velimir dreamt of returning to his family, but the disappearance of the [[Чёрное Сердце|Black Heart]] compelled him to undertake another journey. The trail led the Guardian to [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]], where similar black stones had been found alongside weapons capable of casting their victims into endless sleep. Velimir does not hasten to declare every discovery the lost relic. He gathers testimony, shields others from dangerous fragments and hopes to finish his journey as Velissa teaches: to restore what was lost without allowing the search to destroy that for which he left home.
