# Batar Sag

![[Assets/Images/Batar_Sag.jpg]]

## Main text

**Batar Sag** is the reigning khan of [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]] and a member of the young Sag dynasty. He maintains neutral relations with neighbouring countries while promoting commerce, writing and agriculture.

Although Batar is regarded as a wise and respected ruler, many [[Авгарцы|Avgars]] resent the Great Khan's “soft” reign. Old traditions, hot blood and memories of a wandering past remain deeply rooted in the people of Wildlands, making it difficult for them to accept the peaceful order the Sag dynasty seeks to build.

The threat of [[Аксель Хан|Axel Khan]] became the greatest trial of his policy. Rather than march for plunder, Batar sent an army to aid [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]], hoping to halt the nomads and prevent an ancient legend from casting nations into endless war once more. The alliance demonstrated that the Great Khan's peacefulness was founded not upon weakness, but upon a willingness to use force in defence of an order capable of outliving a single victory.

Under Batar, Avgar scouts and hunters operate far beyond Wildlands, while trade routes bind the steppe to the east and south. Even his opponents acknowledge that the young dynasty has transformed scattered arbats into a state able to negotiate with empires as an equal.
