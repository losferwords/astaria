# Lake Nekhmet

![[Assets/Images/Myra_City.jpg]]

## Main text

**Nekhmet** is a great freshwater lake in [[Регион Аментир|Amentir]], in the south of [[Амон-Астат|the Empire of the Sun]]. Its waters transformed the surrounding country into the Great Oasis and made possible the growth of the ancient settlements of the [[Хефат|Khefat]].

Fields, orchards and reed-filled shallows cover the lakeshore. Canals draw water towards remote villages, while boats carry the harvest to [[Город Тиза|Tiza]] and the neighbouring cities. At dawn the surface of Nekhmet reflects the sunlight so fiercely that priests regard it as one of the natural sanctuaries of [[Аст|Ast]].

The lake appears tranquil, yet its shallows change constantly. Silt raises new islets, old channels become choked with growth, and a strong wind can build a dangerous swell within moments. Fishers mark their routes with poles and bundles of grass; without a local guide, a foreign boat may easily run aground among the reeds.

Pottery, seals and fragments of buildings from settlements older than the present cities are found in Nekhmet. When the water level fell, several ruins emerged once more, drawing scholars, plunderers and those convinced that a forgotten sanctuary lies beneath the silt. The lake therefore remains not only the source of life, but an immense archive of Khefat history.
