# Schism of Qadir

![[Assets/Images/Kad-Kharad_Disbandment.jpg]]

In the ancient world, the eastern kingdom of [[Кадир|Qadir]] was a formidable power across central and eastern [[Архея|Archaea]]. That age ended with the invasion of the [[Авгарцы|Avgar]] tribes, who came from the north in search of new lands. By 667 ChE, the [[Мектиг|Mektigs]] had already taken territory from [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]] and settled the [[Хангорская долина|Hangor Valley]], where they formed a new realm: [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]].

King [[Астиаг II|Astyages II]] sought to exploit the upheaval, seize the valley and enlarge Qadir. He gravely underestimated his new northern enemy. The Mektigs not only held against his army, but launched a counter-offensive; within a year, the Avgars had reached [[Озеро Зураиф|Lake Zuraif]], intent upon ending both king and war.

Desperate to preserve his life, Astyages turned to divine aid. He assembled a company of four — himself, his general, his vizier and his bodyguard — and descended into the catacombs beneath the [[Храм Дар Мараат|Temple of Dar Maraat]] to petition [[Альзаман|Alzaman]].

After days of wandering in darkness, they entered a hall dominated by an immense hourglass, around which the god paced. Astyages implored him for a weapon with which to punish his enemies and drive them from Qadir. Alzaman offered a bargain: the [[Кадийцы|Qadians]] would receive a mighty weapon, but it could be used only to recover their ancestral lands. Qadir must then make peace with the northerners and leave them the Khangorian Valley.

The king was outraged by what he called the god's lack of foresight, but had no other recourse. When he agreed, Alzaman instructed the three companions to lay their hands upon the hourglass. Time stopped around them. The king and the torch-flames became still, while the chosen three continued to move and speak. Years of instruction and discipline awaited them, the god explained, though to Astyages and the outer world only a moment would pass. Thus were the first [[Мститель|Avengers]] of Qadir made.

When their training ended, the warriors returned to the common flow of time. To Astyages scarcely an instant had passed, yet his companions were transformed: wise and battle-worn elders seemed to inhabit the bodies of young men. The king thanked Alzaman and led them back to his palace.

The Avengers reversed the war. The Mektigs were powerful warriors, but even their companies could not withstand three masters whose blades swept Avgar warbands aside in moments. Within months Qadir was free, and its enemy had retreated into the Khangorian Valley.

Astyages remembered his compact with Alzaman, but vengeance and newfound power blinded him. He ordered the war to continue until all of Wildlands had been conquered. At that command, [[Абу Али аль-Хассан|Abu Ali al-Hassan]], general of Qadir and one of the Avengers, beheaded the king with a single stroke.

The three had long suspected Astyages would betray his oath, and had prepared accordingly. They divided authority among themselves and took the title of Eternal Princes, so that no single ruler would ever again stand above Qadir. Henceforth, the gravest decisions would be made together, denying private ambition another chance to drag the realm into ruin.

General Abu Ali al-Hassan remained in the palace at [[Город Сераф|Seraph]] and became Eternal Prince of central Qadir. The dead king's bodyguard, [[Малик Аль-Азвар|Malik al-Azwar]], went west to [[Город Самаан|Samaan]] upon [[Залив Фиальсахра|Fialsakhra Bay]]. The former vizier, [[Умар ибн Ла-Ахад|Umar ibn La-Ahad]], travelled east to negotiate peace and trade with the Avgars. The Mektigs accepted his terms, bringing the war to an end, and Umar settled in [[Город Кафат|Kafat]] as Eternal Prince of the east.

## Outcome

Qadir was divided into three domains: western, central and eastern.
