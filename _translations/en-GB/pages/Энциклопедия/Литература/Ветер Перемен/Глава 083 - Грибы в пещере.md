# Chapter 083 — Mushrooms in the Cave

*[[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]], 106 NE, spring*

The group left Tanassar and headed south. Near an old temple they found signs that [[Нага|nagas]] had fought someone, yet there was no blood. Their victims might have been taken alive. The travellers followed the trail to a cave in the rock.

Luminous mushrooms grew inside. [[Урист Китин|Urist]] hastened to taste one and was swept by a wave of relaxation and crystalline thought. He persuaded the others to eat as well, and the entire group continued deeper beneath the mushrooms' influence.

They saw a naga warrior, escaped notice and reached cages holding prisoners. Filled with peculiar serenity and unwilling to begin a fight, the travellers purchased the sole living captive for one of [[Мерката|Mercate's]] feathers and returned to camp.

Only there did they notice the small, genuine horns upon the young woman's head. In the morning, they learnt that she was a northerner from [[Иомар|Iomar]] and a [[Друид|Druid]] travelling at her goddess's command. Her purpose was to save the elephants from extinction—local animals resembling the huge creatures that had nearly destroyed Sajar's cargo. She agreed to join the company, hoping to learn more in the capital.

Before reaching the capital, the group decided to visit [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja's]] home village of [[Деревня Бадракали|Badrakali]]. It was a simple place of temple, tavern and market. At the market, Urist bought a naga head, drained its venom and received a lead on where to sell the rest. In the tavern, the travellers were swept into a local celebration of dancing, broken furniture and minor injuries.

The innkeeper bought the naga head in exchange for five nights with breakfast. As the mushrooms' effects receded, Urist began considering how their new companion might repay her rescue and the expenditure of a divine feather. She offered to help with [[Мэйлун|Meilong]], and the dragon taught her the common tongue so they could speak.

That night, the travellers declined to join the next stage of the festivities involving red root infusion. In the morning, they learnt that the participants had willingly offered themselves to [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari]] as sacrifices. After a breakfast of naga teeth, Urist felt a surge of strength and reinforced his hammer with black quartz.
