# Chapter 040 — The Valley Trap

*[[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], 104 NE, autumn*

When the veil of darkness dispersed, [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] saw [[Рон Эллиот|Ron]] bristling with arrows. [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] rushed to the young man and began dragging him back from death's grasp: pulling out arrows, staunching blood and binding the wounds tightly. He managed to keep Ron on this side.

[[Вигдис Меткая|Vigdis the Unerring]] did not soften her words. She pointed out [[Урист Китин|Urist's]] obvious error: he had thrown everyone into the attack and left the rear undefended. Smoke was now rising from the camp. It had been a trap.

The force hurried back. [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] saw a great fire rising behind her and disguised herself as one of the soldiers, joining the effort to extinguish it. The flames were subdued with difficulty, but the damage had been done.

Vigdis defied Urist again. He struck her leg with his hammer, leaving a severe bruise. Yet rather than demand immediate payment, Vigdis decided to discover what metal this man was made of. Their quarrel became a peculiar trial of spirit, and in the end she took his side.

During the firefighting, Gilja broke the [[Рактапатр|Raktapatra]]. To another, it would have been damage to a weapon. To him, it was almost a wound in his own fate.

Tom and Vigdis followed the possible arsonist's trail. It led across the river and seemed to belong to a young woman, perhaps even an [[Аватар|Avatar]]. Vigdis advised against the risk and suggested returning to camp. One thing was already clear: their enemy fought not only with force, but with lures.
