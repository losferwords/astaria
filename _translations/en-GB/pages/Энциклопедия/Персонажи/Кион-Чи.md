# Qiong-Qi

![[Assets/Images/Quiong-Qi.jpg]]

## Main text

**Qiong-Qi**, also called the Winged Terror, is among the oldest and most powerful beings in [[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]]. The giant tiger stands upon four powerful legs, while a separate pair of leathery wings scored with baleful patterns grows from its back. Its tawny fur rises in sharp quills like clotted blood, while its amber gaze is said to drown a victim's will. [[Лес Шеньянь|Shenyan]], where Qiong-Qi makes its lair, is forbidden ground: even the boldest hunters will not willingly enter its depths.

Legends claim that Qiong-Qi can scent fear before its prey has understood the danger, and takes pleasure in prolonging the hunt. Its roar is thunder strong enough to shake the earth; the beat of its wings raises gusts that cast travellers from their feet. Still, brute force is only part of the creature's peril. Qiong-Qi lays traps, herds quarry towards dead ends and can imitate the voices of those it has killed to lure the trusting from a safe path.

The beings known as [[Менада|Shen-Hu]] revere Qiong-Qi as both supreme patron and curse. To them it is the vestige of an ancient god angered by humankind in a forgotten age. They believe that it does not hunt solely for food, but punishes those who profane the forest and break the world's harmony. Its judgement, however, is capricious: it may spare a thief, then tear apart a righteous traveller merely for becoming an irritation.

Qiong-Qi is no creature of instinct alone. Tales of the Empire of Fire say that before the coming of [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Lang-An]], the people of the valley worshipped it and made numberless sacrifices in its honour. It now roams the forest in pursuit of purposes no mortal understands. Some say it seeks an adversary worthy of a final contest; others that it gathers strength until it can reclaim its former dominion and cast down the Dragon. Whatever the truth, an encounter with Qiong-Qi remains a trial from which few return.
