# Onmyoji

When an unfamiliar voice speaks from a quiet home and a loved one's eyes flood red, the people of [[Амато|Amato]] do not summon an executioner. They light a lantern at the threshold and send for one who can follow the unseen.

## Main text

The **Onmyoji** (陰陽師) are the Imithei of Amato and priests of the goddess [[Ицунэ|Itsune]]. In the common tongue they are often called **demon hunters**. They travel between Edzo settlements in search of the [[Они|Oni]], striving to free the possessed whom a terrified crowd may already have condemned as monsters.

Future Onmyoji spend years tempering body and will in the temples of Itsune before undergoing a sacred initiation. Each returns from the rite changed: with red eyes, a new name and a secret of which the hunters speak only reluctantly.

## Abilities

Onmyoji perceive invisible traces and the presence of an alien will. Itsune also grants them **神風—the Divine Wind**: command of air currents, sudden gusts and the mist in which spiritual sight gives the hunter a profound advantage. In battle an Onmyoji relies upon precision, restraint and the peculiar virtues of a ritual blade, seeking first to weaken the Oni and prepare the rite of cleansing. Every hunter's gifts are singular; no two Onmyoji wield their power in precisely the same fashion.

## Attributes

A cloth band conceals the hunter's red eyes and distinguishes an Onmyoji from other priests of Itsune. Their indispensable weapon is a ritual katana whose forging the temples of Amato refuse to reveal to foreigners.
