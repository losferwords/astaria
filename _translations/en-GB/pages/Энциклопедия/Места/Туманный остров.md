# Mist Island

![[Assets/Images/Myst_Island.jpg]]

## Main text

**Mist Island**, or **霞島** in the Edzo tongue, is the largest island in [[Астария|Astaria]], dividing the [[Хтоническое море|Chthonic Sea]] from the [[Рассветное море|Dawn Sea]]. Warm and cold currents meet along its shores, leaving the lowlands veiled in mist for most of the year. The damp air softens the winter and keeps the summer cool.

Both [[Амато|Amato]] and [[Обитель|Obitelj]] claim the island, yet life here has long ceased to fit within the borders of two realms. Roofs with high, upturned eaves stand beside carved wooden window frames in the coastal settlements; Edzo and Wend languages mingle in the streets, and family celebrations often unite customs from both shores. Arguments over sovereignty sound loudest in the capitals. The islanders themselves are more often concerned with fishing waters, the harvest and the road washed away by yet another rain.

The [[Призрачные горы|Ghost Mountains]] rise at the island's heart, feeding its rivers and mist-bound forests. Smoke pines grow upon their slopes: the bark holds moisture, while the dry needles yield a dense white smoke with which mariners test the direction of the wind. Within the [[Дымовая Чаща|Smoke Thicket]], the fog is so thick that the sound of a bell arrives before the hand holding it can be seen.

Small coves and reefs cut into the coastline. Local pilots steer by the colour of the water, the voices of seabirds and the scent of plants carried from shore. To a foreign captain, the island appears welcoming only from afar. One wrong turn in the mist may bring a ship not to harbour, but to rocks absent from every old chart.
