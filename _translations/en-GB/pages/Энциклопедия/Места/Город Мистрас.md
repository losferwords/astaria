# Mystras

![[Assets/Images/Mistras_City.jpg]]

## Main text

**Mystras** is a trading city of [[Гилас|Gilas]] upon the north-western shore of [[Озеро Митра|Lake Mitra]]. Stone walls protect its quays, market and warehouses, where lake fish, Elessian grain and goods from the borderlands are gathered. The city possesses an arena of its own for martial competitions and [[Танатомахия|Thanatomachia contests]].

The arena shapes not only festivals, but the entire life of the city. Wealthy promoters maintain fighters, bargain with slaveholders and turn the strength of others into spectacle. One may easily encounter a bodyguard who was a captive only recently, or a person for whom service has proved merely a different kind of cage.

The bustle of trade also provides cover for those who oppose this order. Cells of [[Жаждущие|the Thirsty]] once operated in Mystras, smuggling slaves out of Gilas and conveying fugitives towards the capital. Two roads therefore coexist beneath the city walls: the official road to the market and arena, and the secret one beginning in drains and strangers' houses.

Travellers remember Mystras for its noise: the cries of stevedores, the blows of practice weapons and the bargaining at the fish stalls. Beneath that din, however, lies the vigilance of a city accustomed to distinguishing buyer, spy and fugitive by a single ill-judged question.
