# Chang-Sha

![[Assets/Images/Chang_Sha_City.jpg]]

## Main text

**Chang-Sha** is a great city of [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]], built upon the western shore of [[Озеро Чанг|Lake Chang]]. Regarded as the Empire's second capital, it is ringed by fertile land where rice, millet and barley are grown. Palaces and gardens stand beside farmers' houses, while the authority of its elders remains more grounded than the courtly life of [[Город Джу-Суо|Dju-Suo]].

For many years the city lived upon the glory of the Wu Legion, which was said to defend [[Долина Гармонии|He-Dji-Gu]] against the nomads. It was later revealed that the legion had long since vanished. A thousand local farmers had been abducted in secret, dressed as soldiers and sent to the [[Гарнизон Пасть Дракона|Dragon's Maw]] in place of a true army. The deception became one of the darkest passages in Chang-Sha's recent history.

When the army of [[Аксель Хан|Axel Khan]] approached after the fall of [[Город Найян|Nayan]], the elder [[Шень Ву|Shen Wu]] refused to sacrifice the inhabitants anew and arranged their evacuation across the lake to the capital. The house of the Wu family remained an important place within the city: here [[Ниса|Nisa]], [[Сао Ву|Sao Wu]] and [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]], newly returned from the west, all found shelter.
