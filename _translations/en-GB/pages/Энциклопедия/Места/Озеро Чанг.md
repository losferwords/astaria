# Lake Chang

![[Assets/Images/Chang_Lake.jpg]]

**Chang** (昌) is a large lake at the heart of [[Долина Гармонии|He-Dji-Gu]], formed by the many branches and tributaries of the [[Река Тяо Хэ|Tiao He]]. Its shores are densely populated, and fishing boats, merchant barges and small passenger vessels fill its water all year.

Two of [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire's]] most important cities stand on it: capital [[Город Джу-Суо|Dju-Suo]] and ancient [[Город Чанг-Ша|Chang-Sha]]. A lasting rivalry divides them. The capital prides itself on the court and the [[Храм Дракона|Temple of the Dragon]], while Chang-Sha sees itself as guardian of a quieter farming tradition.

Rice fields, mulberry gardens and paper-drying workshops line the shore. Communities manage a system of canals and locks, since one keeper's mistake can flood a neighbour's field. Water disputes are therefore settled faster here than many matters of the palace.

During festivals, thousands of lights cover Chang. Families set lanterns bearing ancestors' names upon the water, and boatmen leave clear channels among them so memory does not keep the living from returning home. In foul weather the lake changes: its broad surface darkens swiftly, shores vanish, and a stray light can lead a vessel into overgrown channels.
