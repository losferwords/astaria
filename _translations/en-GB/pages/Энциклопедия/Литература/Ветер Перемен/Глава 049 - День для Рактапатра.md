# Chapter 049 — One More Day for Raktapatra

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 104 NE, winter*

[[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] sat imprisoned under the watch of a young woman named Xin when [[Рактапатр|Raktapatra]] began drinking away his life. The guards had prepared a trap for [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari]], and the goddess appeared to reclaim her weapon. Xin herself nearly became her first victim.

[[Бао Си|Bao Xi]] descended into the dungeon, but it was not only the former Avatar who spoke through him. [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Lang-An]] had entered his body to address the goddess of blood. Shubhankari admitted that she wandered between corporeal and incorporeal states and could not reach her native country.

When the others were brought below, the goddess saw [[Ошу Мати|Oshu Mati]] and promised knowledge of [[Руфу|Rufu's]] return. Lang-An interrupted her with a wall of flame. Shubhankari then told Oshu to find her later in [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]]. Seeing [[Томас Робинсон|Tom's]] necklace, she turned back the moon's phase and granted Gilja one more day to forge his raktakatars and rid himself of her blade.

After the goddess departed, Lang-An asked them to save Xin. Gilja and Oshu healed her, and Bao Xi carried her to the temple. On the way out of the dungeon, the travellers also freed [[Рон Эллиот|Ron Elliot]], who had been captured in the woods.

Gilja and [[Урист Китин|Urist]] found a forge. They fashioned two raktakatars from Raktapatra, after which the goddess's former weapon disappeared and returned to Shubhankari.

Ron brought alarming news: [[Аксель Хан|Axel Khan's]] army had reached the northern border of the Empire of Fire and was preparing to attack. The travellers tried to warn [[Чани Сан|Chani San]], but the palace was preparing for the arrival of an imperial herald.
