# Chapter 080 — The Trail to Badrakali

*[[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]], 106 NE, winter*

Having hidden [[Мэйлун|the dragon]], the travellers decided to speak with the boatbuilder constructing a ship for [[Кирида Ариса|Arisa]] and [[Айсан Баят|Aysan Bayat]]. At the harbour they found Prakash, a good-natured Rajati and an exceedingly slow craftsman. The vessel was nowhere near completion. [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] blessed him, and Prakash went off to celebrate in a tavern.

Meanwhile, Arisa had found the trail of a merchant dealing in sakura petals, though the man himself had vanished. At the House of Seven Petals, [[Анвика Нарай|Anvika Narai]] said his name was Sajar Viraan. He carried petals from [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]] to Tanassar and was the father of the young smith Shankar Viraan.

The travellers learnt more from Shankar. Sajar had been carrying the petals as an offering to [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari]], but monsters attacked him in the jungle. Afterwards, the merchant sank into guilt and began venturing ever farther from the city.

The travellers questioned [[Мерави Марджари|Meravi]] at the gates, then entered the [[Джунгли Шанкари|jungle]] with Aysan under the pretext of a hunt. After long wandering they found the trail of [[Нага|nagas]] moving south. Nearby stood a man-shaped stone and the beginning of a hidden path.

Gilja understood where it led. Deep within the jungle lay his home village, [[Деревня Бадракали|Badrakali]]. He resolved to stop the nagas before they drew near it and, at the same time, enjoy a hunt with Aysan.

[[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] suggested luring out a single naga and cutting it off from the brood. In the southern jungle, a hunt is seldom only a hunt; sometimes it becomes a homecoming.
