# Shadow

## Main text

The Shadows, or the Forgotten, serve the cult of [[Мерката|Mercate]], goddess of night.

A person left alone in the frozen wastes of [[Антра|Antra]] has scarcely any hope of survival. Those few who return from that realm of endless cold and darkness, however, are changed for ever. Rumour holds that Mercate herself gathers such lost souls into her service. Whatever life a person led before, once they become a Shadow they cease to recognise those they once knew.

No one can say with certainty whether the Shadows act for an earthly organisation or receive their commands from the goddess herself. The Forgotten generally preserve a silence like the grave, and their true purpose remains veiled in mystery.

## Training

Shadows are masters of combat at great distance. Their quivers hold specialised arrows for nearly every need, while the spells of night allow them to escape danger, unnerve an enemy and turn darkness into a weapon. A Shadow's strength lies as much in dread and uncertainty as in the arrow that finally strikes.

## Attributes

The Forgotten carry a bow and a quiver filled with many kinds of arrow. Some are steeped in poison; others are enchanted with the darkness of night itself.
