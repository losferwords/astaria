# Spine of Archaea

![[Assets/Images/Archaean_Spine_Mountains.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Spine of Archaea** is the largest mountain range in [[Астария|Astaria]], lying across southern [[Архея|Archaea]]. It stretches from the [[Рассветное море|Dawn Sea]] in the east to [[Регион Аментир|Amentir]] in the west.

The range forms a natural barrier between inhabited country and the [[Пепельные земли|Ashlands]]. In the east, the [[Горный хребет Шафар|Shafar Mountains]] branch from the Spine at a right angle.
