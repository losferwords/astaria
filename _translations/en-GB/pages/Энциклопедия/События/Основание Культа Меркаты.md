# Founding of the Cult of Mercate

![[Assets/Images/Merkate_Cult.jpg]]

The founding of the [[Культ Меркаты|Cult of Mercate]] is dated to 1486 ChE. A religious community devoted to [[Мерката|Mercate]], goddess of night, dreams and secrets, took shape upon [[Равнина Вайтфилд|Whitefield Plain]]. It emerged amongst the Dair settlers of north-western [[Кельн|Keln]], before the kingdom of [[Лунаар|Lunaar]] existed.

## Before the Kingdom

For the ancestors of the [[Лудаир|Ludair]], worship of Mercate offered a way to speak of things difficult to voice openly: fear, loss, recurring dreams and promises that bound the living to the dead. The goddess's attendants preserved vows and interpreted dreams; a lasting community gradually formed around that care.

The chronology distinguishes the Cult's beginnings from the foundation of Lunaar in 1457 ChE. Its religious tradition was therefore already established when royal authority arose. That seniority helps explain the independence the organisation retained at court.

## Service to the Night

Priests, shrine keepers and scribes sustained the Cult's daily life. In time, its best-known servants became the [[Тень|Shadows]], Mercate's [[Имитей|Imithei]]. They might serve the king as advisers, bodyguards and envoys, but their foremost allegiance remained with the goddess.

The founding of the community should not be confused with the appearance of all its later institutions. The date in the chronicles marks the tradition's emergence on Whitefield Plain; it does not establish when particular offices or rites developed, or when the first Shadow was made.

## Remembering the Beginnings

The [[Храм Меркаты|Temple of Mercate]] in northern Lunaar later became the Cult's principal home. Its archives preserve accounts of the goddess's appearances, interpretations of dreams and ancient vows. People still seek its servants' help in grief and words that will carry them through the night. Born before the kingdom, the community retained its place beside royal power without becoming part of it.
