# Djevahar Ashma

![[Assets/Images/Djevahar_Ashma.jpg]]

**Djevahar Ashma** is a [[Раджати|Rajati]] merchant and jeweller from [[Город Ванпур|Vanpur]]. His family keeps a small shop in the city market: his wife handles the trade and bargains with customers, while Djevahar prefers to work in the workshop behind the counter. There he receives established clients, appraises stones and repairs jewellery whose owners are unwilling to entrust it to street craftsmen.

Djevahar works in gold and silver, making earrings, signet rings, fine chains and small ritual vessels. He pays particular attention to the weight of the metal and to flaws hidden within a piece. The jeweller charges well for faultless work, but values returning customers and can keep the origin of an object or the name of its patron private for years.

Djevahar is cautious with strangers and rarely leaves the western quarters without good reason. He dislikes naming the people for whom he works and regards silence about another person's purchases as part of a craftsperson's honour. Wealthy visitors appreciate such discretion, but it also feeds market rumours about commissions delivered to him through the back door rather than across the counter.

In the summer of 106 NE, Djevahar's shop was damaged by fire. At about the same time, the city watch began to take an interest in certain pieces bearing his hallmark. That alone proved nothing: hundreds of Vanpur's residents wore Ashma's work. After the incident, however, the jeweller appeared at the counter even less often and became more selective about those admitted to his workshop.
