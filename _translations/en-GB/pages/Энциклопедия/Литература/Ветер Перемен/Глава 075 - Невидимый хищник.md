# Chapter 075 — The Invisible Predator

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 105 NE, autumn*

Without horses, the travellers would have to carry their burdens themselves. [[Урист Китин|Urist]] asked the elder of [[Деревня Тай-Линь|Tai-Lin]] for help, and the man agreed to provide bearers if the travellers rid the village of its nocturnal predators.

[[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] and [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] followed the trail of the creature that had torn apart their horses. It ended at an ancient [[Раджати|Rajati]] altar where sacrifices had once been offered to [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari]]. Fresh horse blood stained the stone. When Urist and [[Мэй Ву|Mei]] arrived, the group decided to set an ambush.

That night, an invisible creature sank dozens of needle-like teeth into Gilja's neck. As it filled itself with his blood, its body became visible. The travellers attacked, and Urist sheared away half its head. Yet on the road back to the village, the corpse began to lose weight before vanishing altogether and leaving them without proof.

Before dawn, they decided to lure another creature to the village itself. Mei stole a chicken and left a coin in payment. The elder happened upon the decapitated bait, but before he could accuse the travellers, another predator emerged from the silence and attacked Gilja once more.

A Rajati woman wielding raktakatars severed the creature's head. She was a [[Ракша|Raksha]] from [[Город Танассар|Tanassar]] on the shores of Crimson Bay. After a brief exchange more akin to an interrogation, she hastened south.

In the morning, the elder gave the travellers three similar-looking young Dju to carry their supplies as far as the border. That night upon the road, an unusual rider caught up with them: [[Велимир Сажин|Velimir Sazhin]] from distant [[Обитель|Obitelj]]. He was searching for a lost artefact resembling a great black crystal.
