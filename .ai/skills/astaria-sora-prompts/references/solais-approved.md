## Scene-specific lessons

Earlier Anima iterations repeatedly lost city scale and harbor life, produced identical boats or oversized people, turned stone quays into wooden piers, and introduced modern road lighting. These motivated the brief's functional architecture, distributed work groups, varied vessels, modest foreground framing, and selective illumination. They are not established Sora failure modes.

Preserve for Solais: a large inhabited port, deep sheltered bay between rocky shores, extensive stone-and-timber neighborhoods, substantial stone piers, archaic Celtic visual culture, and coherent overcast daylight. Do not transplant these features wholesale into other locations.

## Approved prompt

```text
Create a single landscape illustration in 16:9 format: a vast, inhabited ancient Celtic highland port city surrounding a deep sheltered bay between steep black cliffs.

Art direction: polished fantasy digital illustration with the visual clarity of richly painted anime backgrounds. Semi-realistic architecture and natural proportions, crisp selective contours, softly painted atmospheric distance, nuanced materials, and carefully composed areas of light and shadow. Detailed but readable, with restrained surface texture and rich natural colors. The image should feel illustrated, not photographed or rendered in 3D.

An elevated panoramic view from a rocky hillside overlooks the harbor and extensive neighborhoods on both shores. Hundreds of overlapping stone-and-timber buildings climb irregular terraces and recede into rain haze. Keep the foreground framing modest so the city and bay occupy most of the composition. The winding shoreline leads the eye through the working harbor toward the distant sea.

Buildings vary in height, shape, and purpose: small homes with shuttered windows, broad warehouses with heavy wooden doors, open-sided boat sheds, long clan halls, and a welcoming waterfront tavern. Weathered slate roofs mingle with turf and wooden shingles. One large Celtic gathering hall overlooks the harbor from an upper terrace. Rough masonry, exposed timber, and occasional carved knotwork give the city an archaic, handcrafted character.

Substantial stone quays and long, broad stone piers serve several harbor basins. Worn steps descend to lower landing platforms. Small, naturally proportioned townspeople work in uneven groups: carrying rope-bound wool bales, unloading timber, sorting fish, and repairing a wooden hull. Leave clear passages between the activity. Figures remain secondary to the city, with no foreground character portrait.

Several large, broad-hulled wooden merchant ships, each with a single mast and a simple square sail, share the harbor with smaller fishing boats. Vary their size, orientation, and distance; moored ships have furled sails. Keep their construction simple, without towering stern cabins or elaborate multi-deck superstructures.

Cold overcast afternoon, a continuous heavy cloud ceiling, fine rain, wind-blown chimney smoke, blue-grey water, charcoal stone, dark weathered wood, and moss-green slopes. Soft daylight keeps the city legible. Only a few sheltered doorways show subdued amber firelight.

Avoid modern roads, asphalt, streetlights, festival decorations, identical buildings, evenly spaced crowds, and rows of duplicated boats. No text or watermark.
```
