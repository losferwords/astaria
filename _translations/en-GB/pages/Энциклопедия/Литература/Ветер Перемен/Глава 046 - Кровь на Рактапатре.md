# Chapter 046 — Blood upon Raktapatra

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 104 NE, autumn*

[[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] and [[Ошу Мати|Oshu Mati]] had no wish to remain in the palace and went to the tavern. [[Урист Китин|Urist]], [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] and [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] returned to the palace with pork. Urist talked his way past a guard and entered the kitchen, where he prepared pork pilaf with such confidence that even the local cooks had to acknowledge his skill.

Upstairs, Tom heard weeping behind [[Ли Сан|Li San's]] door. She tried to conceal her anxiety, but the absence of her guard struck him as suspicious. [[Мусака|Musaka]] found the trail, which led Tom back to the tavern.

Li San rejoiced at the food. After a long conversation, she kept her promise to Urist and conceived an heir with him, though their marriage remained an alliance of duty, politics and cautious tenderness.

At the tavern, Gilja and Oshu ordered a dish that proved to be a living octopus. Startled, Gilja struck it with [[Рактапатр|Raktapatra]], but the blade lodged in the table. Oshu tried to help, touched the weapon as a conduit and suddenly formed a connection with [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari]]. The goddess of blood commanded her to find and kill a northern woman somewhere in the southern jungles of [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]].

The bond with the goddess became torture. Blood poured from Oshu's nose, eyes and ears; she vomited, and the drops fell upon Raktapatra. Gilja saw the spirit world, where every soul shone as a point of light during the Harvest. His mind broke, and he began killing indiscriminately.

Meanwhile, Tom found Li San's missing guard. The man was hiding something in a chest, then tried to flee. Tom and Mei caught him, only to be confronted at once by a maddened Gilja, ready to enact the Harvest in the tavern itself. Weakened, Oshu regained consciousness and rushed to save her friends from the consequences of another's goddess.
