## The Empire of the Sun

Amon-Astat, the Empire of the Sun, is one of the oldest states in [[Астария|Astaria]]. Its history began among settlements of the [[Хефат|Khefat]] at almost the same time as the rise of [[Хтониды|Chthonid]] civilisation. Dynasties, borders and even the courses of rivers have changed across the millennia, yet the image of the country endures: bright cities amid the sands, green banks beside the water, and roads along which caravans pass from one refuge to the next.

Most of the empire lies within the [[Пустыня Сехет|Sekhet Desert]] and fertile [[Регион Аментир|Amentir]], known as the Great Oasis. Water determines not only the harvest but politics itself: canals, wells and river landings are guarded as diligently as city walls. A settlement offers a traveller a cup of water before asking their name, according to an ancient custom whose violation is seen as a mark of heartlessness.

The empire's culture is inseparable from [[Аст|Ast]], God of the Sun. The state faith of [[Хат-Астат|Khat-Astat]] keeps the calendar and archives, and maintains temples, infirmaries and schools. Its highest priests spend years learning to wield the power of the sun before becoming [[Светоносный|Lightbringers]]. At dawn, temple doors open to the first ray; evening is regarded not as light's defeat, but as the promise of its return.

The throne passes from parent to eldest child. Pharaohs answer not only for the law, but for the preservation of the country itself. Scribes, surveyors, priests and keepers of the canals serve at court, and sound rule is measured by whether water reaches the furthest field and whether the granary opens in a year of famine. The craft cities around [[Гора Атафет|Mount Atafet]] are famed for metalwork and masonry, while the libraries of [[Город Тиза|Tiza]] gather maps and accounts of ancient Archaea.

The present pharaoh, [[Рамхотеп II|Ramhotep II]], preserved the realm through the Great Drought, a religious schism and the [[Война Жаждущих|War of the Thirsty]]. These trials have left the empire changed. Faith in Ast remains its heart, but its people now understand that sunlight does not absolve either ruler or priest of their duty to humankind.
