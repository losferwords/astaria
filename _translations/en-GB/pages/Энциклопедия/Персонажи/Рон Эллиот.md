# Ron Elliot

![[Assets/Images/Ron_Elliot.jpg]]

## Main text

**Ron Elliot** is a young man from [[Лунаар|Lunaar]], born into the Elliot family, renowned hunters and trackers of the [[Лудаир|Ludair]] people. In his youth, stubbornness and a desire to prove his worth drove him from his homeland and into a nomad host campaigning south against [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]]. There Ron endured a harsh education in survival and learnt archery under the experienced [[Томас Робинсон|Thomas Robinson]], who saved his life more than once.

The first years of wandering tempered the young man. Once a hot-headed novice, he became a hardy and observant scout capable of finding his bearings in almost any country. In time, Ron grew disillusioned with the nomads' purpose, left their camp and joined the army of [[Батар Саг|Batar Sag]], where he swiftly distinguished himself as one of the khan's finest scouts. He is cautious, attentive and possessed of an instinctive sense of direction. Unlike most warriors, he prefers to avoid a direct fight, relying instead upon stealth and precise calculation.

Ron is the younger brother of [[Селина Эллиот|Selina Elliot]].
