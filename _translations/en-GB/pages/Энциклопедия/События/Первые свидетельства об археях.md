# First Evidence of the Archaeans

![[Assets/Images/Archaeans.png]]

The oldest known evidence of [[Археи|Archaean]] civilisation is dated to 5025 ChE. It is the earliest milestone in the surviving chronology of [[Астария|Astaria]], but records the age of known traces rather than an established beginning of the people themselves.

## Traces of a Vanished Civilisation

Knowledge of the Archaeans comes from scattered ruins, unusual objects and enigmatic images. Their legacy suggests a highly developed culture long before the rise of the [[Хтониды|Chthonids]], yet tells us almost nothing certain about its creators' appearance, daily life or origins.

Different peoples interpret the same finds in different ways. What one keeper of antiquities considers a shrine, another may regard as a storehouse; the purpose of an object or the meaning of an image often remains disputed. The age of a trace alone cannot explain whom a building served or what place it held in Archaean life.

## The Limits of Knowledge

The date of 5025 ChE does not establish that the Archaeans first appeared in the world, founded a unified state or experienced a particular catastrophe in that year. Their origins and the reasons for their disappearance remain unknown. The connections between individual sites have yet to be understood, and later legends cannot replace lost evidence.

Archaean history is pieced together from discoveries, each of which may change an earlier interpretation. That is why this milestone is called the first evidence: it preserves the ancient civilisation's place in history while leaving unanswered questions open.
