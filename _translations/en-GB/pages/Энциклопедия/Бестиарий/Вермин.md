# Vermin

![[Assets/Images/Vermin.jpg]]

## Main text

**Vermin** are ratlike anthropomorphs found throughout [[Астария|Astaria]]. They walk upright and have red or black eyes, sharp claws and long teeth slick with venomous saliva. Their bodies are covered in coarse, bristling fur.

Unusually aggressive, Vermin commonly descend upon settlements in bands of five to fifteen. Despite their parasitic way of life, they show no interest in weapons, jewellery or other treasure. Hunger alone drives their raids, and they rely upon tooth and claw rather than tools.

These scavengers have earned an especially foul reputation by living close to humankind and feeding upon scraps and refuse within towns. When food grows scarce, however, even a group of armed travellers may become prey.
