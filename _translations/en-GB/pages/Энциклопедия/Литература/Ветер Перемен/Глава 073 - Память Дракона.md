# Chapter 073 — The Dragon's Memory

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 105 NE, autumn*

After saving the Dragon, the travellers spent the night in the [[Храм Дракона|Temple of the Dragon]]. [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] helped a local physician tend [[Томас Робинсон|Tom's]] terrible wounds, while Tom tried to understand what it meant to be a [[Тень|Shadow]] of [[Мерката|the Goddess of Night]]—no longer as a metaphor, but as a law written into his soul.

In the morning, the group went to [[Сунь Ляньши|Sun Lianshi]] to speak with her and [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Lang-An]]. In the Hall of Trysts, the Dragon once again thanked [[Мэй Ву|Mei]], Urist, Gilja and Tom for saving him and the Dju people. He promised to create Ribbons of Memory and raise a monument to those who had delivered the Great Dragon.

Their conversation swiftly passed from gratitude to warning. Lang-An feared a calamity advancing from the south, from the lands of [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]], and asked Mei and her friends to investigate, since their road led there in any case. He also spoke of [[Мэйлун|Meilong]] with keen interest: [[Морской дракон|sea dragons]] had long been thought extinct, and Lang-An had believed himself the last of their kind.

In the throne room, the empress introduced her new adviser, [[Чень Ляньши|Chen Lianshi]]. He was pleased to have returned to the temple and intended to revive cultural and commercial exchange with neighbouring countries, especially [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]].

The travellers struck a bargain with him and purchased the [[Бордель Уй-Джан|Ui-Jan]] in the red-light district. The arrangement benefited everyone: the capital needed order and ready coin, while the travellers already possessed experience in running [[Бордель Халык-Бындыр|Halyk-Byndir]].

Thereafter, Gilja began planning the road home to Suraj Ka Ghar. Lang-An had been saved, but the south was already calling them with another kind of blood.
