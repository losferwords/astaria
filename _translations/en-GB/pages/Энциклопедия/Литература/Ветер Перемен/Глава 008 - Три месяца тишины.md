# Chapter 008 — Three Months of Silence

*Amon-Astat, 103 NE, autumn*

The ship stolen from Argos carried the fugitives to [[Город Уасет|Waset]], a port of the [[Амон-Астат|Empire of the Sun]] in the [[Река Хапи|Hapi]] delta. After Gilas's stone arenas and foreign streets, Waset seemed almost impossible: crowded docks, salt wind, fishers, merchants and priests, fierce light over water and the sense that the chains had finally remained beyond the sea.

No one now commanded them to stay together. Each could choose a path, and the three quiet months drew them apart.

[[Хонсу-Намун|Khonsu-Namun]] returned to books, languages and strange traces of the past. Aboard [[Персефона Раптис|Persephone's]] ship, he recovered an ancient book taken from him when he was captured near the Archaean temple. In [[Город Тиза|Tiza]], he and his teacher deciphered it as though listening to a vanished age through a stone wall. Scattered diagrams suggested a device capable of holding an object above a surface without touching it.

[[Урист Китин|Urist Kitin]] reached [[Город Атафет|Atafet]] and finally found [[Арно Эклунд|Arno Eklund]]. The city of craftspeople greeted him with forges, hot furnaces and the almost homelike scent of metal. Arno agreed to take him as an apprentice, but after cage and arena the word felt too narrow. Urist wished to work beside him as an equal.

Arno set him a test: obtain [[Золотая сталь|Golden Steel]]. Khonsu discovered that iron and coal were not enough; the alloy required anatase and red pyrite, found near hydrothermal springs in the [[Пепельные земли|Ashlands]].

[[Данис ибн Синай|Danis ibn Sinai]] opened a small shop in Tiza. For the first time since Gilas, his life gained a rhythm: medicine and alchemy by day, scientific conversation with Khonsu over henket by evening, and perhaps by night the questions that had once drawn him to observe Hyperion.

One day Danis saw guards chasing a dark-skinned woman through the market. They caught her and drove a spear through her body. Once the guards turned to calm the crowd, the dying woman drank from a flask; her wound closed, and she rose and vanished into Tiza's alleys. Danis was shocked as both physician and scholar. What draught could return someone from the edge of death?

[[Томас Робинсон|Thomas Robinson]] chose [[Город Натшепсут|Natshepsut]], home to a temple of [[Мерката|Mercate]] and many Lunaar pilgrims. He settled there with [[Мусака|Musaka]] and became a wealthy merchant's hunter, gaining a home, food and work his hands understood.

While tracking a wounded gazelle, Tom fell through loose sand into an ancient chamber resembling the Archaean temple where he had once sheltered. Two black rectangles lay among the stones. Touching one produced a harsh, repeating sound; through a glove it remained silent. Tom took it. It would later be called the [[Чёрный Вестник|Black Herald]], but then it was merely a troubling object.

Tom travelled to Tiza to show Khonsu. There he learnt his companions again needed a hunter able to read tracks and find people across difficult ground.

[[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] received a small commission from an anonymous courier: [[Рамат-Хат-Су|Ramat-Hat-Su]], an officer of [[Жаждущие|the Thirsty]], had disappeared. Khonsu said Ramat was considered a criminal in Amon-Astat and unlikely to be seen openly. His younger sister [[Шани-Хат-Су|Shani-Hat-Su]] might know more.

Khonsu remained in the library while Urist, Tom and Mei searched for Shani. At her temple they learnt she had been missing since the previous evening. A Nadair gardener outside, [[Блейн из Руше|Blaine of Ruche]], had not seen her, but suggested asking those who knew the city's low passages better than people: rats.

The rats led them to the city prison. Urist, Tom, Mei and Blaine entered secretly, crossed its endless corridors and found Ramat-Hat-Su. Yet in the pharaoh's capital, good fortune seldom comes without cost. Guards discovered the intruders, and three months of silence ended in the ring of weapons.
