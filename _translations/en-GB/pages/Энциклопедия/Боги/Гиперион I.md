# Hyperion I

## Main text

Hyperion I is the founder, first and still only king of [[Гилас|Gilas]]. In 156 ChE he led a company of [[Хтониды|Chthonids]] into the western lands of [[Архея|Archaea]], where an independent state would eventually arise. After the [[Падение Хтона|Fall of Chthon]], Hyperion severed Gilas from the ruined empire and forged a military league of cities into one of the continent's greatest powers.

The cause of his extraordinary longevity is unknown. Generations of Ellians have been born, served and died while the king's appearance scarcely changed. To the people of the republic, that constancy became proof of Hyperion's divine nature. He himself has never demanded to be called a god, nor has he ever separated royal service from a warrior's duty.

Hyperion's cult has no need of a distant celestial figure: the king lives among his people, receives ambassadors, attends contests and may address the army in person. He is revered as the embodiment of war, courage, heroism and physical perfection. Spear, shield and lion signify not only victory, but the responsibility of the strong towards those who stand beside them in the line.

For many [[Эллийцы|Ellians]], discipline has effectively become a religious practice. Conscientious service, composure in the face of fear and the defence of a comrade are held to be more sincere prayers to Hyperion than words spoken before an altar. The [[Идеал|Paragons]] are therefore not merely great warriors, but mortals who have drawn near to the measure of perfection he established.

The presence of an undying king lends Gilas remarkable stability, yet leaves one troubling question unanswered: will the republic ever be able to imagine a future without Hyperion I?
