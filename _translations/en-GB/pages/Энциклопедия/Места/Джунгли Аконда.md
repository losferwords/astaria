# Akonda Jungle

![[Assets/Images/Akonda_Jungle.jpg]]

## Main text

**Akonda** is a vast tropical jungle in south-western [[Архея|Archaea]], south of the [[Река Мвиди|Mvidi River]]. From above it appears an unbroken green sea; beneath the canopy lie marshy lowlands, dry stone ridges and rivers that change course after rain. Sunlight seldom reaches the earth, and humid air smells of resin, flowers and rotting wood.

The [[Ваку|Vaku]] read the jungle as closely as outsiders read maps. Broken grass tells a hunter who passed in the night, insects warn of rain, and water's taste reveals whether a camp is safe. Trail signs are cut into bark or woven from grass, but only the trained can see them.

Akonda provides fruit, game, timber, rare resins and medicines. Some roots break fever; others induce ritual visions or make poison. Dosage turns one into another, so gatherers study for years and never identify a plant by colour alone.

To [[Вакумара|Vakumara]], the forest is sanctuary as well as land. Ancestors' carved images stand in old groves, and paths to burial places are deliberately confused. The dead accompany their kin in leaves, birdsong and returning dreams. What seems haunted to a foreigner is filled with familiar voices to a Vaku.

Crossing Akonda requires a guide, patience and respect for prohibitions: never sleep beside a funerary carving, drink still water or answer a voice calling from the thicket in a familiar name. Survivors seldom argue over which warning saved them.
