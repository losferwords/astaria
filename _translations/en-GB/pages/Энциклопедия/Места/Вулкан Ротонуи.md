# Rotonui Volcano

![[Assets/Images/Rotonui_Volcano.jpg]]

## Main text

**Rotonui** is a great volcano in the centre of the [[Полуостров Туануку|Tuanuku Peninsula]]. Ancient eruptions formed its mountain slopes, black shores and fertile soil, where [[Хамоа|Hamoa's]] gardens grow. Rivers rise at its foot, and helmspeople see its summit long before the coast appears.

The Manai regard Rotonui as the father of goddess [[Икатерра|Ikaterra]]. In their songs, the mountain's fire met the sea and brought forth warm springs, healing water and a land able to receive the Wanderers. They treat it not as an evil monster, but as a stern ancestor whose gifts cannot be separated from a dangerous temper.

Rotonui sleeps most of the time. Herbs and volcanic clay used for wounds and skin disease are gathered on its lower slopes. Higher up lie hot ground, sulphurous vapours and brittle lava fields, where guides test every step with a long pole.

Smoke has appeared above the summit more often in recent years. Stream temperatures change, animals leave some slopes, and elders compare new cracks with signs in ancient songs. No general evacuation has been declared, but Wardens inspect the old routes to the sea and ask villages to keep their boats intact even in calm seasons.

For pilgrims, climbing Rotonui is a trial of patience rather than conquest. A worthy traveller stops where the keeper of the path commands. The Manai say anyone wishing to prove mastery over the mountain has already shown they learnt nothing from it.
