# Soul Crystal

![[Assets/Images/Power_Crystal.jpg]]

**Soul Crystals**, also known as Soul Stones, exist in every creature of [[Астария|Astaria]], including ordinary humans and animals. They are not exclusive to the [[Имитей|Imithei]]: possessing a crystal does not, by itself, mean possessing superhuman abilities.

The power of these crystals is associated with greatly enhanced mental and physical capabilities. Large specimens are exceedingly rare, and the connection between a crystal's development and becoming an Imithei has no generally accepted explanation.

Soul Stones may be found in many colours, though shades of red are mentioned most often: claret, violet, crimson and dark orange. Their surface is invariably smooth and their shape either oval or round. A crystal's diameter may range from that of a grain of sand to that of a cherry.

Some scholars believe that the culture of the ancient [[Археи|Archaeans]] was intimately bound to these crystals, but no conclusive evidence has yet been found.
