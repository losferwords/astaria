# The Thirsty

![[Assets/Images/Thirsty.jpg]]

## Main text

During the Great Drought and the [[Кризис веры в Амон-Астате|Crisis of Faith in Amon-Astat]], a movement arose among those who had lost faith both in the pharaoh's government and in the divine protection of [[Аст|Ast]].

At first, the rebellion drew chiefly upon the poorest ranks of society. In time, disaffected priests, mercenaries and merchants joined them. As their influence and military strength grew, the Thirsty began taking food, water and shelter from wealthy householders by force. When even this proved insufficient, the revolt spread from [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]] into [[Гилас|Gilas]], provoking the [[Война Жаждущих|War of the Thirsty]]. The conflict claimed many lives and left enduring consequences throughout western [[Архея|Archaea]].

The organisation survived the war's end, but withdrew underground. Its agents, spies and assassins dispersed across [[Астария|Astaria]] with a single purpose: to rid the world of slavery for ever.

The motto of the Thirsty is:

> The Wind of Change is coming.
