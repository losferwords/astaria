# Waset

![[Assets/Images/Uaset_City.jpg]]

## Main text

**Waset** is a port city in the north of [[Амон-Астат|the Empire of the Sun]], built in the delta of the [[Река Хапи|Hapi]]. River craft transfer grain, cloth and stone here to seagoing vessels, while a fortified harbour protects the realm's northern coast.

Channels and canals divide the city. Certain streets exist only during the dry season; others become waterways between the houses after the flood. The inhabitants read the movement of water by its colour and scent, strengthen the quays in advance and raise their warehouses upon stone foundations.

Birds nest, river predators hunt and smugglers conceal their small boats among the delta reeds. Military patrols regularly search the channels, yet the labyrinth changes after every great flood. A guide who knew yesterday's course may run a vessel aground today.

For a traveller, Waset is the gate between desert and sea. One may hire a riverboat, hear tidings from distant ports or discover a cargo absent from every temple record.
