# Sunset Valley

![[Assets/Images/Sunset_Valley.jpg]]

## Main text

**Sunset Valley**, or **Mvidi-Mukulu** in the language of the [[Ваку|Vaku]], follows the [[Река Мвиди|Mvidi River]] from the country around [[Озеро Нехмет|Lake Nekhmet]] towards the [[Закатное море|Sunset Sea]]. A green band of water, trees and settlements lies between the arid highlands, making the valley the swiftest natural road between [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]] and the lands of [[Вакумара|Vakumara]].

The road is convenient, but not always safe. The boundary between the two states shifts, individual crossings are controlled by local families, and [[Гнолл|gnolls]] and other predators roam away from the river. Caravans try to discover in advance which standard presently flies over a checkpoint and who commands the ferry.

At sunset, light reflected from the water colours the valley slopes red and gold. To the Vaku, this is more than a beautiful sight: many ancient rites and songs are bound to the sun's journey towards the sea. Offering stones and groves that local people avoid during days of mourning still stand along the banks.

The valley is rich in medicinal herbs, river fish and fruit growing in its damp lowlands. Floodwater uncovers old channels and sometimes washes objects from settlements abandoned long ago into the open. Such finds may equally interest a scholar from Tiza, a Vaku elder and a border official certain that everything unearthed belongs to the pharaoh.

Mvidi-Mukulu remains a meeting place of cultures. Solar emblems stand beside Vaku carvings in its villages, and the people are accustomed to speaking several languages. A traveller may find a guide to the west here—but must first understand which customs govern this bank today.
