# Warden Bridge

![[Assets/Images/Warden_Bridge_Isthmus.jpg]]

**Warden Bridge** is a broad isthmus linking the [[Полуостров Туануку|Tuanuku Peninsula]] to mainland [[Архея|Archaea]]. It is Hamoa's only dependable land route and thus trade road, cultural boundary and the country's most vulnerable frontier.

Its origin is unknown. Ancient records do not mention a land road to the eastern islands, and stretches of shore appear too straight to be natural. After heavy rain, flat stones laid in rows emerge from the soil, though nobody has traced this supposed masonry deeply enough.

The name comes from the [[Хранитель|Wardens]], Hamoa's [[Имитей|Imithei]]. Small refuges with water, medicine and dry food stand along the road. Nearby villages care for them, since custom requires aid for every traveller until they show hostile intent.

The contrast between Tuanuku and [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]] is clearest here. Fish, fruit and medicinal plants arrive from the east; spices, cloth, strange rumours and jungle refugees from the west. Trade continues even in tense times, but Wardens watch every armed party closely.

The bridge seems easy only in the dry season. Rains turn low ground to marsh, tides cut off side trails and night fog conceals signal lights. Old guides advise keeping to the central road and never following a voice from the mangroves: it may be a lost person, a predator, or something that has learnt to imitate both.
