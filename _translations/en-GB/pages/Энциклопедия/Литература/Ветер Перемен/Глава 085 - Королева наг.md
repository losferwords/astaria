# Chapter 085 — Queen of the Nagas

*[[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]], 106 NE, spring*

The cavern greeted the travellers with a forest of stalactites and stalagmites. They stood so thickly that even [[Томас Робинсон|Tom's]] night sight could not pierce them. While following the wall, [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] stumbled into a rope snare and was hauled to the ceiling. From above he saw a thin, dark-skinned man eating a flatbread before taking up his pick and returning to the wall.

Gilja cut the rope, and his friends caught him in the net bridge [[Урист Китин|Urist]] had prudently brought along. The worker took fright and began calling for someone named Shasha, begging the travellers to leave. Plainly, he was a prisoner or slave. The group seized him and hurried back as scales already rustled in the depths.

In the tunnel, Urist and Gilja brought stalactites down upon their pursuers. When they returned to finish the nagas, they found three dead and one alive. At the prisoner's plea they spared her and revived her. Her name was [[Шаша|Shasha]].

Shasha offered a surprising account: the nagas wished to become more like people and studied culture, craft and tradition. They even worshipped [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari]], albeit clumsily. Many abducted humans supposedly worked here, taught the nagas and lived tolerably well. For fuller answers, Shasha proposed taking them to her queen.

Bound as a precaution, she led the travellers into an immense cavern. At its centre rose a pyramid of glass and metal. Its design recalled [[Кион-Чи|Qiong-Qi's]] underground temple: doors bearing [[Чёрный Вестник|Black Harbingers]], strange furniture and incomprehensible mechanisms.

The queen was not a naga, but a northern woman: [[Селина Эллиот|Selina Elliot]]. Tom recognised her from [[Мерката|Mercate's]] stories. When the goddess lost her power, Selina had been carrying out a task in these lands, became disoriented and was captured by the nagas. Sensing the remnant of divine power within her, they asked her to teach them and eventually made her their queen, charged with leading them towards humanity.

Yet the queen was forbidden to leave the city surrounding the pyramid, and within the cavern all connection to Mercate vanished. Tom could no longer feel his goddess either. The travellers resolved to smuggle Selina to the surface, for Tom's sake, Gilja's and Mercate's own.
