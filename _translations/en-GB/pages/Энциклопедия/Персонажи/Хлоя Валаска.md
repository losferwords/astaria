# Chloe Valaska

![[Assets/Images/Chloe_Valaska.jpg]]

## Main text

**Chloe Valaska** is a young [[Венды|Wend]] woman from [[Деревня Мраковица|Mrakovitsa]], raised between her village home and the temple of [[Велисса|Velissa]]. Orphaned in early childhood, she was taken in by another family. Her adoptive parents never concealed her origins and did their best to surround the girl with warmth, although Chloe long felt like a guest obliged to prove anew each day that she had the right to remain.

She spent most of her youth at the temple. Chloe knows the songs of prayer, tends a garden of medicinal herbs and helps prepare simple remedies for colds, burns and sleeplessness. She never possessed the commanding voice of a Bereginya, but listened with such care that people discovered words for pain they had long kept hidden.

Chloe was later expelled from the temple for a grave offence whose circumstances are never told the same way twice. Some villagers insist that she broke a sacred prohibition; others whisper that Velissa herself laid a curse upon her. After the expulsion, former friends began to avoid Chloe, and every misfortune near her home became further proof of guilt in her neighbours' eyes.

She now helps her adoptive family with the household, seldom attends celebrations and meets distrust with deliberate gentleness. Beneath that composure lies not submission, but a stubborn wish to continue living among people who have already passed judgement upon her. Chloe still leaves the first flowers of spring before the household image of Velissa—perhaps as a prayer for forgiveness, or perhaps as a sign that she does not believe the goddess's love has forsaken her.

To a passing traveller, Chloe appears a quiet village woman with green eyes and long chestnut hair. Only an attentive companion may notice how she falls silent at the temple bell and how closely the people of Mrakovitsa watch her every step. The truth of her expulsion remains concealed, yet it may decide whether Chloe becomes a victim of another's fear or the person who one day compels her village to reckon with its own cruelty.
