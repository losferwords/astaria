# Chapter 019 — Time Is Running Out

*Qadir, 104 NE, spring*

After the battle in [[Город Самаан|Samaan's]] sewers, [[Ошу Мати|Oshu Mati]] wished to use the gigantic spider's soul to create a construct. Yet the [[Корона Душ|Crown of Souls]] had already shown that such acts could not be treated as simple craft. When [[Урист Китин|Urist Kitin]] tried to touch the stone, [[Топор Смертоносец|Deathbringer]] stopped him and spoke of fusion.

In a fusion, it explained, two souls become imprisoned within one Crystal. The stronger may subdue the weaker, deny it control of the body and reduce it to the faintest voice, but cannot destroy the rightful bearer or take its place forever. For Urist, who carried an ancient voice beside his own, the warning felt painfully personal.

Meanwhile, [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] secretly planted one of [[Мерката|Mercate's]] coins upon [[Амира Аль-Азвар|Amira al-Azwar]]. His bargain with Night continued to spread through the fates of others, and now its sign rested near the Eternal Princess of western [[Кадир|Qadir]].

Before long, all the travellers stood in Amira's dining hall. She was furious, cursing them for allowing [[Данис ибн Синай|Danis]] to lead her people into the sewers. Yet wrath gradually yielded to calculation. Amira had received a letter from her father, the city was free of the monstrous spider's nest, and both Danis and the [[Сердце Огня|Heart of Fire]] were in her hands.

She had resolved to send the travellers to [[Город Сераф|Seraph]] as prisoners in a cage when [[Малик Аль-Азвар|Malik al-Azwar]] entered the hall. He embraced his daughter and calmly said there was no need for a cage. He would lead these people across the desert himself and deliver them to [[Абу Али аль-Хассан|Abu Ali]].

Malik addressed [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]]. He had spoken with [[Рамат-Хат-Су|Ramat]] and learnt the particulars of the task entrusted to her. The deception at the oasis camp had been uncovered, though Malik seemed less concerned by the offence than by where the affair might lead.

He returned Tom's planted coin. Malik agreed to take part in the venture, but made one condition plain: his daughter was not to be touched by it.

The journey to Seraph passed in conversation. Malik and Danis recalled old techniques studied in the distant past. To the others, their exchanges might have sounded like familiar phrases shared between teacher and pupil, but behind them lay years of Qadian schools, sand, time and lost possibilities.

At the end of the road, they reached [[Город Сераф|Seraph]] on the shore of [[Озеро Зураиф|Lake Zuraif]] and went directly to an audience with Abu Ali.

The Eternal Prince of central Qadir looked gravely unwell. Part of his mind seemed to dwell elsewhere, while his body crumbled from within along brilliant yellow scars. He spoke only what mattered: “Time is running out.”

Abu Ali led the travellers into the [[Храм Дар Мараат|Temple of Dar Maraat]], where they met [[Альзаман|Alzaman]]. There Danis received the blade [[Клинок Айкаб|Aiqab]] from the god and entered [[Вневременье|Timelessness]].

Within that motionless realm, he saw his past: his touch upon the [[Часы Времён|Clock of Time]], his flight from the temple, and the power that caught him up and carried him away before casting him into a city. Danis managed to return both his own time and that of the Heart of Fire to the clock.

Alzaman then sent the travellers to [[Умар ибн Ла-Ахад|Umar ibn La-Ahad]]. In his laboratory, the Heart of Fire could be studied more closely. Yet a god's request is seldom only a request, and their road through Qadir was not finished.
