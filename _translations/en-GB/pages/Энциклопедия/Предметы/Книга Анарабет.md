# Anarabet

![[Assets/Images/Anarabet.jpg]]

**Anarabet**, also called the Seer's Almanac, is a singular book carried by every [[Профитис|Profitis]]. To an ordinary reader its pages appear entirely blank; to a Profitis, they form a guide to events past and yet to come.
