# Amato

## At a glance

- People: [[Эдзо|Edzo]]
- Imithei: [[Онмёдзи|Onmyoji]]
- Deity: [[Ицунэ|Itsune]]
- Ruler: [[Аматсу Рин|Amatsu Rin]], empress and shogun
- Capital: [[Город Кайто|Kaito]]

## The Isles of Souls

Amato (阿魔都), known to travellers as the Isles of Souls, is the ancient empire of the [[Эдзо|Edzo]] in the eastern [[Рассветное море|Dawn Sea]]. Its lands lie scattered across an archipelago of mist, coniferous forests and restless straits. Here, the wind is held to be the voice of a goddess and purity a safeguard against corruption, while the beauty of every rite carries the memory of those who could find no rest.

In 2695 ChE, the ancestors of the Edzo, having left a vanished homeland, [[Основание Амато|founded Amato]] upon the islands they had reached. Tradition says their ships were guided by [[Ицунэ|Itsune]], the unseen Goddess of Wind, whose whisper the sailors heard within their sails. Beside her first shrine on [[Остров Ояширо|Oyashiro]], the settlers established [[Город Кайто|Kaito]] in the same year, the future capital and seat of the Amatsu dynasty.

## Islands and sea

Oyashiro remains the heart of the empire. [[Остров Рюгу|Ryugu]], [[Остров Акасака|Akasaka]] and the lesser islands are bound to it by sea lanes, ancestral alliances and pilgrimage routes. Even within a single province, a journey by water is often swifter than one by road.

Frequent rain, heavy fog and strong winds govern life along the coast. Settlements shelter in coves and on wooded slopes, with fields rising in terraces above the sea. The Edzo cultivate rice and tea, fish the surrounding waters and make salt, paper, lacquerware and ceramics. Blades forged in Amato are prized above all: their smiths approach the forge as a rite and believe that metal remembers the purpose of the hand that shaped it.

## The imperial court

Amato is ruled by the hereditary Amatsu dynasty, whose line reaches back to the founding of the empire. Empress [[Аматсу Рин|Amatsu Rin]] also holds the office of **shogun**, the supreme ruler and head of military authority. Her power is absolute: the governors of islands and towns obey her commands, and the final decision in affairs of state belongs to her.

The **bakufu** serves under the shogun: a government comprising a council and officials. It conducts the realm's affairs, prepares decisions for the sovereign and oversees the execution of her orders. Through the bakufu, the will of the court in [[Город Кайто|Kaito]] reaches the island domains and towns.

Local rule rests with the **daimyo**, governors of island and urban domains. Each island domain has its own daimyo, and daimyo also govern towns. Every daimyo belongs to the samurai class and answers to the shogun for order within the lands entrusted to them. A daimyo holds considerable authority over local life while remaining a subject of the empress and an executor of her will. Even in a distant trading port, the daimyo's authority reminds its inhabitants of Amato's laws.

The **samurai** form a military class devoted to service, whose standing in society is defined by loyalty, discipline and the fulfilment of duty. A strict hierarchy binds the capital, island domains and towns; a merchant's wealth or an ancient house's influence offers no exemption from obedience to the shogun.

Rin is known for faultless composure and an austere devotion to duty. Her rule hardened greatly after Crown Prince [[Аматсу Кай|Amatsu Kai]] fell in the war with [[Обитель|Obitelj]] and her husband, [[Тацуро Тору|Tatsuro Toru]], died. Tension at court now grows between the will of the empress and the priesthood of Itsune.

## Faith, souls and the divine wind

To the Edzo, Itsune is both Goddess of Wind and guide to the dead. Her shrines stand beside mountain passes, harbours and burial grounds. Paper lanterns are lit for living travellers and for souls that have lost their way alike. Funeral rites end with a prayer towards the western horizon, beyond which the goddess bears the departed.

The nearness of the spirit world is felt particularly keenly in Amato. The [[Они|Oni]] inspire fear and compassion in equal measure, and so the Edzo call upon the [[Онмёдзи|Onmyoji]]: hunters able to see the unseen, cleanse the possessed and command **神風**, the divine wind of Itsune.

## Edzo customs

Life in Amato is ordered by ritual. Travelling shoes are removed upon entering a home, serious conversations begin over tea, and a clean dwelling is considered a defence against troubled thoughts and malevolent spirits. Paper screens fill wooden houses with light; in gardens, emptiness is prized, in poetry, what remains unsaid, and in painting, the freedom of open space.

The passing seasons are marked by the contemplation of flowering trees, lantern processions and remembrance of the ancestors. Martial skill is understood first as discipline: a worthy warrior must master the self before mastering the blade. Edzo reserve should not be mistaken for indifference. Care is more often expressed through an act than a confession.

## Mist Island and Obitelj

The chief dispute beyond Amato's shores concerns [[Туманный остров|Mist Island]], where Edzo and [[Венды|Wends]] live side by side. The balance was shattered when King [[Ивар Свирепый|Ivar the Fierce]] began a war of conquest against Amato's holdings. Prince Kai was killed, while the [[Призрачные горы|Ghost Mountains]] and part of the island became a contested frontier. The bloodshed ended only after [[Велисса|Velissa]] intervened and the [[Страж|Guardians]] rose to overthrow Ivar.

Obitelj no longer pursues his campaign, but peace has yet to become friendship. Amato is not prepared to forget its dead or surrender its claim to the island. Merchants are returning to the ports and priests speak of reconciliation, while frontier garrisons continue to watch one another through the mist.

Such is Amato: an empire where the chime of a bell mingles with the surf, an unyielding will may hide behind a courteous bow, and any breath of wind might be a goddess's greeting or the whisper of a soul still seeking its way home.
