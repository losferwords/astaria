# Nereid

![[Assets/Images/Nereid.jpg]]

## Main text

**Nereids** are rare and extraordinarily secretive creatures who live among humans in the cities and settlements of [[Астария|Astaria]]. They are drawn to busy ports, where a stranger may easily vanish among other foreigners, but they are not a seafaring people and do not inhabit open water.

Biological mimicry, bodily adaptation and the power to cloud an observer's senses make a nereid almost impossible to distinguish from an ordinary human being. This is no magical transformation into another creature: she adjusts the traits others perceive and inclines the mind to overlook anything that does not fit. A few observant [[Имитей|Imithei]] claim that a nereid's true form has blue-tinged skin and a multitude of long tentacles in place of hair.

No reliable study of these mysterious beings has survived; even the most ancient archives contain no clear mention of them.

There is no evidence that nereids are inherently aggressive. Indeed, many sailors tell of their affectionate disposition and attraction to humankind, though few ever understand whom they have truly met.
