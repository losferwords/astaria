# Chapter 062 — Forged Passes

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 105 NE, spring*

[[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] and [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] tried to find [[Урист Китин|Urist]] and [[Мэй Ву|Mei]] in [[Город Джу-Суо|Dju-Suo]], but the city swiftly bared its bureaucratic jaws. The guards rejected their passes: their guarantor, [[Бао Си|Bao Xi]], was himself wanted. Tom used [[Мерката|Mercate's]] feather and escaped beneath a veil of darkness with Gilja.

[[Мусака|Musaka]] caught the pass's scent and led them to the home of the official who had issued the forged papers. Tom broke into the upper floor and forced him to replace the guarantor's name with that of [[Нуа Си|Nuwa Xi]], though he failed to recover their silver and had to content himself with a few figs.

The friends later reunited at Bao Xi's shack. In the morning, after exchanging news, they decided to rob the official in earnest and take his blank forms, seal and money. Mei disguised the group as municipal labourers. They entered the house and found the seal, passes, coins and jewellery, but Urist lost his footing upon the stairs and fell into the courtyard. A guard saw him, and Urist slammed the man into a wall before the group fled.

The pursuit reached Bao's shack. Mei tried to hide beneath a plank, while Urist brought down the roof, inadvertently saving the fugitives: the guards assumed that the house had collapsed upon them. Tom was pinned beneath a beam, but Urist pulled him free.

The travellers were left in the rain, without shelter and almost without food. Mei went in search of an inexpensive tavern. It did not serve food to take away, so she had to order five portions and wait at a table while the city outside continued hunting them with paper seals and very real spears.
