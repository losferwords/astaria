# Chthonic Sea

![[Assets/Images/Gilas_Nature.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Chthonic Sea** divides the two great continents of [[Астария|Astaria]]. The realms of [[Кельн|Keln]] lie to the north, the shores of [[Архея|Archaea]] to the south, while at its heart the waters surround the ruined [[Остров Хтон|Chthon Island]]. The world's foremost trade routes, naval passages and pilgrim ways all cross this sea.

Its shores are far from uniform. The western straits become narrow and treacherous in a storm; the central waters lie open to every wind; and the eastern routes thread their way among islands and fickle currents. Knowing the heading is not enough. A captain must reckon with the season, the draught of the vessel, the temper of the crew and whose waters the next dawn will bring.

The ruins of Chthon make the central sea especially dangerous. Remnants of buildings, stone ridges and wreckage lie hidden below the surface, ready to tear open a hull. Heavy seas sometimes lift fragments of ancient workmanship from the depths. Relic hunters are drawn to the island nonetheless, despite every prohibition and every tale of those who never returned.

[[Сирена|Sirens]] inhabit these waters, though most disasters have more ordinary causes: a poor chart, a sudden current or human greed. A merchantman may find itself caught between the warships of two realms, while a nameless cove may prove to be a pirate haven.

To northerners and southerners alike, the Chthonic Sea is both a barrier and a road held in common. It divides their cultures, yet its ports are where people who might otherwise never meet most often stand face to face.
