# Chapter 016 — Night's Coin

*Qadir, 104 NE, winter*

[[Город Самаан|Samaan]] greeted the travellers with the clamour of western [[Кадир|Qadir]]: harbour winds, spices, merchants' cries, copper vessels, road dust and the restrained pride of a city ruled by [[Амира Аль-Азвар|the Eternal Princess]]. After Khalisat, even its walls seemed merciful.

[[Урист Китин|Urist Kitin]] went first to a smithy. The haft of [[Топор Смертоносец|Deathbringer]] was no longer a mere discovery, and the voice within it had become part of his inner strain. In Samaan he received aid from a smith he had once met in [[Гилас|Gilas]]. Amid hammer blows and the heat of the forge, Urist began work upon a new weapon, seeking to unite his craft, the [[Золотая сталь|Golden Steel]] he had won and an object clearly older than his understanding.

Meanwhile, [[Данис ибн Синай|Danis ibn Sinai]] pursued a question that troubled him ever more deeply: where had [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] obtained the Lunaar coins, and what purpose did they serve? To Danis, every mystery demanded examination. To Tom, that examination touched upon his bargain with a goddess. Tension rose swiftly between them.

In Samaan, [[Ошу Мати|Oshu Mati]] met an aunt of the [[Ваку|Vaku]]. The woman gave her potions and reminded her that even far from Vakumara, the blood and memory of a people could find someone in a foreign land. The encounter was gentler than [[Рувамба Мати|Ruwamba's]] tent, but no less unsettling: a Vaku gift was seldom merely medicine.

The quarrel between Tom and Danis ended when Oshu accidentally touched a coin. [[Мерката|Mercate]] spoke within her mind. Waves of darkness rolled outward, and for an instant Samaan itself seemed to retreat before another's night.

The coin was no harmless token of a bargain. It was a door that might open without the bearer's consent. Now Oshu had found something looking through it.
