# Chapter 023 — Nisa Captured

*[[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], 104 NE, spring*

[[Неарх Мелит|Nearch Melit]] explained why the port of [[Город Бахара|Bahara]] had been blockaded. The sea that should have fed the city and bound it to [[Кадир|Qadir]] had become a line of suspicion. Some saw protection in the blockade; others, a noose around the throat of trade.

The travellers sought not a political answer, but a living trail. Nisa had already shown her skill at theft and disappearance. Now they prepared an ambush for her.

She attempted a bold deception, posing as [[Мерката|Mercate]]. To some, such a guise might have been a blasphemous jest; to [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]], a trial of faith; and to the rest, a measure of how far the nereids would go for the Heart of Fire.

The lie could not withstand those who had seen too many genuine traces of the gods. Nisa was captured and questioned. There was little triumph in her imprisonment: the artefact remained lost, and with every hour Bahara grew more confined and dangerous.
