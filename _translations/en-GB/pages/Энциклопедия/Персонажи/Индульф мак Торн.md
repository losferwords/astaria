# Indulf mac Thorn

![[Assets/Images/Indulf_mac_Thorn.jpg]]

## Main text

**Indulf mac Thorn** is the present First [[Горец|Highlander]] and the acknowledged representative of all the [[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]]. He is no king in the customary sense: each chieftain retains their independence, and the decisions of the alliance require the consent of people accustomed to answering only to kin and sky. Indulf's power therefore rests chiefly upon personal authority, the patience to listen and a willingness to bear the consequences of a common decision before anyone else.

Indulf is among the oldest living [[Гойдаир|Goidair]]. For more than forty years he has held the place of First Highlander, outliving allies, rivals and those who once called him untested. Tall, immensely strong and now entirely grey-haired, he gives the impression of a man whom time has not broken, but only made weightier and more composed.

During the [[Рунштоирм|Runstoirm]], Indulf shared the force of the storm with only his twin sister, [[Элеонора мак Торн|Eleanor]]. That rarest of outcomes bound their names for ever. Even after his sister's death, he continues to speak of her as his equal, never merely as the companion of a great chieftain.

Indulf seldom raises his voice in council. He lets an argument burn until everyone has said all they are prepared to defend before the rest, and only then draws the matter to its conclusion. Yet when the alliance itself is threatened, his deliberation vanishes: the old Highlander can decide more swiftly than young warriors and take his place in the foremost line himself.

To the Thunder Clans, Indulf has become living proof that freedom need not mean division. His greatest victory lies not in any single battle, but in the fact that so many proud kindreds are still willing to gather at his call.
