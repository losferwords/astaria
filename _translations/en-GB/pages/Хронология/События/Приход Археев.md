# First Evidence of the Archaeans

> [!timeline] 5025 ChE
> The oldest known traces of [[Археи|Archaean]] civilisation are dated to approximately 5025 ChE: scattered ruins, enigmatic images and objects whose purpose remains disputed. Neither the origin of the Archaeans nor the cause of their disappearance is known.
