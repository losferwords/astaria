# Kaito

![[Assets/Images/Kaito_City.jpg]]

**Kaito** (海都, ‘sea capital’) is the oldest city of the [[Эдзо|Edzo]] and the heart of island [[Амато|Amato]]. It spreads around a sheltered bay on [[Остров Ояширо|Oyashiro]], climbing from fishers' quays to temple gardens and the palace of the Amatsu dynasty. The first settlers raised a shrine to [[Ицунэ|Itsune]] here; their small village slowly became a capital receiving ships from every island in the archipelago.

Wind never falls silent in Kaito, even on clear days. Bells sound beneath curved roofs, paper streamers flutter on temple steps, and fog turns bridges and narrow streets into an endless maze. Fish, tea, lacquerware and blades are traded beside the water, while courtiers and priests conduct conversations in the upper quarters that can change an island's fate.

After sunset, rows of lanterns mark the way to the quays. Among pilgrims and sailors, blindfolded [[Онмёдзи|onmyoji]] sometimes pass by, returning from a hunt or hurrying where someone has heard another will in a familiar voice. Kaito receives foreigners with perfect courtesy, but never reveals all its secrets at once.
