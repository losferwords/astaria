# Storm Bay

![[Assets/Images/Storm_Bay.jpg]]

## Main text

**Storm Bay** lies in western [[Кельн|Keln]]. It belongs to the [[Хтоническое море|Chthonic Sea]] and washes the eastern shore of the [[Полуостров Таллас|Tallas Peninsula]]. At its northern end stands [[Город Солаис|Solais]], capital of the [[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]].

The name is no poetic exaggeration. Here the wind may turn within minutes, while waves collide as though the sea were quarrelling with itself. Local captains know the brief intervals between squalls and make use of them where foreign vessels would sooner wait.

Rune-storms haunt the bay: tempests in which lightning behaves like a directed will. They can shatter a ship, yet sometimes leave a trace of power upon those who survive—a power associated with the [[Громовик|Thunderers]]. Mariners therefore greet every storm with fear and reverence in equal measure.

The coast is cut by narrow coves where a small vessel may weather the gale, provided its captain knows the entrance beforehand. Some refuges belong to individual clans and are marked by carved stones. Any traveller in distress may use them, but to depart without leaving food, rope or aid for the next voyager is held dishonourable. Wreckage lies between these havens, and the sea will sometimes return a lost cargo to the shore years after the ship went down.
