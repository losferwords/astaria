# Chapter 089 — The Road to Vanpur

*[[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]], 106 NE, summer*

[[Велимир Сажин|Velimir]] was the next to seek the gods. He borrowed [[Гиперион I|Hyperion's]] artefact from [[Урист Китин|Urist]] in case he had to look upon [[Велисса|the earth goddess]], and the conversation passed without disaster. Velissa confirmed that the black fragment bore a trace of her power, though its origin and connection to the legendary [[Чёрное Сердце|Black Heart]] remained unclear.

The conversation had an unexpected consequence: the sealed shard began to sprout as the goddess's power filled it. After so many revelations, the group decided to celebrate the people's “rescue” from death, not least because the villagers already expected festivities.

Gilja scattered dancing powder. The others returned to the tavern to eat [[Шанкар Марджари|Shankar Marjari]] out of house and home while he slept and brewed the legendary Avgar Ur-Blyat from whatever they could find.

A merry [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] noticed something immense falling from the sky nearby. It was a black dragon veined with red. Blood poured from its jaws, and Gilja, in true Raksha fashion, held the Raktapatra beneath the stream.

The blood brought visions. He saw a battle between celestial and aquatic dragons, with [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Lang-An]] amongst them. Then he saw this black dragon flying south with two [[Сердце Огня|Hearts of Fire]] in its claws. A third vision showed a young Qadian touching an immense clock of time with a black casket, while a black-clad figure ran from a cave and rose into the sky. In the fourth, Urist, Mei and Tom killed the dragon.

Gilja tried to drive the dragon away, was struck by its paw, crashed into a tree and fell asleep. His friends found him in the morning and decided it was time to leave the village.

On the road they encountered Meilong in a trance. Mei forced her way into his mind and understood that he had joined the ocean of thought belonging to the [[Профитис|Profitides]] and was telling them everything about his life. The dragon asked the travellers to keep their distance while he could not control the voices. Wintra's consorts still pursued him.

Farther on, they met [[Мерави Марджари|Meravi]]. [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari]] had appeared to her in person and given her the same charge: find the renegade [[Ракша|Rakshas]]. Gilja welcomed an ally, agreed routes through the cities and gave her two of the goddess's raktakatars to lend authority to her words. [[Город Ванпур|Vanpur]] lay ahead.
