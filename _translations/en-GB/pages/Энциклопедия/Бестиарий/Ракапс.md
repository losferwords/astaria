# Rakaps

![[Assets/Images/Rakaps.jpg]]

## Main text

**Rakaps** are tiny members of the cat family that dwell chiefly underground. They live in large families, each with a chamber of its own, while their tunnel systems also contain communal rooms for meals, stores and company. These small cats eat plant matter, especially vegetables, and display an intelligence remarkable for creatures of their size.

Rakaps are found across almost the whole of [[Астария|Astaria]]. Their many distinct varieties are adapted to environments ranging from the harsh deserts of [[Архея|Archaea]] to the snowy expanses of [[Кельн|Keln]].

There is a close bond between Rakaps and their larger kindred, the [[Менада|maenads]], though no reliable account of their dealings has yet been assembled.
