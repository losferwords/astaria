# Danis ibn Sinai

![[Assets/Images/Danis_ibn_Sinai.jpg]]

## Main text

**Danis ibn Sinai** is a travelling scholar and physician from [[Кадир|Qadir]]. He has devoted much of his life to alchemy, human physiology and the study of [[Кристалл Души|Soul Crystals]]. He is fascinated by the boundary between the body and the living force contained within it: why some wounds heal, why others change a person for ever, and how divine nature allows certain beings to endure for centuries.

Danis can be a patient healer and an exceedingly uncomfortable conversationalist. He asks questions that priests consider blasphemous and rulers dangerous, yet he seldom pursues knowledge for the sake of glory. In his view, every theory ought one day to save a life or explain suffering that was previously accepted as inevitable.

In 102 NE, the alchemist arrived in [[Гилас|Gilas]] under the name Philoctetes in order to observe [[Гиперион I|Hyperion I]]. The undying king of the Ellians offered an extraordinarily rare opportunity to study a being regarded at once as man, sovereign and god. Danis spent a year assembling his observations while carefully concealing the true purpose of his work.

During the harvest festival of 103 NE, an uprising by [[Жаждущие|the Thirsty]] resulted in the destruction of his notes. Danis barely escaped Gilas and fled to [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]]. The loss of many years' work did not persuade him to abandon his research, but it made him more cautious: he now keeps his most important conclusions in more than one place and does not trust a person merely because they promise to protect learning.

In Tiza, Danis remains a foreigner with valuable knowledge and a suspiciously keen interest in immortality. To another researcher, he may become a mentor; to the sick, a final hope; and to the keeper of a forbidden truth, a man who has come much too close to asking the right question.
