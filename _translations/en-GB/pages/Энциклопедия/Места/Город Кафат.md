# Kafat

![[Assets/Images/Kafat_City.jpg]]

## Main text

**Kafat** is the capital of eastern [[Кадир|Qadir]], standing upon the shore of [[Озеро Альсалим|Lake Alsalim]].

It is a great city and home to the principal school of the [[Мститель|Way of the Desert]], founded by [[Умар ибн Ла-Ахад|Umar ibn La-Ahad]]. Here the discipline of the Avengers exists beside lake trade, while the prince's court serves at once as school, laboratory and centre of government for eastern Qadir.

One of Kafat's hardest trials was a long isolation. [[Аль-Тахим|Al-Tahims]] blocked the overland caravans through [[Пустыня Халисат|Khalisat]], while the sea road to [[Город Бахара|Bakhara]] was closed. The city faced hunger, a shortage of water and overcrowded infirmaries; the crisis showed how deeply even a powerful school depends on open roads and the trust of its neighbours.

Kafat's architecture is adapted to heat and sand. Shaded passages join houses, carved screens protect the windows, and entrances to courtyards are arranged so that the wind leaves most of its dust upon the threshold. On their training grounds, Avengers raise curtains of sand and learn to read an opponent's movement through changes in the air.

After the isolation, the city watches its stores and caravan routes with particular strictness. At the gates, scribes record not only arriving goods, but also the state of wells, signs of monsters and every change to a route. For an investigator, these records may be worth more than the court archive: they show the desert as guides see it each day.
