# Astaria Project Context

`Астария` is Maksim's long-running mythological fantasy world: ancient civilizations, peoples, gods, Imitei, conflicts, legends and sagas. The canonical, self-contained Obsidian vault supports private tabletop play and selective bilingual Quartz publication. The central campaign is `Ветер Перемен`, using Fate Core.

## Where material belongs

- `Энциклопедия/` contains canonical lore. Its topical folders include `Места`, `Страны`, `Народы`, `Боги`, `Имитеи`, `Персонажи`, `Организации`, `Знания`, `Предметы`, `Бестиарий`, `Флора`, `Кухня` and `События`.
- `Энциклопедия/Литература/` contains canonical prose, one chapter per file. Sagas include `Ветер Перемен`, `Зов Бури` and `Пока Боги Спят`.
- `Энциклопедия/Секреты/` holds GM-only truths; `Хронология/` holds dated records; `Карты/` holds the interactive map and marker definitions.
- `Assets/Images/` and `Assets/Maps/` hold the actual illustrations and map layers. Article/image conventions belong to [the vault skill](../skills/astaria-obsidian-vault/SKILL.md).
- `Идеи/` is the private workbench, governed by `.ai/AGENTS.md`; its drafts do not become canon merely by being saved.

## Public lore boundaries

Ordinary readers encounter mythological fantasy. Humans are one of the world's ordinary humanoid peoples; the Archeans are a mysterious, highly developed vanished civilization of unknown origin.

Do not publicly state or imply that the Archeans came from another world, were Earth colonists, created gods in laboratories, or founded the Chthonid civilization from five hundred human settlers. Keep those truths in `Энциклопедия/Секреты/`. Where public chronology touches secret history, use the earliest known evidence, an archaeological interpretation, chronicle or in-world legend; preserve uncertainty.

GM mechanics and secrets stay private. A public literary text may reveal what the author explicitly approved for that text; that does not authorize exposing other secrets or expanding ordinary public profiles. The personal Losfy/Losfer/Misty boundary is maintained in [its context](losfy-losfer-misty.md).
