# Chapter 084 — The Dead Harvest

*[[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]], 106 NE, spring*

[[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] decided to revisit the old days and went to the village temple in search of his mentor, Ajay Verma, who had taught him the calling of a [[Ракша|Raksha]]. An attendant offered him food and prayer in the name of [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari]], mentioning almost in passing that nagas had killed Gilja's family.

Gilja had never been close to his parents: they neither understood nor supported his path as a Raksha. Yet death remained death, and the news weighed heavily upon him. He found Ajay at the market. Their reunion was warm, filled with embraces, memories and old words between pupil and teacher. Ajay said the nagas had become cleverer and bolder and had even begun using money.

The following morning, as custom required, the innkeeper offered Gilja a glass of red root. Gilja prepared to speak with his goddess. Shubhankari appeared swiftly and was displeased: there had been no harvest in the night, and no blood had reached her. More than that, according to the goddess, Gilja's teacher—the man who had supposedly conducted the rite—had long been dead.

Shubhankari was troubled by the nagas and suspected that someone with feathers was helping them. Before departing, she bestowed power upon Gilja so he might better fulfil his duty.

Gilja persuaded his friends to search for the nagas in the marshes. They soon found a cave serving as the creatures' refuge. A simple diversion deceived the two guards, and the group entered.

In one passage, they nearly crossed a bridge made from a fishing net, but [[Урист Китин|Urist's]] hammer brought the structure down. The nagas left in search of a replacement bridge, abandoning an arrow bearing one of [[Мерката|Mercate's]] feathers in the support. The travellers raised the bridge by their own strength and, using Urist as an anchor, crossed to the other side. A new cavern opened before them.
