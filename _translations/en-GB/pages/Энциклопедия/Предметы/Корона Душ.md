# Crown of Souls

![[Assets/Images/Souls_Crown.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Crown of Souls** is a strange artefact, probably of [[Археи|Archaean]] origin, occasionally recovered from abandoned temples and crypts. Its purpose is entirely unknown, though some scholars suppose that it once served in the rituals of the ancient civilisation.

Descriptions liken it to a crown with a circular base, yet no living person could wear it unless the three long black spikes were turned downwards. Surviving examples are commonly damaged, with one or more spikes broken away. A rectangular opening, roughly the size of a palm, pierces the underside of the base.

The name most likely began as a jest: only the spirits of the dead, it was said, could ever place such a crown upon their heads.
