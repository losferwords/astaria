# Aristea Kronos

![[Assets/Images/Aristea_Kronos.jpg]]

## Main text

**Aristea Kronos** is a pirate whose name is known in ports throughout the [[Хтоническое море|Chthonic Sea]]. She commands the *Proteus*, a swift vessel beneath black-and-crimson sails, and prefers to strike where merchant ships feel secure under the protection of treaties and flags.

Aristea despises maritime law and takes particular pleasure in breaking the rules imposed by [[Талассия|Thalassia]]. She does not consider herself a common thief. Before an attack, she may invite a captain to gamble for part of the cargo, exchange prisoners for an ancient manuscript or release a crew willing to surrender a Thalassian official. A refusal, however, seldom earns mercy.

Aristea has dark-blond hair, brown skin and a damaged left eye. She makes no effort to hide the scar and regards each new acquaintance as though judging in advance how dearly they might sell their convictions. Aboard the *Proteus*, the captain maintains harsh discipline: spoils are divided according to shares declared beforehand, and anyone who steals from the crew is thrown overboard regardless of former service.

She says almost nothing of her past. Sailors who have served aboard her ship claim that she sympathises with the ancient [[Хтониды|Chthonids]] and collects anything connected to the forbidden teachings of [[Тиресий|Tiresias]], the god of wisdom. Some believe her a fanatic dreaming of restoring the lost civilisation; others, a hunter of knowledge who merely finds such a reputation useful.

Thalassian captains place bounties upon the *Proteus*, but rarely pursue it for long. Aristea knows the straits intimately, can alter her ship's appearance and leaves false intelligence in every harbour. Hunting her has consequently become a maritime trade of its own—one in which the pursuer may discover that the pirate has been guiding them along a course of her choosing from the very beginning.
