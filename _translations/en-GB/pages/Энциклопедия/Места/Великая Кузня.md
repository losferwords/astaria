# Great Forge

![[Assets/Images/Great_Forge.jpg]]

The **Great Forge** is a mysterious structure raised by the Smith-God [[Хангор|Khangor]] near [[Город Даркхан|Darkhan]]. More than seven hundred years, uncounted materials and builders' lives went into this monumental creation. It became a cornerstone and point of origin for the [[Авгарцы|Avgars]], their realm of [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]] and the [[Мектиг|Mektigs]], who helped the god build it in return for new powers.

Around 30 NE, Khangor completed the Forge and released all who had worked upon it to their families. He then closed it to unrestricted entry and warned that the finished structure remained mortally dangerous. Yet the Smith-God did not abandon the Avgars entirely. The bravest and worthiest may attempt to find a path through the sealed gates, survive his trials and earn the god's support.

To some, this restriction is bitter proof that Khangor used his people and withdrew from them once their labour was complete. Others believe that the Iron God is protecting the Avgars from danger and admits only those capable of accepting it knowingly. The few who returned—among them [[Айсан Баят|Aysan Bayat]] and [[Урист Китин|Urist Kitin]]—claim to have met Khangor, but reveal neither the road to him nor what they saw in the depths. During violent storms, a rumble sometimes rises from the rock that resembles neither a hammer blow nor thunder.

From outside, the structure resembles an immense fortress cut into the rock, its metal gates covered in intricate designs. Anyone may come to admire it, but the gates will not open for an ordinary traveller. To a future [[Мектиг|Mektig]], the Great Forge itself becomes the last and hardest trial, while Khangor alone decides who is worthy to enter.
