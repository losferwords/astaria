# Rufu

## Main text

The Dead God, or God of the Dead, is the sole deity worshipped by the [[Ваку|Vaku]]. The tribes of [[Вакумара|Vakumara]] credit him with profound knowledge of anatomy, alchemy and medicine, as well as the power to commune with the world of the dead. Yet all of this belongs to the past: the god himself is dead, and his remains lie within a necropolis near the village of [[Деревня Намибуа|Namibua]].

Many legends tell how Rufu, while still a living god, taught the people of south-western [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]]. Life along the border of the blazing [[Пепельные земли|Ashlands]] was harsh, and Rufu helped mortals survive in return for the promise that their bodies would pass to him after death. In time, tribes arose that worshipped the God of Death alone. Neither the pharaoh nor [[Аст|Ast]] would tolerate the new faith, and the [[Светоносный|Lightbringers]] drove it far to the west along the [[Река Мвиди|Mvidi River]].

Rufu commanded his new people to submit and led them west without resistance. For many years the tribes lived in accord with nature and their ancestral spirits, mastering rites, potions and healing salves. Everything changed in 854 ChE, when the [[Хтониды|Chthonids]] arrived upon the continent under [[Гиперион I|Hyperion I]]. The settlers sought a foothold in Archaea and intended to establish the republic of [[Гилас|Gilas]]; the Vaku tribes were the first obstacle in their path. Rufu defended his people for several years, but fell near the source of the [[Река Неда|Neda]] in 849 ChE. The heavily armed [[Эллийцы|Ellians]] then advanced swiftly south and drove back the surviving defenders.

The death of their beloved god dealt the Vaku a wound from which they have never recovered. The [[Шаман|Shamans]], however, drew upon all their accumulated ritual knowledge to find Rufu's body and carry it far into the south. An immense temple complex rose in his honour beside Namibua. To the bitter disappointment of the conquerors, grief only made the Vaku fiercer and deadlier, uniting them around a new symbol: the Dead God.

Rufu's mummy is guarded by the highest Shamans of Vakumara. They maintain that, even in death, the god instructs the initiated and shares the knowledge of the world beyond. To the Vaku, his silence is not absence. Pilgrims come to the necropolis for counsel, healing and the assurance that their fallen patron still remembers his people.

Vaku chronicles portray Rufu as an upright black jackal whose eyes burn with green flame. Some give him leathery or skeletal wings and spines along his back. In his hands he bears the enormous bone scythe he wielded in battle against the Ellians.
