# Lake Sharadi

![[Assets/Images/Sharadi_Lake.jpg]]

## Main text

**Sharadi** is the south-western Garden of [[Кадир|Qadir]] and the source of the [[Река Макаба|Makaba River]]. Water gathers here from springs and rare rain, then flows north, joining the lake to [[Озеро Джамсар|Jamsar]] and [[Залив Фиальсахра|Fialsakhra Bay]].

Sharadi's shores are famed for gardens of fragrant flowers, pomegranates and citrus trees. At [[Город Сафрум|Safrum]], petals are turned into oils, incense and dyes, then sent to Samaan and onward by sea. In the flowering season, the wind carries their scent far into the desert, and caravaners recognise the nearness of water before they see greenery.

The lake is called the Garden of Memory. Families plant a tree to mark an important event and tie a ribbon bearing a person's name to its lowest branch. If the tree dies, they do not replace it at once; first they try to understand what changed in the soil and water. In this way, remembrance becomes a duty of care rather than merely a beautiful sign.

The Makaba makes Sharadi an important trading hub. Fruit, oil and craftspeople's wares travel by water, while sea salt and foreign goods return. Control of the dams brings considerable influence, so the keepers are chosen from several families to prevent any one of them from ruling the entire route.

Abandoned pavilions and dried fountains stand in the distant gardens. Some associate them with former rulers of western Qadir; others say they are older than the kingdom itself and were only later incorporated into the Qadian Gardens.
