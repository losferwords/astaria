# Shaman

## Main text

Those who enter the path of the Shaman become interpreters of the will of [[Руфу|Rufu]], the Dead God, and keepers of the most ancient mysteries of the [[Ваку|Vaku]]. Night does not frighten them, for it is filled with the voices of their ancestors; death is no ending, but a gate leading towards a source of power. A Shaman is a sombre sage whose words may heal or curse and whose rites reach beyond the world of the living. Drums stretched with human skin, necklaces of bone and the flames of sacrificial fires fill those ceremonies with the presence of the ancestral realm.

Becoming a Shaman is a passage through pain and initiation. A future servant of Rufu first spends years among the priests, learning the arts of potions, curses and healing infusions. The decisive step is the choosing of the Sacred Skull. Every Shaman must carry into eternity the memory of the person dearest to them. The skull of a parent, beloved or sibling becomes the initiate's principal guide to the world of the dead. Only then may the young priest bear the name of Shaman and approach Rufu's crypt, where the god's body preserves not only memory, but power.

To be a Shaman is to stand for ever upon the border of two worlds. The Vaku fear and honour them, seeking a curse for an enemy or healing for a child. Upon the battlefield, their presence makes even resolute warriors tremble. Each stroke of a bone knife is more than a wound: it is a mark opening the way towards the realm of the dead. When a Shaman raises the ritual skull overhead, the living feel the weight of numberless dead standing beyond sight.

## Training

Shamans command the arts of death and ritual. They can lay curses, invite possession, subjugate an enemy's mind with visions or drain strength through touch. Their lore includes secret signs tattooed into the skin with ash and blood, and mystical masks that conceal the face during sacred ceremonies. Yet a Shaman is also a healer. Through ancestral power, sickness may be drawn from a body and sealed within a doll or totem, or treated with a bitter draught of marsh herbs.

## Attributes

In battle, a Shaman relies not upon physical strength but upon the power of ritual. Their faithful weapon is a knife of bone, used to make sacred wounds and inscribe signs upon the bodies of enemy and ally alike. The second and foremost attribute is the ritual skull, through which a Shaman calls upon the strength of the departed, awakens memory and opens a path towards the secrets beyond death. The closer its former owner stood to the Shaman in life, the stronger the resulting magic is believed to be.
