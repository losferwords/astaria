# Chapter 043 — The Lantern above the Watchtower

*[[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], 104 NE, autumn*

[[Урист Китин|Urist]] began searching for whoever had tried to kill him in the camp and [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] in the village. He first confronted [[Сатор Саг|Sator]], but received no direct answers. His questions uncovered the truth: Sator was not a Kitin, but a Sag, cousin to [[Батар Саг|Batar Sag]], and might have claimed the khanate were it not for Urist.

Urist offered to settle the matter in a duel with Sator's finest warrior, but Sator refused another's aid. The fight ended in his death. Urist ordered him buried upon the mountain summit beside the mines and appointed a fellow villager, Batyr Bayat, commander of the arbat.

[[Мерката|Mercate]] awoke and thanked [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] for rescuing her. She gave him a lunar necklace capable of changing the moon's phases and promised to fulfil any wish. Tom did not know what to ask. The goddess said that without her scythe she could not regain the power she had lost, and the loss of that object might have catastrophic consequences.

[[Рон Эллиот|Ron Elliot]] ran up to ask for a blessing. Mercate flew away in search of [[Кирида Ариса|Kirida Arisa]], the newly made [[Тень|Shadow]] who was draining her strength.

[[Нуа Си|Nuwa Xi]] emerged from a house and announced that she had found the scythe in the middle of a field while searching for her brother, but had carried it to the [[Храм Дракона|Temple of the Dragon]] as the completion of her pilgrimage. She then saw [[Мэйлун|Meilong]] and began worshipping him as the sea dragon depicted in the temple. Later, she trained with [[Мэй Ву|Mei]], demonstrating a miniature fire tornado that could be detonated.

That evening, Mei noticed Dju lights in the mountains: torches and festive spherical lanterns. The procession had not come to fight. [[Ли Сан|Li San]] helped persuade Urist not to attack first, promising him a descendant if he restrained his army.

While Urist was distracted, Mei raced to meet the procession. On the way she disabled a group of nomads who might have encountered the Dju and ruined everything. From the guards she learnt that the San family was travelling to Li San's wedding. For safety, one guard asked for a signal: hang a lantern upon the watchtower if all was peaceful in the village, or cast it into the river if the procession should turn back.

[[Бао Си|Bao Xi]], still searching for his sister, travelled among them. He helped Mei defeat a band of nomads and hurled a ball of fire into their midst.

The village heard the explosion and became alarmed. Tom climbed the watchtower, ready to defend it, and saw smoke and heard a rumble from the mines, accompanied by the same corpse-stench he had encountered underground. Mei reached Ulehey in time, confirmed that no attack upon the Dju would occur, and raised the lantern above the tower.

The procession continued. Yet something else was already coming from the mines.
