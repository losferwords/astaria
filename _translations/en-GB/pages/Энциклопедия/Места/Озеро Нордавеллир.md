# Lake Nordavellir

![[Assets/Images/Nordavellir_Lake.jpg]]

## Main text

**Lake Nordavellir** is the largest lake in [[Астария|Astaria]], in central [[Кельн|Keln]]. It is ice-bound for most of the year; even in summer, only shoreline strips and isolated dark leads thaw. At its centre rise the halls of [[Винтра|Wintra]]—[[Храм Винтерхейм|Winterheim]].

Here, in 1637 ChE, an expedition of the future [[Вактары|Vaktars]] found the body of a she-dragon beneath the ice. For two years, people worked their way to the bottom and in 1635 ChE raised her to the shore. This event became the foundation of [[Вактар-Йорден|Vaktar-Jorden]] and changed the fate of the descendants of the [[Надаир|Nadair]].

Pilgrims walk onto the ice only with experienced guides. Its thickness is deceptive: cracks, young ice and leads opened by moving water hide beneath snow. Stone cairns and poles mark the route, but a strong blizzard can erase them in minutes.

Fishing, hunting and Winterheim's attendants sustain settlements along the shore. In winter they set nets beneath the ice; in summer they take boats into thawed bays. The water is extraordinarily cold and a person soon loses strength, so every camp keeps ropes, dry clothes and heated stones for those rescued from it.

Nordavellir is considered a place of truth. Before a grave oath, a Vaktar may spend a night on its shore, hearing the ice crack and considering the words they intend to speak. The lake makes one truth especially clear: its surface may seem still, but beneath it movement never ceases.

To a foreigner, it appears desolate, yet its ice holds roads, places of remembrance and traces of an ancient battle. Leaving a known path is more than a risk to life—it may lead where Wintra's attendants admit not even their own warriors.
