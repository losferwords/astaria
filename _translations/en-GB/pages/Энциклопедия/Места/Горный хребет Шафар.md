# Shafar Mountains

![[Assets/Images/Shang_Feng_Mountains.jpg]]

The **Shafar Mountains** are a long eastern spur of the [[Горный хребет Спина Археи|Spine of Archaea]], running from the [[Хтоническое море|Chthonic Sea]] towards the southern chain. They form a natural wall between [[Кадир|Qadir]], [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]] and [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]].

Western slopes are dry and stony above [[Пустыня Халисат|Khalisat]], while eastern gorges receive moisture and grow conifers and bamboo. A traveller may leave a dusty track in the morning and reach cold rain among misty trees by evening.

Shafar yields ore and fine building stone. Neighbouring states have disputed its deposits for centuries, and many old mines passed to new masters after the Avgar arrival. Others stand abandoned because of collapse, poison air or creatures from the depths.

Several fortified passes carry the main roads, foremost [[Гарнизон Пасть Дракона|Dragon's Maw]]. Away from them are shepherd paths, narrow, winter-shifting and often passing through vermin territory. To Qadians Shafar is the desert's eastern edge, to Dju a lost shield, to Avgars a harsh ancestral conquest; one peak may therefore bear three names.
