# Dawn of Chthonid Civilisation

> [!timeline] 4950 ChE
> Ancient traditions place the revelation of [[Тиресий|Tiresias]] to the first [[Хтониды|Chthonid]] communities on [[Остров Хтон|Chthon]] in this age. His teachings are held to mark the beginning of Chthonid civilisation, though later legend can no longer be separated from history. [[Город Орион|Orion]] was built afterwards.
