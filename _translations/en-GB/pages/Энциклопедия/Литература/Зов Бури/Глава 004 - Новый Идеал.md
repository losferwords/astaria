# Chapter 004 — The New Paragon

*The New Paragon*

The raft touches the shore of [[Архея|Archaea]]. Solid ground lies beneath [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth]] and [[Кассиопея Атанатос|Cassia]] once more; the sea and the ruins of [[Остров Хтон|Chthon]] recede behind them. The road to the southern swordsmiths seems open. But [[Эллийцы|Ellians]] meet the travellers on this foreign shore, and soon the pair are being taken under guard to [[Город Аргос|Argos]].

Their captors quickly recognise what has fallen into their hands. A Highlander and a blind prophetess are [[Имитей|Imithei]], bearers of a power for which the finest warriors of [[Гилас|Gilas]] will risk a mortal duel. Kenneth and Cassia are sent to the border city of [[Город Ахей|Achaeus]], where an arena rises above the city's noise.

They are well fed and allowed to read, bathe and train. Kenneth can walk, feel the sun upon his face and grow familiar again with the weight of a weapon in his hand. Warders watch every step. Here, a captive is prepared for a battle whose victory must appear honourable before people and gods. Half a year passes as he waits for someone to name him their opponent.

In spring 99 NE, that person is [[Персефона Раптис|Persephone Raptis]].

Years of service lie behind her. Now she earns the right to enter the [[Танатомахия|Thanatomachia]], the final trial before the rank of [[Идеал|Paragon]]. Long training teaches her to wield spear and shield, endure an assault and stand firm where fear makes others retreat. She faces a Highlander who takes the power of lightning into himself. Persephone chooses him. She wants a victory that leaves no doubt of her right to lead the warriors of Gilas.

Kenneth, too, has a reason to fight to the end. If he kills the challenger, the law restores his freedom. Beyond the arena lie Cassia, an unfinished journey and the sword for which he leaves home. To see any of it again, he must survive this day.

Sand beneath his feet. Sun above his head. Open sky on every side, and a narrow circle he cannot leave of his own will. Before Kenneth, a spearpoint rises.

The New Paragon carries Persephone's voice. Impatiently she calls her opponent into battle, demanding his full strength, as though she wants to feel every promise of the storm for herself. Her long wait finally takes shape: the distance between them, the weight of the shield, the first step towards a blow. Today she intends to leave the arena a new Paragon.

Kenneth attacks, and victory proves hard to win. Persephone meets his assault with her shield, retreats, changes her angle and brings the spear to bear again. He slips out of its path and answers with his sword. Sand shifts beneath their feet; each misplaced step exposes the body to a point. They search for weakness in one another as the surrounding noise becomes a single, sustained roar.

Persephone endures. Her movements grow more precise as her opponent begins to lose control of the fight. She catches the moment when Kenneth's strength can no longer keep pace with her spear and turns his mistake against him. Pain breaks the Highlander's rhythm. Now she presses him, allowing no respite.

Her voice insists ever more fiercely that he rise and fight again. Even the repeated lines of the song seem to prolong the duel: another blow, another attempt to stand, another promise to claim his power. For Persephone, victory is almost within reach. In the chorus she names herself the new Paragon, though the rite is not yet complete.

At last, Kenneth falls upon the sand.

Persephone stands above him with her spear. The final blow will bring her the power of his [[Кристалл Души|Soul Crystal]] and fulfil everything she strives for. Between her and the new rank remains the life of the man at her feet.

Then an alarm rings across the arena.

[[Вторжение Амон-Астата в город Ахей|Achaeus is under attack]] by rebels from [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]]. Soldiers whose eyes are fixed on the duel must return to the walls immediately. Persephone is forced to lower her spear. Her fury turns towards the enemy who breaks into this day and snatches away the initiation she has almost won.

Persephone takes command of the defence. She must lead her soldiers to the walls without the power of a Paragon. Kenneth is carried from the arena wounded, but alive.
