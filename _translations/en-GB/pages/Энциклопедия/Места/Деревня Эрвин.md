# Erwin

![[Assets/Images/Ervin_City.jpg]]

## Main text

**Erwin** is a mountain village on the slopes of the [[Горный хребет Грань Тьмы|Edge of Darkness]], grown around the road to the [[Перевал Путь Забытых|Pass of the Forgotten]]. Its houses have stone foundations sunk into the slope, roofs weighed down with heavy beams, and narrow streets deliberately curved to weaken the winter wind.

The inhabitants raise sheep, dry meat and herbs, and receive travellers before the difficult crossing. Local guides can recognise an approaching landslide by the sound of stone and know shelters invisible from the road. Their payment is usually modest, but anyone who breaks their word in the mountains is unlikely to find a second guide here.

Erwin is known as the birthplace of [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth the Stormcaller]] and his mother, [[Лисандра мак Рейн|Lisandra mac Rayne]]. Their fame is a source of pride, yet elders prefer to speak not of great battles but of the family's life before it left the village. Such tales remind a foreigner that Thunder Clan legends begin not with lightning, but with a small house, a mother's care and a road one must someday take.
