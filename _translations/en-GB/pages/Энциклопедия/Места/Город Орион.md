# Orion

![[Assets/Images/Orion_City.jpg]]

## Main text

**Orion** was the ancient capital of [[Хтониды|Chthonid]] civilisation and one of [[Астария|Astaria's]] oldest known cities, apart from the mysterious ruins of the [[Археи|Archaeans]]. Later chronicles say construction began after [[Тиресий|Tiresias]] appeared to the first Chthonid communities in 4950 ChE, though no exact foundation year survives. For millennia Orion was the political, religious and scholarly heart of [[Остров Хтон|Chthon Island]].

Surviving images show broad circles built around a temple and palace centre. High terraces served as observatories, canals joined districts to the sea, and public gardens stood beside archives and schools. Chronicles describe unsupported bridges and halls where stone reflected light even at deepest night, though fact can no longer be wholly separated from legend.

The city held immense collections of maps, laws and observations of the stars. Here the [[Профитис|Profitises]] attained exceptional power, while Chthonid learning reached a height later peoples both admired and feared.

During the [[Падение Хтона|Fall of Chthon]], Orion was destroyed and sank with the island. The sea above it is too dangerous for an ordinary expedition, and stories of towers visible in the depths cannot be verified. Fragments bearing Orion script sometimes wash onto distant shores, but none can replace the lost archives.

To modern Chthonids, Orion is a painful symbol: proof of ancestral greatness and the place where knowledge ceased to serve life. Some dream of seeing the drowned capital; others believe certain doors should remain beneath the water forever.
