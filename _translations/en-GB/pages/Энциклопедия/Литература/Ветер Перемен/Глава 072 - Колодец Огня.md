# Chapter 072 — The Well of Fire

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 105 NE, autumn*

[[Сунь Ляньши|Sun Lianshi]] reluctantly showed the travellers the Hall of Trysts. A secret passage from her chambers led to a circular room with a shaft so deep that its bottom could not be seen. Here [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Lang-An]] could speak with his wife, beyond the palace floors and the gaze of others.

The travellers procured two hundred metres of jute rope. [[Урист Китин|Urist]] secured it with his hammer, and [[Мэй Ву|Mei]] descended first. Near the bottom, the rope caught fire from the heat of the Well of Fire. She could neither jump nor swing herself across. They had to climb back and seek more rope from the tailor Liao Shen, who gave them the rope from his own room.

This time Mei reached the Halls. [[Кион-Чи|Qiong-Qi]] spoke to her through one of the Avatars. [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] and Urist descended after her. The Avatars explained that Mercate's Scythe must be drawn from the Dragon's body, but Qiong-Qi could not be allowed access to the Well of Fire. Mei would have to dive, for fire had already failed to harm her many times.

Within the sea of Qi, Mei found the mutilated Lang-An and pulled out the Scythe. The Dragon awoke and helped her escape the flames. Then a black arrow struck the god's eye, and [[Артур Бомейн|Arthur Beaumain]] appeared. He seized the Scythe and wounded both Mei and Tom. Urist held back the Avatars and drew the arrow from the Dragon's eye.

Lang-An began reclaiming control over his servants. The gates flew open, and [[Бао Си|Bao Xi]] and the monks joined the battle. Yet Tom suffered terrible wounds from the Scythe and died.

When Qiong-Qi lost his hold and departed with a curse, Arthur laid down his weapon. Lang-An thanked the travellers, but said only Tom's goddess could save him, and only if they carried his body beyond the temple. Mercate appeared at the gates. She reclaimed the Scythe and offered a single path: Tom could become a [[Тень|Shadow]], surrendering the right to disobey his goddess's command. Tom agreed.

He forsook the light and embraced the darkness. He was now one of the Forgotten, a Shadow in the service of the Goddess of Night.
