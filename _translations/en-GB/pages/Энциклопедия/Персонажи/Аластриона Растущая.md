# Alastriona the Growing

![[Assets/Images/Alastriona_The_Growth.jpg]]

## Main text

**Alastriona the Growing** is the High [[Друид|Druidess]] and present ruler of [[Иомар|Iomar]]. Small of stature, composed and attentive to whoever speaks with her, she bears little resemblance to the stern sovereigns of foreign legend. Her authority reveals itself differently: Alastriona can silence an argument with one precise question, and seldom makes a decision before hearing those who must live with its consequences.

She attained high office at an exceptionally young age. At fourteen, Alastriona became a junior priestess of [[Церунна|Cerunna]] and earned respect not through flawless obedience, but through her rare gift for perceiving the bond between human hardship and the condition of the land. At eighteen she passed her initiation and entered the [[Круг Друидов|Druid Circle]]. Four years later, the Circle elected her High Druidess.

The elder guardians did not readily accept so young a ruler. Some saw in her the will of the Mother Goddess; others feared that inexperience would destroy the delicate balance between [[Город Муир|Muir]], the meadows and the woodland communities. Alastriona answered not by struggling for authority, but by travelling the breadth of her country: listening to fishers after a flood, farmers after a failed harvest and Druids demanding that exhausted land be closed to further use.

Her epithet, *the Growing*, refers not to her modest height, but to the swiftness with which she grew in spirit. Alastriona herself understands it as a promise never to become still: a tree that ceases to grow begins to die, and a ruler must never regard the wisdom already won as final.

In Muir, she remains a mediator between the south, open to the wider world, and the cautious woodland north. Her decisions seldom please everyone at once, yet even her opponents concede that Alastriona asks no sacrifice of others that she would not be willing to make herself.
