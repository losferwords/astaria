# Sunset Sea

![[Assets/Images/Sunset_Sea.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Sunset Sea** is the boundless expanse of water west of the known lands of [[Астария|Astaria]]. No reliable chart records a single confirmed island within it. The coast gradually vanishes beyond the horizon, the birds turn back, and nothing remains ahead but water, heavy clouds and a sunset that seems to settle directly upon the sea each evening.

Even the [[Талассийцы|Thalassians]] rarely venture far to the west. Long opposing currents run here, and storms may drive a ship away from familiar shores for days at a time. Supplies cannot readily be replenished in open water, while the absence of islands leaves a crew nowhere to shelter from foul weather or repair a damaged hull.

Coastal captains know a handful of safe seasonal courses, but do not mark them upon charts made for common use. They read the sea by the colour of the waves and the height of the clouds. At the first hint of unnatural stillness, they shorten sail long before the wind arrives. The singing of [[Сирена|Sirens]] is sometimes heard beside a ship, though in the fog sailors cannot always distinguish a stranger's voice from the shriek of the rigging.

The Sunset Sea remains the last great frontier of Thalassian seafaring. Some believe that nothing lies beyond it; others dream of finding a new continent; still others insist that any ship sailing too far simply loses the road home. There are enough survivors of the western storms to keep the fear alive, but too few to extinguish the hope of the greatest discovery of all.
