# Crimson Bay

![[Assets/Images/Crimson_Bay.jpg]]

## Main text

**Crimson Bay** is an arm of the [[Рассветное море|Dawn Sea]] that washes the shores of [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]], [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]] and the lands of [[Хамоа|Hamoa]]. Numerous rivers empty into it, among them the [[Река Рави|Ravi]] and the [[Река Бхаратхи|Bharathi]], while [[Остров Хао|Hao]] and [[Остров Ратти|Ratti]] rise above its trade routes.

Fishing villages and Dju ports line the northern coast. Farther south, dense jungle reaches the water, and the river mouths disappear among mangrove roots and warm shallows. Sailors must reckon not only with the wind, but with powerful seasonal currents capable of carrying a small vessel far from its intended harbour in a single night.

The origin of the name remains disputed. Some connect it with the rites of the Great Harvest, when blood stains the waters off Suraj Ka Ghar. Others point to the red-leaved flora of the coast: scarlet manjara, blood banyans and ruby figs. At sunset their reflections truly lend the shallows a deep crimson hue.

Merchants value the bay as a road between the eastern realms, but treat it with caution. After storms, wreckage from unknown vessels washes onto the island beaches, while traces of settlements long since swallowed by the jungle can be found in the southern channels. To an explorer, Crimson Bay promises rare plants, forgotten harbours and a passage between cultures that behold the same sea in profoundly different ways.
