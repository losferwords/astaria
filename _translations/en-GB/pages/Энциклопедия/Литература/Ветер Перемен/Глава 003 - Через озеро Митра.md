# Chapter 003 — Across Lake Mitra

*Gilas, 103 NE, summer*

[[Мэй Ву|Helena]] led the fugitives from [[Город Фибас|Phibas]] to [[Озеро Митра|Lake Mitra]]. Soldiers, city noise and Persephone's oppressive presence lay behind; ahead spread water so dark and broad at night that the far shore seemed less a place than a promise.

Going around was too slow. Gilas patrols knew every road, inn and crossing, while wounded [[Хонсу-Намун|Khonsu]] could not move as quickly as fear demanded. Helena proposed camping by the shore and deciding at dawn how to cross—a brief pause between two blows rather than true rest.

Morning brought equipment that had not been there before: a fine smith's hammer for [[Урист Китин|Urist]], a stout staff for Khonsu and a dark-yew bow for [[Томас Робинсон|Thomas]]. No one had seen the giver. The objects lay as though they had always awaited those hands. Thomas, accustomed to night's silent signs, may have sensed more than chance, but no answer was spoken.

They met a fisherman's curious son and treated him with patience rather than fear. A little play and a few honest smiles led them to his father. The fisherman did not betray them; he despised the arena and refused to call another's suffering entertainment. Without a grand speech, he gave them a boat and a chance to leave.

The crossing remained tense. Every gleam might be a steel helm, every distant voice a patrol. Entering [[Город Мистрас|Mystras]] by water was too dangerous, so they landed early and continued through [[Регион Элессия|Elessia]].

Gilas guards and a local ranger with a dog soon found their trail. Among trees, much depended on Thomas. The Lunaar hunter read tracks, confused directions and chose hollows where scent and footfall disappeared.

He not only escaped the hunters, but won over the ranger's dog. She seemed to choose her own new master, and Tom named her [[Мусака|Musaka]]. Yet the victory was incomplete: in the confusion Thomas separated too far from the others and lost his way. Helena, Urist and Khonsu continued towards Mystras while Tom remained in Elessia's woods—no longer alone, but cut off from those with whom he had shared cage, arena and freedom's first taste.
