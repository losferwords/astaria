# Avatar

## Main text

The monks of the secluded [[Храм Дракона|Temple of the Dragon]], far in the east of [[Архея|Archaea]], call themselves Avatars. With rare exceptions, the temple admits only novices of the [[Джу|Dju]] people, who raised the sanctuary in honour of their patron, [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Lang-An]]. Its halls shelter a great many novices and monks, yet only a few ever find the courage to undergo the rite of rebirth. No outsider knows precisely what occurs during the ritual. After several days in the temple's depths, however, the monk returns as a living incarnation of Lang-An: an Avatar.

## Training

Avatars excel at unleashing immense magical force both at range and in close combat. Their broad discipline combines devastating attacks with powers that hinder enemies and strengthen allies, allowing an incarnation of [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Lang-An]] to overwhelm an opponent in moments. The initiated may also call forth the spirit of the dragon upon the battlefield, doubling the fury of their flame against invaders.

## Attributes

Avatars carry no ordinary weapon, for the whole body becomes a living weapon after rebirth. In battle they kindle fire around their hands and strike with searing force. To master the violence of that flame, the initiated wear specially forged [[Наручи Иджи|Idji Bracers]].
