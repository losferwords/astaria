# Lepha

![[Assets/Images/Lepha.jpg]]

## Main text

**Lepha** is the small capital of [[Катахтонос|Katachthonos]] on the eastern shore of the [[Полуостров Таллас|Tallas Peninsula]]. It is built upon stone terraces above the sea: upper streets are protected from the waves, lower quays descend into the water, and stairs, arches and lifting platforms join the two.

After the state was founded, Lepha was assembled almost from wreckage. Pale local stone stands beside dark blocks recovered from drowned Chthonid structures, making the city seem both new and ancient. Columns differ in height, old reliefs are inverted or plastered over, and corridors absent from building plans are found beneath inhabited quarters.

Under [[Эвридика Лефийская|Eurydice of Lepha]], the vulnerable colony became an administrative centre. Archives, workshops for navigational instruments and schools were founded, teaching young [[Хтониды|Chthonids]] to handle their ancestral legacy with more care than their great forebears had shown.

Today [[Арета Лефийская|Areta of Lepha]] rules the city. Its people see Lepha as proof that a ruined people can build a home without forgetting its guilt. At sunset they gather on sea terraces, where distant lights of [[Талассия|Thalassia]] mark an ally, neighbour and reminder of the young country's dependence upon the wider world.
