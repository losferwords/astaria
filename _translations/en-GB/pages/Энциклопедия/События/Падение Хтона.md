# Fall of Chthon

![[Assets/Images/Chthonid_Fall.jpg]]

Before the New Era, the island of [[Остров Хтон|Chthon]] lay at the centre of the [[Хтоническое море|Chthonic Sea]]. Upon it flourished the learned and noble civilisation of the [[Хтониды|Chthonids]], builders of magnificent cities and temples, and students of astronomy, the nature of existence and the mysteries of time.

Yet the deeper the people of Chthon delved into knowledge, the more children were born with the ‘white-eyed sickness’, later named [[Хтонотема|Chthonotema]]. Those afflicted were blind in the ordinary sense. Instead, they perceived subtle boundaries of being hidden from others: the energies of earth and stars, life and death, and the currents of time itself. The Chthonids first received these children as gifts of the gods, for the future stood open before them. The [[Профитис|Profitis]], called Oracles in the common tongue, attained high station; temples and gardens were raised in their honour.

In time, every Profitis began to utter the same prophecy: Chthonid civilisation must be extinguished, and its island swallowed by the sea. Few believed them at first, but the Profitis proved implacable. United, they waged war upon their own people. The Chthonids had no answer to the combined power of hundreds of Oracles, and so the prophecy was fulfilled.

After the island sank, the surviving Profitis came ashore in [[Кельн|Keln]]. They withdrew into solitary lives and only rarely intervened in the fates of other peoples.

With the Chthonid empire destroyed, the republics of [[Гилас|Gilas]] and [[Талассия|Thalassia]] immediately declared their independence and became sovereign realms.
