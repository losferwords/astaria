# Vigdis the Unerring

![[Assets/Images/Vigdis_the_Accurate.jpg]]

## Main text

**Vigdis the Unerring** is a battle-hardened [[Вактары|Vaktar]] raider who plies her trade across the southern lands of [[Архея|Archaea]]. She has devoted her life to battle and the honing of every art of survival, hoping one day to become one of the [[Вознесённый|Ascended]].

Vigdis earned her best-known epithet through her mastery of throwing axes, although less flattering names follow her as well: Vigdis the Widow and Vigdis the Cruel.

For a time in [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], Vigdis fought beside [[Урист Китин|Urist Kitin]]. She never hesitated to dispute his tactics, named his mistakes openly and treated respect as a prize that had to be won in battle. Not even a punishing blow from Urist made her retreat; in his strength, Vigdis saw an adversary worthy of the trial.

When the Vermin attacked [[Деревня Улехей|Ulekhei]], Vigdis was ready to accept death as the completion of an Ascended warrior's path. [[Винтра|Wintra]], however, personally carried the wounded woman to Winterheim, refusing to let her story end in the mud of a village road.
