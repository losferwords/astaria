# Tamano Meadows

![[Assets/Images/Tamano_Grasslands.png]]

## Main text

The Tamano Meadows are a broad agricultural region of [[Амато|Amato]] in the southern part of [[Туманный остров|Mist Island]]. Rice fields alternate with open meadows, small groves and thickets of shrubs. Meadow herbs fill the air with a bitter fragrance, and birds call above the fields.

Mist rolls in from the sea. A change of wind can quickly hide fields that stood clear only moments before. The white veil fills the hollows, leaving higher ground and treetops above it. To the north, near [[Город Кири|Kiri]], the meadow vegetation thins and gives way to cold, dry barrens.

## Rice fields and rural trades

Tamano gives the island realm room for farming. Many of the other islands have less land, while forests cover much of [[Остров Рюгу|Ryugu]] and marshes occupy much of [[Остров Фурудэ|Furude]]. Here, rice is the principal crop, supplying local towns and being shipped to other islands.

Farming around the rice fields is varied: pastures, hay meadows, vegetable plots and patches of flax. The inhabitants gather meadow herbs and keep bees. Fields, grassland and small stands of trees together form Tamano's settled landscape.

## Towns

On the southern coast stands [[Город Рэйкацу|Reikatsu]], a major port for rice exports, also known for sake and rice vinegar. About thirty kilometres to its north-east as the crow flies lies [[Город Кири|Kiri]], whose inhabitants fish, produce salt and make pottery. Its vessels serve the surrounding households and the workshops of the southern port. [[Город Акуно|Akuno]] and [[Город Шиори|Shiori]] also belong to the Tamano region.
