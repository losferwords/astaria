# Fiery Mountains

![[Assets/Images/Fiery_Mountains.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Fiery Mountains** are a southern [[Архея|Archaea]] massif of active volcanoes and rugged ridges. Extreme heat and constant volcanic activity leave their surroundings almost uninhabited. Two passes, one north and one south of the range, lead into the [[Пепельные земли|Ashlands]].
