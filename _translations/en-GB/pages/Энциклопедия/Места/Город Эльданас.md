# Eldanas

![[Assets/Images/Eldanas.jpg]]

## Main text

**Eldanas** is the capital of [[Лунаар|Lunaar]], founded with the kingdom in 1457 ChE. It stands on the [[Река Горсафон|Gorsafon]], between the [[Болота Забвения|Marshes of Oblivion]] and the [[Равнина Вайтфилд|Whitefield Plain]], at a vital river crossing where the northern road meets the route to the western coast.

Its fortress wall is built of dark stone and strengthened with towers designed for archers. From the river, the gates appear low; behind them rise the roofs of the palace, temples and noble houses. Quays, warehouses and workshops occupy the lower city, while markets, barracks and the royal hill lie above.

Eldanas is seldom truly bright. Clouds and fog linger over the Gorsafon even in summer, so its streets are lit by oil lamps and its signs made large and sharply contrasting. The city does not fall entirely quiet in the evening: watches change, boatmen unload vessels, and servants of the [[Культ Меркаты|Cult of Mercate]] begin their night offices.

The Crown and the Cult stand closer here than anywhere else. The court of [[Роберт Бомейн|Robert Beaumain]] receives envoys and settles worldly affairs, but matters involving ancient vows, dreams or [[Тень|Shadows]] may require the assent of priestesses. This makes the capital a place of subtle politics, where silence can weigh more than a formal speech.

To a traveller, Eldanas is a safe harbour before the road to Whitefield or the marshes, yet it keeps many doors closed. Old cellars retain traces of earlier sieges, boats vanish in the river fog, and ravens on the royal wall gather rather too often where something important is about to occur.
