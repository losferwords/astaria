# Foundation of Vaktar-Jorden

> [!timeline] 1635 ChE
> Two years after the discovery of [[Винтра|Wintra's]] body, an expedition led by [[Церунна|Cerunna]] frees the goddess from her icy prison at the bottom of [[Озеро Нордавеллир|Lake Nordavellir]]. In gratitude, Wintra creates the first [[Вознесённый|Ascended]] and names them the [[Вактары|Vaktars]]. They found a new realm: [[Вактар-Йорден|Vaktar-Jorden]].
