# Khali Dharti Marshes

![[Assets/Images/Khali_Darti_Swamp.jpg]]

**Khali Dharti** is a broad marsh west of the [[Джунгли Шанкари|Shankari Jungle]] in [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]]. Beneath thick fog lie warm pools, sucking mire and black-soil islands that can vanish after one hard rain. Trails change with the water level, so guides hang bone beads from branches.

No permanent settlements stand here: wood rots, stone sinks into silt and food stores attract predators. Herb gatherers, temple seekers and poison hunters come for plants that reveal their healing properties only after days in marsh water.

Cold lights flare above the water at night, while cries sound like a child weeping or human laughter. Rajati warn never to answer. [[Нага|Nagas]] hunt among flooded roots, and a translucent [[Ветал|vetal]] is often noticed only after it attacks.

After droughts, carved steps and idol tops from former Cycles sometimes rise from the water. The marsh does not guard its secrets deliberately; it simply does not care who enters its dark water.
