# Chthonotema

![[Assets/Images/Chthonotema.jpg]]

**Chthonotema**, also known as white-eye disease, is a genetic condition that causes loss of sight while heightening the sufferer's mental abilities, including telekinesis and telepathy. The first recorded cases appeared on the island of [[Остров Хтон|Chthon]] in 30 ChE.

The condition is known not to be contagious. A child with chthonotema is born without pupils.

Between the ages of twelve and fourteen, those affected begin to show the ability to read thoughts, communicate across great distances and move objects with the mind. By the age of eighteen, their psionic gifts have reached their full extent. It was at this age that the [[Хтониды|Chthonids]] initiated their “special” children into the ranks of the [[Профитис|Profitis]].

What caused the condition to appear among the Chthonid people remains a mystery.

Chthonotema is considered a grave social danger, for reasons still unknown: in the past, it led to the [[Падение Хтона|Fall of Chthon]].

After the island of [[Остров Хтон|Chthon]] sank beneath the sea, the number of children born with the condition fell markedly. In present-day [[Катахтонос|Katachthonos]], those affected by chthonotema are kept under strict control. The authorities of [[Талассия|Thalassia]] take every child between the ages of ten and twelve who shows signs of the condition and place them in research camps.

No treatment, nor any means of even partially relieving its symptoms, is known.
