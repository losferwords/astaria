# Makaba River

![[Assets/Images/Makaba_River.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Makaba** is the principal river of western [[Кадир|Qadir]]. It rises in [[Озеро Шаради|Lake Sharadi]], runs north, widens into [[Озеро Джамсар|Lake Jamsar]] and then reaches [[Залив Фиальсахра|Fialsakhra Bay]]. Through it, the inland Gardens are joined to the sea by an unbroken waterway.

In its upper reaches, the Makaba passes among gardens and fields. Small canals carry water to trees, dye workshops and mills. Below Jamsar the river deepens, allowing cargo boats laden with oil, fruit, cloth and incense to travel downstream.

Its current changes with the seasons. After rain, the Makaba bears fertile silt and floods the low terraces; in dry months it exposes sandy banks. Boatmasters mark the channel with poles and woven signs, but a strong flood can make the old markers useless overnight.

Caravanserais, gardens and small sanctuaries of [[Альзаман|Alzaman]] line the banks. Travellers cast a petal from Sharadi upon the water and try to notice where it vanishes from sight; it is said to reveal how far a person is truly willing to follow their chosen path.

The Makaba is convenient for trade, and so attracts brigands and those wishing to evade Samaan's duties. Old, dry branches of the river serve as secret roads, though some end in salt flats where even an experienced guide loses their bearings.
