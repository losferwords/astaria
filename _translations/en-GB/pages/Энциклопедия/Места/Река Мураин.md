# Murain River

![[Assets/Images/Murain_River.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Murain** is the longest and greatest river of [[Кельн|Keln]]. It rises at sacred [[Озеро Сильвиан|Lake Silvian]], passes through [[Лес Иомар|Iomar Forest]] and the [[Даирские луга|Dair Meadows]], then enters the [[Хтоническое море|Chthonic Sea]] beneath the walls of [[Город Муир|Muir]]. For the country, it is at once road, water source and a living boundary between different faces of the land.

Within the forest, the Murain runs slow and deep. Branches close above its water, broad water-lily leaves grow in its coves, and roots form natural quays. Narrow, shallow-draught boats travel here; their steersmen push with poles and know the stretches that must not be disturbed during spawning season.

On the meadows, the river grows broader and busier. Honey, grain, wool, timber and medicinal herbs are carried upon it. Spring floods enrich the fields, but sometimes sweep away footbridges and change the channel. Rather than force the water back to its former bounds each time, local people move trails and build new crossings—another expression of Iomar's habit of adapting to nature rather than commanding it.

Old boatmen say the Murain remembers every tree reflected in its water. Before a long journey, they cast a leaf from their home shore into the river. If it stays beside the boat for a long while, the passage promises calm; if it vanishes at once into the depths, the crew watches fog and snags more carefully.
