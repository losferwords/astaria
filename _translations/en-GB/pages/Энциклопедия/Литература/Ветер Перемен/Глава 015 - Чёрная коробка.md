# Chapter 015 — The Black Box

*Qadir, 104 NE, winter*

With [[Малик Аль-Азвар|Malik's]] letter, the travellers continued across [[Пустыня Халисат|Khalisat]] towards [[Город Самаан|Samaan]]. The letter did not quench thirst, replenish food or ward off the sand, but in [[Кадир|Qadir]] the sign of an Eternal Prince might open doors before which an ordinary traveller would die waiting.

The desert road became a trial of patience. By day the world blinded them with white light; by night, cold crept beneath their clothes; and sand entered sacks, hair and every fold of cloth. The [[Сердце Огня|Heart of Fire]] remained their greatest concern. They had not merely to carry it, but contain it so that the artefact did not consume their hands, provisions and the very hope of continuing.

During one crossing, the company met Bedouins. Among them, [[Ошу Мати|Oshu Mati]] unexpectedly recognised a childhood friend of the [[Ваку|Vaku]]. It was an encounter with a past that had seemed almost severed: with memories of home, language and people capable of seeing beneath the dust of the road not only a wanderer, but the girl she had once been.

The Bedouins offered more than water and advice. They gave the travellers a black box that the Heart of Fire could not scorch. Plain in appearance, it proved more precious than many splendid gifts: the fiery artefact could now be carried without a constant struggle against its heat.

The journey to Samaan ceased to be a slow death. Malik's letter led them towards the city of western Qadir, the box subdued the flame, and ahead they could already feel not only sand, but the authority of those who had ruled the desert for centuries.
