# Bao Xi

![[Assets/Images/Bao_Hsi.jpg]]

Bao Xi is a senior [[Аватар|Avatar]] of the fire god, [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Lang-An]], entrusted with missions far beyond the Empire's borders.

Bao and his younger sister [[Нуа Си|Nuwa]] were orphaned as children amid palace intrigues between two rival families. While living on the streets of [[Город Джу-Суо|Dju-Suo]], they met a monk who offered them work at the [[Храм Дракона|Temple of the Dragon]] in return for food and shelter. Thus began the siblings' path towards becoming Avatars.

Despite his youth, Bao achieved extraordinary mastery of body and will. He earned the Great Dragon's blessing and became an Avatar at only twenty years of age.

Bao later lost his rank after investigating the death of the former empress and becoming an inconvenience to her court advisers. Exile did not sever his bond with the Great Dragon; instead, he became one of the few Avatars whose name was known far beyond the Empire of Fire.

During the crisis in Dju-Suo, Bao helped others enter the Dragon's Halls and rallied the monks to protect the wounded god. His loyalty proved vital in freeing the temple from [[Кион-Чи|Qiong-Qi]]. Once the Dragon was saved, Bao's former dignity ceased to be merely a title bestowed by the court: it had become a devotion proven through sacrifice.
