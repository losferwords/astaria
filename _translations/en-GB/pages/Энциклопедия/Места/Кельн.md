# Keln

![[Assets/Images/Edge_of_Darkness_Mountains.jpg]]

## Main text

**Keln** is Astaria's northern continent, greeting the traveller with a thousand shades of green, grey stone and winter sky. Its lands appear calmer than scorching [[Архея|Archaea]], yet distances here are deceptive, the weather is changeable and several days of cold rain can turn a familiar road into an impassable mire.

Plains, hills and broad canyons carved by deep rivers cover most of the continent. In western Keln, the ancient range known as the [[Горный хребет Грань Тьмы|Edge of Darkness]] separates the [[Болота Забвения|Marshes of Oblivion]] from the [[Предгорья Битхорн|Bithorn Foothills]]. Entirely different lands lie on either side of its passes: sodden lowlands where the road vanishes beneath the water, and stony slopes exposed to the cold wind. Farther north, the forests thin into taiga, tundra and the ice desert of [[Антра|Antra]].

Water governs life in Keln. Rivers serve as trade routes, lakes sustain entire settlements, and the Marshes of Oblivion preserve peat, healing plants and traces of roads long since lost. The spring floods bring fertile silt, but can alter a river's course in a single night, leaving bridges stranded upon dry ground and carrying away villages thought safe for generations.

Keln's depths are rich in iron, stone and coal, while its forests yield game and timber fit for ships. These riches have given rise to very different cultures, from the Druid communities of [[Иомар|Iomar]] and the free [[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]] to kingdoms accustomed to defending every harbour worth holding. Travel is easiest along Keln's rivers, but its most remarkable places tend to lie where the landing stages end and an old path begins beneath the trees.
