# Ayliz River

![[Assets/Images/Ayliz_River.jpg]]

The **Ayliz** runs through [[Хангорская долина|Hangor Valley]] between the two jaws of the [[Горный хребет Зверя|Beast Range]]. Cold mountain streams make it fast upstream; below, it broadens into central [[Дикоземье|Wildlands']] chief road.

[[Город Даркхан|Darkhan]], [[Город Андерхан|Underkhan]] and craft towns stand on its banks. Ore, timber, wool and grain move by broad Avgar rafts, whose crews often turn a temporary landing into a small market.

Spring melt carries stones, trees and old bridge fragments, altering shallows and roads. Boatmen trust no previous year's channel map. Before a long campaign, Avgars wash weapons in the Ayliz and promise to return and answer for deeds done away from home; reconciled families drink from one current after swearing peace.
