# Velissa

![[Assets/Images/Velissa.jpg]]

Velissa is the chief goddess of the [[Венды|Wends]] and patron of [[Обитель|Obitelj]]. She embodies love, beauty, fertile earth and the riches held beneath it. The spring thaw, a loved one's return, the birth of a child and bread shared with a guest are counted among her gifts no less than a jewel found in the mountain depths.

Faith in Velissa teaches that beauty is no fragile ornament. It lives in health, dignity, fidelity and the strength to remain kind after a bitter winter. The goddess is therefore asked for more than love: her name is invoked before sowing, at the reconciliation of a family, during healing and upon the road home.

Velissa seldom promises freedom from labour or grief. Her blessing is known by other signs: exhausted soil accepts seed again, estranged people find words for one another, and someone long awaited sees a light in the window. Wendish tales emphasise that the goddess's love does not abolish mortal freedom. She may reveal the road home, but will not force anyone across the threshold.

## The goddess's form

Velissa is portrayed as a beautiful young woman whose body seems fashioned from the earth in bloom. Translucent crystals, raised from the depths of [[Астария|Astaria]], grow from her arms, legs and back. Linen, wreaths and ears of grain adorn her, while a squirrel almost always sits beside her—a sign of domestic joy, prudent provision and untroubled trust.

From time to time, a loose-lipped Bereginya claims that the goddess has soft squirrel ears. The priestesses usually fall silent or contradict one another afterwards, while temple interpreters dismiss the notion as an affectionate fancy born from Velissa's beloved animal. The rumour cannot be tested: no ordinary mortal may look upon Velissa without consequence.

Her true divine beauty takes no account of the witness's sex or former inclinations. Any man or woman who sees her falls hopelessly in love. Old attachments and cares pale beside the memory of perfection, while love becomes an obsessive longing. Some victims wander in endless search, some lose their reason, and some simply fade from grief. To Velissa, this is both gift and curse. She therefore does not speak directly to ordinary people and veils her face even when her voice is heard within a temple.

## From hatred to love

The story of the first Wends begins among the [[Девичьи Холмы|Maiden Hills]], where Vaktar hunters began to speak of a beautiful maiden. She tended the men she met, gave them smiles and blessed them with long marriages. Yet one glance was enough to make them forget their wives and children, then leave home in search of her.

Fearing for their families, the Vaktar women led the settlers farther east towards [[Сребролесье|Silverwood]]. Hunters and woodcutters continued to encounter Velissa in its forests and returned consumed by thoughts of her. Yet the more people came beneath the goddess's influence, the more abundant the harvests and the richer the soil became, for her unwitting gift nourished all life around her.

Several women whose husbands had left went to take vengeance upon the cursed maiden. They found her beside the [[Река Красивая|Krasivaya River]], where [[Город Мирград|Mirgrad]] would later stand, and fell in love with her at first sight as their husbands had. Anger and care for others nevertheless gave them the strength to speak. For the first time, Velissa learned of the families destroyed by her presence. She had never meant harm and had hidden from mortals for millennia because she knew the danger of her gift. The grief she had caused filled her with horror.

The women chose peace in place of revenge. They became the first Beregini and accepted three duties: to hide Velissa's face from mortals, carry her will, and bring her gifts and apologies to the families whose lives had been broken. Velissa became patron of the settlers, granting rich harvests, healthy children, runes that protected the home, and a teaching of love and fidelity. Their compact became the [[Основание Обители|foundation of Obitelj]] in 1236 ChE.

## Beregini and Oberezhniki

Every Bereginya knowingly repeats the ordeal of the first priestesses. She looks upon Velissa's true face and accepts a lifelong, unrequited love for perfection as the burden of her office. To keep from forgetting the mortal world, she is taught to transform impossible longing into service: blessing marriages, guarding families, attending births and carrying the words of a goddess who cannot safely appear herself.

Most of Velissa's clergy are women. Wendish tradition holds that women generally learn more readily to bear this love without allowing it to consume everything else. Male priests, called Oberezhniki, are rare. Temple chronicles record that many lost their reason, suffered for years from longing or quite literally died of grief. No candidate is therefore persuaded into initiation, and its cost is never concealed.

## Faith in Obitelj

Velissa's worship unites the tending of the earth, family rites and the magic of love. Beregini bless marriages, attend births, lead the rites of spring and mediate disputes. They know runes that heighten mutual desire, strengthen fidelity and guard the home, but also rites that break dangerous enchantments and lift curses born from betrayed vows. They are respected not for command of weapons; knowingly lying to a Bereginya is an affront to the very love upon which a household stands.

Velissa's temples resemble spacious timber homes more often than fortresses. Gardens, granaries and rooms for travellers stand beside them. The first ears of grain are laid upon the altar, but are not burnt after the rite: they join the common store and are given to those whose fields have failed. In spring, images of the goddess are dressed with red ribbons and living flowers; in autumn, with flax, nuts and fruit.

## Sleep and awakening

For many years Velissa lay in an unfathomable sleep. Prayers reached her only as distant voices, and although the Beregini could preserve the rites, they received no clear answer. Nobody knows why the goddess slept; the Wends themselves speak cautiously of it, unwilling to turn ignorance into a convenient legend.

Her awakening coincided with the war of [[Ивар Свирепый|Ivar the Fierce]]. For Obitelj, this was not only divine intervention but a moral trial. Velissa did not destroy the king or demand blind obedience. She gave people the power to defend one another, leaving them responsible for their choices and their consequences.

## Guardians

For many years Velissa rejected the very thought of creating [[Имитей|Imithei]], unwilling to answer violence with greater violence. That changed during Ivar's reign, when he waged war against Vaktar-Jorden and [[Амато|Amato]] for possession of [[Туманный остров|Mist Island]].

When persuasion could no longer stay the king, the goddess created the [[Страж|Guardians]]. She granted them the strength of stone, the patience of earth and a duty to defend rather than conquer. The Guardians helped overthrow Ivar and end the bloodshed. Ever since, their path has recalled Velissa's difficult truth: love must be merciful, but mercy sometimes needs strong hands with which to shield the weak.
