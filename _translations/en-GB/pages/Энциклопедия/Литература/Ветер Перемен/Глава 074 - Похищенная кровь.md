# Chapter 074 — Stolen Blood

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 105 NE, autumn*

After descending into the Well of Fire and touching [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Lang-An]], [[Мэй Ву|Mei]] became an [[Аватар|Avatar]]. She was allowed to choose the [[Наручи Иджи|Idji Bracers]], though putting them on proved difficult: the bracers resisted the great power bound within her blood.

The travellers then went to the [[Бордель Уй-Джан|Ui-Jan]], now as its proprietors. [[Сяо Ша|Xiao Sha]] showed them the establishment's accounts, and they discussed improvements and an exchange of expertise with [[Бордель Халык-Бындыр|Halyk-Byndir]]. The sauna attracted particular interest.

Their respite ended in calamity: the vial of [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari's]] blood was missing. [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] followed the trail of spices left by the thieves to the eastern gates of [[Город Джу-Суо|Dju-Suo]]. The guards said that diplomats from [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]] had recently ridden through. There was no hope of catching them at once.

In the morning, [[Сунь Ляньши|Sun Lianshi]] caught up with the travellers at the gates to bid them farewell and offer parting counsel. She gave Mei the [[Чёрный Вестник|Black Harbinger]] so the two friends could speak across great distances. [[Ли Сан|Li San]] came with her to say goodbye to [[Урист Китин|Urist]] before taking the children to [[Деревня Улехей|Ulehey]].

The group followed the thieves south. Along the road they met people abandoning southern villages because of man-eating predators. At last, the travellers entered the [[Джунгли Минь-Тао|Min-Tao Jungle]] and reached [[Деревня Тай-Линь|Tai-Lin]].

The village was almost deserted. The inn had nothing but rice. During the night, Urist went to fetch water and was attacked by a starving tiger. With Gilja's aid he killed the beast, providing the group with meat and a hide.

By morning it was clear that the danger had not passed. Creatures of the night had torn apart the horses that brought the travellers to Tai-Lin.
