# Chapter 011 — Feathers of Sun and Night

*Amon-Astat, 103 NE, winter*

On the road to the [[Пепельные земли|Ashlands]], the desert seemed motionless. The sun stood above the sands so brightly that [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]] might have been fashioned entirely from light, dust and expectation. Yet amid that silence, the travellers found a crater.

Within it lay [[Аст|Ast]].

The God of the Sun was unconscious, but alive. To mortals, that alone was an impossible sight: a deity fallen into the sand, vulnerable, silent, almost human in his stillness. When Ast awoke, he gazed at the sun and said that he had succeeded. His words sounded like a fragment of a tale begun somewhere far beyond the travellers' understanding.

Then [[Мерката|Mercate]] attacked him.

She exploited his weakness without hesitation. Night struck at Sun, and the desert crater became the ground of a conflict in which mortal bodies stood far too close to divine will. Mercate took control of [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]], while Ast claimed [[Мэй Ву|Mei]]. Through their hands, steps and weapons, alien powers entered the world.

The battle was terrible not for the sweep of armies, but because so little of the people themselves remained within it. Tom moved with cold darkness in his eyes. Mei became a vessel of the sun's will, sharp and blinding. [[Урист Китин|Urist]] and [[Данис ибн Синай|Danis]] became witnesses and participants in a struggle where one false movement might bring death not from an enemy, but from a god's intent.

Neither Sun nor Night prevailed. At last Ast chose flight, and Mercate sped after him, leaving behind the crater, the sand and the people who now had to belong to themselves again.

After the battle, feathers of Ast and Mercate remained upon the ground. Urist gathered them and took them with him. Perhaps he did not yet know what he held. But that winter, on the road to the Ashlands, he carried away from a divine struggle not only fear and questions, but tangible proof that the wars of the gods now touched mortals directly.
