# Larudan Tree

![[Assets/Images/Larudan_Tree.jpg]]

## Main text

The **larudan** is a rare deciduous tree found in the northern reaches of [[Лес Иомар|Iomar Forest]], chiefly around [[Озеро Сильвиан|Lake Silvian]]. It is easily recognised by pale bark seemingly threaded with green veins and broad leaves that remain fresh long after falling. In still weather, a larudan's crown rustles almost inaudibly, as though touched by a distant wind that cannot be felt below.

Larudan wood is at once strong and supple, but to the [[Надаир|Nadair]] its worth cannot be measured in coin. The tree's roots interlace closely with neighbouring plants and help the soil retain moisture. Grasses near a mature grove better withstand drought, while wounded animals often seek shelter beneath its boughs. To fell a healthy larudan without the keepers' leave is therefore considered a grave crime against the whole forest.

At initiation, a [[Друид|Druid]] receives a staff cut from a larudan branch, strengthening their bond with life and earth. The tree itself is never felled. An aspiring servant tends a chosen grove until one of its branches is judged ready. Elder Druids say that wood taken in haste remains deaf to its bearer, while a branch freely given slowly changes hue to reflect their character.

Nadair myth names the larudan as the first tree to have appeared in [[Астария|Astaria]]. Its seeds are never sold to foreigners, though merchants have repeatedly offered chests of silver for them. A traveller may enter a sacred grove only with a guide, and even then will be asked to lower their voice: beside Lake Silvian, it is believed that the oldest trees remember those who no longer walk the world.
