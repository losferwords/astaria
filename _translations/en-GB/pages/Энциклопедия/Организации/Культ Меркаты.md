# Cult of Mercate

![[Assets/Images/Merkate_Cult.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Cult of Mercate** is an ancient religious community of the [[Лудаир|Ludair]], devoted to [[Мерката|Mercate]], goddess of night, secrets and dreams. Its best-known servants are the [[Тень|Shadows]], Imithei transformed by the goddess, though most of the Cult's daily work is performed by ordinary priests, sanctuary keepers, interpreters of dreams and copyists of old vows.

In [[Лунаар|Lunaar]], people seek the Cult's servants for more than blessings. They help the bereaved endure a grievous loss, unravel recurring dreams, safeguard confessions that might destroy a family and reconcile those unable to speak the truth by daylight. Voices are kept low in Mercate's sanctuaries: a sincere word, it is said, has no need of loudness.

The Shadows remain the most secretive part of the organisation. They serve the king as counsellors, bodyguards and emissaries, yet their first allegiance belongs to the goddess. At times, a Shadow vanishes from court without explanation and returns months later; at others, one appears in a foreign city where no summons was ever openly made. Such independence troubles the Crown, but Lunaar cannot wholly command the Cult. The faith is older than the kingdom itself, having arisen in north-western [[Кельн|Keln]] among the first Dair settlers.

The organisation's principal home is the [[Храм Меркаты|Temple of Mercate]] in the kingdom's north. Its archives hold interpretations of dreams, accounts of the goddess's appearances and vows that have long outlived those who swore them. Pilgrims leave black feathers, silver thread and sealed letters without an addressee.

Among ordinary Ludair, reverence for the Cult is tempered by caution. A priestess will be invited to a sickbed and asked to shield a child from nightmares, yet after she has gone, the household may still look for a raven's track in the snow. In Lunaar, people know that Mercate's servants help mortals cease to fear the night, but belong to it far more completely than they do to the kingdom.
