# Foundation of Qadir

> [!timeline] 2374 ChE
> Refugees leave [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]] for the eastern [[Пустыня Халисат|Khalisat Desert]]. They build [[Город Сераф|Seraph]] and the temple of [[Храм Дар Мараат|Dar Maraat]], and the [[Кадийцы|Qadians]] emerge as a distinct people.
