# Ksen-Ast

![[Assets/Images/Qsen-Ast.jpg]]

## Main text

**Ksen-Ast** is a frontier city of [[Амон-Астат|the Empire of the Sun]] upon the eastern shore of [[Озеро Митра|Lake Mitra]]. Its stone quays receive fishing boats, merchant vessels and military patrols, while an overland road connects the lake with the interior of the realm.

The city's foremost landmark is a great temple of [[Аст|Ast]], built so that the dawn reflects from the lake and illuminates its inner court. Pilgrims and priests of [[Хат-Астат|Hat-Astat]] come here, and infirmaries, prayer copyists and workshops producing votive offerings cluster around the sanctuary.

Owing to its position upon the frontier, Ksen-Ast has passed repeatedly into the hands of [[Гилас|Gilas]]. The changes can be read in its architecture, family names and old fortifications. Some inhabitants honour the solar processions, others preserve Ellian martial customs, while many have learnt not to ask which flag flew above a neighbour's home during the last war.

The city now belongs to Amon-Astat, but the guard watches the road to Achaeus closely. Two languages are spoken in the market, scouts pose as ferrymen, and a guide across the lake may prove more valuable than an entire company of soldiers. Ksen-Ast lives in a peace that everyone considers temporary, and cherishes each tranquil morning all the more for it.
