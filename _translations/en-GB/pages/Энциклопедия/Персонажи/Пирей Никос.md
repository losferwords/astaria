# Piraeus Nikos

![[Assets/Images/Pirros_Nikos.jpg]]

## Main text

**Piraeus Nikos** is the son of Pelemeus Nikos, a celebrated explorer and [[Наварх|Navarch]]. In his youth he accompanied his father upon voyage after voyage, although the sea seemed to him an endless succession of identical days. The instant their ship reached harbour, Piraeus disappeared into taverns and gambling dens, lost his own money—and occasionally someone else's—and became entangled in dubious ventures. He acquired many companions, still more creditors and left Pelemeus with scarcely any hope of teaching his son caution.

When Pelemeus vanished during a voyage, Piraeus set out to find him. The trail led to [[Город Бахара|Bahara]], where his father had become the captive of a nereid and lay close to death. Pelemeus lived long enough to recognise his son, initiate him as a Navarch and entrust him with his weapons, ship, family inventions and artefacts. Only then did the old mariner die.

His inheritance did little to change Piraeus. Within a week, the ship and most of its cargo had been gambled away or squandered upon an extravagant life. A few possessions, however, will never be offered at any stake: his ostentatious clothes, his father's inventions and the [[Глаз Калипсо|Eye of Calypso]], which binds the Nikos family to the mystery of the sleeping goddess of fortune. Piraeus's recklessness is easily mistaken for emptiness, but his search for his father proved otherwise. When something truly matters to him, he will follow it through sea, debt and mortal danger.
