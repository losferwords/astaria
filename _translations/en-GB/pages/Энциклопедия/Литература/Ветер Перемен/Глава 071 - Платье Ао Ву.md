# Chapter 071 — Ao Wu's Dress

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 105 NE, summer*

[[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] and [[Артур Бомейн|Arthur Beaumain]] landed upon an eighth-floor balcony beside the palace baths. Arthur's aim was nearly flawless, and one arrow pierced Tom's collarbone. Their friends arrived in time to help, but Arthur seized [[Мерката|Mercate's]] necklace and leapt away upon a hook. [[Урист Китин|Urist]] caught him below, yet Arthur escaped even from his grasp and vanished into the night. The necklace remained with Urist.

Inside the palace, [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] removed the arrow from Tom's collarbone, only to discover that it was poisoned. [[Мэй Ву|Mei]] visited the tailor Liao Shen and learnt that the tale of a seamstress who had descended into the Halls of the Dragon concerned the former dressmaker of Empress [[Лин Си|Lin Xi]]—[[Ао Ву|Ao Wu]], Mei's mother.

In the morning, Gilja asked Mei to cauterise the wound, and Tom survived. The group then went to the [[Таверна Красный Журавль|Red Crane Tavern]] to see Ao Wu. She served them tea and, having satisfied herself that [[Цинь Ша|Qin Sha]] had been arrested, asked everyone but her daughter to leave.

Ao removed the precious dress without which Mei had never seen her and gave it to her daughter. A story had been embroidered upon its lining in golden thread. Ao had secretly loved [[Ксан Ляньши|Xian Lianshi]]. When she became pregnant, Xian faced punishment. Ao descended into the Halls of the Dragon from the Hall of Trysts on a rope woven from palace dresses and persuaded Lang-An to spare her, her child and the man she loved.

Qin Sha discovered the secret and exploited it. Ao Wu caused Lin Xi's death, lost her tongue and was exiled. Mei realised that she had seen her father in Nayan, but he was now dead, slain by Urist's hand. She did not tell her mother.

The group now knew that the path below began in the Hall of Trysts. Before returning to the palace, they visited [[Нуа Си|Nuwa Xi]], who had grown weary of the Rajati nurses' excessive care. Tom made her plain rice without spices, while the others went back to the empress.
