# Elessia

![[Assets/Images/Elessia.jpg]]

## Main text

**Elessia** is the historic heartland of [[Гилас|Gilas]] in western [[Архея|Archaea]]. Low plateaux rise above the broad valleys of the [[Река Неда|Neda]] and [[Река Тавропос|Tavropos]]. The [[Закатное море|Sunset Sea]] bounds the region to the west and the [[Хтоническое море|Chthonic Sea]] to the north.

The river valleys yield grain, olive oil and fodder for great herds. Watchtowers, sanctuaries and fortified estates stand upon the dry uplands, while roads between the cities are broad enough for armies to march swiftly. Elessian farmers are accustomed to working beside military camps: the army protects the harvest, but is also the first to demand its grain when a campaign begins.

Beyond the cultivated fields, the countryside becomes markedly more perilous. [[Гарпия|Harpies]] nest in stony ravines, [[Сатир|satyrs]] occupy the woodland margins, and abandoned granaries sometimes become lairs for packs of [[Вермин|vermin]]. A journey between two settlements visible from the same hill may therefore prove far longer than the map promises.

Elessia preserves traces of its Chthonid past: the foundations of old roads, fragments of masonry and inscriptions that modern Ellians prefer to call the inheritance of nameless ancestors. A scholar may find here the story of a new people's birth, but should tread carefully when asking that kinship to be acknowledged.
