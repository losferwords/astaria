# Chapter 002 — The Chase through Phibas

*Gilas, 103 NE, summer*

After the escape from [[Город Ахей|Achaeus]], the three former gladiators reached [[Город Фибас|Phibas]] on [[Озеро Митра|Lake Mitra]], where the border between [[Гилас|Gilas]] and [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]] always felt thinner than its rulers wished. Ellian and Hefat voices mingled with the smells of lake fish and market dust. A foreign face could disappear in the crowd—unless a chase followed it.

[[Урист Китин|Urist]] first sought to remove his shackles. The iron was pain, shame and a loud confession that he was a fugitive slave. The group separated: Urist went in search of a forge, while [[Томас Робинсон|Thomas]] and [[Хонсу-Намун|Khonsu]] tried to pass the night in a small tavern.

The smith asked few questions of an exhausted stranger with iron on his wrists. He removed the shackles and, recognising a fellow craftsperson, gave Urist a little smithing equipment. After cage and arena, Urist held tools rather than fetters for the first time.

With no coin, Thomas and Khonsu paid for lodging through work. Yet Khonsu's arena wound refused to obey caution. When Gilas soldiers entered seeking escaped gladiators, disaster might still have been avoided—until Khonsu struck his injured leg against a table and cried out loudly enough to turn every head.

He leapt through a window, only to find [[Персефона Раптис|Persephone Raptis]] in the street. Khonsu somehow slipped her grasp and fled through Phibas. The pursuit spread like sudden fire: soldiers shouting, doors slamming, frightened merchants and narrow alleys where every turn might be the last. Thomas and Urist rejoined him, but escaping Persephone seemed almost impossible.

Then a young woman calling herself Helena appeared in an alley. She was truly [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]], a Dju wanderer, though her name mattered less than her skill. With a few swift movements, gestures and words, she hid the fugitives and convinced the soldiers their quarry had fled elsewhere.

Helena later revealed part of the truth. She was connected to [[Жаждущие|the Thirsty]], an organisation devoted to freeing those held in chains. Her commander, [[Рамат-Хат-Су|Ramat-Hat-Su]], could help them leave Gilas, but first they had to reach [[Город Мистрас|Mystras]].

The fugitives now had both a road and a guide. Phibas gave them no refuge, but their chance rescue became the first step towards people for whom freedom was not a dream, but a command to act.
