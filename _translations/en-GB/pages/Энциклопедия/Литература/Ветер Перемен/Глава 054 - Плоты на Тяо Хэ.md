# Chapter 054 — Rafts upon the Tiao He

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 105 NE, winter*

The travellers led the people out of [[Город Найян|Nayan]]. [[Ли Сан|Li San]] ran to [[Урист Китин|Urist]], rejoicing that he lived, though the news that [[Ван Сан|Wan San]] had remained in the city darkened their deliverance.

The refugees were taken to the [[Река Тяо Хэ|Tiao He]] to travel downstream towards [[Город Чанг-Ша|Chang-Sha]]. Six rafts were built from bamboo. As the work continued, [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] crossed to the opposite bank to watch for pursuit.

Almost at once, he saw a company of Avgar women approaching the river. He raised the alarm, drew their attention to himself and barely reached a raft. One Avgar climbed aboard beside [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]], but instead of attacking, she helped Gilja from the water. Her name was [[Навчин Урт|Navchin Urt]], and she had seen the travellers before in [[Бордель Халык-Бындыр|Halyk-Byndir]]. She liked Gilja and decided to abandon nomad life.

The rafts set off, but arrows rained from above. [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] and Nuwa met the first volley with fire. Urist shielded Li San's raft through sheer force of will. The third strike fell upon Gilja and Tom's raft.

Then every raft ran aground, and a pride of [[Менада|sheng-hu]] appeared upon the bank. They forbade the refugees to continue along the river or return to their territory. The only road led through [[Лес Шеньянь|Shenyan]], but the matriarch warned that everyone might die within that forest.

The refugees camped upon the bank that night. Tom went on watch with several guards and quickly realised his mistake. Someone was observing them. Mei saw a pair of eyes in the darkness. Only Tom and Mei returned from the forest. The other guards had vanished.
