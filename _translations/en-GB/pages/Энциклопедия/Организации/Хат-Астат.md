# Hat-Astat

![[Assets/Images/Hat-Astat.jpg]]

## Main text

**Hat-Astat**, also called the Religion of the Sun, is the state faith of the Empire of [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]]. At the head of its pantheon stands [[Аст|Ast]], god of Light. Temples throughout the empire are tended by priests of the Sun, while the pharaoh serves as the god's herald and bears his word to the people. Hat-Astat is among the most ancient of living faiths: its birth marked the foundation of Amon-Astat itself.

The solar clergy follows a formal hierarchy divided into civil and military branches.

- Civil priests of the Sun practise medicine, teach, pursue learning, perform funerary rites and aid the people in times of hardship. Their highest office is that of High Priest.

- Military priests guard the principal trade routes, the empire's borders and its inhabitants. Their highest order is that of the [[Светоносный|Lightbringers]].

Ast's greatest temple stands in the city of [[Город Мира|Myra]], upon the shore of [[Озеро Нехмет|Lake Nekhmet]].
