# Chapter 002 — Storm Bay

*[[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]], 104 NE, spring*

Leaden clouds hung over [[Город Солаис|Solais]]. [[Эйдан мак Кинсли|Aidan mac Kinsley]] felt the approaching rain upon his skin: thunder troubled him more than other men, recalling the [[Рунштоирм|Runstoirm]] for which he had been prepared from childhood. He knew he was meant to become what his clan and father expected, but fear of his own destiny would not leave him.

At a distant quay, Aidan noticed a lone woman in a wet cloak. She looked directly at him, silently shaped the words “thank you”, and her voice sounded within his mind. Then the stranger stepped into the sea. No one but Aidan saw her fall. He plunged into the water, fought the waves, found her beneath the dark surface and carried her onto the quay's stone steps. She breathed, but did not wake.

Rain broke over Solais, and Aidan sheltered the woman he had saved. Soon [[Пирей Никос|Piraeus]] arrived aboard the *Typhon*, aided by the veteran [[Наварх|Navarch]] [[Неарх Мелит|Nearch Melit]], an old friend of his father. His search for the *Atron* led him to the very ship beside which Aidan had rescued the stranger. Thus Piraeus found his vessel, while Aidan was drawn into another man's fortune.

The *Atron's* hold was packed with crates of red stones the size of cherries. Their cloying scent betrayed a dreadful cargo: [[Кристалл Души|Soul Crystals]], forbidden and priceless contraband. Piraeus understood that such a cargo would ruin him faster than his debts in any civilised port, yet casting so much wealth overboard was no easier.

On the quay, Nearch loudly told tales of distant Bahara, nymphs and perils at sea. His stories only emphasised the murky current into which Piraeus had fallen. To reach his future, he needed a crew, oarsmen and at least one person capable of enduring the sea's wrath. Aidan, the rescued prophetess and the *Atron* became the first threads of that new fate.

The ship soon entered the [[Хтоническое море|Chthonic Sea]]. There, amongst the waves, the travellers found [[Ишикути Макото|Ishikuchi Makoto]], barely alive after the loss of a pirate ship. He clung to a broken mast and bore upon his back a heavy family relic he could not bring himself to abandon. Words in the ancient Edzo tongue reached him: “Hold fast. I am beside you. Follow the wind.” Guided by the whisper, he saw a sail and was brought aboard the *Atron*.

A storm caught the vessel in open water. The rescued prophetess, [[Кассиопея Атанатос|Cassia]], spoke to Aidan in the words of a vision: “Break the chains. Grant freedom to the Stormcaller. Swing the sword one final time.” She told him that the Highlander's path might be closed, but the path of a great man remained open. Aidan raised his sword to the heavens. Lightning struck the blade again and again, all the storm's fury pouring into him. When the blows ceased, the tempest dispersed and the sea calmed. Aidan lived and took his first step towards a different destiny, both higher and more terrible.

The silence did not last. A ship bearing a black-and-crimson flag with a white eye appeared upon the horizon. It was the *Proteus*, vessel of [[Аристея Кронос|Aristea Kronos]], whose name sailors spoke in whispers. Cassia warned that the prophecy had been distorted and that this meeting would bring blood, death and a cage. The *Proteus* overtook the *Atron*, hooks bit into its sides and the boarding began. Aristea, beautiful and terrifying, flew onto the deck, killed an oarsman with one blow and hurled Cassia overboard upon seeing her. There the fate of the *Atron* ended in blood and foam.
