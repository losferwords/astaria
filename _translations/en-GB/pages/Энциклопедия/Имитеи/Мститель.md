# Avenger

## Main text

The Avengers are a warrior order created by [[Альзаман|Alzaman]], Lord of Time, during the [[Раскол Кадира|Schism of Qadir]]. The wise deity remade ordinary soldiers into agile and deadly fighters, able to plunge into the midst of an enemy host and cut through it before their victims can turn. They can also turn the strength of the desert against their foes and bend the very flow of time to deliver attacks with impossible speed.

## Path of the Blade

An Avenger can perform a startling number of actions in the span of a breath, dealing tremendous damage with paired blades. Their discipline also offers ways to control an opponent's movement, evade an attack and seize the instant in which a defence lies open.

In western [[Кадир|Qadir]], future Avengers train in schools devoted to the Path of the Blade. [[Малик Аль-Азвар|Malik al-Azwar]] founded the tradition and its network of schools around 650 ChE. Its teaching centres upon swordsmanship, speed and the use of two complementary blades: [[Клинок Айкаб|Aiqab]] in the leading hand and [[Клинок Рахма|Rahma]] in the other. Aiqab serves the swift assault, while Rahma provides control, defence, parrying and tactical guile—turning aside a blow so that the Avenger may answer at once with a decisive thrust.

Among the Path's best-known pupils are [[Амира Аль-Азвар|Amira al-Azwar]] and [[Рашаад аль-Сакхар|Rashaad al-Sakhar]].

## Attributes

Every Avenger traditionally bears a pair of curved blades.
