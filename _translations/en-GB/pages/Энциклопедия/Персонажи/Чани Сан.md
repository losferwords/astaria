# Chani San

![[Assets/Images/Chani_San.jpg]]

## Main text

**Chani San** is a woman of the [[Джу|Dju]] people, head of the San family and elder of [[Город Найян|Nayan]]. Her manner is calm and almost maternal, but beneath that gentleness lies a woman accustomed to remembering every promise and weighing each decision years in advance.

Nayan lies far from the capital, allowing the San family greater independence than many provincial houses. Chani maintains order through marriage agreements, mercantile obligations and the respect of farming communities. She prefers not to oppose [[Город Джу-Суо|Dju-Suo]] openly, yet knows how to carry out an imperial decree while sparing her city as much harm as possible.

More than twenty years ago, Chani sought a trade alliance with [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]] to the south by betrothing her daughter [[Ли Сан|Li San]] to an Avgar of good family. She hoped to secure ore from the [[Горный хребет Шафар|Shafar Range]] for Nayan and turn a family bond into a dependable road across the frontier. The wedding was delayed, the shipments ceased after deaths in the mines became more frequent, and a plan that had seemed farsighted condemned Li to many years of waiting.

Besides Li, Chani has two sons, [[Ван Сан|Wan San]] and [[Дзин Сан|Dzin San]], both of whom hold high office at the court of Nayan. She loves her children sincerely, but is accustomed to expressing that love by arranging their futures and does not always perceive the price they pay for her designs.

After the calamities endured by Nayan, Chani grew more wary of elegant alliances and promises made by distant rulers. She remains a skilful political steward, but now asks not only what a treaty may gain, but who will still be there when the treaty ceases to serve its purpose.
