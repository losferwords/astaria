# Aysan Bayat

![[Assets/Images/Aysan_Bayat.jpg]]

## Main text

**Aysan Bayat** is a [[Мектиг|Mektig]] woman from western [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]]. Among the [[Авгарцы|Avgars]], a female [[Имитей|Imithei]] is an extraordinary rarity.

Aysan was born into a wealthy family of craftspeople and grew up surrounded by comfort and the attentions of numerous relatives. Yet the young girl always longed for adventure and great deeds. Tales of [[Аксель Хан|Axel Khan's]] exploits inspired her so deeply that, at fifteen, she secretly left her family home and travelled to the [[Великая Кузня|Great Forge]] to meet [[Хангор|Khangor]] in person. The gates appeared sealed, but for more than a year she searched for a way inside and faced the trials hidden within the Forge. Her persistence compelled Khangor to take notice of the courageous Avgar and her fierce will to live. Aysan entered the Great Forge, earned the Smith-God's recognition and support, and became a [[Мектиг|Mektig]]. Her conversation with Khangor gave her new strength and revealed a different path through life in Wildlands.

Aysan Bayat subsequently became known as a wandering mercenary who accepts the most difficult commissions—including contracts to kill.
