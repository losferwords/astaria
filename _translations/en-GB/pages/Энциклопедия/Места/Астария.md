# Astaria

![[Assets/Images/Silvian_Lake.jpg]]

## A world beneath the Sun

**Astaria** is the name by which all known peoples call the world they inhabit. In the tongue of the [[Хефат|Khefat]], it may be rendered as ‘She Who Lies beneath the Sun’ or ‘Beneath the Shelter of the Star’.

Learned scholars believe Astaria to be an immense sphere of land and water. Only a small part of its surface, however, has been charted. To the inhabitants of the known lands, the rest of the world remains a mystery.

## Shores of the Chthonic Sea

The known lands are divided by the [[Хтоническое море|Chthonic Sea]]. Two continents lie upon its shores: [[Архея|Archaea]] to the south and [[Кельн|Keln]] to the north. The [[Рассветное море|Dawn Sea]] stretches eastwards, while the [[Закатное море|Sunset Sea]] lies to the west.

Life gathers around the Chthonic Sea. The farther one travels from its waters, the harsher the climate becomes: the deserts of the [[Пепельные земли|Ashlands]] begin in the south, while the frozen wastes of [[Антра|Antra]] await in the north.

## Peoples, gods and Imithei

The [[История Астарии|history of Astaria]] spans several thousand years. Peoples settled new lands, gathered knowledge and built realms, each with customs and memories of its own. Traces of the vanished [[Археи|Archaean]] civilisation found throughout those domains still make it impossible to say with certainty how ancient the inhabited world truly is.

In Astaria, gods dwell beside mortals and intervene in their fates. Those who attain superhuman mastery through craft, art or devoted service are known as [[Имитей|Imithei]].

## The year 106 of the New Era

The [[Война Жаждущих|War of the Thirsty]] has recently ended, and the deeds of Kenneth mac Rayne have been immortalised in the saga *[[Зов Бури|Call of Thunder]]*. Yet the long-awaited peace between the realms is no nearer. Tensions are rising, and the gods are playing dangerous games with mortal souls.
