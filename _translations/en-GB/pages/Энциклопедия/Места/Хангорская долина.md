# Hangor Valley

![[Assets/Images/Hangorian_Valley.jpg]]

**Hangor Valley** follows the [[Река Айлиз|Ayliz]] between the two stone jaws of the [[Горный хребет Зверя|Beast Range]]. Its high slopes shelter the lowland from part of Archaea's dry winds, allowing grass, sparse groves and pasture to grow. From above, it resembles a green tongue in an immense petrified beast's mouth.

Before the Avgar conquest, these lands lay under the influence of [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]]. Old crossings still bear Dju stonework and inscriptions, but the [[Авгарцы|Avgars]] gave the valley its present character: they named it for smith-god [[Хангор|Hangor]] and built towns, workshops and heavy-wagon roads beside the Ayliz.

The [[Великая Кузня|Great Forge]] lies deep within it, the destination for which the Avgars spent centuries gathering metal, stone and other materials. After the Iron God closed his creation to unrestricted entry, the surrounding settlements kept the rhythm of forges: mornings begin with hammer blows, and a family's wealth is measured in good tools and trained masters rather than ornament. Seekers of Khangor's recognition sometimes pass through the valley—future [[Мектиг|Mektigs]] who must still find a path beyond the sealed gates and endure the god's trials.

The road seems safer than mountain passes, but harpies, maenads and immense rukhs inhabit the quiet slopes. Spring floods carry away bridges, while pre-Avgar workings remain beneath the hills. Guides warn travellers not to leave the road for a bell, a light or traces of unfamiliar smithing.
