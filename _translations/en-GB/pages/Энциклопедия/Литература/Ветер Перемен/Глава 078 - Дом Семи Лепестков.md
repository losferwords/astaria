# Chapter 078 — The House of Seven Petals

*[[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]], 105 NE, winter*

After dispatching the predator in the jungle, the travellers emerged upon a broad trade road. [[Город Танассар|Tanassar]] rose before them: high walls amidst the [[Джунгли Шанкари|Shankari Jungle]], timber gates and the humid air of the south, fragrant with spices, sea and the blood of ancient rites.

Two [[Раджати|Rajati]] guards met them at the gates: the stern Jarag and a young [[Ракша|Raksha]] named [[Мерави Марджари|Meravi]]. The travellers' foreign appearance and the immense [[Мэйлун|Meilong]] behind [[Мэй Ву|Mei]] naturally raised questions. Meravi found a compromise: the dragon could not be led through the city streets, but might be housed temporarily in the harbour docks. She escorted Mei and Meilong around the walls to the sea herself.

The others entered the city and soon encountered [[Анвика Нарай|Anvika Narai]], the Raksha who had saved [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] in Tai-Lin. She invited the travellers to rest at the House of Seven Petals, an establishment where weariness was soothed with fragrances, massage, warm bathing barrels and a sauna.

After their treatments, the guests gathered in the common hall. Anvika announced that the finest dancer, Naika, would perform that evening. Yet when the tall, beautiful Rajati woman saw Mei, terror betrayed her before any word could. Naika was [[Сао Ву|Sao Wu]], the daughter of [[Ниса|Nisa]] and Mei. [[Нереида|Nereids]] mature swiftly: the little girl from the Wu estate had become an adult, quarrelled with her mother, gone south and assumed a new appearance.

Sao asked [[Урист Китин|Urist]] whether Nisa had truly lifted his hammer once. On hearing the tale, she wished to try. Touching [[Топор Смертоносец|Deathbringer]] brought a scream, a spray of black ink and unconsciousness. When Sao awoke, she kept insisting that she loved [[Ли Сан|Li San]], Urist's wife.

Some portion of Urist's soul, previously hidden within the hammer, had somehow passed into the nereid. Tom could lift Deathbringer, while Sao herself had become heavy, as though not only a feeling but a portion of an Avgar destiny had been placed within her. The House of Seven Petals, made for repose, received the travellers with yet another fracture of the soul.
