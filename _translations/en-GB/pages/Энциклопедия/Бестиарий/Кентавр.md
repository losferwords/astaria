# Centaur

## Main text

**Centaurs** are exceedingly rare anthropomorphs found in forests and meadows throughout [[Астария|Astaria]]. A human torso rises from the shoulders of a horse's body, whose coat is soft and glossy.

Centaurs live in large communities and jealously defend their territory from humans and other thinking beings. They are especially hostile towards armed strangers.

They generally use weapons of stone—spears, bows, axes and clubs. Centaurs can run with great speed, but their weight, length and four powerful legs make them cumbersome in confined ground.

They are herbivorous and feed chiefly upon fruit, berries, mushrooms and certain grasses.
