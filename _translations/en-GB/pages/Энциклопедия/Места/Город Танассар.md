# Tanassar

![[Assets/Images/Tanassar_City.jpg]]

**Tanassar** is a major port on the northern edge of [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]], quietly hidden among the dense, spice-warm [[Джунгли Шанкари|Shankari Jungle]]. It stands on [[Багровый залив|Crimson Bay]], whose sunset colour made the region famous beyond the [[Раджати|Rajati]] lands.

Though it opens onto the sea, Tanassar lives cautiously: local boats seldom leave the coast and foreign vessels appear rarely. It grew from fishers sheltering beneath jungle crowns, then became a trade centre through rare spices, timber and resins gathered in Shankari rather than long sea routes.

Its aromatic spice blends, oils, healing pastes and carved wares are renowned. Rakshas are a familiar part of the streets' rhythm, respected because the city would long ago have been swallowed by the dangerous jungle without them.

At evening, people set rose-quartz lamps along the quay. Their gentle light is said to guide those returning home, though no one remembers for whom the custom began.
