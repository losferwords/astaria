# Kiran Varma

![[Assets/Images/Kiran_Varma.jpg]]

**Kiran Varma** is a [[Ракша|Raksha]] and temple preceptor who resides at the House of Red Silence in [[Город Ванпур|Vanpur]]. Monks turn to him when ordinary knowledge of ritual is not enough: during disputes over the Great Harvest, at the appearance of dangerous signs or when the power of blood must be brought under control before it harms the faithful.

He is a tall, broad-shouldered man with long black hair and a short beard. He wears red ritual robes trimmed with gold, together with jewels the colour of fresh blood. A crimson sign marks his brow, while the tattoos of a Raksha cover his hands and chest. Kiran's eyes ordinarily appear almost black, but blaze red whenever he invokes the power of [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari]].

Kiran speaks quietly and precisely. He seldom repeats himself and never tries to overwhelm another person through sheer volume. His authority makes itself known in other ways: he knows the temple's precepts down to their final line, notices a single mistaken movement within a rite and can end an argument with one question. Even senior monks take his words seriously, although Kiran himself shuns elaborate titles.

In Vanpur, Kiran is known as an adherent of strict tradition. He believes that during the Cycle of Destruction, a Raksha has no right to turn away from duty, however grievous it may seem. Yet he also demands that every rite be performed with understanding: to him, carelessness in the presence of blood is as unacceptable as cowardice.

He treats newcomers with reserve. Kiran does not seek friendship, but will hear those who speak plainly and accept responsibility for their choices. It is easy to sense danger in his presence, yet the Raksha never bares his blades merely to make an impression. To him, strength has value precisely because it should not be wasted upon empty display.
