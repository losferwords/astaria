# Chapter 025 — Trial in Bahara

*[[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], 104 NE, spring*

After the fire, the travellers returned secretly to [[Бордель Халык-Бындыр|Halyk-Byndir]], like people best kept from the city guard's notice. [[Урист Китин|Urist]], however, was incapable of remaining inconspicuous for long: he climbed upon the stage and sang of [[Аксель Хан|Axel Khan]], turning anxiety into Avgar bravado.

Not for long. [[Армар Саг|Armar Sag]] burst into the establishment with [[Нефела|Nephele]] and accused the travellers of setting fire to Ungol's villa. [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] slept upstairs throughout, his repose almost insulting in its serenity against the general calamity.

The company was taken to the town hall. The trial before Bahara's khan proved long and stifling, like an argument among people who had already decided to punish someone but had yet to choose the shape of justice. In the end, both the travellers and Ungol himself were sent to prison.

Bahara's dungeon was not empty. Nereids were held there, and one came to [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] to claim what she desired. Nisa visited [[Мэй Ву|Mei]]. Galatea praised her work, while Nisa confessed a strange and troubling consequence of their encounter: she was pregnant and intended to leave for [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Dragon Empire]].

Six days later, the prisoners were released. Yet prison did not close Bahara's tale. It merely gave the story time to ripen.
