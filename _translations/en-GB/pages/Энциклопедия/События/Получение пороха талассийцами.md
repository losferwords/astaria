# Thalassian Discovery of Gunpowder

![[Assets/Images/Thalassian_Gunpowder.png]]

In 38 NE, the [[Талассийцы|Thalassians]] discovered how to manufacture gunpowder. The achievement became a major support of [[Талассия|Thalassia's]] maritime power: shipboard guns gave its fleet an advantage that neither greater numbers nor skill in boarding could provide alone.

## The Craftsmen's Inheritance

The Thalassians inherited an interest in calculation and intricate devices from their [[Хтониды|Chthonid]] ancestors. After the [[Падение Хтона|Fall of Chthon]], that inheritance acquired a different meaning. Magic was distrusted as an unpredictable force capable of bringing another catastrophe; preference went to mechanisms and crafts whose workings a master could reproduce and explain.

The discovery of gunpowder continued this approach. The surviving chronology records the year in which the formula was obtained, but names no individual inventor. There is therefore no basis for assigning the achievement to a particular captain, workshop or act of divine intervention.

## A Maritime State Secret

The formula became a state secret. Workshops permitted to manufacture gunpowder are guarded as closely as the royal treasury. Thalassia protects both its weapons and the knowledge upon which its fleet's standing depends.

Gunpowder weapons changed the possibilities of naval combat, but remained expensive, scarce and dangerous to their own crews. Their appearance did not make firearms commonplace amongst the peoples of Astaria. Even amongst captains, a gunpowder pistol remains an exceptional possession; it forms part of the traditional equipment of a [[Наварх|Navarch]], alongside a kopis for boarding.

## An Advantage with Limits

Thalassian influence at sea still depends upon shipwrights, knowledge of winds, trained crews and agreements with harbours. Gunpowder strengthened those advantages without replacing them. The year 38 NE marks the beginning of a new craft, rather than the sudden arrival of plentiful and reliable firearms upon every sea.
