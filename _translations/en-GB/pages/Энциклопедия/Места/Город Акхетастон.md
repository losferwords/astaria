# Akhetaston

![[Assets/Images/Akhetaston_City.jpg]]

## Main text

**Akhetaston** stands at the foot of [[Гора Атафет|Mount Atafet]], the surface counterpart of the subterranean city of [[Город Атафет|Atafet]]. Not every inhabitant of [[Амон-Астат|the Empire of the Sun]] is willing to descend into the dark caverns, so most caravans unload here. Ore is tested and stone sorted in the open squares, while travellers hire guides before taking the road into the mountain.

Trade, craft and animal husbandry sustain the city. Pack animals able to endure the heat and stony trails are kept along its outskirts, while its workshops produce hilts, scabbards and leather fittings for the wares of the underground smiths. Each morning, dust, ringing hammers and the calls of porters fill the air.

Akhetaston appears safer than Atafet, yet the nearness of the great forge brings dangers of its own. Buyers of rare ores, military envoys and those prepared to pay for weapons without inconvenient questions all meet here. A traveller may find the finest craftsperson, hear a rumour of a sealed mine or notice that one caravan has returned from the mountain without half of its escort.
