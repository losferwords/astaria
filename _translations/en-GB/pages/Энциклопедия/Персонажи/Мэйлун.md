# Meilong

![[Assets/Images/Meilong_Adult.jpg]]

**Meilong** is a young dragon who, through a curious turn of circumstance, took [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] for his mother. He is no tame beast: Meilong makes his own decisions, speaks, protects those dear to him and often ventures wherever he senses another's distress. [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Lang-An]] recognised him as one of the [[Морской дракон|sea dragons]], a species long believed extinct.

Meilong's appearance combines the traits of different dragons. Four clawed legs bear his long, supple body, red spines run along his white scales, and a broad pair of wings opens behind his shoulders.

Meilong also suffers from serious health problems. He appears to be an albino, a condition that affects his strength, appetite, eyesight and speed of reaction.

In his short life, Meilong has already saved [[Деревня Улехей|Ulehey]] from traitors, survived captivity in the temple of [[Кион-Чи|Qiong-Qi]] and carried the inhabitants of [[Деревня Вэйган|Veigan]] beyond the reach of a naga attack. Nereids mature swiftly, but Meilong's growth proved more remarkable still: within a few months, the tiny hatchling became a creature almost ten metres long, capable of fighting and carrying people.

Yet his body remains vulnerable. Albinism weakens his strength, appetite, eyesight and reactions, while his mind echoes with the voices of a sea monster and the ocean of thought belonging to the [[Профитис|Profitis]]. At times Meilong must keep his distance from his friends for fear of losing control, even as the emissaries of [[Винтра|Wintra]] and Qiong-Qi continue to hunt him.

In water, Meilong moves chiefly through undulations of his long body, much like a sea snake or eel. He folds his four legs against his flanks, while the wings lie along his back and help him hold direction. To take to the air, the dragon accelerates beneath the water and bursts above the surface, turning the leap into an extended glide. His wings let him ride rising currents and cross great distances, although prolonged flapping flight exacts a disproportionate toll upon his strength.
