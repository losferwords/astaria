# Astyages II

![[Assets/Images/Astiag_II.jpg]]

## Main text

**Astyages II** was the last king to rule [[Кадир|Qadir]] alone, and his reign brought the age of hereditary monarchy to its end. He grew up at a court accustomed to treating the expansion of the realm's borders as proof of a sovereign's strength, and from an early age sought to surpass the achievements of his predecessors.

War with the [[Авгарцы|Avgars]] was meant to restore Qadir's influence in the east and bring the [[Хангорская долина|Hangor Valley]] under its rule. Astyages underestimated the northern warriors and soon found himself compelled to seek aid from [[Альзаман|Alzaman]]. The god agreed to create the [[Мститель|Avengers]] on the condition that the king do no more than liberate the occupied lands and then make peace.

When the first Avengers turned the course of the war, victory persuaded Astyages that he had been chosen for greatness. He broke his compact and ordered the advance to continue, intending to conquer the whole of [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]]. To those who had fought beside him, this was not merely an error of judgement, but an act of sacrilege and a threat to repeat the very catastrophe from which Alzaman had sought to preserve the country.

General [[Абу Али аль-Хассан|Abu Ali al-Hassan]] beheaded the king before the order could be carried out. The Avengers then divided authority among themselves and took the title of Eternal Princes. Official memory portrays Astyages as a proud man destroyed by his hunger for glory. Some historians, however, see a more tragic figure: a ruler who succeeded in saving his country but could not accept a boundary to his own victory.

The name of Astyages is still spoken in the schools of Qadir as a warning. Every pupil is reminded that the greatest strength becomes most dangerous at the moment when a person convinces themselves that promises made before their triumph no longer apply.
