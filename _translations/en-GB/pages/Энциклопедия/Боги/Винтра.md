# Wintra

## Main text

Wintra is the chief goddess of the [[Вактары|Vaktars]] and their realm of [[Вактар-Йорден|Vaktar-Jorden]]. She embodies killing cold, purity of purpose and righteous vengeance.

For centuries Wintra paid no heed to humans or other lesser creatures; only the [[Небесный дракон|dragons]] of her own blood held her interest. Three thousand years ago, a terrible event transformed the goddess's existence. Her strength spent in a mortal battle, she fell into [[Озеро Нордавеллир|Lake Nordavellir]]. Its waters preserved her body for many years and concealed her from the rest of the world.

There she remained until the goddess [[Церунна|Cerunna]] sent an expedition of [[Друид|Druids]] eastward to study the ancient [[Лес Сольвид|Solvid Forest]]. In 1637 ChE the [[Надаир|Nadair]] reached the frozen lake and discovered the she-dragon beneath its ice. The Druids who led the expedition sensed a faint warmth of life in the depths. For two years the settlers methodically cut their way down, until in 1635 ChE they drew Wintra ashore. Her blood began slowly to thaw after its age-long stillness. When she saw her rescuers, the she-dragon rewarded them with a measure of her power and created a new order of [[Имитей|Imithei]], whom she named the [[Вознесённый|Einherjar]].

After her deliverance, Wintra guarded the expedition from danger, cold and cruel winters. She hunted for them and shared her strength and wisdom. Thus the new realm of Vaktar-Jorden arose. Yet Wintra never forgot her defeat, and still seeks vengeance upon the sworn enemies who condemned her to those long years beneath the frozen water.

That tradition of reward endures. The warriors most worthy and devoted to the she-dragon may be granted ‘ascension’. Wintra watches each of them; should one fall in glorious battle after showing courage and purity of purpose, the Queen of Dragons comes in person to bear the warrior from the field to her home in the [[Храм Винтерхейм|Winterheim Temple]]. They remain in her hall for a time, recovering, resting and feasting. At last the hour comes for their return to the world. Wintra grants them her power and ancient knowledge, naming them her [[Вознесённый|Einherjar—the Ascended]].

Wintra resembles a [[Небесный дракон|Sky Dragon]], though she is far larger than any other of her kind. Her scales are white, and a blue jewel of unknown origin crowns her head.
