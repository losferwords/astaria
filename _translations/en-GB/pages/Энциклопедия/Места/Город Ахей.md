# Achaeus

![[Assets/Images/Acheus_City.jpg]]

## Main text

**Achaeus** is a major city of [[Гилас|Gilas]] upon the shore of [[Озеро Митра|Lake Mitra]], close to the frontier with [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]].

As in the other great cities of Gilas, contests for the title of [[Идеал|Paragon]] are held here between the finest Ellian soldiers and the strongest warriors of [[Астария|Astaria]]. The arena is not solely a place of honoured competition: captives and slaves are also forced to fight for the amusement of the citizens, turning martial virtue into a cruel public order.

Its frontier position has made Achaeus a symbol of the long rivalry between Gilas and Amon-Astat. The city has endured sieges, changing garrisons and the [[Вторжение Амон-Астата в город Ахей|invasion of the Thirsty]], while its prisons have held people whose escapes later changed the fate of both realms. To the Ellians, Achaeus remains a school of endurance; to their neighbours, it is the first reminder of the price of the strength of Gilas.

Daily life is governed by the expectation of another war. Jars of water are kept upon rooftops against fire, boatfolk know several concealed landings, and merchants keep their accounts in two languages and two systems of measure. Yet the inhabitants do not consider the city doomed. Every rebuilt home is taken as stubborn proof that the frontier belongs to more than armies alone.
