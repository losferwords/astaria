# Antal

![[Assets/Images/Antal.jpg]]

**Antal** is the capital of [[Талассия|Thalassia]], on the southern coast of the [[Полуостров Таллас|Tallas Peninsula]]. Its harbour is considered the largest in [[Астария|Astaria]]: war triremes, merchant ships, fishing vessels and the floating homes of sailors all lie there together—sailors who acknowledge the port's authority only while it does not prevent them putting to sea.

The city rises from the water in terraces of pale stone. Shipyards, warehouses, tackle markets and loud taverns occupy the lower quarters; higher up are engineers' workshops, shipowners' houses, port-council halls and the royal residence of [[Телемах II|Telemach II]]. Broad stairways and covered galleries join its streets, from which masts are almost always visible.

Antal lives by ships' wagers, Navarch councils and tales of vessels won at a gaming table. It is easy to change crews, buy a chart of a distant shore or hear a song about someone who staked their own name. Gambling fills the city, yet a contract made before a port scribe is strictly honoured: no far voyage can exist without trust among strangers.

The capital's workshops test sail systems, lifting mechanisms and powder weapons. Access is limited and plans are guarded as military secrets. A foreign craftsperson may be invited for exceptional talent, but anyone attempting to steal a secret risks vanishing from crew lists forever.

For a traveller, Antal is a gate to all three seas. One may sign on to a merchant ship, war trireme or an explorer's vessel whose captain will not explain its purpose until it leaves harbour. The city is wealthy, loud and dangerously alluring: it promises a new path, never a return.
