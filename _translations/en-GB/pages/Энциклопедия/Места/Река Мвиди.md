# Mvidi River

![[Assets/Images/Mvidi_River.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Mvidi** is a great river of western [[Архея|Archaea]], flowing from [[Озеро Нехмет|Lake Nekhmet]] to the [[Закатное море|Sunset Sea]]. Along its course it forms the long [[Закатная долина|Sunset Valley]] and joins the lands of [[Амон-Астат|the Empire of the Sun]] to the domain of [[Вакумара|Vakumara]].

Its upper reaches pass through arid country, where small canals divert water to the fields. Farther downriver the banks grow greener, the channel wider and the current slower. When the Mvidi floods, it leaves fertile silt behind and fills its side channels, turning the familiar road into a chain of temporary islands.

The river holds both commercial and religious importance for the [[Ваку|Vaku]]. Boats bearing food, timber and wares from the western lands travel upon it, while ancient ritual sites endure along its banks. Even settlements that have passed beneath Khefat rule continue to use Vaku names for its shallows and bends.

The Mvidi is a convenient road, but it belongs to no single authority. Imperial outposts, guides from Vakumara and independent boatfolk offer different answers to the question of who may charge for passage. In its quiet backwaters one may discover rare waterside plants, the traces of an old sanctuary or a boat without a flag whose crew has no wish to explain the origin of its cargo.
