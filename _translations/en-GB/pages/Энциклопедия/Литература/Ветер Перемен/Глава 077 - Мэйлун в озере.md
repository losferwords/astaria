# Chapter 077 — Meilong in the Lake

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 105 NE, winter*

In the [[Джунгли Минь-Тао|Min-Tao Jungle]], the [[Наручи Иджи|Idji Bracers]] upon [[Мэй Ву|Mei's]] arms flared and began to burn her skin. Qi drew her onwards. The group followed her through the undergrowth to a precipice overlooking a great many Dju gathering their belongings. [[Велимир Сажин|Velimir Sazhin]] used earth magic to help everyone descend.

They were the people of [[Деревня Вэйган|Veigan]]. A mysterious saviour had led them away from the village. Mei's bracers drew her towards a lake, where she found [[Мэйлун|Meilong]]. The young dragon had grown to ten metres in length and lay amidst the corpses of strange creatures. [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] recognised them as [[Нага|nagas]], six-armed marsh monsters from [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]] with serpentine tails.

Mei channelled all the Qi she could muster to cauterise and heal the dragon's wounds. Meilong awoke and said that he had sensed the nagas and hurried to save Veigan. The village elder was the only person to die; the bodies in the trees belonged not to the Dju but to diplomats from Suraj Ka Ghar. Nagas take trophies from their victims, and Gilja rushed to search for the stolen blood of Shubhankari. After a long search, he found the vial at the bottom of the lake.

Meilong told Mei that [[Кион-Чи|Qiong-Qi]] and [[Винтра|Wintra]] were hunting him, forcing him to hide and move with care. Urist, Velimir and [[Бао Си|Bao Xi]] urged the people of Veigan to abandon the perilous frontier and go north. After a long dispute, they agreed, and Bao promised to escort them to the capital.

With matters in Min-Tao settled, [[Урист Китин|Urist]], Mei, [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]], Gilja, Velimir and [[Рон Эллиот|Ron]] continued towards [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]]. [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]] lay behind them. Ahead waited the [[Джунгли Шанкари|Shankari Jungle]] and the road to [[Город Танассар|Tanassar]].

That night in camp, another bloodthirsty beast sprang from the thicket. It sank its teeth into Gilja's arm, but he drove a raktakatar into its body. The creature's blood filled him with insatiable hunger, and he bit into its flesh. His wounds began to close, but the battle had only begun.
