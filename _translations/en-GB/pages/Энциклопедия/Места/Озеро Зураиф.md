# Lake Zuraif

![[Assets/Images/Zuraif_Lake.jpg]]

## Main text

**Zuraif** is the central and most revered of the Seven Gardens of [[Кадир|Qadir]]. The lake lies at the heart of the [[Пустыня Халисат|Khalisat Desert]], and legend says that [[Альзаман|Alzaman]] led the ancestors of the [[Кадийцы|Qadians]] to its waters. [[Город Сераф|Seraph]] grew upon the western shore, while the island in its midst bears the [[Храм Дар Мараат|Temple of Dar Maraat]].

Gardens, canals and ancient quays surround Zuraif. Its water appears dark blue even beneath the noon sun, and on a windless day it reflects the temple so clearly that a boatman sees two Abodes of Time—one above and one beneath the oars.

Pilgrim boats, merchant vessels and small temple barges cross the lake. Near the island, shouting, throwing coins and racing are forbidden: the Qadians believe no one should turn the road to Alzaman into a fairground amusement. Even a ruler arrives at the temple without a military escort.

Zuraif is called the Garden of Beginnings. New agreements are blessed here, children receive their names and the first day of important journeys is counted from its shore. At the same time, the lake recalls endings: funeral processions pause briefly by the water, so that loved ones may speak the words they have delayed for too long.

The lower terraces of Seraph and the island's ancient fortifications especially interest curious travellers. When the water falls unusually low, stone steps emerge from the depths, leading farther than the modern jetties allow. The keepers of Dar Maraat do not forbid looking, but do not permit a descent: Alzaman favours the search for knowledge, yet does not ask people to mistake curiosity for recklessness.
