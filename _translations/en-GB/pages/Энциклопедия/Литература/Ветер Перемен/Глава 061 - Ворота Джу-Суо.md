# Chapter 061 — The Gates of Dju-Suo

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 105 NE, spring*

The battle with [[Кион-Чи|Qiong-Qi]] proved that even an army was too little against an ancient god. The travellers and Avgars wounded him, but could not prevail. When the winged tiger took to the air and vanished amongst the trees, the host withdrew from the forest.

From there the road followed the river to [[Город Джу-Суо|Dju-Suo]], capital of the Empire of Fire. The travellers agreed with [[Батар Саг|Batar Sag]] that they would enter the city first and warn the nobility: the Great Khan had come to aid his neighbours and stop the nomads of [[Аксель Хан|Axel Khan]].

At the gates, the bridge was raised. A guard demanded their passes, but after prolonged entreaty agreed to consult his superiors. The bridge descended. No sooner had [[Урист Китин|Urist]] and [[Мэй Ву|Mei]] entered than the guards surrounded them. Urist was wanted for murdering an [[Аватар|Avatar]], while Mei was charged with deserting her duties at the imperial court. They did not resist arrest.

[[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] and [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] were invited to apply for documents. The official pretended not to know the common tongue and made them fill in incomprehensible forms. Gilja realised that they needed a resident to stand surety for them. Since they knew no one suitable, they had to offer a bribe. The official entered the name of [[Бао Си|Bao Xi]] and issued their passes.

Meanwhile, a whirlwind of flame struck the escort carrying Urist and Mei. It was Bao Xi, freeing them in an alleyway. He told them he had lost his standing as an Avatar after investigating the death of the former empress. Mei heard something troubling in his account: the Wu family might have been involved in the empress's murder.
