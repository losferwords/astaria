# Maenad

![[Assets/Images/Maenad.jpg]]

## Main text

**Maenads** are rare anthropomorphs found throughout [[Астария|Astaria]]. They resemble various great cats—panthers, tigers and leopards—but walk upright and are covered in soft fur. These graceful creatures run and leap with extraordinary speed and see superbly in darkness.

Maenads are exceedingly wary and jealously defend their territory from humans and other thinking beings. They generally live in large communities with a predominantly matriarchal order.

In battle and upon the hunt, they may use stone weapons such as spears and bows, as well as captured swords, though they more often rely upon sharp claws and fangs.

The eastern forest of [[Лес Шеньянь|Shenyan]] is home to tiger-like maenads known to local people as the Shen-Hu.
