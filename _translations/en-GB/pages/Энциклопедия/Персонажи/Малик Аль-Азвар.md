# Malik al-Azwar

![[Assets/Images/Malik_Al-Aswar.jpg]]

## Main text

**Malik al-Azwar** is one of the first [[Мститель|Avengers]] and the former Eternal Prince of western [[Кадир|Qadir]]. Before the [[Раскол Кадира|Schism of Qadir]], he served as the personal bodyguard of King [[Астиаг II|Astyages II]], standing closer than any other to the ruler and his perilous certainty of his own grandeur.

After the king's death, Malik assumed authority over [[Залив Фиальсахра|Fialsakhra Bay]] and the Gardens of [[Озеро Кхарим|Kharim]], [[Озеро Джамсар|Jamsar]] and [[Озеро Шаради|Sharadi]]. Of the first three Avengers, he was regarded as the finest swordsman. In 650 ChE, Malik founded a network of schools devoted to the Way of the Blade, transforming his personal mastery into a tradition open to later generations.

In battle, Malik joins a swift assault to a patient defence. He teaches that two blades must never quarrel: [[Клинок Айкаб|Aiqab]] opens the way, while [[Клинок Рахма|Rahma]] guards the warrior against error. Power, in his view, likewise requires both resolve and the wisdom to halt.

After more than seven centuries of rule, Malik willingly entrusted western Qadir to his daughter, [[Амира Аль-Азвар|Amira]]. The decision astonished the country no less than many of his victories. The former prince offered a simple explanation: an eternity of service need not mean an eternity in the same place, and a ruler incapable of yielding power to a worthy heir will eventually serve no one but themselves.

Malik remains an Avenger and a mentor, but strives not to turn counsel into a hidden command. To Amira, his presence is at once a source of support and a heavy shadow: every success is measured against her father, while every failure is blamed upon the great swordsman having left his throne too soon.
