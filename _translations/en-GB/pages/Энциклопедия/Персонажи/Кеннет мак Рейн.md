# Kenneth mac Rayne

![[Assets/Images/Kenneth_mac_Rain.jpg]]

## Main text

**Kenneth mac Rayne** was a [[Горец|Highlander]] of the [[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]] who entered history as the Stormcaller. Born in [[Деревня Эрвин|Erwin]] during a thunderstorm, he spent his youth preparing for the [[Рунштоирм|Runstoirm]]. When every other Named One lost consciousness during the ordeal, Kenneth bore the whole force of the lightning alone and emerged as an exceptionally powerful master of the storm.

After his initiation, Kenneth journeyed south to commission a lightning-conducting [[Меч Де'лаим|De'laim sword]] in [[Город Атафет|Atafet]]. His voyage aboard the Thalassian ship *Litei*, captained by [[Рея Мелит|Rhea Melit]], ended in shipwreck off [[Остров Хтон|Chthon]]. There he was rescued by [[Кассиопея Атанатос|Cassia]]. Kenneth built a raft and not only found a way off the accursed island, but also showed a Profitis for the first time that a foretold future could be changed.

On the shores of [[Архея|Archaea]], the companions were captured by the [[Эллийцы|Ellians]] and imprisoned for half a year. Kenneth faced [[Персефона Раптис|Persephone Raptis]] in the arena of the [[Танатомахия|Thanatomachia]], but their duel was interrupted by the [[Вторжение Амон-Астата в город Ахей|invasion of Achaeus]]. A captive priestess, [[Шани-Хат-Су|Shani-Hat-Su]], nursed the gravely wounded Highlander back to health. An escape arranged by her brother then allowed Kenneth and Cassia to continue across the [[Пустыня Сехет|Sekhet Desert]].

In Atafet, Kenneth trained the city's warriors to pay the smith [[Арно Эклунд|Arno Eklund]] for his work. When the [[Война Жаждущих|War of the Thirsty]] reached the city walls, Kenneth resolved to end not merely one siege, but the cause of the war itself. From the summit of [[Гора Атафет|Mount Atafet]], he summoned a storm out of distant [[Кельн|Keln]]. The first rain in five years ended the Great Drought and compelled [[Гилас|Gilas]] and [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]] to make peace.

Kenneth was never seen again. Neither his body nor his sword was found, and the few travellers who cross Atafet by night speak of an immense shadow wandering its slopes. His journey is recounted in the saga [[Зов Бури|*Call of Thunder*]], though his ultimate fate remains unknown.
