# Chapter 059 — Batar's Army

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 105 NE, spring*

As [[Город Чанг-Ша|Chang-Sha]] prepared to depart, [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] treated his broken arm with a splint made by [[Урист Китин|Urist]]. A boat landed upon the shore of [[Озеро Чанг|Lake Chang]], and [[Айсан Баят|Aysan Bayat]] stepped out. She was searching for a particular [[Нереида|nereid]] on orders from the capital, though she did not know her quarry's name.

As the refugee boats began to leave, [[Мэй Ву|Mei]] noticed that [[Ниса|Nisa]] had vanished and [[Сао Ву|Sao Wu]] could not find her. Fearing that Aysan was hunting Nisa and the child, Mei asked [[Мэйлун|Meilong]] to follow Sao's scent. The dragon carried them back into the city.

[[Томас Робинсон|Tom]], Urist and Gilja were already crossing towards the capital when they heard gongs and signals from the watch. An army was approaching Chang-Sha, but these were not Axel's nomads. They were [[Авгарцы|Avgars]] marching beneath the banner of [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]]. Urist nearly capsized the boat carrying his wife and the refugees, but Gilja and [[Нуа Си|Nuwa Xi]] helped save them.

[[Батар Саг|Batar Sag]] met them at the gates. The Great Khan had come to aid the Dju because he had chosen peace, diplomacy and trade. Shen Wu admitted his army into the city.

Meanwhile, Mei found Nisa in the cellar of a brothel, fighting another nereid whom [[Галатея|Galatea]] had sent to return her to [[Город Бахара|Bahara]]. Aysan burst into the cellar, intent upon capturing them both. Mei rescued Nisa, and together with Sao they escaped through streets already filled with Avgars.

The following morning, one of Batar's scouts arrived at the estate. The travellers did not immediately recognise [[Рон Эллиот|Ron]], now grown and counted among the finest trackers. He brought troubling news: the nomads had no intention of attacking Chang-Sha. They were circling around it, bound for [[Город Джу-Суо|the capital]]. The travellers resolved to go there and stop Axel once and for all.
