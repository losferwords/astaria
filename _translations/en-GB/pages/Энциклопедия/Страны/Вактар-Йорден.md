## The Icebound Kingdom

Vaktar-Jorden is the Icebound Kingdom of central [[Кельн|Keln]], a land of frozen lakes, coniferous forests, stony shores and frigid sea lanes. The [[Вактары|Vaktars]] have learnt to live where winter can cut a village off from its neighbours overnight and one helmsman's mistake can doom an entire ship. They therefore prize not recklessness, but courage sustained by endurance and loyalty to one's companions.

## The freeing of Vintra

The Vaktars descend from the [[Надаир|Nadair]]. In 1637 ChE, an expedition of [[Друид|Druids]] travelled east to explore the ancient [[Лес Сольвид|Solvid Forest]]. Beneath the ice of [[Озеро Нордавеллир|Lake Nordavellir]], they found the body of an immense she-dragon. For two years the settlers laboured through the ice, until in 1635 ChE they raised [[Винтра|Wintra]] onto the shore. The grateful goddess shielded them from hunger and cold, and her rescuers embraced her as their patron.

Thus a new people was born, and Vaktar-Jorden arose around the lakes and sea inlets. In the old Dair tongue, the name *Vaktar* means ‘protectors’. To the northerners it is not merely a memory of Vintra's rescue, but a charge to protect ship, home and those close at hand, even when no one will witness the deed.

## Sea, land and the long winter

Settlements follow the rivers, lakes and shores of the [[Хтоническое море|Chthonic Sea]]. During summer the Vaktars gather timber, fish, salt, grain and fodder; in winter they repair ships, forge weapons, carve runes and recount family sagas. Houses are built around a common hearth, and a guest is seated close to the fire before any questions are asked. Inquiry can wait until the traveller has ceased to shiver.

The fleet binds distant shores more closely than many roads. Vaktar seafarers pass between the islands, explore cold waters and escort merchantmen where drifting ice changes course faster than any map. A skilled helmsman commands nearly as much respect as a renowned warrior: both are answerable for the lives entrusted to their judgement.

## Truth and a glorious death

Vintra embodies cold, truth and righteous vengeance. The Vaktars say that a lie is like a fracture in the ice: it may go unnoticed at first, but one day it will destroy everyone standing nearby. Admitting guilt is difficult, yet worthier than preserving honour through deceit. Vengeance, too, must be just, directed at the guilty rather than at their children or an innocent neighbour.

The finest warriors dream of becoming [[Вознесённый|Ascended]], or Einherjar. If a worthy Vaktar dies gloriously in battle with pure intent, Wintra may bear them to [[Храм Винтерхейм|Winterheim]], restore them to life and grant them the power of frost. Death is therefore not always an ending, though the northerners know well that their goddess can distinguish self-sacrifice from a hollow thirst for fame.

## Sigrid's crown

The kingdom is ruled by [[Сигрид Дракендоттир|Sigrid Drakendottir]], daughter of the late King [[Фрости Дракенсон|Frosti Drakenson]]. Vintra raised her after the battle for [[Обитель|Obitelj]], and she returned home both chosen warrior and heir to the throne. Her right to rule rests upon the goddess's will and her own deeds, although rumours concerning her mother's origin still tempt her enemies to test the queen.

Vaktar-Jorden may appear severe to an outsider, but beneath its icy reserve lies a culture that prizes fellowship, an honest word and the warmth of a shared hearth. It is easy to die here after setting out unprepared—and just as easy to win friends for life by refusing to leave them behind in a blizzard.
