# Bithorn Foothills

![[Assets/Images/Bithorn_Foothills.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Bithorn Foothills** extend east of the [[Горный хребет Грань Тьмы|Edge of Darkness]] in a succession of stony hills, heather moors and narrow valleys. For most of the year the land seems harsh and nearly colourless, but late summer turns the heather into an unbroken lilac sea, above which storms gather especially often.

This is the homeland of the [[Гойдаир|Goidair]] and of most [[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]]. Settlements are built on slopes above the spring flood line, and roads follow old sheep tracks. Oath-stones stand between clan lands: flat slabs carved with a lineage's marks. To break such a stone is considered a graver insult than to destroy an empty house.

Bithorn is rich in peat, iron-bearing clay and herbs that survive the icy wind. Thunder-heather is particularly valued: after lightning strikes it, its roots remain alive and send out dark-blue shoots. Healers add them to warming infusions, while smiths burn dry branches when tempering ritual weapons.

Travellers must fear more than weather. [[Вепречеловек|Boarfolk]] hunt in foggy hollows, [[Вермин|vermin]] lurk among stone screes, and some old roads pass through the lands of [[Кентавр|centaurs]]. A guide is easily found here, but almost no one can be hired to cross another clan's border without invitation.
