# Myra

![[Assets/Images/Myra_City.jpg]]

## Main text

**Myra** is the religious centre of [[Амон-Астат|the Empire of the Sun]], standing upon the western shore of [[Озеро Нехмет|Lake Nekhmet]]. The city rises from the water in broad terraces: quays and markets occupy the lower streets, while schools for scribes, pilgrims' houses and bright temple courts stand above them.

At dawn, the first rays reflect from the lake and illuminate the eastern facades before the sun appears above the rooftops. Myra's architects employ the effect deliberately: passages and colonnades are aligned so that on certain days of the year light touches the altars in sequence. Temple astronomers watch over this precision, and any deviation prompts an examination of the calendar, the water level and the condition of the ancient foundations.

Priests, healers and keepers of archives are trained in the city. Pilgrims come not only to pray, but to seek counsel: the temples preserve records of droughts, illnesses, the movement of the stars and the properties of medicinal plants. The markets around the sanctuaries sell simple solar amulets, oils, papyrus and intricate instruments of glass.

Myra gives the impression of a severe city, yet in the evenings families, musicians and students gather upon the waterside steps. While the temples preserve their ceremonial silence, the embankment fills with conversation and the scent of baking bread—a reminder that in Amon-Astat light is cherished not only as a divine power, but as the warmth of ordinary life.
