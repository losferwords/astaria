# Mavka

![[Assets/Images/Mafka.jpg]]

## Main text

Mavka is the young queen of [[Обитель|Obitelj]], chosen after the overthrow of [[Ивар Свирепый|Ivar the Fierce]]. When commanders, elders and Wendish nobles gathered in the throne room, each believing themselves worthy of power, the Beregini—priestesses of [[Велисса|Velissa]]—led an unknown girl before them. They declared her the goddess's choice, seated her upon the empty throne and ended the struggle before it could become another war.

No family has claimed Mavka, and the girl herself says almost nothing about her life before the palace. She names no home village and usually turns any question about the past towards the person beside her. This remains a mystery to the country, but the Beregini answer only that Velissa's choice should be judged by what it brings to the living.

Mavka appears about seven years old. Her hair is white as fresh snow, her skin pale with a lively flush, and she prefers a simple white cotton dress to heavy ceremonial clothes. The new queen is extraordinarily kind, trusting and affectionate: she embraces weathered warriors, gives wildflowers to courtiers, draws pictures for neighbouring sovereigns and sincerely fails to understand why adults cannot make peace before discussing their borders.

Mavka cannot govern in the conventional sense and listens carefully to the counsel of her faithful [[Страж|Guardians]]. Her decisions often strike the council as childish whims. She may demand that an unknown family be fed instead of holding a feast, send seed rather than a threatening letter, or refuse to punish someone who has honestly confessed an offence. In time, however, her simple acts reconcile hostile households, restore trust in the throne and serve Obitelj better than the most calculating designs of its former rulers.

Guardians, Beregini and town elders turn the child's wishes into affairs of state while trying to preserve their original meaning. An unspoken rule now governs the court: a complicated dispute must first be explained to the queen in simple words, concealing nothing about who will suffer from each choice. This slows the council, but makes it harder for courtiers to hide cruelty inside elegant speeches.

## The little queen

Mavka sees the country as one great household and other rulers as future friends. She calls [[Сигрид Дракендоттир|Sigrid]], Queen of [[Вактар-Йорден|Vaktar-Jorden]], her elder sister, sending pressed flowers, drawings and questions about northern dragons. She approaches Amato with careful kindness. The girl cannot return those killed in Ivar's war, but asks that prisoners and missing people be sought on both sides of the sea.

Ceremony quickly tires Mavka. She likes sitting upon the palace steps, watching artisans work and memorising the names of those who bring petitions. At formal meals, the girl makes certain that food is first sent to the watch, the servants and those waiting beyond the gates. The court has learnt that the surest way to win her favour is not with a gift, but with a story about someone who was helped.

To some, Mavka remains an oddity upon the throne; to others, she has become a sign that Obitelj has at last survived its long winter. While adults search her actions for hidden wisdom, the little queen simply follows her kind heart—and, against every expectation, the country prospers.
