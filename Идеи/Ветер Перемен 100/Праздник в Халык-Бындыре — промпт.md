```text
Create a single still illustration in an exact 16:9 landscape composition. A candid anniversary celebration among exactly four adult adventurers in the timber common room of a stout old harbor tavern, with their tricolor hunting dog lying at their feet. It should feel like a real shared moment among old friends, warm and playful.

Input images in order: 1 is a four-portrait identity sheet, left to right: the bald blacksmith, the hooded woman, the hunter, the priest in a turban. Use these for individual faces and clothing only, and do not use the sheet's four-panel layout. 2 is the dog's identity, markings and collar; 3 is the tavern's architecture and atmosphere; 4 gives the weapon's engraved metalwork, leather grip and elongated wooden haft; 5 is a rendering-style reference only. Retain individual features and clothing cues from the portraits. Redraw every person, animal, prop and the interior in the same modern anime 2.5D illustration style, with the dimensional facial modeling, clean grouped forms, selective contours and soft intermediate tones of image 5. Use the tavern's own low-key warm lantern light, deeper colored shadows and subdued cool light from a distant doorway. Keep every face distinctly recognizable and clearly illuminated.

Compose a medium-wide, lightly oblique view from the open side of a rectangular wooden table, using the floor in the lower foreground for the dog. From viewer left to right, arrange the four friends naturally in a shallow arc around three sides of the table rather than a frontal row; all four faces and gestures should remain distinct. At left, the muscular bald northern blacksmith with a thick dark-gray beard and curled rust-colored mustache wears his worn leather apron and laughs openly, lifting a hefty mug. Just to his left, in a clear patch of wall, an oversized two-handed forged war hammer leans upright, with its entire blocky blunt metal head visible, angular gold-steel engravings, a long dark wooden haft and wrapped grip inspired by image 4. Its head has two blunt striking faces and no axe blades. Left of center, the dark-haired East Asian woman wears her warm brown cloak with the hood up and has an amused, lively face. A single tiny orange flame nibbles the outer hem of one cloak sleeve, plainly attached to the cloth and away from her fingers; her own mug is visible. Right of center, the lean, short brown-haired hunter in his slate blue-gray cowl and leather shoulder strap turns toward his companions with a modest closed-mouth smile and an easy grip on his mug. At right, the brown-skinned bearded priest wears his burgundy-and-charcoal wrapped turban, dark traveling clothes, and small pendant. He smiles faintly as he releases a visible pinch of ground spices from the fingertips of one hand straight into his own mug, with several small open spice trays he has set out beside it. Give each character a separate mug and anatomically clear hands.

Show the dog from image 2 actually reclined on the floor by the hunter's boots, with her sturdy body, relaxed forepaws, upright ears, white facial blaze and chest, rust markings and crescent charm readable at bottom center. Keep the space beneath the table open and use table legs rather than a solid front so the dog is clearly visible. Set the group inside the recognizably weathered, two-story wooden tavern: broad beams, an upper railing, a battered plank table, a few amber hanging lanterns, and layered wood in the background. Keep the room behind these four quiet. Distinguish warm skin, soft fur, worn leather, woven cloth and forged metal by their highlights and edge treatment. Prioritize the friends' expressions, the hand sprinkling spice, the small sleeve flame, and the dog, with the hammer as a clear secondary silhouette. The image is a unified illustrated fantasy painting with no text, graphics, panel borders or watermarks.
```
