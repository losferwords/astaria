# Chapter 050 — Li San's Letter

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 104 NE, winter*

[[Ошу Мати|Oshu Mati]] and [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] entered the [[Храм Дракона|Temple of the Dragon]] to speak with the spirit of [[Ли Сан|Li San's]] guard. Avatar [[Ксан Ляньши|Xian Lianshi]] met them at the entrance, and the women persuaded him that they had come to honour the fallen Dju.

Inside, they met [[Нуа Си|Nuwa Xi]], who helped them find the bodies awaiting burial. Oshu's incense proved too much for her, and Nuwa went outside. Oshu performed the rite. In return for information, the guard's spirit asked her to heal his son. He revealed that he had carried the letter at Li San's own request, and that [[Бао Си|Bao Xi]] was meant to deliver it to the capital.

When Oshu and Mei brought Nuwa to [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]], he realised she was pregnant. Nuwa did not know where children came from, and the innocence of her bewilderment was almost painful. Gilja advised her to remain at the palace.

Meanwhile, the imperial herald arrived. He accused [[Чани Сан|Chani San]] of treason and of supplying the nomads with weapons. Chani denied the charge, but was taken away under guard.

Later, Li San confessed to [[Урист Китин|Urist]] that she had sent the letter accusing her own mother. It was no act of simple cruelty. Li chose to be neither daughter, wife nor hostage to another's will, but a woman no longer prepared to remain silent about what she had seen.
