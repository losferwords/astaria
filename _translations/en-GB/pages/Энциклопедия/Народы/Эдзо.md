# Edzo

![[Assets/Images/Edzo.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Edzo** (蝦夷) are an ancient people of the islands in the [[Рассветное море|Dawn Sea]], whose roots reach into an age when human memory had yet to distinguish myth from history. The Edzo say that they came from a land that no longer exists. No one remembers where that homeland lay or why their ancestors fled.

After settling the northern islands of the Dawn Sea some 2,800 years ago, the Edzo swiftly developed a culture in which discipline and tranquillity became not merely virtues, but means of survival. Their settlements were built in harmony with the natural world: houses hid beneath the trees or stood upon piles beside the water, and every structure possessed a name and meaning of its own.

The new world did not yield to them freely. Within the first centuries, the [[Они|Oni]] appeared upon the islands—people driven mad by malignant spirits. Possession changed the character of the whole nation. The Edzo learnt to restrain fear and emotion, for powerful feelings drew the spirits near. A tradition of inner balance took root: reserve that resembles coldness from without, yet shelters a deep reverence for life.

During the ceaseless struggle against possession, the goddess [[Ицунэ|Itsune]] appeared before the Edzo and brought them the art of deliverance: the path of the [[Онмёдзи|Onmyoji]], spirit-hunters who seek out the possessed, perform rites of purification and guide wandering souls into the other world. Faith in Itsune has remained the heart of Edzo spiritual life ever since. She is held to be unknowable and mysterious, keeper of the balance between living and dead. Her shrines are quiet places where prayers are whispered so as not to disturb the spirits.

The Edzo are a people of measured gestures and exact words. Their culture is permeated by respect for ritual: the tea ceremony, the forging of a blade and even bathing take place on appointed days beneath the proper sign of the moon. They revere purity of body, spirit and space, and regard disorder as a sign that corruption draws near.
