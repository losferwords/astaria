# Underkhan

![[Assets/Images/Underkhan_City.jpg]]

**Underkhan** is Wildlands' second-largest port after [[Город Бахара|Bahara]]. Its smaller bay cannot receive great fleets, but is sheltered from most waves; fishers, merchants and northern sailors favour its calm quays over Bahara's costly bustle.

The [[Авгарцы|Avgars]] built it to trade with [[Вактар-Йорден|Vaktar-Jorden]]. Its first warehouses resembled fortified winter camps, and later grew into fish markets, smokehouses, ropewalks and inns.

Underkhan values regular buyers, honest weights and contracts that can be renewed next spring. Northerners bring furs, timber, salt and bone-work; metal, leather, grain and Avgar tools go back. Families on both shores mean Vaktar songs are heard beside steppe melodies.

Its chief danger is weather: sudden squalls can imprison vessels in the bay. On its quays, refusing aid to a drowning person is shameful regardless of their people.
