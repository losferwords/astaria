# Invasion of Achaeus

![[Assets/Images/Acheus_Invasion.jpg]]

By 98 NE, the [[Кризис веры в Амон-Астате|Crisis of Faith in Amon-Astat]] had reached its height. The first rebels had largely been the poor and merchants; now soldiers and nobles from the northern cities of the Empire of the Sun joined them.

Pharaoh [[Рамхотеп II|Ramhotep II]] would not abandon [[Хат-Астат|Hat-Astat]] or his faith in [[Аст|Ast]]. He defended the priests and thwarted attempts to seize neighbouring, more prosperous lands, fearing an international war. The rebels therefore resolved to act without him and crossed into [[Гилас|Gilas]].

Their first target was the border city of [[Город Ахей|Achaeus]]. On the day of the siege, the city was dangerously exposed: [[Персефона Раптис|Persephone Raptis]] was fighting her [[Танатомахия|Thanatomachia]], and the attention of the inhabitants and most of the garrison was fixed upon the arena. Achaeus fell within hours. The rebels occupied it and held its walls for several weeks, awaiting the army of Gilas.
