# Gnoll

![[Assets/Images/Gnolls.jpg]]

## Main text

**Gnolls** are rare anthropomorphs found in the deserts and prairies of [[Архея|Archaea]]. They resemble upright jackals or hyenas, with a long and dangerous muzzle and a body covered in coarse fur.

A gnoll is aggressive, but seldom bold enough to attack without a pack. Such packs may number between ten and fifteen individuals.

Gnolls can fashion primitive implements—sticks, clubs and sharpened stakes—though they readily resort to tooth and claw.

These predators chiefly hunt large mammals such as gazelles, buffalo and antelope. Attacks upon small human caravans and solitary travellers, however, are far from unknown.
