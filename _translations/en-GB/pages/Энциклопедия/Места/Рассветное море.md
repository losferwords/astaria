# Dawn Sea

![[Assets/Images/Sunrise_Sea.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Dawn Sea** washes the eastern shores of [[Астария|Astaria]] and is the first to receive the morning light. Unlike the open reaches of the [[Закатное море|Sunset Sea]], it is scattered with islands, small archipelagos and submerged ridges. Even an experienced mariner seldom sees an entirely empty horizon here: a distant shore, a sail or the crest of another island almost always darkens the view.

The currents of the Dawn Sea are intricate and change with the season. In spring they carry warm water and shoals of fish northwards; in autumn they turn back, raising fog around [[Туманный остров|Mist Island]]. The sailors of [[Амато|Amato]] read the water by drifting petals, the warmth of the wind and the colour of the clouds. Foreign charts soon become obsolete, for sandbars and passages between the reefs shift after every great storm.

The island harbours trade in rice, timber, salt, dyes and dried seaweed. Pearl oysters are cultivated in the shallows, while fibres from coastal grasses are woven into ropes that scarcely rot in salt water. On moonless nights, minute sea creatures kindle a blue light around the oars, leaving a luminous wake behind each boat.

Beauty does not make the sea safe. [[Сирена|Sirens]] hunt among the rocks, sudden squalls dash boats against the reefs, and in certain deep waters nets return torn as though something immeasurably stronger than a whale had pulled upon them from below. Old mariners give no names to such tales and silence any question that touches upon the bottomless trenches. The islanders therefore seldom mock a seafaring taboo: even the strangest custom may once have saved an entire crew.
