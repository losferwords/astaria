# Chapter 039 — Two Arbats

*[[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], 104 NE, autumn*

[[Урист Китин|Urist]] went in search of the two arbats entrusted to him by [[Аксель Хан|Axel Khan]]. Authority in a nomad camp rested not only upon orders, but upon how confidently a person could name himself commander among people accustomed to measuring words by strength.

First he found [[Вигдис Меткая|Vigdis the Unerring]], a stern Vaktar who was in no haste to acknowledge him as a war leader. She agreed to march if Urist found her a field cook: the former one was the same Mektig murdered by an unknown woman the night before. Urist offered the duty to [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]], who accepted with unexpected enthusiasm. The command to the sixth arbat was simple: march at dawn.

Vigdis showed Urist several runic tricks: cold carried in a handshake, simple signs of inspiration traced upon the earth. She herself treated them without vanity. In battle she sought not guile, but a glorious death after which she hoped to become an Einherjar.

The fourth arbat waited at the southern end of the camp. It was commanded by [[Сатор Саг|Sator Kitin]], whose legs Urist had recently broken in their duel. Despite the old grievance, Sator agreed to serve under him: ahead lay the defence of a village where he too might have kin. The archers lacked a spotter, and Urist gave that role to [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]].

That evening, Tom trained the weak archers. Among them stood out the young Ludair [[Рон Эллиот|Ron]], too young for war but too skilled a marksman to be readily set aside.

At dawn, Vigdis brought her arbat and the force marched south towards [[Деревня Улехей|Ulehey]]. [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] noticed the army's movement and chose to follow unseen.

After midday, scouts advanced near Blood Blossom Valley. Tom and Ron came under a hail of arrows. Tom escaped behind a veil of darkness, while Ron froze at the sight of the power of [[Мерката|the Goddess of Night]]. Urist ordered an attack upon the slopes, but the archers withdrew, leaving only one Dju prisoner.

Watching from afar, Mei saw the camp stand empty and decided to enter. The war had not truly begun, but it was already testing everyone's vigilance.
