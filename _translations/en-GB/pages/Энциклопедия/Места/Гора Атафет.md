# Mount Atafet

![[Assets/Images/Atafet_from_desert.jpg]]

## Main text

**Mount Atafet** rises above south-western [[Архея|Archaea]], a dark island of stone amid arid plains. Its summit reaches 2785 metres, yet the mountain's greatest riches lie not above ground, but deep within it: veins of iron, copper, silver and rare minerals run so far into the rock that the oldest mines have long since become a world beneath the earth in their own right.

Natural caverns are joined by galleries, bridges and stairways hewn from the stone. Fissures open to the outer air and create a constant draught that carries forge smoke away; local craftspeople can tell the weather by the voices of these currents. Before a storm, Atafet gives a deep, lingering hum, as though an immense beast were waking inside the mountain.

[[Город Атафет|Atafet]], known as the City of Masters, has grown within the most spacious chambers. Its distant branches, however, remain uninhabited. Old supports collapse there, underground waters suddenly engulf passages, and colonies of [[Вермин|vermin]] drag tools and glittering ore into crevices too narrow for any human to follow. A good guide at Atafet is therefore valued no less highly than a good smith.
