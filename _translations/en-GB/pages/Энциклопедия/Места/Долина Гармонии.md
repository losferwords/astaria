# He-Dji-Gu

![[Assets/Images/Harmony_Valley.jpg]]

## Main text

**He-Dji-Gu**, the Valley of Harmony, is a fertile region of eastern [[Архея|Archaea]], shaped by the [[Река Тяо Хэ|Tiao He]] and the web of its tributaries. It reaches from the foothills of the [[Горный хребет Шафар|Shafar Mountains]] to the [[Рассветное море|Dawn Sea]], where stony slopes and bamboo groves gradually yield to lakes, fields and a humid delta.

According to tradition, this was where [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Lang-An]] led the ancestors of the [[Джу|Dju]]. Water carried them safely beyond the deserts, while the gentle climate allowed them to found a state sustained not by endless conquest, but by farming, craft and the patient keeping of order. The valley is therefore revered as the cradle of [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]] and as living proof of the Dragon's covenant.

Generations of inhabitants have carefully reshaped the landscape of He-Dji-Gu. Rice terraces follow the curves of the hills, canals bind field to lake, and shade trees line the roads. Groves and pools are preserved between settlements so that cultivation does not still the breathing of the land. Yet this order has not made the valley uniform: every province takes pride in its own teas, patterns, dishes and festivals.

Three great lakes — [[Озеро Жень|Zhen]], [[Озеро Чанг|Chang]] and [[Озеро Шуан|Shuan]] — gather the waters of the Tiao He and serve as centres of trade. Grain, paper, cloth and pottery pass across them towards the capital and the sea. When a single canal is closed, dozens of settlements feel the loss; wardens of the water consequently command a respect seldom accorded to anyone but judges.

The harmony of the valley demands unceasing labour. A flood, a quarrel between clans or a predator emerging from [[Лес Шеньянь|Shenyan]] can upset its balance more swiftly than the court can issue an edict. To a traveller, He-Dji-Gu appears tranquil and welcoming, yet beneath the beauty of its gardens lies an intricate web of obligations. Every bridge, grove and teahouse has already been promised, dedicated or entrusted to someone.
