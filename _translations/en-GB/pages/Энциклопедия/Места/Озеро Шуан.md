# Lake Shuan

![[Assets/Images/Shuan_Lake.jpg]]

**Shuan** (爽) is a large lake in the northern part of [[Долина Гармонии|He-Dji-Gu]], feeding tributaries of the [[Река Тяо Хэ|Tiao He]]. Its name means ‘clear’ or ‘refreshing’: cool wind comes down from the mountains, and a light freshness remains above the water even in high summer.

Bamboo groves, tea slopes and low wet fields alternate around the lake. People of [[Город Юйфу|Yufu]] build narrow rain-canopied boats and use them as moving shops. In the morning, greens, tea, fish and small paper lanterns for long journeys are sold on the water.

Reeds cover Shuan's northern coves. Birds nest there, and small river creatures hide among the roots. In the rainy season the water rises over old paths, leaving fertile silt and many lost things behind, from children's toys to temple tablets.

The lake is considered tranquil, but dense morning fog can entirely conceal the opposite shore. Boatmen steer by bells on shore pavilions and by water sounds against the pilings. If every bell falls silent at once, local people advise mooring until the wind finds its path through the valley again.
