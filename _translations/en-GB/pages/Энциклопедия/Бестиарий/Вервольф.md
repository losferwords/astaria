# Wolven

## Main text

The **Wolven** are a rare people of the [[Первые|Firstborn]], born with permanent lupine features. They may be found upon the [[Хельвандский полуостров|Helvand Peninsula]], on [[Туманный остров|Mist Island]], among the [[Призрачные горы|Ghost Mountains]] and within the [[Дымовая Чаща|Smoke Thicket]], as well as in Obitelj's [[Веданский лес|Vedan Forest]] and [[Сребролесье|Silverwood]].

Their appearance varies greatly between bloodlines. Some have wolf's ears, heavy fur across the shoulders and powerful claws set against an almost human face; others possess an elongated muzzle, formidable jaws and a heavy gait. These traits are theirs from birth and owe nothing to the moon, a bite or a curse. A Wolven neither transforms into a wolf nor passes their nature to a human.

They hear with extraordinary acuity, read scents with ease and find their way where rain has long since washed away every human trail. Most live in small communities of kin, cherish a shared memory and prefer woodland paths to great roads. To the Wolven, family need not mean blood alone: once a person has been welcomed into the circle of the hearth and the familiar scents of home, they are defended as one of their own.

Foreigners often mistake Wolven reserve for savagery and a bared fang for a threat. The Firstborn themselves consider such conclusions evidence of a poor nose and still poorer manners. They are dangerous when defending a child, a home or a wounded companion, but bear no innate hatred of humankind.

For a traveller, meeting a Wolven may prove a deliverance. Few guides are more sensitive to changing weather, a nearby predator or an old path buried beneath snow. Their trust, however, must be earned: a Wolven will sooner believe the scent of fear, blood or falsehood than the fairest words.
