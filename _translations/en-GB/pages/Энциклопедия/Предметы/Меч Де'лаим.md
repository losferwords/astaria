# De'laim Sword

![[Assets/Images/De'laim.jpg]]

## Main text

The **De'laim** is a long two-handed sword carried by the [[Горец|Highlanders]]. Its purpose reaches beyond close combat: Highlanders use their De'laim swords as lightning rods through which they command a storm. The weapon is ceremonial as well. After the [[Рунштоирм|Runstoirm]], every aspiring Highlander must make the long journey through [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]] to [[Гора Атафет|Mount Atafet]], where the blade is forged for them.
