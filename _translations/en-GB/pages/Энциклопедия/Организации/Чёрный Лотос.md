# Black Lotus

![[Assets/Images/Black_Lotus.jpg]]

## Main text

The Black Lotus is a mysterious underground organisation spoken of in rumours throughout [[Город Ванпур|Vanpur]] and its surroundings. Its aims, structure and true members remain unknown.

The brotherhood's founding date is unknown. Rumours of the Black Lotus may have circulated as early as 96 NE, but this does not establish when the organisation itself came into being.

The name of the Black Lotus most often surfaces in connection with disappearances, strange coins bearing a scratched flower and talk of ‘black injections’. Some consider the organisation a criminal network, others a figment of frightened townspeople's imaginations, while still others prefer not to speak its name aloud.

Reliable information about the Black Lotus is scarce. Even those who claim to have encountered its people can seldom say whether they met genuine members or merely followed someone else's traces, rumours and fears.
