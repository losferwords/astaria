# Chapter 034 — The Morning after Halyk-Byndir

*[[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], 104 NE, autumn*

The morning after the celebration in [[Бордель Халык-Бындыр|Halyk-Byndir]] resembled a trial without a judge: everyone awoke beside evidence against themselves.

[[Урист Китин|Urist]] regained consciousness naked in a henhouse, embracing a chicken and his hammer. He realised he was in a small village near [[Город Бахара|Bahara]]. [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] awoke covered in blood. [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari's]] blade, the [[Рактапатр|Raktapatra]], clung to his arm and refused to come free. Six vials of unknown blood lay in his bag.

Then they heard an explosion. [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] was found nearby without her dragon, her hands scored by countless tiny wounds, at the centre of a charred circle of trees. Soon a barn caught fire, and [[Бао Си|Bao Xi]] emerged naked and shaven bald. The world around them seemed to be reconstructing the night from its most absurd fragments.

The travellers searched for their belongings, Gilja's turban and the trail of the missing [[Мэйлун|Meilong]]. In the home of a young Avgar woman they met her husband, a [[Мектиг|Mektig]] who had also visited [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]]. Then fresh trouble arose: a madman with black eyes was shooting indiscriminately in a field.

It was [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]]—or rather the dark part of his being, escaped into the world. They found him tied to a post, but the attempt to free their friend descended once more into chaos: Bao Xi attacked, and [[Мерката|Mercate]] carried him away.

Back in Halyk-Byndir, the heroes learnt fragments of the truth. They had become drunk, thrown the dragon from a window to see whether he would return, and then wandered in every direction. Mei also heard that a blindfolded woman—[[Кирида Ариса|Kirida Arisa]]—had attacked her in the harbour.

Gilja used one of the blood vials in a hunting rite and heard a woman scream from below. The revelry was over. Now he had to discover what price he had already paid the goddess.
