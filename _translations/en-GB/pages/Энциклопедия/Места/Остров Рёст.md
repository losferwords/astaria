# Ryost

![[Assets/Images/Ryost_Island.jpg]]

## Main text

**Ryost** is a small uninhabited island in the [[Хтоническое море|Chthonic Sea]], north of [[Остров Закра|Zakra Island]]. Its stony shores offer scarcely a sheltered cove; strong winds prevent tall trees from taking root, while fog can conceal the rocks until the final moment.

The [[Вактары|Vaktars]] discovered Ryost some five centuries ago and regard it as territory of [[Вактар-Йорден|Vaktar-Jorden]]. No permanent settlement ever took hold. There is too little soil for fields, fresh water gathers only in a few hollows of stone, and a winter storm may prevent any ship from approaching the shore for months.

Seabirds nest upon the island. In spring their cries carry far across the water, and the cliffs become covered in feathers and grass. Fishers collect eggs only from the lower ledges and leave most clutches untouched. To violate this custom is believed to invite hunger aboard the entire ship.

Ryost was once used for rites preceding long voyages. Stone circles, pillars bearing weathered runes and hearths in which no ordinary fire was kindled remain upon the heights. Modern Vaktars seldom hold ceremonies here, but captains still leave a broken piece of an old vessel upon the shore before taking a new ship to sea for the first time.

The island draws seekers of ancient vows and mariners wishing to escape pursuit. Yet shelter readily becomes a trap: if the weather turns, a small crew may be stranded among the rocks with no means of departure. Ryost tests not a person's strength, but their wisdom in knowing when to refuse a risk.
