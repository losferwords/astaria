# Thalassians

![[Assets/Images/Talassians.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Thalassians** are a people of sailors, shipwrights and inventors who arose upon the [[Полуостров Таллас|Tallas Peninsula]]. Their ancestors were [[Хтониды|Chthonids]] who founded the north-western republic of their empire here. Separated by sea from [[Остров Хтон|Chthon]], the settlers gradually developed customs of their own, and after the fall of the old civilisation made [[Талассия|Thalassia]] an independent power.

To a Thalassian, a ship is more than a means of travel. It is home, workshop, livelihood and proof of its captain's character. Children in harbour families learn early to tie knots, distinguish winds and recognise when the deck is answering a wave incorrectly. Even someone who has never sailed is usually bound to the sea by trade: sewing sails, drying fish, keeping accounts or making parts for the shipyards.

Thalassians delight in contests, public argument and risk. In a tavern, one may wager silver upon a vessel's speed, an archer's aim or the truth of an improbable tale. A reckless loss earns no admiration, however. Honour belongs to the person who understands the danger, accepts it freely and then answers for the consequences.

Their mistrust of magic exists beside the worship of [[Калипсо|Calypso]], goddess of fortune and discovery. Thalassians do not expect their patron to rescue a poorly prepared vessel. In their eyes, her favour notices the sailor who inspected the rigging, studied the chart and still chose to go where earlier crews turned back.

Shipbuilding, mechanics and the craft of gunpowder are guarded with professional pride and secrecy. A maker will gladly demonstrate a beautiful device, but will not surrender its design to a foreigner. Rare compasses are found among [[Наварх|Navarchs]] and state navigators; most crews rely upon stars, currents and accumulated experience. This culture of close observation makes the Thalassians seem inventive even when they possess no unusual instrument at all.
