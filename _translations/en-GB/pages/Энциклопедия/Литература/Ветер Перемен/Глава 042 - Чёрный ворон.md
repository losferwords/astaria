# Chapter 042 — The Black Raven

*[[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], 104 NE, autumn*

The festivities in [[Деревня Улехей|Ulehey]] continued, but [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] had little thought for the wedding. He grieved over the damaged [[Рактапатр|Raktapatra]] and resented [[Урист Китин|Urist]], who had promised to make him a weapon but had become a bridegroom instead.

Seeking blood for the blade, Gilja went to the river in hope of finding Dju scouts, but encountered [[Нуа Си|Nuwa Xi]]. She recognised him and immediately showered him with questions about the nomads, Urist, [[Мэй Ву|Mei]] and [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]]. Gilja brought her into the village and decided to punish Urist by introducing Nuwa as his former lover. The wedding table fell silent. Mei hurriedly smoothed over the embarrassment, seated Nuwa beside her and gave her Avgar spirits. Nuwa became drunk almost at once and lost consciousness.

Urist withdrew with [[Ли Сан|Li San]] to the wedding tent. There he learnt that Li did not desire intimacy: the marriage was her means of strengthening the alliance between the Avgars and the Dju. Urist received this with understanding. So the village would not suspect deception, they staged a clamorous performance, rocking the tent pole and feigning passion.

Gilja went back on watch. Across the river, two Avgars sent with him by Sator attacked. One impaled himself upon Raktapatra; the second nearly severed Gilja's head before an arrow from Tom stopped the blow. Tom had noticed Gilja's absence and gone in search of him, arriving just in time. The attackers' blood almost restored the blade to its former size.

A vast black raven with utterly black eyes settled upon one corpse. Tom recognised it as a northern bird, alien to these lands and unnatural besides. The raven croaked while staring directly at him, as though showing the way.

Meanwhile, Mei prevented [[Вигдис Меткая|Vigdis]] from marching jealously upon Urist's tent. Vigdis saw the raven and called it an ill omen of calamities to come. She then ordered Gilja, her field cook, to prepare a dish from his homeland. Unable to disobey, he cooked a spiced meal that inflamed her desire. Their night continued in the nearest house.

Thoroughly drunk, Mei created a display of fiery lights. Tom followed the raven alone. It led him to the abandoned mines. Deep within, he found a chamber smelling of corpses, with a mountain spring, a waterwheel and five tunnels. The echo of his shout shook dust from four passages, leaving one clear.

At the tunnel's end, Tom discovered a mound of bodies. [[Мерката|Mercate]] lay among them. So did [[Ошу Мати|Oshu Mati]], whom the vermin had mistaken for dead. Nearly suffocating, Tom dragged the goddess out, while Oshu used her last strength to cling to Mercate's leg.

At the exit, Tom tried to lower Mercate by rope, but his strength failed. The rope pulled him down, and the fall paralysed his back. Oshu landed upon a stone rise.

In the morning, Gilja spoke of Tom, and the heroes found their friend upon the slope beside Mercate's body. Back in the village, Tom awoke as Matthew and spoke with the goddess in the halls of his mind. Mercate explained that without her stolen scythe she could neither sever her bond with Arisa nor regain her strength. The only path back was to break her connection with every Shadow, including Matthew.

Matthew agreed.

Tom remembered his past in [[Лунаар|Lunaar]]: his elder brother Matthew, a winter hunt upon the Gorsafon River, cracking ice and the death after which the Goddess of Night came to a freezing boy. Tom's mind had wished to serve Mercate, but deemed itself unworthy. Thus it was not Tom himself who became a Shadow, but the protective presence of his brother within him.

Now the bond was broken. Matthew ceased to exist separately, and for the first time Tom became whole. His back still needed healing. Gilja undertook the task and restored feeling to his legs by feeding Raktapatra with Tom's blood and part of the dark power hidden within him.
