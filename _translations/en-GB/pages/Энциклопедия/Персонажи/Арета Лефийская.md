# Areta of Lepha

![[Assets/Images/Areta.jpg]]

## Main text

**Areta of Lepha** is the young queen of [[Катахтонос|Katachthonos]], daughter of [[Эвридика Лефийская|Eurydice of Lepha]] and [[Телемах II|Telemachus II]], king of [[Талассия|Thalassia]]. She grew up between two courts and was regarded from childhood not merely as a child of ruling houses, but as a living bond between the states of the [[Полуостров Таллас|Tallas Peninsula]].

After her mother's death, Areta inherited the throne of Katachthonos. At the same time, she remains the lawful heir to Thalassia, for Telemachus II has no other acknowledged successors. To some subjects this promises an eventual union; to others, it threatens the disappearance of the young country into a mightier maritime power.

Areta must govern a society frightened of its own past. Every decision concerning education, ancient discoveries or people with unusual gifts recalls the [[Падение Хтона|Fall of Chthon]]. Excessive caution will rob Katachthonos of its future, while any reform judged too daring will make its people see their queen as an heir to their ancestors' perilous ambitions.

Areta's youth is often mistaken for weakness, yet it enables her to ask questions her elders long ago ceased to voice. Must the Chthonids forever pay for the choices of the Profitis? Can the legacy of Chthon be studied without being worshipped? Can a dependent colony ever become a homeland in its own right?

In public ceremony, Areta remains composed and emphasises continuity with her mother's reign. In private council, she must choose between Thalassian interests, the expectations of Lepha's nobility and the needs of people who care more for a sound harvest than a dynastic dispute. Upon those choices rests whether Katachthonos remains a refuge for survivors or becomes an independent realm.
