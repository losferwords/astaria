# Nayan

![[Assets/Images/Nayan_City.jpg]]

## Main text

**Nayan** (耐陽) is a great city in the west of [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]], on a tributary of the [[Река Тяо Хэ|Tiao He]]. It is considered the industrial capital of the Empire of Fire: forge smoke hangs constantly above its quarters, and its workshops make weapons, tools and fittings for cities throughout the lake country.

Ore once came directly from the mines of the [[Горный хребет Шафар|Shafar Mountains]]. After the [[Джу|Dju]] lost part of their lands to the [[Авгарцы|Avgars]], Nayan became more dependent on distant supplies, and the San family made metal trade the foundation of its power. The city was formally governed by elder [[Чани Сан|Chani San]], but her attempt to protect her younger son from abductors led to secret arms shipments for nomads and an accusation of treason.

In the winter of 104 NE, the army of [[Аксель Хан|Axel Khan]] besieged Nayan. Its defenders could not hold the walls, but made the city a corridor for civilians: people were led out through the eastern gates and sent down the Tiao He towards [[Город Чанг-Ша|Chang-Sha]]. Much of the city was destroyed or burned.

Nayan did not remain a dead monument to defeat. [[Ван Сан|Wan San]] survived the assault and led its rebuilding. Forges are burning again among damaged streets, but their fire now reminds the people not only of craft glory, but of the price of weapons once turned against them.
