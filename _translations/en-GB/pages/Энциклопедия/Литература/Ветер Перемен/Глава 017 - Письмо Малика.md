# Chapter 017 — Malik's Letter

*Qadir, 104 NE, spring*

The darkness rising from the coin did not have time to consume [[Ошу Мати|Oshu]]. [[Руфу|Rufu]] spoke to her. The voice of the God of Death, terrifying yet familiar to the [[Ваку|Vaku]], intervened where [[Мерката|Mercate]] had tried to reach too deeply. He drove the Goddess of Night from Oshu's mind, leaving not a sense of rescue, but of stern belonging: Rufu did not readily release his own.

Meanwhile, the consequences of the disturbance mounted in Samaan. [[Урист Китин|Urist Kitin]] completed a new hammer, but the guard of western [[Кадир|Qadir]] had already tired of the noise, darkness and suspicious artefacts these foreigners had brought into the city. Where [[Мститель|Avengers]] ruled, chaos was not treated as a traveller's inconvenience.

[[Данис ибн Синай|Danis ibn Sinai]] left the company beyond the city walls. He needed to read [[Малик Аль-Азвар|Malik al-Azwar's]] letter away from prying eyes. Malik wrote that he knew of Mercate's coins and that his disappearance after the oasis had been feigned. He had not been deceived as easily as it might have seemed.

Those lines changed much. Malik saw more than he revealed; Tom's bargain with Night was no longer secret, at least from one of the first Avengers; and the travellers' entire passage through Qadir had unfolded beneath another's watch.

Danis passed the scroll to [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] and went to prison. It was neither defeat nor simple surrender to the guard, but an attempt to enter Qadian order upon its own terms. Sometimes, in the land of time and sand, the only way forward is to let a door close behind you.
