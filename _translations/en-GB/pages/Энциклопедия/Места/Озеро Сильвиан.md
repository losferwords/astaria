# Lake Silvian

![[Assets/Images/Silvian_Lake.jpg]]

## Main text

**Lake Silvian** is a large freshwater lake in northern [[Лес Иомар|Iomar Forest]]. Its water remains clear even after heavy rain, and its shores are overgrown with fern, white sedge and trees whose roots descend beneath the surface. In the morning a thin mist lies above it, making its islands resemble the backs of immense sleeping beasts.

To [[Друид|Druids]], Silvian is among the foremost sacred places of [[Иомар|Iomar]]. Here, tradition says, the first settlers met [[Церунна|Cerunna]] and heard her teaching on living in accord with the forest. The goddess is still believed to walk these shores, so pilgrims try to speak quietly and leave behind nothing but tracks in damp earth.

Fishing is permitted only in certain coves, and cutting shore trees is forbidden. Water for rites is drawn at dawn into small clay vessels and never sold. It is thought not to heal by itself, but to help a healer hear what the ailing body truly asks for.

To an ordinary traveller, Silvian is beautiful and calm, but the forest around it has almost no permanent roads. Trails shift after rain and growing roots, animals are unafraid to draw close, and one careless fire can forever close the path to the local keepers' hospitality.
