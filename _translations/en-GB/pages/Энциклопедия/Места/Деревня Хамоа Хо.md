# Hamoa Ho

![[Assets/Images/Hamoa_Ho.jpg]]

**Hamoa Ho** is a large coastal settlement and capital of [[Хамоа|Hamoa]], at the mouth of the [[Река Мангайа|Mangaia]] on the eastern shore of the [[Полуостров Туануку|Tuanuku Peninsula]]. In the [[Манаи|Manai]] language, its name means ‘Shelter of the Wanderers’.

The Manai ancestors first landed here more than two and a half thousand years ago. Their earliest homes stood on sandy rises between sea and river, while boats sheltered settlers until they learned to use local timber and palm leaves. Oldest families still call stretches of shore by the ships' names.

Hamoa Ho is not a city in the usual sense, but an enormous village stretching along the coast and gradually entering tropical forest. Homes stand beside communal meal shelters, boatbuilders' workshops, fish-drying grounds and medicinal gardens; river channels and beaten paths connect many districts instead of streets.

There are almost no stone walls or heavy towers. Gardens, woven fences and high bamboo platforms mark its boundaries. During danger, signal drums carry warning faster than a messenger, and people lead children to the river while preparing boats for evacuation.

At its centre is the council house, where [[Атуа Та'Вай|Atua Ta'Vai]], First [[Хранитель|Warden]] and Hamoa's chief, receives people. Its open-sided building is scarcely larger than a common festival shelter: a ruler, the Manai say, must hear sea and neighbours' voices, not hide behind palace walls.
