# Chapter 008 — No Way Home

*No Way Home*

[[Кассиопея Атанатос|Cassia]] succeeded in changing the future she had seen. Her visions shifted once more, but [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth]] no longer appeared in them. Grieving for her fallen beloved, she journeyed north to tell the [[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]] of the great deed performed by the young [[Горец|Highlander]], who gave his life to save thousands of others.

In [[Деревня Эрвин|Erwin]], Cassia found [[Лисандра мак Рейн|Lisandra]] and told her the wondrous story of Kenneth's journey and death. His mother was seized at once by fierce pride and sorrow for her son. Cassia remained in the mac Rayne household for several more days, then departed for an unknown destination.

Lisandra carried word of [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth the Stormcaller]] throughout the Thunder Clans. From these tales, the saga *Call of Thunder* was composed in [[Город Солаис|Solais]].

In its final chapter, Lisandra recounts her son's deed and the great sacrifice that brought life and peace to distant southern lands.
