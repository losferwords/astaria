# Lake Hornindal

![[Assets/Images/Hornindal_Lake.jpg]]

## Main text

**Lake Hornindal** is a large lake in central [[Кельн|Keln]], encircled by ancient [[Лес Сольвид|Solvid Forest]]. At the place where the [[Река Нид|Nid]] flows from it stands [[Вактар-Йорден|Vaktar-Jorden's]] capital, [[Город Трелла|Trella]].

Narrow bays deeply cut its shores. Fishing quays, forest villages and hunters' winter camps lie hidden among the trees. Morning mist can wholly conceal the opposite shore, so boatmen keep to familiar headlands and listen for the wooden chimes hung by settlements.

Hornindal feeds the Nid and, through it, connects to the [[Белый пролив|White Strait]]. Timber is floated across the lake, people are carried over it, and the capital receives supplies through its waters. In spring, immense rafts wait for the outlet to break free of ice; in autumn, crews race to finish their journey before the first lasting frosts.

Fishers catch silvery lake fish in its deep cold waters, but avoid certain hollows where nets return torn or coated in dark slime. Elders blame Solvid's roots, young hunters an exceptionally large predator; neither explanation has convinced everyone.

To Trella's people, the lake is both provider and defence. Its water tempers part of the winter cold, the forest shields settlements from wind, and the narrow exit through the Nid is easily defended. Yet in years of late ice-break, the capital is cut off from the sea, a reminder that a favourable position is nature's gift, not a city's eternal right.
