# Vanpur

![[Assets/Images/Vanpur_City.jpg]]

**Vanpur** stands on the trade route between northern [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]] and [[Город Сангалла|Sangalla]], south of [[Город Танассар|Tanassar]] and [[Деревня Бадракали|Badrakali]]. Here caravans from [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]] are weary of jungle but have not yet become part of the capital.

Rajati shops, Dju tea houses, itinerant workshops, drovers' yards and small shrines share its streets. Traditions have not vanished, but their edges have softened: they are discussed, sold, altered and adorned with foreign words, sometimes broken with the confidence of another ancient custom.

The city grew around a broad red-stone caravan road. Clay Rajati walls gain carved Dju balconies; store sheds become tea houses; old temple courts become markets for charms, spices and false antiquities. Multilingual signs are often misspelt, but locals say a sign has done its duty if a foreigner understands where to go and what to pay.

Vanpur is more tolerant than most Suraj Ka Ghar cities, but not safe. It is crowded with debts, secret commissions and people who arrived under one name and plan to leave under another.
