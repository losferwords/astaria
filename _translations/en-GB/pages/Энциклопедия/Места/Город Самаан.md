# Samaan

![[Assets/Images/Samaan_City.jpg]]

## Main text

**Samaan** is a great port upon [[Залив Фиальсахра|Fialsakhra Bay]] and the capital of western [[Кадир|Qadir]]. It is ruled by the Eternal Princess [[Амира Аль-Азвар|Amira al-Azwar]], whose authority joins courtly order to the need to bargain with merchants, desert guides and people arriving by sea.

Goods from the western coast and caravans from the [[Пустыня Халисат|Khalisat Desert]] pass through the city. Above ground, Samaan smells of spices, hookah smoke and salt wind; beneath its streets run old drains in which creatures that became local legends long ago can still survive. The discovery of an enormous she-spider there showed that the city's history runs much deeper than its official chronicles.

Samaan values the order of the [[Мститель|Avengers]], but it is not a silent palace. It is a place of audiences, secret letters and bargains, where even a prisoner may become a guest if their knowledge is more useful than immediate punishment.

The city comes most alive after sunset, when the heat yields to the sea breeze. Tea houses open on the roofs, music sounds in the courtyards and merchants discuss the next caravan's route. Beneath the outward festivity lies careful calculation: Samaan can turn news, a rarity or another person's secret into merchandise before the rest of Qadir understands its worth.

For travellers, it is a convenient gate to the Kingdom of the Seven Gardens. Here one may hire guards and a guide, exchange a sea chart for a desert route or hear a rumour about what lies beneath the city. The important thing is to remember that in Samaan, even a chance acquaintance rarely remains entirely accidental.
