# Chapter 037 — Fire in the Kitchen

*[[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], 104 NE, autumn*

[[Ошу Мати|Oshu]] followed [[Кирида Ариса|Arisa]] to the khan's palace, but could not enter. [[Нефела|Nephele]], who had reasons of her own for infiltrating the palace, came to her aid. Oshu brewed a sleeping draught, and Nephele led [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] inside.

Thus Oshu witnessed Arisa stealing the dragon, then rejoined the others after the pursuit. Yet [[Город Бахара|Bahara]] allowed no one the peace to gather their thoughts.

[[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] decided to test butterfly blood upon the [[Рактапатр|Raktapatra]] and found himself seized by sudden desire. His attempt to find agreeable company was thwarted by poisoned potatoes, after which he also cut open his leg.

Mei decided to set fire to the kitchen of [[Бордель Халык-Бындыр|Halyk-Byndir]]. [[Урист Китин|Urist]] and [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] tried to extinguish the blaze while Gilja became trapped in a hole in the floor. Attempting to help, Oshu pulled him down by his injured leg, and he fell directly upon her.

The fire was finally put out. Oshu healed Gilja's leg and the effects of the potatoes. Love flared between them, unexpected and hot as the kitchen after the blaze.

On the ground floor, Urist, Tom and Mei argued, each defending in their own fashion what mattered most to them. The company then left Bahara and travelled south towards Dragon's Maw, following the [[Река Улын-Гол|Ulyn-Gol]] upstream.

On the road, they passed Avgars marching to join [[Аксель Хан|Axel Khan's]] army. At night, their camp stood beside another familiar company: [[Пирей Никос|Piraeus]], [[Эйдан мак Кинсли|Aidan]] and [[Ишикути Макото|Makoto]].
