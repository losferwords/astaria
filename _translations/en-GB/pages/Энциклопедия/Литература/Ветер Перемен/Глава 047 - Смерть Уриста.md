# Chapter 047 — The Death of Urist

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 104 NE, autumn*

After his night with [[Ли Сан|Li San]], [[Урист Китин|Urist Kitin]] awoke dying. Agony tore through his abdomen, darkness filled his sight, and a body accustomed to wounds and drink suddenly refused to obey. He staggered into the corridor and demanded a physician, but [[Мэйлун|Meilong]] reached him first. The young dragon forced him towards his hammer.

Li San awoke in tears but could do nothing. Urist died before her eyes. At the final instant, Meilong closed his teeth upon the hand gripping [[Топор Смертоносец|Deathbringer]].

Urist's spirit looked upon his body from outside. [[Ицунэ|Itsune]] came to guide him into the realm of the dead. They sailed west and spoke of life, death and what a person leaves behind. Retsu Ishida interrupted their passage, pleading for her grandson's return: his soul was bound to Deathbringer and his road was not yet ended. Itsune took pity and returned Urist to his body.

He awoke changed. His love for Li San now returned only when he held the hammer. It was salvation shaped like a sentence.

[[Чани Сан|Chani San]] returned to the palace. Outraged by rumours of disorder—particularly at the tavern—she commanded everyone involved to attend an audience. Tom showed Li San the letter he had found, and she asked him to return it immediately to where it had lain. He managed to slip into the tavern and replace it in the chest, but arrived late for the appointed hour.

At a great table, Chani San conducted a polite meal like an interrogation, drawing information from her guests with gentle words and precise questions. When Tom appeared, she questioned him as well. At the reception's end, a merchant asked to take Tom upon the imperial hunt, while Chani herself requested to speak privately with Mei.
