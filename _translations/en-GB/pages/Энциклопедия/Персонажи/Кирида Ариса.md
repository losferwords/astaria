# Kirida Arisa

![[Assets/Images/Arisa_Kirida.jpg]]

## Main text

**Kirida Arisa** (霧田 有咲) is an [[Онмёдзи|Onmyoji]] from the port of [[Город Осуми|Osumi]]. Her long grey hair and near-silent tread make her easy to mistake for someone far older. The chill in her manner, however, comes not from age but from remembered humiliation and a stubborn resolve never again to be rendered helpless.

From an early age, Arisa served as a guardian and junior priestess at the temple of [[Ицунэ|Itsune]]. She protected the shrine, escorted pilgrims and watched over its head priest, [[Ишикути Макото|Ishikuchi Makoto]]. Arisa took these duties so seriously that she seemed to believe a single lapse in order might affront the goddess herself.

In 103 NE, Thalassian pirates attacked Osumi. The temple's defenders were overwhelmed, and Arisa was taken captive with the other priests. Several months of slavery shattered her former understanding of honour. She survived, escaped and recovered her weapon, but regarded her own deliverance as proof of disgrace rather than courage.

Her attempt to commit seppuku did not end her path. Itsune refused the death of her devoted priestess and returned Arisa to life as an Onmyoji. Arisa never speaks of what occurred beyond death. When asked directly, she replies only that the goddess left her work that must not be abandoned a second time.

Arisa now hunts the [[Они|Oni]] with a tracker's dispassionate focus. She cannot abide pity, yet treats those who have endured captivity, possession or the loss of command over their own lives with particular care. Where others see a broken person, Arisa sees someone who may yet reclaim the right to choose their next step.
