# Namibua

![[Assets/Images/Namibua_Village.jpg]]

## Main text

**Namibua** is an ancient [[Ваку|Vaku]] village deep in the [[Джунгли Аконда|Akonda Jungle]] and the spiritual capital of [[Вакумара|Vakumara]]. No broad road reaches it: pilgrims follow narrow trails, cross dark streams and navigate by carved posts bearing the names of dead guides.

Once a settlement of hunters and healers, Namibua changed after [[Руфу|Rufu's]] death, when [[Шаман|Shamans]] brought the god's preserved body here and raised a necropolis above it. Tradition says Rufu had taught the first priests anatomy, healing and funerary rites nearby.

Traditional homes now surround a complex temple. Outer courts welcome pilgrims; initiated Shamans guard the inner rooms. Smoke, resins and bitter herbs scent the air. The sick are treated and tribal envoys received by day; at night drums accompany speech with ancestors.

Chiefs bring disputes to the square before the necropolis, where the Dead God's presence recalls the price of bloodshed. High Shaman [[Кинто Мулунгу|Kinto Mulungu]] also makes decisions affecting the whole confederation here.

This capital of death is strikingly alive. Markets sell fruit, cloth and painted masks, children play among enormous roots, and families prepare food for visiting kin. Namibua proves that memory of the dead can sustain life rather than hold it captive to the past.
