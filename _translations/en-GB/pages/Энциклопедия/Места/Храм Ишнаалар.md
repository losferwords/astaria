# Temple of Ishnalaar

![[Assets/Images/Ishnalaar_Temple.jpg]]

## Main text

**Ishnalaar** is the religious centre of the [[Круг Друидов|Circle of Druids]], hidden in the central part of [[Лес Иомар|Iomar Forest]]. It has neither one high tower nor a single enclosing wall: the temple consists of stone halls, wooden galleries, sacred groves and open spaces where tree crowns take the place of vaults.

A small settlement has grown around the complex. Its people grow food, receive pilgrims, tend the paths and assist the priests. They try not to build more than is necessary: when an old tree begins to press against a house, the wall is more often moved than the trunk cut down. Ishnalaar's buildings therefore seem as if the forest is slowly drawing them into itself.

Four times each year, Druids from throughout [[Иомар|Iomar]] gather here. At equinox and solstice celebrations, they exchange news, discuss the state of rivers and forests, resolve disputes between settlements and decide where help is most needed. Solemn rites stand beside long practical conversations about sick trees, failed harvests and animal migrations.

A traveller is not driven from Ishnalaar without cause, but access to its inner groves is granted only with the keepers' permission. Some paths are intended for the initiation of future Druids; others lead to places where people ask Cerunna not for a miracle, but for the strength to accept a natural loss.
