## The warrior republic

The Republic of Gilas is one of the strongest states in [[Астария|Astaria]], raised among the river valleys and fortified cities of western [[Архея|Archaea]]. Its inhabitants call their country a republic, although [[Гиперион I|Hyperion I]] has ruled it without interruption since independence. To the Ellians there is no contradiction: individual cities retain their councils and laws, while the king is regarded above all as supreme warrior, judge and living symbol of the common state.

In 854 ChE, Gilas began as a [[Хтониды|Chthonid]] colony in western Archaea. General Hyperion founded [[Город Аргос|Argos]] and led the new settlements, which remained part of the Chthonid realm for centuries. Only after the [[Падение Хтона|Fall of Chthon]] in 0 NE did Hyperion declare the kingdom-republic independent and assume the title of its first—and still its only—king. In the centuries that followed, this Chthonid inheritance became an uncomfortable family secret. The [[Эллийцы|Ellians]] gladly honour the antiquity of their cities, but seldom acknowledge kinship with the people of the fallen island.

After the catastrophic deeds of the [[Профитис|Profitis]], the study of magic was forbidden throughout the republic. Ellian children with [[Хтонотема|Chthonotema]] continued to be born during the first centuries of the New Era, but such cases gradually became vanishingly rare. Fear of uncontrolled power survives in the law to this day: a foreign mage may expect respect for their weapon, but never trust.

Military service is prominent in the public life of Gilas, though it does not wholly define it. Roads move armies swiftly, but also carry trade between the cities; a square may muster the militia one day and host a play, festival or contest the next. Old veterans are honoured, yet philosophers, physicians, architects and artists are heard beside them. The republic's finest commanders, the [[Идеал|Paragons]], embody its measure of courage. A hero, however, is expected to possess not blind severity, but a clear mind, a sense of humour and the ability to defend a life worth living.

Many cities hold official contests for athletes, wrestlers, throwers and warriors, with prizes bestowed by local authorities. The games celebrate not only victory, but the beauty of human skill; during a festival, a visitor may be invited to the common table before anyone asks which contender they support. An ordinary bout follows strict rules, and killing an opponent is a crime against the Ellian code and the honour of the arena. The grim exception is the [[Танатомахия|Thanatomachia]], a trial to the death for the right to be named a Paragon.

Gilas thrives on trade along the [[Река Неда|Neda]] and [[Река Тавропос|Tavropos]], on grain from [[Регион Элессия|Elessia]], on workshops and busy ports. Its cities maintain public baths, drainage and irrigation, paint their colonnades, stage plays and debate the nature of justice for hours at a time. A traveller soon encounters the country's contradictions: harsh law beside generous hospitality and boisterous festivals; admiration for valour beside the cruelty of the arena; promised liberty for citizens beside the existence of slavery. This tension makes Gilas a reliable ally, a dangerous foe and a place where the word *hero* is never simple.
