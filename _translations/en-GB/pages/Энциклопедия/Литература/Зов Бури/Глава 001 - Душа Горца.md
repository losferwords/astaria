# Chapter 001 — The Highlander's Soul

*The Highlander's Soul*

*79–97 NE*

Thunder rolls over Erwin. Wind drives the rain along its narrow streets, and stone houses rooted in the mountainside are barely visible through the downpour. Somewhere above, beyond the clouds, lie the passes of the [[Горный хребет Грань Тьмы|Edge of Darkness]]. Yet for a young woman in one of those houses, the whole world fits between two peals of thunder: her first child is about to be born.

[[Лисандра мак Рейн|Lisandra]] is afraid. Still, with each new peal, hope mingles with her fear. Among the [[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]], people believe that the sky itself takes notice of a child born during a storm. Such children are called **the Named**. One day, their names may be sung beside the clan hearths; their hands may hold the lightning from which others seek shelter.

When her son is born, Lisandra names him [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth]]. The storm still rages outside, but beside her now breathes someone for whom she can both fear and hope. She sings “Il la ba far noelly”, an old lullaby in the Dair tongue. To the infant, her voice becomes the first familiar sound in a vast world he cannot yet understand.

This is where the road into [[Астария|Astaria]] begins: a small house in [[Деревня Эрвин|Erwin]], a mother's song and rain upon the threshold. Beyond its walls lie the windswept hills of northern [[Кельн|Keln]], mountain roads, the sea and foreign shores. For now, the warmth of her arms is enough.

The Highlander’s Soul speaks in Lisandra's voice. Her words accompany her son from his first days to his hardest trial: listen to the wind, remember your home, hold to your path even when shadows gather. There is pride in Kenneth's future, and a tenderness that the promise of glory cannot drown.

He grows up among the [[Гойдаир|Goidair]], for whom a clan's name means the people gathered around one fire, winters weathered together and a promise kept. In these lands, a future chieftain is taught to be the first to meet danger. His mother speaks of a homeland where the mountains keep the memory of his ancestors and the sea carries their names. One day, he too will become a [[Горец|Highlander]]: a warrior whom the storm answers, whose strength must protect those who trust him.

But years of preparation stand between a child born beneath the thunder and a person able to command lightning. Every spring, the adult Named climb the slopes of the Edge of Darkness. Some return transformed. Others are carried down unconscious. For some, that ascent is their last.

In spring 97 NE, Kenneth's turn comes.

On an exposed slope, the participants in the [[Рунштоирм|Runstoirm]] form a circle and join hands. Their families and mentors remain below. Before the first lightning strike, they can still hear the wind and feel one another's palms; afterwards, everything will depend on the endurance and will of those who hold the chain.

They must pass the charge through the entire circle without letting it escape into the earth. Accept the pain. Stay on their feet. Keep their fingers closed. As soon as one participant falters, the others have to bear more of the force. Whoever lets go of their neighbours may save themselves — at the expense of those who remain in the circle.

Lightning enters the chain, and years of preparation narrow to a single effort: endure. The Named lose consciousness while the storm still courses through their bodies. By the end of the ordeal, only Kenneth remains awake. All its power passes to him.

Lisandra's words of courage and glory take on the weight of pain endured. Her son is now a Highlander. Ahead lies the sea: beyond it, at [[Гора Атафет|Mount Atafet]], weaponsmiths forge swords that can channel lightning. To obtain his own, Kenneth must leave the home at the heart of his mother's songs since his birth.
