# Lake Shinda

![[Assets/Images/Shinda_Lake.png]]

## Main text

**Shinda** is a lake of [[Амато|Amato]] in the southern part of [[Туманный остров|Mist Island]]. It stretches approximately 11.6 kilometres from north to south. [[Город Касумито|Kasumito]] stands on its shore, beside the outflow of the [[Река Кумокава|Kumokawa River]]. The river runs south and enters the [[Закатное море|Sunset Sea]] at [[Деревня Аманохама|Amanohama]].

## Water and lakeside livelihoods

Shinda and the Kumokawa feed irrigation canals supplying the surrounding fields of the [[Луга Тамано|Tamano Meadows]]. Life on the lake is closely tied to the harvests of the farming settlements in the south of the island.

Fishing villages and boat landings line its shores. Fish from the lake and produce from nearby farms supply Kasumito. Residents travel to the town by water, carry provisions and maintain links with neighbouring settlements.

## The wooded shore and shrines to Itsune

On the side facing the foothills of the [[Призрачные горы|Ghost Mountains]], trees reach the water's edge. Paths are being laid between them to small new shrines to [[Ицунэ|Itsune]]. The wooded shore is becoming a place of worship as the people of the lake district establish these sanctuaries.

For the [[Эдзо|Edzo]], Mist Island is a relatively new land, distant from Amato's principal islands and the old shrines to [[Ицунэ|Itsune]]. Temples are being built, torii gates erected and new places of worship established for the Goddess of Wind along Shinda's shores. There are still few local architects and shrine-building craftspeople; they are being trained by architects from the capital, [[Город Кайто|Kaito]], invited on the orders of [[Мориока Масанори|Daimyo Masanori]].

Unsettling rumours from the forests and mountains strengthen the inhabitants' devotion to Itsune. People seek to make the lakeshores worthy of their beloved goddess and create more sacred places where they can pray and ask for her protection. Temple construction continues alongside the familiar lives of fishers and farmers.

One small shrine on the wooded shore is being built jointly by fishers, returning soldiers and settlers. It has become their shared post-war undertaking and a place visited by the relatives of missing people. Here they pray to Itsune for their loved ones to return home.

## Winter on the lake

Winters at Shinda are damp and maritime, with rain, sleet and strong winds. The water remains largely free of ice, and boat services continue when the weather permits.

## The search for the princess

Questioning and checks have already been carried out at lake and river landings following the disappearance of [[Аматсу Ами|Amatsu Ami]], but they yielded no information about her whereabouts. Small armed parties continue to search the shores and forest paths. The dangers of the thicket and the approaching winter hinder their progress.

A reward has been offered for reliable information about the princess, attracting both willing helpers and people hoping for easy money.
