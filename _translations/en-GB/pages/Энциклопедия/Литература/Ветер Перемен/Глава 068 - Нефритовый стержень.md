# Chapter 068 — The Jade Rod

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 105 NE, summer*

In the hall of judgement, the travellers debated what to do with [[Цинь Ша|Qin Sha]]. The adviser begged them to spare his life. Soon [[Сунь Ляньши|Sun Lianshi]] led everyone into the square to calm the people and publicly name the guilty man. The travellers told of the battle, the rescue of [[Дзин Сан|Dzin San]] and General Li's true role. The empress ordered Qin Sha taken away in chains.

She then asked the group to descend beneath the [[Храм Дракона|Temple of the Dragon]] and discover what had happened to her husband. Urist revealed that General Guan Li was [[Гиперион I|Hyperion]] and proposed a meeting between the rulers at the barrier, where the god of war and the empress could speak in safety.

On their way, the travellers visited [[Нуа Си|Nuwa Xi]], who opened the door by announcing that her waters had broken. [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] saw her through a difficult labour, and Nuwa gave birth to a girl, [[Чинь Си|Chin Xi]]. [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] was left to care for mother and child.

The others went in search of Hyperion and [[Чень Ляньши|Chen Lianshi]]. Their search brought them to the [[Бордель Уй-Джан|Ui-Jan Brothel]], the “Jade Rod”, where Hyperion had used General Li's authority to reserve the entire establishment. An evening of Avgar moonshine ended in a complete loss of memory.

The morning spoke more eloquently than any witness. [[Мэй Ву|Mei]] awoke in the bed of one of the brothel's women and saw [[Ниса|Nisa's]] name carved into the wood with a knife and surrounded by hearts. A burnt hole gaped in the ceiling, revealing a naked Gilja bound and suspended from the roof. Urist awoke in a nightdress inside the privy, with Hyperion in the adjoining cubicle holding one of his fingers.

Downstairs, Chen was apologising to the proprietress, [[Сяо Ша|Xiao Sha]], who had spent the night saving the establishment from destruction and subduing the god of war with chains. Hyperion thanked her for her hospitality and set off for the western gate of the Temple of the Dragon.

Urist went to find Li San and tell her of Dzin's rescue and Chani's release, but at the [[Дом Белых Парусов|House of White Sails]] learnt that his wife had already gone to the [[Таверна Красный Журавль|Red Crane Tavern]].
