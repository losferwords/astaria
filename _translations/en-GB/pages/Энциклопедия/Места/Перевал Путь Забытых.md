# Pass of the Forgotten

![[Assets/Images/Path_of_Forgotten_Pass.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Pass of the Forgotten** is a harsh crossing in the northern [[Горный хребет Грань Тьмы|Edge of Darkness]], one of the few known ways through the range. Its narrow road joins the lands of the [[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]] and [[Лунаар|Lunaar]], so watch-posts, boundary stones and signs of old clashes are more common here than safe lodging.

Weather changes without warning. Fog hides the route, rain turns stone grit into slippery sludge, and after a cold night the trail takes on nearly invisible ice. Without a guide, a traveller can mistake a side gorge for the road and find themselves somewhere from which no return ascent is possible.

The pass is named for people whose names could not be brought home. Goidair leave tied threads and small memory stones beside the trail; Ludair light shuttered lanterns so a lost soul may see the way through fog. [[Мерката|Mercate]] may find a future [[Тень|Shadow]] anywhere in Astaria, but Lunaar's nearness and the pass's especially high death toll have made the Forgotten return from here notably often.

Despite the danger, the pass remains an important road for merchants, envoys and fugitives. During the thaw, whole caravans gather at its entrances to await several quiet days. Then one reliable word about the weather can be worth more than a horse laden with silver.
