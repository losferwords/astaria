# Tiao He

![[Assets/Images/Tiao_He_River.jpg]]

**Tiao He** (迢) is the largest river in [[Астария|Astaria]] and the chief waterway of [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]]. It gathers water from the [[Горный хребет Спина Археи|Spine of Archaea]], the [[Горный хребет Шафар|Shafar Mountains]] and distant southern lands, then divides across [[Долина Гармонии|He-Dji-Gu]] and runs east.

To the [[Джу|Dju]], it is more than water. Their oldest cities rose upon its banks, its floods feed their fields, and grain, tea, paper, timber and people travel with its current. They call it the Dragon's long road, joining the mountains where water is born to the sea where all completed things depart.

Its many branches form [[Озеро Жень|Lake Zhen]], [[Озеро Чанг|Lake Chang]] and [[Озеро Шуан|Lake Shuan]]. Families of keepers watch their canals, crossings and dams. Their work is barely noticed in a good year, but one wrongly opened lock can leave a village without water or drown a harvest.

Flat-bottomed barges, fishing boats and covered passenger vessels move along the wide river. Quays, temple bells and watch-fires mark the banks. In the rains, familiar landmarks vanish, the current quickens, and tree trunks swept from the mountains become more dangerous than any bandit.

Tiao He is generous, but not obedient. The Dju believe harmony lies not in halting a river, but in directing part of its force while leaving enough freedom for the water itself. After a hard flood, they therefore restore the passage to old floodplain lakes before the palace road.
