## The Seven Gardens

Qadir is the ancient Kingdom of the Seven Gardens, founded by the [[Кадийцы|Qadians]] in central [[Архея|Archaea]]. Amid the burning expanse of the [[Пустыня Халисат|Khalisat Desert]], its lands seem miraculous: gardens and fields flourish around seven great lakes, markets murmur beneath awnings, and cities of pale stone rise above the water. To a Qadian, water means life, and time is the price life inevitably pays for every change.

Ancient scripture tells that [[Альзаман|Alzaman]] appeared in [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]] and led several thousand people eastward to [[Озеро Зураиф|Lake Zuraif]]. There the migrants founded [[Город Сераф|Seraph]], planted the first gardens around its waters and established a realm in which the motion of stars, sand and human life became an act of faith.

Qadians esteem learning, trade, eloquence and hospitality. The inner courts of wealthy households shelter shade, water and fruit trees from the desert wind. In the caravanserais, physicians, merchants, stargazers, alchemists and guides share the same meal. It is said here that haste can rob a person of their future, while delay may destroy their present.

Alzaman has no priesthood of his own. His favour is sought by sages, physicians, astronomers, alchemists and students intent upon reaching the heart of a matter and putting what they discover to human use. They work in gardens, infirmaries, libraries and workshops, debating beneath shaded awnings. They resemble elders and pupils in travelling robes and turbans rather than servants of a single temple. Their curiosity is constructive: Qadians do not roam the world merely for the sake of motion, nor seek at any cost to break through the boundaries of the future.

## Three parts of one kingdom

King [[Астиаг II|Astyages II]]'s war against the [[Авгарцы|Avgars]] brought about the [[Раскол Кадира|Schism of Qadir]] and the making of the first [[Мститель|Avengers]]. After the king's death, Alzaman's three warriors divided authority so that no ruler could ever again lead the whole people after their own hunger for glory. Qadir was parted into three realms, yet kept one faith, one language, shared trade roads and the conviction that the Seven Gardens form a single land.

## Western Qadir

The west holds the Gardens of [[Озеро Кхарим|Kharim]], [[Озеро Джамсар|Jamsar]] and [[Озеро Шаради|Sharadi]]. Here the breath of [[Залив Фиальсахра|Fialsakhra Bay]] is felt most strongly: caravans meet sea traders, and foreign wares become Qadian fashion most readily in the capital, [[Город Самаан|Samaan]]. The region is ruled by [[Амира Аль-Азвар|Amira al-Azwar]], daughter of [[Малик Аль-Азвар|Malik al-Azwar]]. Western schools of the Way of the Blade teach future Avengers to wield two swords and decide their course before an opponent has completed a movement.

## Central Qadir

The central realm embraces lakes [[Озеро Зураиф|Zuraif]] and [[Озеро Суэйд|Suwayd]]. Here stand Seraph, the sacred [[Храм Дар Мараат|Temple of Dar Maraat]] and the court of [[Абу Али аль-Хассан|Abu Ali al-Hassan]]. Its schools follow the Way of Times, cultivating patience, perception and the ability to act between two moments. The region's religious importance means that its decisions are often treated as the voice of all Qadir, although the other rulers jealously guard their independence.

## Eastern Qadir

The eastern lands surround [[Озеро Рашах|Lake Rashah]] and [[Озеро Альсалим|Lake Alsalim]]. They are governed from [[Город Кафат|Kafat]] by [[Умар ибн Ла-Ахад|Umar ibn La-Ahad]]. His Way of the Desert joins learning about the world to mastery of the sand: a pupil must understand the land, endure hardship and make an ally of the desert itself.

The three realms argue over taxes, caravan routes and the meaning of Alzaman's will, but open war between them is regarded as the greatest sacrilege. Every Qadian knows that if one Garden dies, the desert draws nearer to all the rest.
