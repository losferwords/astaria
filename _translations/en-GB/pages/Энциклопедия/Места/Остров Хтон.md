# Chthon Island

![[Assets/Images/Chthon_Island.jpg]]

## Main text

**Chthon Island** is a tiny fragment of a once-vast land that sank beneath the [[Хтоническое море|Chthonic Sea]] during the [[Падение Хтона|Fall of Chthon]]. Only a small part of the former island remains above the surface, like the summit of a shattered mountain. Its cliffs descend almost sheer, while in calm weather the clear water reveals broken roads and buildings fading into the depths.

[[Город Орион|Orion]], once an immense city, did not survive above the water. Only fragments of walls, isolated steps and nameless ruins remain among the stone—shards of a past too slight to convey the former scale of the capital. Most of the city was destroyed or sank with the ground upon which it stood.

The surrounding waters are perilous to ships. Sunken debris forms reefs, currents run through what were once streets, and ancient metal structures can tear open a vessel's hull. The few helmsmen willing to approach Chthon use charts that mark not only the depths, but the outlines of the drowned ruins.

Most inhabitants of Astaria fear Chthon as they would a flame. The memory of the catastrophe and dread of the [[Профитис|Profitis]] have made the island an ill omen, and sailors prefer a long detour even in fair weather. Only desperate hunters of relics attempt to reach the ruins. The [[Хтониды|Chthonids]] regard such expeditions with unease: to an outsider, the drowned remains may be a treasury; to them, they are homes, graves and the final testimony of a world destroyed by its own arrogance.
