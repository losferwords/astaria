# Blaine of Ruche

![[Assets/Images/Blane_From_Ruche.jpg]]

## Main text

**Blaine of Ruche** is a [[Надаир|Nadair]] man born in the little village of [[Деревня Руше|Ruche]]. His family had kept bees since his childhood, although Blaine himself was more interested in the study of plants and the effects their properties could exert upon honey and the human body.

At twenty, he resolved to devote himself entirely to the flora of [[Лес Иомар|Iomar Forest]] and become his village's [[Друид|Druid]]. Such an ambition is difficult to fulfil in [[Иомар|Iomar]], for [[Церунна|Cerunna]] teaches the Druidic arts only to women. Blaine was not one to surrender easily. He chose to uncover the mysteries of nature by his own efforts, determined to equal—and perhaps surpass—the members of the [[Круг Друидов|Circle]].

For more than ten years, Blaine conducted experiments in the selection and cross-breeding of plants, until he discovered the influence of [[Кристалл Души|Soul Crystals]] upon their growth and physiology. Such practices are strictly forbidden throughout Iomar, and Blaine was offered a grim choice: death or exile. He chose the latter.

For several years thereafter, Blaine wandered ceaselessly through the southern lands in search of remarkable plants for his forbidden experiments, until he came to [[Город Тиза|Tiza]]. There, a young priestess named [[Шани-Хат-Су|Shani-Hat-Su]] helped him find work as a gardener in one of the temples of [[Хат-Астат|Hat-Astat]].

More recently, Blaine was seen in the eastern city of [[Город Бахара|Bahara]], where he opened a shop of his own and even managed to heal the young dragon [[Мэйлун|Meilong]].
