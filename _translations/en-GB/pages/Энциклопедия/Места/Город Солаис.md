# Solais

![[Assets/Images/Solais_City.jpg]]

## Main text

**Solais** serves as the customary capital of the [[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]], owing to its fortunate position in the delta of the [[Река Изгелуат|Isgeluat]] and to the largest harbour in central [[Кельн|Keln]]. Here, ‘capital’ means a meeting place above all else. No clan wishes to submit entirely to another, but all require a city in which ships may be repaired, alliances sealed and disputes argued beneath the protection of common custom.

The harbour opens onto [[Штормовой залив|Storm Bay]], so the sea determines both the wealth and the character of Solais. Fishers, warriors and captains are accustomed to putting out in weather that people of other realms would consider madness. Songs of thunder are heard here not as warnings, but as promises of a trial to come.

The upper city spreads across stone ledges above the harbour. Here stand the longhouses of the clans, the sanctuaries of [[Тарун|Tarun]] and the hall where the Highlanders assemble. No banner holds the place of honour permanently: their order is changed before every great council, lest even the arrangement of a room make one lineage master over the rest.

Lower Solais smells of pitch, wet wool and sea salt. Upon its quays one may find a guide to the passes, hire a ship or hear news before it reaches the inland settlements. During a great thunderstorm, the townspeople kindle lights in their windows and gather beneath the eaves to listen. Some await a blessing, some remember the dead, while the Named try to discern whether the sky is speaking to them alone.
