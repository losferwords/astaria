# Navchin Urt

![[Assets/Images/Navchin_Urt.jpg]]

## Main text

**Navchin Urt** is a bold, flirtatious and somewhat childish [[Авгарцы|Avgar]] woman blessed with vivid charisma and an easy disposition. Since childhood she has relied more upon beauty and agility than strength or judgement. Carefree and cheerful, Navchin finds a place in any company with ease—and leaves chaos behind her just as readily, seldom considering the consequences.

In her youth, she stole the lover of [[Айсан Баят|Aysan Bayat]], a woman of a considerably harsher temperament. The deed began a lasting feud. Aysan never forgave her and, according to rumour, is prepared to settle the matter with an axe.

For a time, Navchin worked at [[Бордель Халык-Бындыр|Halyk-Byndir]], enjoying the attention of its patrons and living extravagantly. She eventually left to join the nomads as a mercenary, motivated less by conviction than by a hunger for adventure and memorable stories.

Navchin took part in the siege of [[Город Найян|Nayan]] in 105 NE, but soon tired of war's horrors and deserted without feeling that she had done anything wrong.

She now travels with [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja Kangarlavehotri]]—perhaps out of a concealed affection, or merely from an unwillingness to be bored.
