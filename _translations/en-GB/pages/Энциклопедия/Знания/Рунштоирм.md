# Runstoirm

![[Assets/Images/Runstoirm.jpg]]

## Main Text

The **Runstoirm** is the rite that initiates future [[Горец|Highlanders]]. Only the Named may attempt it: children of the [[Гойдаир|Goidair]] born during a thunderstorm, after years spent preparing body and will.

Each spring, the Named who have come of age climb the eastern slopes of the [[Горный хребет Грань Тьмы|Edge of Darkness]]. On an exposed stretch of ground they form a circle, join hands and wait for the storm. Their families and mentors remain below; once the rite has begun, no one outside the circle may break the chain.

The trial begins when lightning strikes one of the participants. The Named must prevent the charge from passing into the earth and conduct it around the entire circle until its force has been shared among them. The pain, fear or loss of consciousness of one person increases the burden upon all the others. To unclasp one's hands is to save oneself at the cost of everyone still standing in the chain.

Even after years of preparation, the Named suffer terrible burns, lose consciousness and sometimes die. The survivors are carried down from the slope and left undisturbed for several days: the thunder continues to sound within their senses, and an uncontrolled discharge of power could injure anyone nearby. Only after they have recovered does it become clear who has truly gained command of lightning.

The fewer participants who remain conscious at the end of the Runstoirm, the greater the share of power each receives. There have been occasions when a single member of the Named bore the entire storm. One such Highlander was [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth the Stormcaller]].

The rite is regarded not as a contest, but as a test of the courage to share danger. Anyone who desires another's fall for the sake of greater power has already betrayed the very meaning of the circle. The Goidair therefore honour not only the mightiest survivor, but the one who kept the weakest companion upon their feet.
