# Chapter 006 — The Price of the Antidote

*Gilas, 103 NE, summer*

The Red River inn proved a trap rather than refuge. In [[Город Аргос|Argos]], where walls, markets and ports lived under [[Гиперион I|Hyperion I's]] gaze, even a chance roof could belong to people with designs of their own.

Local criminals told the travellers they had been poisoned. The antidote would come only in exchange for stealing an important artefact from Hyperion's palace. The demand was nearly absurd: yesterday they had hidden from guards; today someone wanted them to enter the heart of Gilas's power.

[[Урист Китин|Urist]], [[Томас Робинсон|Thomas]], [[Хонсу-Намун|Khonsu]] and [[Мэй Ву|Helena]] doubted the threat. The poison had appeared too conveniently when desperate agents were needed. Yet doubt did not remove danger: if real, time was against them; if false, someone was still driving them towards a near-inescapable trap.

They asked [[Данис ибн Синай|Philoctetes]] to determine whether poison was truly in their bodies. The strange Qadian scholar was one of the few in Argos whom they could approach with both wound and question.

Meanwhile, they searched for [[Никандр Иеропонтид|Nikandros Hieropontid]], the fruit merchant meant to receive [[Рамат-Хат-Су|Ramat-Hat-Su's]] letter and place them on a ship to Amon-Astat. They found him in a warehouse closely connected to the same gang.

Talk collapsed into a brawl. The smell of fruit mingled with dust, sweat and fear. Gang members fell one after another, every blow making the old plan less possible. Nikandros fled once Ramat's letter could no longer shield him from consequences.

When silence returned, the warehouse belonged to the travellers, but their road out of Gilas was closed again. The promised sailors were untrustworthy, the would-be palace thieves dead, and Nikandros gone without help or explanation. Argos was becoming too small, and every delayed hour brought the guards nearer.
