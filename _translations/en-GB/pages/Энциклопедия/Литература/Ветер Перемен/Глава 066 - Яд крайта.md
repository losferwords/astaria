# Chapter 066 — Krait Venom

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 105 NE, summer*

[[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] awoke amongst the Rajati and found that his friends were gone. That evening he heard the call of [[Нуа Си|Nuwa Xi]]: her brother had vanished, and the door was covered in blood. A hunting rite pointed to [[Бао Си|Bao Xi]] inside the [[Храм Дракона|Temple of the Dragon]], where he was fighting someone.

The Avatars at the gate refused Gilja entrance until one of them, half Rajati himself, understood him and provided him with red palace robes. Gilja found Bao near the library, fighting both a monster afflicted by a disease from [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]] and a cloaked man. They killed the monster, but the man escaped with a casket of jewels.

Meanwhile, [[Сунь Ляньши|Sun Lianshi]] demanded that the charges against [[Урист Китин|Urist]] and [[Мэй Ву|Mei]] be withdrawn, but the captain of the guard invoked [[Цинь Ша|Qin Sha's]] signature. The trial of [[Чани Сан|Chani San]] was about to begin, and the group escorted the empress there disguised as a guard.

A disturbance in the dungeon interrupted the trial. Below they found a broken door and a dead guard, then discovered Chani San beside the temple wall. She said that [[Айсан Баят|Aysan Bayat]] and [[Кирида Ариса|Kirida Arisa]] had escaped, while she herself had been unable to climb over the wall. The guards surrounded them all, but several hours later the empress personally released the travellers. Chani remained to await trial.

Urist learnt that Chani had indeed sent weapons to the nomads, but only because she was being blackmailed: the abductors held her younger son, [[Дзин Сан|Dzin San]]. The threatening letters remained in Nayan, and Tom proposed reconstructing them from Chani's recollection as evidence.

In the library, Gilja examined a bowl Mei had found in the House of White Sails. Bao Xi recognised it: the former empress, [[Лин Си|Lin Xi]], had eaten her final bowl of soup from it. Traces of blood revealed the venom of a banded krait, a snake native to Suraj Ka Ghar. Gilja remembered that his great-uncle bred such snakes.

When the trial was due to resume, it emerged that Qin Sha and General Guan Li were absent. Both had supposedly departed for an urgent council of war.
