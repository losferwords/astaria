# Harpy

![[Assets/Images/Harpy.jpg]]

## Main text

**Harpies** are exceedingly rare anthropomorphs found both in open country and among highlands and hills. A harpy resembles a bird with a human head and torso, while the rest of the body is covered in grey, brown, red or white plumage. Its arms form the leading edges of its wings but retain hands and long, grasping fingers. A harpy can seize prey in flight, cling to a cliff or carefully lift a small object without fully folding the wing. Harpies live in small family groups, nesting in caves set into sheer cliffs.

Harpies seldom use weapons. They hunt large animals in flocks, including human prey, and may watch a herd or travelling company from a great height for hours. When one creature strays or lingers behind, they seize it in the fingers of their wings and bear it away to their cave-nest.

Among harpies, only the females hunt. The males build, maintain and guard the nest.
