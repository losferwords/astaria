# Rhea Melit

![[Assets/Images/Rhea_Melit.jpg]]

## Main text

**Rhea Melit** is a [[Талассия|Thalassian]] [[Наварх|Navarch]], captain of the armed merchantman *Litei* and daughter of the renowned mariner [[Неарх Мелит|Nearch Melit]]. She carried both lawful and forbidden cargo through the western waters of the [[Хтоническое море|Chthonic Sea]], relying upon experience, courage and the measure of audacity without which a Thalassian captain seldom lives to achieve renown.

In 98 NE, Rhea took [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth mac Rayne]] aboard, expecting the Highlander to protect the ship from foul weather. A storm broke upon the open sea. Kenneth's attempt to subdue it only strengthened the tempest, and the *Litei* sank with her crew off the coast of [[Остров Хтон|Chthon]]. Rhea survived and began gathering money, materials and sailors for a new vessel.

Two years later, her search brought her to besieged [[Город Атафет|Atafet]]. The city council promised to help build her ship if the Navarch joined its defence, and Rhea accepted without hesitation. She encountered Kenneth once more and became one of the few [[Имитей|Imithei]] willing to defend the city against the army of [[Гилас|Gilas]]. These events are recounted in [[Зов Бури|*Call of Thunder*]].

By 104 NE, Rhea had become involved in a far more perilous design. She stole a plan from the archives of [[Калипсо|Calypso]] and delivered it to [[Хангор|Khangor]], hoping to discover a means of waking the sleeping goddess. The smith-god offered two paths: carry Calypso to the [[Великая Кузня|Great Forge]], or free [[Тарун|Tarun]] in return for his aid. In [[Город Бахара|Bahara]], nereids attempted to use Rhea's name to deceive [[Пирей Никос|Piraeus Nikos]], but the true Navarch revealed her purpose to the travellers. Thus her story continued in the chronicle [[Пока Боги Спят|*While Gods Sleep*]].
