# Neda River

![[Assets/Images/Neda_River.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Neda** is a major river of western [[Архея|Archaea]], flowing out of [[Озеро Митра|Lake Mitra]] and across the lands of [[Регион Элессия|Elessia]]. Its course shifts from broad, placid reaches to stony rapids where the water breaks into white foam and can be heard from several hours' journey away.

The banks of the Neda are densely settled. The river waters orchards, fields and potters' workshops, while flat-bottomed cargo boats carry grain, oil and fired clay. After the spring floods, reddish silt remains upon the floodplain. It is worked into sturdy vessels particularly valued for keeping water cool.

The Neda heron lives among the riverside reeds, a tall grey bird crowned with a crimson crest. It hunts without moving, sometimes waiting for hours before striking, and the warriors of [[Гилас|Gilas]] therefore regard it as an emblem of patience before the charge. Feathers are never plucked from living birds; they are gathered after the moult and woven into commemorative bands.

Remnants of old Chthonid supports survive among the rapids. Most of the bridges have long vanished, but when the water is low, steps emerge between the stones and descend towards drowned platforms. Fishers keep their nets away from them, while antiquarians return each year to ask whether this was once a crossing, a quay, or part of a structure whose true dimensions can no longer be imagined.
