# Ruche

![[Assets/Images/Ruche_Village.jpg]]

## Main text

**Ruche** is a village on the border between [[Лес Иомар|Iomar Forest]] and the [[Даирские луга|Dair Meadows]]. Its houses stand in small groups around old lindens and apple trees, while living hedges are left between yards instead of ordinary fences. The air almost always smells of wax, smoke and sweet herbs.

Forest beekeeping remains Ruche's principal craft. Local families know dozens of wild swarms and distinguish honey by flowering season, gathering place and even the weather at the beginning of summer. Some is sent deep into the forest and to [[Город Муир|Muir]], but the finest honey is not sold before the autumn festival, when the community thanks its bees for the year survived.

The beekeepers do not regard hives as property in the usual sense. If a swarm leaves a tree, they do not pursue it beyond the marked trail: the forest is thought to have reclaimed its due. A foreigner who damages a hollow hive or kills a queen may face not only the villagers' anger, but the judgement of a [[Друид|Druid]].

Ruche appears peaceful, yet nearby paths lead to rare plants, old forest shrines and places where even experienced beekeepers do not go after sunset. To an investigator, the village can provide medicines and guides—if they prove they can treat living things more carefully than merchandise.
