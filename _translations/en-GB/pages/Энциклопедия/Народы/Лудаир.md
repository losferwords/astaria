# Ludair

![[Assets/Images/Ludair.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Ludair** are a people of western [[Кельн|Keln]] and the principal inhabitants of [[Лунаар|Lunaar]]. Among the Dair peoples they are considered the youngest: their ancestors moved steadily west from the central lands until they settled the cold [[Равнина Вайтфилд|Whitefield Plain]], its river valleys and the forests beneath the [[Горный хребет Грань Тьмы|Edge of Darkness]].

The severe climate taught the Ludair to store food, build warm homes and spend no word without need. Foreigners often mistake their reserve for dislike, but among their own they value loyalty, dry humour and the willingness to share what little remains through a long winter. A guest is seldom questioned on the first night: they are allowed to warm themselves, and judgement begins only when they choose to speak.

The Ludair are renowned as archers, hunters and trackers. Children learn early to read prints in frozen earth, judge distance through fog and distinguish the calls of night birds. A good shot signifies no love of killing, but the skill to keep oneself and one's companions from needless danger.

Their chief goddess is [[Мерката|Mercate]]. To the Ludair, night is not only a source of fear, but a time of truth, when outward colours vanish and a person's deeds remain. The [[Культ Меркаты|Cult of Mercate]] preserves old vows, interprets dreams and prepares the [[Тень|Shadows]]. Secrets are therefore treated with composure: not every truth must be spoken at once, but every truth will one day demand an answer.
