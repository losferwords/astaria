# Chapter 030 — The Great Khan's Bread

*[[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], 104 NE, autumn*

In [[Город Даркхан|Darkhan]], each traveller pursued a separate purpose. [[Урист Китин|Urist]] went to the market, tried to steal dates and quickly attracted unwelcome attention. He then burst into a smithy, where a craftsman recognised him and offered a reminder: Urist's debts to the city's smiths had not vanished either. The guard took the Avgar before the Great Khan.

[[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] sought herbs and aid from Sanya, a wise woman. Meanwhile, [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] noticed a dark, cloaked figure. It was a [[Тень|Shadow]] resembling [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]], and he was recruiting Imithei for [[Мерката|Mercate]]. The chase led Mei and Tom to Sanya's house, then into the upper quarter.

To proceed, the travellers lied to the guard: Urist was innocent, while the mysterious Ludair had stolen Tom's coins. It was a crude falsehood, but in Wildlands crudity sometimes sounded more convincing than subtlety.

Before [[Батар Саг|Batar Sag]], Urist found himself unexpectedly at ease. The khan asked whether he believed in [[Хангор|Khangor]]. At the sight of Deathbringer, Batar honoured his guest and offered him bread.

Then a representative of the nomads, a [[Мектиг|Mektig]], arrived and challenged the khan to a Tulaan duel. Batar refused to risk his country for the sake of bandits. Deathbringer thirsted for battle, however, and Urist accepted the challenge in his stead.
