# Oshu Mati

![[Assets/Images/Oshu_Mati.jpg]]

## Main text

**Oshu Mati** is a young [[Ваку|Vaku]] woman and the daughter of [[Рувамба Мати|Ruwamba Mati]]. Upon coming of age, Oshu was expected to become a priestess of [[Руфу|Rufu]]. Instead, she turned away from her family's tradition and left [[Вакумара|Vakumara]], determined to govern her own life.

Travellers later encountered Oshu in the western lands of [[Амон-Астат|the Empire of the Sun]], although she carefully avoided her own people. This may have been because of the terrible wounds upon her face, which she conceals behind a mask of moon ebony. The Vaku believe that such wounds are a particular mark of [[Руфу|the God of Death]], but no one knows with certainty what causes the affliction. All that is known is that its victim swiftly withers and loses their reason. In Oshu's homeland, such people are called **Mara**—literally, “one who did not die”.

Her wanderings bound Oshu to Rufu once more, though no longer as the obedient heir to a priestly lineage. She learnt to invoke the God of Death, speak with the departed and wield the [[Корона Душ|Crown of Souls]], gradually becoming a guide between the living, wandering souls and ancient artefacts. Such a gift demands responsibility: failures in her rites taught her that bringing back the dead cannot be separated from her duty to those who may yet be saved.

Oshu's search for a way to restore Rufu led her to [[Ицунэ|Itsune]] and the traditions of the [[Онмёдзи|Onmyoji]]. Her path now leads not only towards the return of her own god, but towards an understanding of the soul itself—knowledge without which any victory over death may become merely another form of captivity.
