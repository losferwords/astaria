# Amentir

![[Assets/Images/Amentir_Region.jpg]]

## Main text

**Amentir** is the name given to the fertile country surrounding [[Озеро Нехмет|Lake Nekhmet]]. To the north, the region gives way to the [[Пустыня Сехет|Sekhet Desert]], while the western roads lead towards [[Гора Атафет|Mount Atafet]]. Its green shores, gardens and network of canals have earned it the name of the Great Oasis.

Much of the agriculture of [[Амон-Астат|the Empire of the Sun]] is concentrated here. Grain, vegetables and flax grow in the fields; dates and sweet fruit ripen in the orchards; and lakeside villages supply the cities with fish. Canals carry water far beyond the natural shore, so the labour of surveyors and sluice-keepers governs the lives of entire settlements.

[[Город Тиза|Tiza]] stands at Amentir's centre, and the roads around the lake connect the capital with craft cities and caravan routes through the desert. Carts travel by day, while pilgrims and travellers avoiding the heat move by night. Old watch stations have grown into markets where news from every part of the realm may be heard.

The prosperity of the region makes it a coveted prize in every crisis. A drought, a damaged canal or a quarrel between landowners quickly affects prices in distant cities. Beneath Amentir's green surface lies a complex web of agreements and obligations: let one link fail, and the Great Oasis reminds its people that desert surrounds it on every side.
