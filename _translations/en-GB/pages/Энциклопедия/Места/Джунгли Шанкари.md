# Shankari Jungle

![[Assets/Images/Shankari_Jungle.jpg]]

The **Shankari Jungle** is dense northern [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]] forest, extending from the [[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]] border along [[Багровый залив|Crimson Bay]]. Humid air and constant shadow favour snakes, spiders, scorpions and disease-bearing insects; roots and vines hide pits, old canals and entrances to underground settlements.

Shankari is sacred as well as dangerous. [[Нага|Nagas]] live in its depths, and after the spread of [[Ветал|vetals]] even expert hunters avoid straying far from fortified camps. A transformed person may keep a familiar voice and habits long before attacking, making solitary travel perilous even for [[Имитей|Imithei]].

To the [[Раджати|Rajati]], it is a land of trials and revelation. Border settlements gather roots, poisons and resins only in small parties, each choosing a return route and a signal in case someone comes back changed.

Abandoned temples and caves preserve old rites, but treasure taken from the thicket often brings unwelcome attention. Entering Shankari means becoming part of what happens there, never merely observing it.
