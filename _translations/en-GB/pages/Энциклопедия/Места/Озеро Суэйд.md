# Lake Suwayd

![[Assets/Images/Suwaid_Lake.jpg]]

## Main text

**Suwayd** is the southern Garden of [[Кадир|Qadir]], far from the great cities between the central roads and the dry expanses of the [[Пустыня Халисат|Khalisat Desert]]. The lake is shallow, its banks gentle and reed-covered; after the rains, water spreads across the lowlands and creates temporary meadows.

There are fewer stone buildings and rich gardens near Suwayd than at Zuraif. People grow millet, keep goats and gather reeds. [[Деревня Вахрия|Vahriya]] is known for its woven awnings and light boats that can be carried overland when the shore retreats.

The lake is a resting place for migratory birds. In spring the sky fills with their cries, and hunting is temporarily restricted so the flocks can rest. Qadians call Suwayd the Garden of Measure: one may take enough to live, but greed will leave the next season empty.

The southern paths around the lake are poorly marked. Sand swiftly hides tracks, and the identical bands of reeds make a traveller feel that they have already passed the same place. Local guides navigate by birds, the direction of the evening wind and the scent of damp earth.

After especially hot years, stone foundations of old houses appear in the shallows. The inhabitants do not know whether the former settlement was abandoned through drought or drowned by a flood, but they do not dismantle its stones: the fate of the vanished village is considered a warning to the living.
