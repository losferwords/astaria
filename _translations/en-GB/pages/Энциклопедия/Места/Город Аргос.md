# Argos

![[Assets/Images/Argos_City.jpg]]

## Main text

**Argos** is the capital of [[Гилас|Gilas]], standing at the mouth of the [[Река Тавропос|Tavropos River]]. Its great harbour supplies the city with grain, metal and people, while its standing army is considered the largest among the known realms of [[Архея|Archaea]]. Military discipline is visible everywhere, from the watches upon the quays to the palace squares.

The city was built both as a fortress and as a gathering place for armies. Broad streets allow formations to move quickly from the barracks to the gates, while the walls command the river approaches and the sea harbour. The residence of [[Гиперион I|Hyperion I]] rises above the central quarters. To the Ellians, the undying king makes Argos not merely a political capital, but a place where the history of Gilas still speaks with a human voice.

At dawn, training companies fill the squares; by day, merchants and petitioners take their place; and in the evening crowds gather for contests. Military festivals stand beside river fairs, and tales of great victories are heard alongside haggling over wine, oil and passage aboard a ship.

Behind the facade of martial virtue lies another Argos: waterside taverns, smugglers, debts and promoters of arena fights. Fugitive slaves and people seeking a ship that asks no questions pass through this half-world. The capital may therefore become with equal ease a symbol of the strength of Gilas or a trap for anyone cast beyond its law.
