# Ravi River

![[Assets/Images/Ravi_River.jpg]]

The **Ravi** is a small but busy river of [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]], flowing north from the south to [[Багровый залив|Crimson Bay]]. [[Город Сангалла|Sangalla]] grew at its mouth, facing it with quays, stepped embankments and temple stairs where worshippers descend to the water before [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari's]] festivals.

In the rainy season, calm Ravi grows wider and darker, eroding low banks and leaving fertile reddish silt in fields. During dry months, water falls and exposes sandbanks where boats are repaired, fish dried and temporary markets held. Canals lead water to gardens, rice fields and dyers' quarters, making dams and locks as important to Sangalla as city walls.

Spices, medicinal roots, cloth and baskets of fruit travel downriver from inland lands. Ravi boatmen use long, narrow shallow-draught vessels, the only boats able to pass shoals and flooded roots after rain. They know the river by the water's scent, birds' movements and the colour of foam at bends.

The Rajati believe Ravi remembers the blood of every Cycle, yet does not refuse to nourish new life. During Creation, flower lamps are set upon it; during the Harvest, funeral songs sound on its banks. To an investigator, it is both a road to the capital and a living chronicle that cannot be read from stone inscriptions alone.
