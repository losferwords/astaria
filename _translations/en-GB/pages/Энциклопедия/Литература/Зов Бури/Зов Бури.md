# Call of Thunder

![[Assets/Images/Call_of_Thunder.png]]

A boy is born in a mountain village, his fate marked by a thunderstorm. His mother sings of home and courage, unaware of how far the storm's voice will carry her son. So begins the story of [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth the Stormcaller]], a [[Горец|Highlander]] of the [[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]]. Across its chapters, the story is told by people whose lives became intertwined with his own.

> [!info] The saga and the music
> **Call of Thunder is the debut album of the power-metal band Astarian.** Each chapter of the saga corresponds to a song on the album. The story opens with The Highlander’s Soul: a mother's voice welcomes the listener to Astaria and accompanies Kenneth towards his first trial.
