# Eurydice of Lepha

![[Assets/Images/Eurydice.jpg]]

## Main text

**Eurydice of Lepha** was the first queen of [[Катахтонос|Katachthonos]] and the ruler under whom a former maritime colony began to become an independent state. She is remembered not for resounding conquests, but for rebuilding cities, strengthening commerce and enacting reforms that allowed the Chthonids to recover a sense of homeland.

In 79 NE, at the age of twenty-eight, Eurydice married the young heir to [[Талассия|Thalassia]], [[Телемах II|Telemachus II]]. She inherited her father's throne at thirty-two. Three years later, Telemachus became king of Thalassia, and their only daughter together, [[Арета Лефийская|Areta]], was born. The marriage bound the two realms of the peninsula not merely by treaty, but through a common heir.

During her reign, Eurydice rebuilt [[Город Лефа|Lepha]], earning the title by which she became known. She also forged a strong alliance between the two states of the [[Полуостров Таллас|Tallas Peninsula]] and established maritime trade with the [[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]].

In 101 NE, Eurydice was poisoned at a feast marking the anniversary of her marriage to Telemachus. A [[Тень|Shadow]] was believed to be responsible, but the guard of [[Город Антал|Antal]] found no trace of the assassin.
