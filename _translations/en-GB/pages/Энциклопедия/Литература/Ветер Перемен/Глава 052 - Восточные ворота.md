# Chapter 052 — The Eastern Gate

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 105 NE, winter*

[[Ксан Ляньши|Xian Lianshi]] leapt from the wall and saved [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]], opening a small gate behind a wall of fire. A raven brought [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] a letter: they must leave, or everyone would die. Tom tried to convince his friends, but the city had already drawn each of them into a different part of its calamity.

[[Ван Сан|Wan San]] asked Tom to save the ordinary people who had failed to escape [[Город Найян|Nayan]]. Tom went to the palace, found [[Ли Сан|Li San]] and [[Нуа Си|Nuwa Xi]], and led them towards the eastern gate.

Meanwhile, [[Ошу Мати|Oshu Mati]] performed a rite over Xin's body and summoned [[Руфу|Rufu]]. The god of the dead heard her, but accused her of irresponsibility. Xin had died innocent, while the promise made to the guard's spirit—to save his son from tetanus—had gone unfulfilled. The boy was dead, and now Rufu commanded Oshu to save his mother, who stood at the brink of suicide.

Gilja came to the eastern gate to help Tom lead the people out. Yet an error in his rite caught up with him: having mistaken cassia for cinnamon, he went blind. Oshu helped him use a trick of blood upon one of the nomads, buying time for escape, though she lost her mask.

At the western gate, [[Урист Китин|Urist]] surrendered to battle fury and hurled nomads aside. [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] and Xian tried to hold back the assault. Axel Khan advanced towards the wall, and Mei brought a ladder down beneath him. As he fell, he hurled his poleaxe; it struck the wall and trapped Mei's neck between its blades. Xian rushed to meet him in battle.
