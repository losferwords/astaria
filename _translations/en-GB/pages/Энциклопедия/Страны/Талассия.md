## The great sea power

Thalassia is the foremost maritime power of [[Астария|Astaria]], grown around the harbours of the [[Полуостров Таллас|Tallas Peninsula]]. The [[Хтониды|Chthonids]] founded it as the north-western republic of their empire, but after the [[Падение Хтона|Fall of Chthon]] the colony followed [[Гилас|Gilas]] in declaring independence. The Thalassians inherited from their Chthonid past a love of calculation, argument and intricate devices, but directed it away from the mysteries of existence and towards ships, charts and mastery of the sea.

## A realm of harbours

Life is centred upon the water. Royal decrees issue from [[Город Антал|Antal]], but the strength of Thalassia also lies with harbour councils, shipowners, shipwrights and captains capable of leading a fleet through an unknown strait. Thalassians value personal fortune, yet never trust it without preparation. Before setting sail, they inspect rigging, water stores and agreements with the crew—and only then cast lots.

Thalassian ships are seen across the Sunset, Dawn and [[Хтоническое море|Chthonic]] seas. The country profits from carrying cargo, guiding vessels through dangerous waters, protecting trade routes and selling knowledge of distant shores. Maritime influence is not absolute power: in every region a captain must bargain with local rulers, pay for harbourage and remember that no flag can subdue a storm.

## Gunpowder and the distrust of magic

Thalassians reject magic as unpredictable and favour mechanisms whose workings can be repeated, measured and explained to a craftsperson. In 38 NE, they discovered how to make gunpowder. Its formula became a state secret, and authorised workshops are guarded as closely as the royal treasury. Gunpowder weapons have given the fleet an immense advantage, yet remain rare, costly and dangerous even to the crews that operate them.

Some captains possess compasses, though even in Thalassia this is an exceptional instrument reserved for [[Наварх|Navarchs]], state navigators and unusually fortunate owners. Most sailors still read their course in the stars, wind, colour of the water and behaviour of birds. Thalassian superiority comes not from an abundance of miraculous instruments, but from a thousand small improvements that crews guard jealously even from one another.

## Calypso and the Navarchs

The country's patron is [[Калипсо|Calypso]], goddess of fortune, adventure and invention. She has not appeared before her people for many years, and to many every voyage is both trade and search: an unknown shore might preserve some trace of the vanished goddess. The most accomplished captains become [[Наварх|Navarchs]], Imithei who unite seamanship, military cunning and the gift of invention.

The present king, [[Телемах II|Telemachus II]], prefers treaties to senseless wars, though Thalassia is hardly pacifist. Its relations with [[Лунаар|Lunaar]] are burdened by old conquests; with Gilas, by the rivalry of Chthon's heirs; and its alliance with [[Катахтонос|Katachthonos]] rests upon the fate of a single family as much as upon politics. To a traveller, Thalassia remains the finest place to find a berth and sail beyond the edge of the map—provided they accept that in Antal even a promise may become a wager.
