# Imithei

![[Assets/Images/imithei.jpg]]

**Imithei** are people whose abilities reach far beyond ordinary human limits. One channels lightning through a blade, another holds a collapsing riverbank in place, a third perceives a foreign will where others see only a frightened person. Extraordinary power unites them, but no single teaching does: every people of [[Астария|Astaria]] knows its own paths towards it.

## More than a crystal bearer

A [[Кристалл Души|Soul Crystal]] exists **in every creature of Astaria**, including ordinary humans and animals. Possessing one does not, by itself, make a person an Imithei. Superhuman abilities are associated with the crystal's development and the power it holds, although the nature of that connection remains a matter of conjecture.

For the peoples themselves, initiation, training and their relationship with a deity matter far more. A gift does not replace skill: lightning must be directed, strength restrained and spiritual sight understood.

## Different paths to wonder

A [[Горец|Highlander]] receives a lightning strike within the shared circle of the [[Рунштоирм|Runstoirm]]. A would-be [[Мектиг|Mektig]] reaches the [[Великая Кузня|Great Forge]] and earns [[Хангор|Khangor]]'s recognition through courage, will and craftsmanship. A [[Идеал|Paragon]] undergoes the [[Танатомахия|Thanatomachia]], a mortal duel after which the victor takes in the power of the opponent's Soul Crystal.

Not every calling begins with war. A [[Хранитель|Warden]] learns healing and service to the community, an [[Онмёдзи|Onmyoji]] learns to free the possessed, and a [[Страж|Guardian]] proves a willingness to protect another's life before receiving [[Велисса|Velissa]]'s answer. Each tradition has its own trials, duties and understanding of worthiness.

## From the first signs to legend

The journey is loosely described in five stages. These help compare experience and ability, but do not replace the titles and rites of individual cultures.

- **Seeker — rank 0.** The gift appears involuntarily, often in danger. The person cannot yet control it and is not regarded as an Imithei.
- **Initiate — rank 1.** Latent gifts become deliberate skills. Training and the trial that will confirm a claim to greater power still lie ahead.
- **Chosen — rank 2.** Initiation is complete. Recognised as an Imithei, the person can perform the wonders of their path and accepts its responsibilities.
- **Hero — rank 3.** Experience makes it possible to shape battles and the fortunes of communities. Such an Imithei may be entrusted with a warband, a settlement or a particularly dangerous task.
- **Legend — rank 4.** Their name is known beyond their homeland, and their deeds enter history. Rulers and gods turn to such people; [[Индульф мак Торн|Indulf mac Thorn]], chieftain of the [[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]], is one example.

A stage does not grant every possible ability at once. A legendary warrior does not thereby become an all-knowing healer, nor does power alone oblige others to obey.

## Greater power, deeper ties

More is expected of an Imithei than victory over an enemy. A Highlander must settle clan disputes, a Warden save the injured, a Guardian help restore peaceful life. They may be revered, feared, asked for help or blamed for what they could not prevent.

Yet an Imithei remains human: with attachments, doubts and the capacity to make mistakes. The choice between personal desire, duty and the fate of companions makes their story more compelling than power alone.
