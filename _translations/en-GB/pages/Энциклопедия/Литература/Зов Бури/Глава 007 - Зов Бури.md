# Chapter 007 — Call of Thunder

*Call of Thunder*

The battle continues at the entrance fortifications of [[Город Атафет|Atafet]]. [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth]] holds his [[Меч Де'лаим|De'laim]], the sword for which he has left home, crossed the sea and endured captivity. At last the blade is ready. Before him stands the army of [[Гилас|Gilas]]; behind him are the people who have shared his daily life and work for months. His weapon finds a use at once.

Yet even victory at these walls will leave [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]] beneath the same cloudless sky. Another soldier's death will not restore the fields. The people [[Кассиопея Атанатос|Cassia]] tends at the refuge will still need water and bread. Kenneth knows his enemy's strength too well to hope to defeat an entire host with one sword. He chooses another purpose for which that sword is made: to conduct the storm's power.

He intends to climb [[Гора Атафет|Mount Atafet]] and call the storms of distant [[Кельн|Keln]] to this place. Across the sea there is rain that can nourish this land. He must reach it.

Call of Thunder carries Cassia's voice. She goes with him. Her first words ask where the new blade will lead; then comes an almost tender relief: Kenneth chooses rain. Once again, they have a road to travel together.

For two days they climb the rocks. Below, the stone still holds the heat; higher up, wind slips beneath their clothes, forcing them to search for footholds and guard every movement. Weariness gathers with each ledge. Kenneth keeps going, and Cassia stays beside him. In the first bridge her encouragement sounds like a promise: however hard the road becomes, she will follow.

By the end of the second day, snow lies beneath their feet. Above them is open sky; far below remains the besieged city. Here it becomes especially clear how little walls and banners matter before a calamity slowly killing an entire country.

The chorus rises with them. Cassia's voice carries the hope of people weary of burying those they love, going hungry and waiting for the next battle. One army's victory will leave the other with its dead. Kenneth tries to give them a chance to live on. His call must break the succession of deaths into which his own journey is woven.

On the snowy slope, he raises his sword and turns the blade north.

Power gathers in the air. The wind changes, clouds mass along the horizon. Cassia hears the thunder approach and fears its voice. Once, Kenneth's attempt to calm a storm ends with the loss of the Litei. Now he calls a storm across the sea himself, towards a land that waits for rain to save it.

The Highlander continues the summoning. The sky darkens and lightning converges upon the summit. The whole long journey towards this sword narrows to the effort of one man holding it before the raging elements.

In the second bridge, Cassia's voice seems to reach those who remain below. Weary, hungry, thirsty, on either side of the fortifications. Wind touches every face. The rain they long for will not choose which army has the greater right to water. The song allows a hope that people may yet stop and grant one another peace.

Then a vision overtakes Cassia.

She sees the end of the rite. Kenneth stands upon the summit, exhausted and happy: the storm answers his call. Lightning has heated De'laim until it glows. His burned hands can no longer hold the sword, and the Highlander stops the summoning. The storm rages on. The rite drains the last of his strength.

In this future he falls, and Cassia remains beside him to take him in her arms. She sees her beloved dead.

For her, this is far more immediate than ordinary fear for someone in danger. The gift of a [[Профитис|Profitis]] reveals the outcome as clearly as others see the ground beneath their feet. Yet when the vision releases her, Kenneth is still standing before the sky. The rite continues.

Cassia can try to stop him. But below are the people for whom he climbs, and a country where every rainless day brings death. She understands the hope she will take from them if she interrupts the summoning.

Then she remembers [[Остров Хтон|Chthon]]. There, too, her visions seem certain. Kenneth builds a raft, and the future changes. The simple work of his hands opens a road absent from her prophecies.

Now Cassia searches what she sees for something she can still change herself. The vision contains them both. He dies beside her. She can leave.

There is no certainty that this choice will work. Only a desperate hope of making the future different without stopping Kenneth. Her promise to follow him to the end of the road still echoes in her memory; now love makes her turn away from the summit. Cassia turns her blind gaze upon him one last time and begins the descent. She leaves while he is still alive, leaving the rite's outcome behind her.

By dawn, clouds cover the sky from [[Озеро Митра|Lake Mitra]] to [[Озеро Кафер|Lake Kafer]]. At Atafet's entrance fortifications the battle stills: defenders and attackers raise their faces towards the approaching front. Then rain begins. Water runs over stone, washes dust from hands and soaks earth dry for years.

[[Персефона Раптис|Persephone]] halts the assault to wait out the storm and regroup her soldiers. The arriving army of [[Рамхотеп II|Ramhotep II]] demands peace, and the siege gives way to negotiations. Later, in [[Город Ахей|Achaeus]], the pharaoh apologises for the rebels' invasion and pays tribute; Gilas agrees to release the conquered lands. The [[Война Жаждущих|War of the Thirsty]] ends.

Below, people greet the rain. Cassia carries away a vision she hopes to change, without knowing what happens to Kenneth after she leaves.
