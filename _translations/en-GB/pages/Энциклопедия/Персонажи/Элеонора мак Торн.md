# Eleanor mac Thorn

![[Assets/Images/Eleanor_mac-Thorn.jpg]]

## Main text

**Eleanor mac Thorn** was a [[Горец|Highlander]] of Clan Thorn and the twin sister of [[Индульф мак Торн|Indulf]]. In the old accounts, she is often called the second thunderclap of a single storm: brother and sister were born on the same day, trained together and stood side by side during the greatest trial of their lives.

At their [[Рунштоирм|Runstoirm]], the other Named lost consciousness one after another. Eleanor and Indulf alone held the lightning's power to the end. Both emerged from the circle as Highlanders, having shared the might of a single storm between them. The feat made them renowned long before Indulf became First Highlander.

Eleanor did not live in her brother's shadow. She was respected as a defender of the clan in her own right, one who could speak to Indulf without deferential fear and remind him of those whose voices were lost amid the quarrels of chieftains. Where her brother preferred to hear a long council to its conclusion, she was quicker to discern a personal wound concealed beneath talk of honour.

After her death in 73 NE, the songs of Clan Thorn preserved the memory of a woman who yielded nothing to the First Highlander in either fortitude or the right to command the storm. For Indulf, her loss meant the passing of the only person who remembered him not as a legend or a chieftain, but as the boy beside whom she had once heard thunder for the first time.
