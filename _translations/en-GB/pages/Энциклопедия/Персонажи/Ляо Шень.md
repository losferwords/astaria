# Liao Shen

![[Assets/Images/Lao_Shen.jpg]]

## Main text

**Liao Shen** is a court tailor in [[Город Джу-Суо|Dju-Suo]] who has served as chief artisan of the [[Храм Дракона|Temple of the Dragon]] for more than thirty years. Born in the capital to a family of craftspeople, he displayed an exceptional gift for sewing and working with cloth from an early age. At twenty-two, following the dismissal of [[Ао Ву|Ao Wu]], he was appointed Master Tailor of the imperial court. His workshop has since produced the ceremonial robes of Empress [[Сунь Ляньши|Sun Lianshi]], her household and the Empire's highest dignitaries.

Liao is known not only for his command of cloth but also as a refined aesthete with a keen eye for symbolic patterns, rare materials and harmonious colours. His garments are prized for their elegance, durability and the concealed signs he works into their ornamentation—some drawn from sacred custom, others from the legends of the court.

He has a cheerful, amiable disposition and treats women and men alike with polished gallantry. Over his long service he has acquired a reputation as a flirt and breaker of hearts; the court chronicles record a great many romances and infatuations attributed to the tailor. Liao nevertheless remains indispensable to the imperial household, combining consummate craft, charm and the rare ability to keep whatever secrets he overhears behind the palace curtains.
