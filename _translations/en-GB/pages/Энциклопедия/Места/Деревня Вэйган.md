# Veigan

![[Assets/Images/Veigan_Village.jpg]]

**Veigan** (卫干) is a village in the south of [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]], at the border with [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]]. It repeatedly changed hands in conflicts between the two realms, leaving its culture and customs intertwined.

Temples of the [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Great Dragon]] stand beside shrines to six-armed [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari]]. Language, dress and food carry traces of both [[Джу|Dju]] and [[Раджати|Rajati]], while the inhabitants' endurance and distrust of strangers reflect long memories of war and shifting rule.

One wedding may include Dju and Rajati songs, and a family altar may keep a dragon figure beside Shubhankari's blood-red ribbon. Authorities can find such doubleness suspicious; to villagers it is simply how they survived every new border.

Travellers find guides who know both sides, rare mixed food and contradictory stories. Veigan remembers every army that passed its road, but does not share that memory before trusting the listener.
