# Khangor

## Main text

Khangor, also known as the Smith-God, the Great Craftsman and the Iron God, is the patron and teacher of the [[Авгарцы|Avgars]] and their young realm, [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]]. His followers prize strength, masculinity and mastery of a craft above all other virtues.

The Smith-God rarely reveals himself to the people of Wildlands. After the [[Великая Кузня|Great Forge]] was completed, Khangor released its workers, sealed himself within his monumental creation and closed it to unrestricted entry. Only the bravest and worthiest Avgars attempt the Forge's trials in hopes of meeting the god and earning his recognition. Some say a perfect weapon capable of defeating any foe is being made beyond the gates. Others believe that the Forge will one day draw craftspeople from all across [[Астария|Astaria]] and eclipse the fame of [[Город Атафет|Atafet]]. The elders dispute whether Khangor's silence expresses disappointment in his people or an attempt to protect them from a danger the master discovered in his own creation.

The elders say that Khangor values silence, diligence and strength. These qualities appear to have drawn his attention to the strange barbarians who came from the north. Their expedition was led by a young [[Вактары|Vaktar]] named [[Аксель Хан|Axel Khan]], who mistook the god for an unknown monster and attacked without hesitation. The courage of the Vaktars impressed Khangor so greatly that he offered them immense power in exchange for help building the mysterious Great Forge. Thus began the history of the [[Авгарцы|Avgars]], Wildlands and an entirely new order of [[Имитей|Imithei]]: the mighty [[Мектиг|Mektigs]].

Many peoples encountered the Smith-God even before the coming of the Vaktars. The archives of the [[Талассийцы|Thalassians]], for example, record that the Iron God was often seen upon the [[Полуостров Таллас|Tallas Peninsula]].

Ancient tales often place Khangor beside [[Калипсо|Calypso]] and [[Тарун|Tarun]]. The goddess found the way and the vision, the smith gave their ideas form, and the thunderer filled their creation with power. To Avgar masters, these stories became a parable: even the greatest craftsperson needs another pair of eyes and a friend willing to test the result.
