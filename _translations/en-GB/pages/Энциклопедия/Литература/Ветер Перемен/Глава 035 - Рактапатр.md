# Chapter 035 — Raktapatra

*[[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], 104 NE, autumn*

[[Шубханкари|Shubhankari]] was displeased that [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] had drawn her into the affairs of [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]] once again. She asked whether he had found Raktaranjit, the blood-smith. The answer was evident, but the goddess explained the price nonetheless.

The previous night, drunk and insistent, Gilja had dedicated himself to the path of the Raksha. Shubhankari had tried to stop him: he was unprepared and did not know all ten sacred circles of Rajati teaching. Yet his joy was so earnest that she relented and gave him the [[Рактапатр|Raktapatra]], from which he was to fashion his own blade.

Raktapatra grows by taking chemical elements from the blood of its victims. Gilja had to feed it worthy blood, triple its length, find the blood-smith and divide the blade into two katars. The weapon must be returned to the goddess by the next moon. If left unfed, Raktapatra would begin growing from its bearer's blood and slowly kill him.

Meanwhile, the events of the night became clearer. [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] had encountered [[Кирида Ариса|Kirida Arisa]] in the harbour. Arisa sought the [[Сердце Огня|Heart of Fire]] for [[Ицунэ|Itsune]]. Drunken Mei attacked first; Arisa defended herself with a katana, then leapt into the sea and swam away.

[[Мерката|Mercate]] appeared to [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] and told him with irritated candour that she saw all his indecent dreams. It was not, however, a true revelation from the goddess, but a reflection of Tom and Matthew's imagination.

[[Пирей Никос|Piraeus Nikos]], [[Эйдан мак Кинсли|Aidan mac Kinsley]] and [[Ишикути Макото|Makoto]] arrived at Halyk-Byndir. Meanwhile, [[Урист Китин|Urist]] conceived a plan to free the spirit of [[Аксель Хан|Axel Khan]] from Deathbringer. It required a dying Imithei, so Urist resolved to ask [[Армар Саг|Armar Sag]] for volunteers among the Mektigs.

Tom fought Aidan and won. Later, his dark personality, Arthur Beaumain, carried the body away, wounded it with an arrow, summoned Mercate and stole her enchanted scythe.

When the company came before the khan to seek volunteers, they learnt that the Mektigs had already gone south to Dragon's Maw after hearing of Axel Khan's return and the destruction of the Wu legion. At that same moment, [[Мэйлун|Meilong]] appeared beneath Mei's cloak. Arisa spotted the dragon, seized him and fled.
