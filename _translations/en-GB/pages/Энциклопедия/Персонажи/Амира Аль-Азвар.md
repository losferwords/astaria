# Amira al-Azwar

![[Assets/Images/Amira_Al-Azwar.jpg]]

## Main text

**Amira al-Azwar** is an [[Мститель|Avenger]] and the Eternal Princess of western [[Кадир|Qadir]]. She was raised at the court of [[Город Самаан|Samaan]] as the only daughter of [[Малик Аль-Азвар|Malik al-Azwar]], one of the first three Avengers, and knew from childhood that her deeds would be measured not against those of her peers, but against her father's centuries of rule.

Four years ago, Malik willingly transferred to her the government of the cities around [[Озеро Кхарим|Kharim]], [[Озеро Джамсар|Jamsar]] and [[Озеро Шаради|Sharadi]]. For Qadir, it was a rare peaceful succession: the title of Eternal Prince had previously been understood as a duty ended only by death.

Amira completed her training in the Way of the Blade with distinction. She is swift and precise, and prefers a brief engagement to a drawn-out contest. Governing the west, however, demands a different mastery. She must bargain with the merchants of Samaan, keepers of the gardens, masters of the schools and captains to whom the ruler's youth appears an invitation to test the bounds of her patience.

The young princess is determined to prove her independence without renouncing her father's legacy. She respects Malik's counsel, but will not allow him to decide in her stead. Amira pays particular attention to pupils of humble birth: in her judgement, the Way of the Blade must choose a person for ability and character, not the wealth of their family.

Samaan sees in her the ruler of a new age—less patient than the ancient princes, perhaps, but better able to understand the lives of people for whom seven centuries are not an acceptable time to wait for change.
