# Chapter 005 — The Priestess

*The Priestess*

The army of [[Город Ахей|Achaeus]] was caught unprepared by a sudden attack from an ally: rebels from [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]]. Faced with overwhelming numbers and lacking any [[Идеал|Paragons]], the defenders had no hope. [[Персефона Раптис|Persephone]] ordered a retreat and surrendered the city. [[Город Ахей|Achaeus]] was [[Вторжение Амон-Астата в город Ахей|captured]] within hours.

[[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth]] and [[Кассиопея Атанатос|Cassia]], like the other prisoners, remained in the city under new keepers: the poor and rebellious of Amon-Astat, driven by the Great Drought into a [[Кризис веры в Амон-Астате|religious war]] against [[Рамхотеп II|the pharaoh]].

The prisoners from Achaeus, confined in old barracks, were joined by captive [[Хефат|Khefat]] gathered across the desert. Amongst them was a young priestess of the Sun named [[Шани-Хат-Су|Shani]].

Amongst all these suffering souls, she noticed the gravely wounded Kenneth and Cassia, who was bound far more securely than the others: her hands and feet were tied and her eyes and mouth covered. For several days, Shani tended the young Highlander as best she could and helped him survive.

The captives spent about a week in the barracks, guarded by watchmen who changed twice each day. On the seventh day, a young soldier entered to count the prisoners. Shani looked into his face and recognised her elder brother, [[Рамат-Хат-Су|Ramat]]. He was both overjoyed and afraid: he had at last found his sister, only to find her a prisoner of his commander. After a long conversation, he promised to arrange Shani's escape from Achaeus. She insisted that she could not reach [[Город Тиза|Tiza]] alone and persuaded him to free Kenneth and Cassia as well.

The following evening, Ramat set fire to a granary near the barracks. Amidst the panic, he freed Shani and her new companions and told them where to find provisions and camels for the desert crossing.

During their journey, Shani recounts the Great Drought in Amon-Astat and the trials endured by [[Хефат|her people]] over five agonising years. This is the tale told in the saga's chapter.

The company barely crossed the [[Пустыня Сехет|Sekhet Desert]] and reached [[Регион Аментир|Amentir]]. Their paths parted in Tiza: Kenneth and Cassia continued to [[Город Атафет|Atafet]], while Shani resumed her studies at Tiza's Temple of the Sun.
