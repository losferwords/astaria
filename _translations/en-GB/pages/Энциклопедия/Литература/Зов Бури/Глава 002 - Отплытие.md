# Chapter 002 — Sailing

*Sailing*

*98 NE*

The harbour of [[Город Солаис|Solais]] is preparing for departure. Ropes creak, sailors call to one another across the quays, and the last of the cargo disappears into the hold. Wind from the shores of [[Кельн|Keln]] promises a swift passage south. They have only to raise the anchor, and the stone city, with its clan houses, lights and familiar voices, will begin to recede astern.

After his initiation, [[Кеннет мак Рейн|Kenneth]] comes to this port. The trial by lightning is already behind him, but he still has no [[Меч Де'лаим|De'laim]] of his own, the sword that helps Highlanders direct the power of a storm. For such a weapon, he must seek the smiths at [[Гора Атафет|Mount Atafet]], far across the sea. Mountain slopes and passes lie behind him. Ahead stretches water beyond which the shore disappears.

He finds a place aboard the [[Талассия|Thalassian]] armed merchantman *Litei*. Captain [[Рея Мелит|Rhea Melit]] knows what to expect of a [[Горец|Highlander]]: sailors welcome such passengers, who can protect a vessel from foul weather. Rhea is a [[Наварх|Navarch]], one of the captains Thalassia trusts with its most difficult voyages. She knows how to read the wind, cares for her ship and relies upon the people beside her. With a Highlander aboard, the familiar crossing to [[Амон-Астат|Amon-Astat]] promises to be calmer still.

Sailing speaks in her voice. At the beginning of the song, Rhea is certain of her ship, her crew and the course ahead. The hold is full of riches, their hearts full of courage, and the south awaits beyond the horizon. The sailors' song brings separate voices together: oars enter the water as one, people take up the chorus, and the *Litei* draws ever further from the harbour.

The [[Хтоническое море|Chthonic Sea]] joins the northern lands to the south. Merchants, warriors and wanderers cross its waters; the distant shore holds a different promise for each of them. Kenneth seeks a sword, Rhea follows a familiar course, and the crew feel themselves part of a strength equal to any encounter. Their defiant chorus seems to challenge whatever may rise to meet them from the waves.

The sky darkens, and the crew's confidence still holds. Wind fills the sails, the ship climbs the crests, and salt water sweeps across the deck. With each new swell, the sea grows heavier, but aboard stands a man whom lightning obeys. Even in the heart of the squall, they can believe he will guide them through.

Rhea turns to him calmly. Her first *“Please calm down the sea.”* still carries courtesy: the captain is asking her passenger to subdue the foul weather. She expects the wind to ease, the waves to subside, and the crew to hear one another again.

Kenneth tries to subdue the storm. It grows stronger.

He reaches for the elements again, and lightning only flashes more brightly around the *Litei*. Thunder swallows the commands, spray strikes their faces, and the ship plunges ever deeper between the waves. His power from the Runstoirm brings no rescue now. Every attempt to drive the storm away makes their plight worse.

*“Hey, calm down the sea!”* — urgency enters Rhea's voice. There is no time left to wait. The ship, the cargo, the people: everything depends on whether he can finally stop what is happening. Her earlier trust gives way to anxiety; now she demands help through the roar of the water.

Questions come one after another. Is this how it is supposed to be? What is he doing? Is it his fault the storm is becoming more terrible? Bewilderment gives way to fury. She wants an answer, wants to make Kenneth master his own strength, to bring the sea back within the measure of danger her crew can withstand. But he has no answer that can save the *Litei*.

Lightning blinds them, and after each flash the darkness seems deeper. Less and less sky remains between the crests. Rhea still looks towards the Highlander, but already understands that another plea will change nothing. Anger ebbs, leaving a heavy clarity: the ship will go down.

In the song's final lines, she accepts the inevitable. The old defiance is gone from her voice: she speaks of the souls the sea will take that night.

The waves close over the *Litei*. Kenneth vanishes into the black water with the people who trust his strength. Later, the sea carries him, unconscious, to the shore of [[Остров Хтон|Chthon]].
