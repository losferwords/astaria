# Atafet

![[Assets/Images/Atafet_City.jpg]]

## Main text

Hidden within [[Гора Атафет|Mount Atafet]] is a city where day and night are distinguished not by the sun, but by a change in the rhythm of hammers. Homes, warehouses and workshops occupy immense natural halls joined by bridges of stone. Above lie the immaculate jewellers' rows and merchants' houses; below are the mines, foundry yards and forges, where heat makes the air tremble.

The City of Masters draws craftspeople from across [[Астария|Astaria]]. Here one may hear dozens of languages, commission the most delicate setting for a gemstone, buy a ship's nail that will withstand salt water or wait months for an audience with a master capable of forging a weapon for an [[Имитей|Imithei]]. Reputation is the true currency of Atafet: one flawless piece opens the doors of the finest guilds, while a single concealed defect may cost a smith their name for ever.

The city's principal square lies beneath a vault threaded with pale mineral veins. During fairs, mirrors and oil lamps magnify their glow, transforming the ceiling into the likeness of a starry sky. Appraisers, assayers, engravers and makers of runic moulds work in the side galleries; the family of [[Арно Эклунд|Eklund]] is especially renowned among them.

Wars have reached the outer defences of Atafet more than once, yet taking the city from without is easier than holding it within. Its people know the hidden galleries, can close the bridges and turn working machinery into fortifications within minutes. The inhabitants therefore say that Atafet is defended not by its walls, but by the hands that built them.
