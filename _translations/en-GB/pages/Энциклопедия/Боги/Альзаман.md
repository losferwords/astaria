# Alzaman

## Main text

Alzaman—the Faceless God, the Nomad God and the Wraith of the Desert—is the god of the [[Кадийцы|Qadians]]. He embodies eternity, knowledge and the unbroken passage of time.

The people of [[Кадир|Qadir]] say that he is broadly human in form, though a golden mask conceals his face and long claws grow from his hands and feet. At times, one is left with the unsettling impression that his robes are empty and that the god himself is made entirely of sand.

According to ancient Qadian scripture, [[Альзаман|Alzaman]] once stopped for the night in the old city of [[Город Тиза|Tiza]] during his long wandering through the deserts of [[Архея|Archaea]]. At dawn he found [[Хефат|Khefat]] builders raising a pyramid in honour of [[Аст|the Sun God]]. Alzaman revealed himself and asked whether they regretted spending their lives upon so vast a monument—not for themselves or their families, but for one whom they might never meet. Some found the divine questions insulting and selfish; others paused to consider the Wraith of the Desert's words. The god then invited the builders to follow him into new lands in the east, where no one would squander the most precious possession granted to mortals: time.

Word of fertile country and a wise patron spread beyond Tiza and throughout [[Регион Аментир|Amentir]]. Several thousand adherents of the new teaching followed the Faceless God to [[Озеро Зураиф|Lake Zuraif]], at the heart of the Seven Gardens of the Desert. There they founded [[Город Сераф|Seraph]] and a new realm: [[Кадир|Qadir]].

The place was no accident. In [[Археи|Archaean]] ruins at the centre of Lake Zuraif, Alzaman tends the constancy of time by means of a colossal hourglass. The people of Qadir believe that the first grain to fall within it created all that exists. When the last grain descends, the End of Time will come and the glass will turn, bringing forth another world.

Like the other gods of [[Астария|Astaria]], Alzaman sometimes speaks with his people. Most often he aids caravans crossing the [[Пустыня Халисат|Khalisat Desert]], a kindness that earned him the name Nomad God.

Alzaman founded no separate priestly caste. In place of a temple hierarchy, he favours physicians, stargazers, alchemists, scribes and inquisitive pupils who can turn observation to the benefit of others. A quiet dispute between sages, a new healing compound or a well-calculated canal pleases him more than honours offered without understanding.
