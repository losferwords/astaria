# Chapter 056 — Qiong-Qi's Well

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 105 NE, winter*

[[Мэй Ву|Mei]] opened the seventh cell, and [[Кирида Ариса|Kirida Arisa]] burst out. Without pausing, she leapt into the well at the centre of the hall. Growling and the sounds of a struggle rose from below.

By the time [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] and [[Урист Китин|Urist]] reached the hall, Mei had already cast down a torch and was trying to make out what was happening. Urist descended first and saw [[Мэйлун|Meilong]] in chains. The young dragon was chasing someone through the depths, and Urist decided to tear the chain from the rock. Mei climbed down to help him.

Arisa took Urist hostage and demanded their aid in escaping. At the far end of the underground cavern lay a pool opening into a subterranean river and, perhaps, a way outside. Then [[Кион-Чи|Qiong-Qi]], an immense winged tiger, rose from the water and attacked Mei, Urist and Meilong.

The battle threw everyone into confusion. Urist hurled his hammer upwards with Meilong's chain tied around it. Gilja ended up below and Arisa above. She found the way out of the temple and led the people to safety, but demanded the key—the [[Чёрный Вестник|Black Harbinger]]. Once she had it, she vanished somewhere overhead with [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] and [[Ошу Мати|Oshu]], while Qiong-Qi bounded after them.

Urist, Mei and Gilja remained below, without the hammer and with no clear way out. They had no choice but to swim through an underwater cave. With great difficulty they emerged upon the riverbank and began deciding where to go next.

Gilja turned his hunter's sight towards the mutilated man from the temple and realised that he was somewhere across the river. Mei, meanwhile, struggled against a fever contracted during her encounter with Qiong-Qi. The young dragon was free, but the forest still held them between its teeth.
