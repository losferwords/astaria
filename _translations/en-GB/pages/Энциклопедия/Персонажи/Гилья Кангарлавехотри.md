# Gilja Kangarlavehotri

![[Assets/Images/Gilja_Kangarlavehotri.jpg]]

**Gilja** was born in [[Деревня Бадракали|Badrakali]] and serves Queen [[Индира Раштра|Indira Rashtra]] as a [[Ракша|Raksha]]. Though born into an artisan caste, he earned the right to become a Blood Acolyte and later completed the path to his own raktakatars through uncommon diligence. His faith admits scarcely any doubt: where others see cruelty in the Great Harvest, Gilja sees duty to [[Шубханкари|Shubhankari]] and the promise of rebirth to come.

He journeyed north in search of a remedy for the [[Ракта-Доша|Rakta-Dosha]] afflicting both the queen and many inhabitants of [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]]. The [[Аль-Тахим|Al-Tahim]] nearly swallowed him in the desert, but rescue by chance travellers became the beginning of a years-long odyssey. To his companions, Gilja became healer, guide through the rites of blood and a man able to speak with the goddess where a mortal's prayer would ordinarily go unanswered.

His service has been tested more than once. Gilja cultivated a [[Рактапатр|Raktapatra]] with his own hands, forged a pair of raktakatars from it and endured a vision revealing the wound borne by Shubhankari herself. His encounter with the nagas forced him to recognise that beings condemned as monsters may seek faith and the order of civilisation, while some Raksha use sacred rites to conceal deceit and abduction.

Despite these contradictions, Gilja remains among the most devoted of the Raksha. He has been charged with restoring blood wrongfully taken from the goddess and gathering the Raksha at the [[Кузница Плоти|Furnace of Flesh]]. To him, this is no mere command: its outcome will determine whether the protracted Cycle of Destruction may finally end—and the fate of the country he left in order to save.
