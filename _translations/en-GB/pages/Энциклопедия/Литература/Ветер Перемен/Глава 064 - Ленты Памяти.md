# Chapter 064 — The Ribbons of Memory

*[[Империя Ланг-Ан|Lang-An Empire]], 105 NE, spring*

At noon it was not the empress who addressed the square, but her secretary, [[Цинь Ша|Qin Sha]]. He announced an attempt upon the [[Дракон Ланг-Ан|Great Dragon's]] life and imposed a curfew. The crowd dispersed uneasily, with some shouting threats against [[Урист Китин|Urist]].

The travellers went to the [[Дом Белых Парусов|House of White Sails]], where visitors without passes were detained. By invoking the name of a monk, Xi Sao, Urist was allowed to enter a local citizen's name upon the documents. He secured a meeting with [[Ли Сан|Li San]], gave her the passes and asked her to free as many people from [[Город Найян|Nayan]] and [[Город Чанг-Ша|Chang-Sha]] as possible.

[[Мэй Ву|Mei]] entered disguised as a courier. She found [[Ао Ву|Ao Wu]] in the dining hall, and her mother embraced her with quiet joy. For a fee, Mei also secured the release of several distant relatives.

Back at their refuge, the travellers found [[Мерката|Mercate's]] raven perched upon [[Бао Си|Bao Xi's]] body. The goddess herself appeared after sunset and said the city's barrier had fallen, allowing her to walk freely through the capital. She advised [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] to look inside Bao's dream. There he saw the way to the [[Храм Дракона|Temple of the Dragon]] and [[Нуа Си|Nuwa Xi]] burning everything around her. The travellers understood that Bao's sister was the key to waking him.

With a Lunaar coin and a black-fletched arrow, Tom found Nuwa's house. She was drowsy, pregnant and frightened for her brother. The travellers carried Bao to her, and Nuwa revived him with dragon magic.

Bao told them that he had discovered a way into the palace: they could hide inside sarcophagi containing the Ribbons of Memory, cloth scrolls bearing the history of the Dju people. He had been wounded while the Great Dragon gazed through his eyes. Someone was attempting to trap Lang-An's spirit within Bao's body.

In the morning, the travellers found Chen Lianshi in a tavern. Mercate slept upon his lap, her wings concealed and her appearance indistinguishable from that of an ordinary northern woman; when she woke, she complained of a headache. Chen surrendered his monastic robes to Mei without hesitation and remained entirely naked, as though this were the most ordinary gesture of assistance.
