# Chapter 001 — The Lucky Wager

*[[Талассия|Thalassia]], 104 NE, spring*

In [[Город Антал|Antal]], the clamorous capital of [[Талассия|Thalassia]], morning began with the cries of fishers, the creak of rigging and the scent of olive oil. [[Пирей Никос|Piraeus Nikos]] awoke in a house where every possession recalled his father's voyages: curios from distant lands adorned the walls, and the [[Глаз Калипсо|Eye of Calypso]], a medallion of the goddess of fortune, lay upon the table. Its blue stone caught a sunbeam and scattered sparks of light across the room. Piraeus took it for a good omen.

His mood was less untroubled. A new doublet of blue velvet and lynx fur had cost far too much, while debts did not vanish at Calypso's blessing alone. At the docks, Piraeus spotted a former friend who had once lent him money and now arrived with hired muscle. To escape an unpleasant reunion, Piraeus disappeared into a crowd of dice players and proposed a wager audacious enough to turn the whole harbour's head.

His opponent was a cunning captain with few teeth and boundless self-regard. He staked a penteconter named the *Atron*. Fortune settled into Piraeus's palm once more, and the dice fell in his favour. Hissing with anger and muttering of trickery, the defeated captain threw down a scrap of sailcloth proving ownership of the ship.

Victory was only the beginning. The dockmaster informed Piraeus that the *Atron* was not in Antal. It had last sailed with cargo for [[Город Солаис|Solais]], a port of the [[Громовые Кланы|Thunder Clans]] beyond [[Штормовой залив|Storm Bay]]. Thus a fortunate cast of the dice became a road north, where luck would have to meet the storm.
