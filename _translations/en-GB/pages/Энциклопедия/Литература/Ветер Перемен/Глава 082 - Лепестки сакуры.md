# Chapter 082 — Sakura Petals

*[[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]], 106 NE, winter*

After speaking with the goddess, the travellers and Sajar set out for the place where he had lost his cargo. At night, [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] spotted the wreckage of the cart, and its tracks led the group to a clearing filled with crates. Great beasts unknown even to the locals surrounded the cargo.

Their attempt to slip past failed. The largest beast charged, striking Gilja a glancing blow. He hurled his fiercest mixture of herbs and spices into its face. Its roar drew the others, allowing the travellers to reach the crates. They had found the sakura petals, and the group returned to [[Город Танассар|Tanassar]].

A dreadful sight awaited them at the gates: a guard had been scored with countless fine cuts designed to drain his blood as swiftly as possible. Gilja recognised the work of raktakatars. He said that Shubhankari herself had probably reaped this Raksha, and mourning him would serve no purpose.

At the tavern, the petals were given to [[Кирида Ариса|Arisa]]. [[Урист Китин|Urist]] brought [[Сао Ву|Naika]], disappointing her new admirer, the smith Shankar. The rite succeeded, and [[Ицунэ|Itsune]] answered their call.

The goddess of souls was astonished by the existence of [[Нереида|nereids]]: they possessed no souls, a thing almost inconceivable in Astaria. Whether from curiosity, mercy or a rare good humour, she reunited the two halves of Urist's soul within his body. His love for Li San, his children, flowers, birds and all that had once sounded faintly within him returned.

Arisa received forgiveness and a new charge: to eradicate the demons that had somehow reached the mainland. The travellers sold the [[Нага|naga's]] head, learnt how to harvest their venom and prepared to journey south towards the capital of Suraj Ka Ghar.
