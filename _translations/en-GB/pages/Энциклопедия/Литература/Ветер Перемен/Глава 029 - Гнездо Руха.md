# Chapter 029 — The Rukh's Nest

*[[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], 104 NE, spring*

[[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] and [[Урист Китин|Urist Kitin]] climbed the winding stone path while [[Гилья Кангарлавехотри|Gilja]] waited below with the camels. At the cave mouth, Urist threw his hammer with a rope tied to it, turning Deathbringer by turns into a weapon, a grappling hook and weighty proof of his ingenuity.

Exhausted by the ascent, Mei briefly fell asleep. [[Мерката|Mercate]] came to her in a dream and invited her to join an order of Imithei that sought to save the world. Mei gave no immediate answer. Then the goddess abruptly departed to aid [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]].

Tom awoke in the nest among three enormous eggs. His attempted escape ended in an attack by the Rukh, but he called upon darkness and broke free towards the entrance. There, by unhappy chance, Urist's hammer struck him. The bird spotted its fugitive prey and plunged after him.

Mercate arrived in time. Her battle with the Rukh was furious, filling the air with feathers and stone dust. When the bird fell, the goddess told Tom that one of the [[Тень|Shadows]], Selina, had disappeared somewhere in the southern lands of [[Сурадж Ка Гхар|Suraj Ka Ghar]] while carrying out an important commission.

Later, the [[Сердце Огня|Heart of Fire]] went dark again and began draining Mei's strength. Urist knocked the artefact from her hands, and it fell into the river. [[Нуа Си|Nuwa Xi]], a guileless pilgrim of the [[Храм Дракона|Temple of the Dragon]], retrieved it. Frightened and irritated, Mei lied that she too was undergoing the temple's trial, but refused to take Nuwa with her.

Nomads came to the camp at night. The travellers refused to meet their leader, and the nomads attacked Nuwa. She defeated nearly all of them, though one managed to seize her by the hair. The heroes intervened and freed her, after which she joined the company after all.

In [[Город Даркхан|Darkhan]], the travellers found a large tent that served as an inn. Gilja remained with Nuwa, whose injured arm had been poorly bandaged, while the others tried to sleep. Rest proved impossible: Nuwa asked questions throughout the night. By morning, she and Mei were already practising boxing as though the journey had only begun.
