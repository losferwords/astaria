## The free clans

The Thunder Clans are a free alliance of the [[Гойдаир|Goidair]], occupying the lands east of the [[Горный хребет Грань Тьмы|Edge of Darkness]]. No single court has the power to command the rest: every clan keeps its own land, history and right to choose a chieftain. The alliance rests upon oaths of mutual aid, shared sanctuaries and the knowledge that in harsh Keln even the proudest lineage cannot survive a long winter alone.

The country has long been divided into regions, each led by a [[Горец|Highlander]], an [[Имитей|Imithei]] chosen by people and sky. The First Highlander is no king. They summon the other chiefs, speak for the alliance before foreigners and claim the right to be first to meet a common threat. Decisions affecting several clans are argued long and loudly, but a word given before witnesses is worth more than any seal.

The principal faith of the Clans is the worship of [[Тарун|Tarun]], the celestial thunderer, although the other Dair gods are also honoured. A storm is at once danger, blessing and the voice of the heavens. Children born beneath thunder are called the Named, and from youth are prepared for the [[Рунштоирм|Runstoirm]], an ordeal from which only a few emerge as Highlanders.

Goidair life is closely bound to mountain roads, forest and a restless sea. The clans trade wool, timber, iron and fish. Their artisans adorn weapons with patterns that record the history of each owner. A guest will be offered a place at the hearth before being asked the purpose of their journey, but a broken oath or insult to a lineage may outlive several generations.

[[Город Солаис|Solais]] serves as the alliance's customary capital, a meeting place for chiefs, captains and merchants. [[Индульф мак Торн|Indulf mac Thorn]] has held the place of First Highlander for more than forty years. His authority rests not upon a crown, but upon a rare gift for holding together people who value freedom too deeply to submit easily to another's will.
