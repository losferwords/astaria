# Khonsu-Namun

![[Assets/Images/Honsu-Namun.jpg]]

## Main text

**Khonsu-Namun** is an antiquarian and pupil of the archivist [[Мерес-Аран|Meres-Aran]] of [[Город Тиза|Tiza]]. Since his youth, he has been fascinated by maps whose coastlines no longer match the present shores, forgotten systems of writing and traces of the [[Археи|Archaean]] civilisation. In the archives, he can spend days studying a single damaged fragment if he believes it conceals a path to a genuine discovery.

While examining ancient maps, Khonsu identified the possible site of an Archaean temple near [[Озеро Митра|Lake Mitra]] and set out to test the theory himself. The expedition ended in captivity: soldiers of [[Гилас|Gilas]] took him for a suspicious foreigner and condemned him to the gladiatorial arena of [[Город Ахей|Achaeus]].

Life among fighters confronted the scholar with a world that could not be understood from scrolls. Khonsu had to learn to study people as attentively as ruins, distinguish fear from falsehood, memorise the arrangement of passages and trust his companions. He eventually escaped and returned to Tiza.

The ordeal did not cure Khonsu of curiosity, but it changed the way he approached his research. He now pays closer attention to those who live beside ancient monuments, knowing that a newly discovered map may lead not only to knowledge, but also to another person's prison, a war or a duty long forgotten. His work remains an invitation into the past—accompanied by an honest warning of the journey's cost.
