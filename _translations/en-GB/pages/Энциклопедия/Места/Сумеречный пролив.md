# Twilight Strait

![[Assets/Images/Twilight_Strait.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Twilight Strait** connects the [[Закатное море|Sunset Sea]] with the [[Хтоническое море|Chthonic Sea]]. The [[Полуостров Таллас|Tallas Peninsula]] forms its northern shore and [[Гилас|Gilas]] its southern one. It is the shortest sea route between western [[Кельн|Keln]] and [[Архея|Archaea]], and so a considerable share of the trade between the two continents passes through this narrow reach of water.

Currents meet at an angle within the strait and change with the tide. With a fair wind, a vessel may pass swiftly; one error in the reckoning, however, can press it against the rocks or sweep it back into the open sea. Captains prefer to take on local pilots who know the hour at which the water uncovers a safe channel between the shoals.

[[Гилас|Gilas]] and [[Талассия|Thalassia]] dispute control of the passage. Both realms levy duties, escort merchant vessels and suspect the other of raising hidden fortifications. Traders must carry several sets of permits and watch carefully to see whose ship first appears upon the horizon.

The strait takes its name from the long shadows cast across the water by its shores at evening. In clear weather the sunset illumines the sails even as it conceals the coastal rocks, turning the loveliest hour of the voyage into its most dangerous. Wrecks from many ages lie upon the seabed, and storms sometimes cast cargo onto the Tallas shore that left Gilas a generation before.

To an adventurer, the Twilight Strait is a place where commerce readily becomes politics. One may be hired to escort a ship, recover a sunken cargo, carry a fugitive across a maritime border—or be caught between two patrols, each convinced that it alone embodies the law.
