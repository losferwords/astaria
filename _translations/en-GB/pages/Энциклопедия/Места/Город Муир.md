# Muir

![[Assets/Images/Muir_City.jpg]]

## Main text

**Muir** is the seaward capital of [[Иомар|Iomar]], at the mouth of the [[Река Мураин|Murain]] on the southern coast of the [[Даирские луга|Dair Meadows]]. It was the first settlement founded by Dair migrants on the shores of [[Кельн|Keln]], and its old quarters are a living memory of the age when the sea still seemed the only road home.

Stone quays receive fishing boats, merchant vessels and river barges. Its markets sell honey from [[Деревня Руше|Ruche]], dried herbs, wool, carved charms and timber gathered with the Druids' permission. Streets near the harbour are noisy and close, but a few blocks inland houses already stand amid gardens, and the branches of old trees are deliberately left above their roofs.

For foreigners, Muir is often their sole introduction to Iomar. One may make a trading compact, find a guide or secure leave to travel into [[Лес Иомар|Iomar Forest]], but the country's greatest decisions are not made in palace chambers. Ruler [[Аластриона Растущая|Alastriona the Growing]] lives in the city, yet her authority is bound to the [[Круг Друидов|Circle of Druids]]; even the city council must heed what the forest temples say.

Muir is called Iomar's outward face: open, talkative and used to foreign coin. An attentive guest, however, notices that beneath the noise of the port the city lives by forest rules. Before major building begins, people consult not only masters but also keepers of the land, and the spring flood of the Murain is greeted as the return of an old relative rather than a disaster to be subdued.
