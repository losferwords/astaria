# Blood Blossom Valley

![[Assets/Images/Blood_Blossom_Valley.jpg]]

**Blood Blossom Valley** follows the [[Река Улын-Гол|Ulyn-Gol]] between the [[Горный хребет Зверя|Beast Range]] and [[Горный хребет Шафар|Shafar Mountains]]. Its narrow route joins Wildlands to [[Империя Ланг-Ан|the Lang-An Empire]], making it a trade road and frontier battlefield for centuries.

Its name predates Avgar–Dju wars. Red cedrela grows by the river: in spring its young leaves are deep crimson, and rain makes the bark seem wet with blood. Later history gave the ancient name a darker meaning.

The timber is strong, fragrant and valued on both sides. Dju demand old groves and selective felling be preserved; northern communities call the forest the lawful prize of whoever can hold it. Old road stones, graves and caches remain, while floods sometimes reveal weapons and ornaments from earlier ages.

The principal passage is closed by [[Гарнизон Пасть Дракона|Dragon's Maw]]. A traveller crosses quickly only when the border is calm; otherwise caravans await leave, hire guides or risk mountain trails where red leaves conceal tracks as well as snow.
