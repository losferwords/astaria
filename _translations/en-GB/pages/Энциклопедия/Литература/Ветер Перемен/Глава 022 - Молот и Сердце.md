# Chapter 022 — Hammer and Heart

*[[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], 104 NE, spring*

In [[Город Бахара|Bahara]], possessions vanished as readily as coins in a harbour crowd. [[Урист Китин|Urist's]] hammer was stolen, and this was no ordinary theft, but something near sacrilege: Deathbringer had become part of his name, his renown and his stubborn Avgar soul.

The trail led to a nereid named Nisa. [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] managed to overtake the thief and recover the hammer, but the victory was incomplete. As one precious object returned to its owner, another slipped from their grasp: the [[Сердце Огня|Heart of Fire]] vanished.

The city pursued a hunt of its own. [[Айсан Баят|Aysan Bayat]] tracked nereids with such determination that every mark upon the paving seemed a personal affront. Urist, meanwhile, encountered the memory of old debts: [[Унгол Баят|Ungol Bayat]] reminded him of money owed for the building of a forge, and the conversation carried the scent not only of silver, but of trouble to come.

Every path converged upon a warehouse, where the travellers met [[Неарх Мелит|Nearch Melit]]. A knot of stolen relics, naval blockade and foreign debts was drawing tight around Bahara. To untangle it peacefully would require a miracle, and miracles in Wildlands seldom came without blood.
