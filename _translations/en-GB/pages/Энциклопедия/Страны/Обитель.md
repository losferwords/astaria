## A country awakening

The goddess of Obitelj is [[Велисса|Velissa]], lady of earth, love and beauty. For a long while she lay in a strange sleep whose cause remains unknown. She was awakened only recently, when grave events overtook her country.

Obitelj's king, [[Ивар Свирепый|Ivar the Fierce]], had been driven mad by power and by delight in the strength of his great army. He began a campaign of conquest westwards into friendly [[Вактар-Йорден|Vaktar-Jorden]], and southwards onto [[Туманный остров|Mist Island]], where the Edzo of [[Амато|Amato]] had lived in peace. The struggle might have become a long and bloody war had Velissa not intervened.

From among the [[Венды|Wends]] who opposed Ivar's tyranny, the goddess created Imithei of her own and named them [[Страж|Guardians]]. Velissa showed these new warriors the path of love and granted them union with their native earth, together with the courage to drive a mad king from his throne.

After months of bitter fighting, the Guardians forced Ivar out of Obitelj's capital, [[Город Мирград|Mirgrad]]. When the time came to choose a new sovereign, the people turned to Velissa. To universal astonishment, she named as queen a slight nine-year-old girl from an ordinary family of ploughmen.

The Guardians have fulfilled the purpose for which they first arose, but their service has not ended. They now help their country heal from the destruction and grief left by Ivar, guarding a fragile peace while Obitelj learns to make a home of itself once more.
