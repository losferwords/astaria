# Great Forge

![[Assets/Images/Great_Forge.jpg]]

The **Great Forge** is a mysterious structure raised by the Smith-God [[Хангор|Hangor]] near [[Город Даркхан|Darkhan]]. More than seven hundred years, uncounted materials and builders' lives went into it. It became a cornerstone for the [[Авгарцы|Avgars]], [[Дикоземье|Wildlands]] and [[Мектиг|Mektigs]], who aided the god in return for new powers.

Around 30 NE, Hangor completed the Forge and released every worker to their families. Neither the god nor the result of his work has been seen since. The entrance was closed to everyone, leaving the Avgars alone after centuries of labour for their beloved god.

From outside it appears as an enormous fortress cut into rock, with elaborate metal gates. Anyone may come to admire it, but no one has entered since the work was finished.
