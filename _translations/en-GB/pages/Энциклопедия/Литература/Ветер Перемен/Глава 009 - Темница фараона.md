# Chapter 009 — The Pharaoh's Prison

*Amon-Astat, 103 NE, autumn*

Everything went wrong inside [[Город Тиза|Tiza's]] prison. The travellers found [[Рамат-Хат-Су|Ramat-Hat-Su]], but also the guards, and every clash in the pharaoh's stone corridors became a trap. Narrow passages denied manoeuvre, torches tore the dark into uneven light, and every shout summoned more footsteps.

[[Шани-Хат-Су|Shani-Hat-Su]] appeared during the battle. Light born of prayer and will blinded everyone—guards and rescuers alike. In that white flash, ally and enemy became indistinguishable. Confused and furious, [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]] wounded Ramat; Shani rushed to pull her brother from the chaos.

Their unexpected guide was [[Диомед Ахейский|Diomedes the Achaean]]. The old curator who had once slipped prisoners a key again appeared at the moment of escape, leading the others by hand from the prison while Shani kept Ramat upright.

Later, in safety, they learnt Shani and Diomedes had already planned Ramat's escape. The travellers' intervention shattered their delicate design. Ramat was free but wounded, with almost no time left.

He told them of the mission he could no longer undertake. [[Жаждущие|The Thirsty]] sought volunteers to enter the [[Пепельные земли|Ashlands]] and recover the [[Сердце Огня|Heart of Fire]]. Heat, lava, poisoned air and the land itself killed travellers there. Survival required preparation and the knowledge of people accustomed to living beside death.

The southern [[Ваку|Vaku]] possessed such knowledge. Ramat had tried to contact a shaman whose medicines and rites might make the journey possible. The meeting ended in a raid: Ramat was captured and the woman Danis had seen in the market was run through with a spear. Her recovery confirmed that the power needed for the expedition lay somewhere near her.

Ramat's wound rapidly festered. Shani asked [[Блейн из Руше|Blaine of Ruche]] to gather khargal leaves in [[Закатная долина|Sunset Valley]], a medicinal shrub used against infection, suppuration and blindness. Saving Ramat became the first step upon a new road.

Gilas lay behind, but freedom brought no peace. The travellers now had to choose: abandon the Thirsty to an impossible mission, or go where even sunlight seemed less illumination than sentence.
