# Nid River

![[Assets/Images/Nid_River.jpg]]

## Main text

The **Nid** is a river in central [[Кельн|Keln]], flowing from [[Озеро Хорниндаль|Lake Hornindal]] into the [[Белый пролив|White Strait]]. [[Вактар-Йорден|Vaktar-Jorden's]] capital, [[Город Трелла|Trella]], stands at its source, making the river the chief route between the inland lake country and the sea.

In summer, the Nid carries timber, fish, furs and stone. Its current is swift but deep enough for shallow-draught boats. Tall poles carved with runes stand at dangerous bends; they do not replace a guide, but show where a stone ridge lies hidden under the water.

In winter, the river freezes unevenly. Sledge roads are laid across calm reaches, while ice remains thin near rapids even in hard frost. Local people test the way with long spears and mark danger with branches. Moving such a marker as a joke is treated as a crime against the whole community.

Spring ice-break turns the Nid into a thundering belt of water and ice. Trella's people come out to strengthen bridges, clear the channel and rescue boats torn from their moorings. After the flood, driftwood remains on the banks; craftspeople make handles, toys and small sailor's charms from it.

Below the capital lie quiet coves where fish winter and waterfowl gather. Hunting there is restricted by old custom: the Vaktars believe that if one robs the river of life in a place of calm, the sea will collect the debt in the next storm.
