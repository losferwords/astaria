# Ascended

## Main text

The Ascended, also known as the Einherjar, are chosen warriors who have earned the favour of [[Винтра|Wintra]], the great she-dragon and formidable goddess of the [[Вактары|Vaktars]]. Their legends begin in the age when Wintra, defeated in mortal combat, was freed from her prison beneath the ice by settlers in central [[Кельн|Keln]]. In gratitude, she ‘raised’ her rescuers, granting them a measure of her strength and wisdom and creating the first Einherjar.

Gifted with the magic of cold, steadfast devotion to truth and a righteous thirst for vengeance, these warriors became both protectors of the new realm of [[Вактар-Йорден|Vaktar-Jorden]] and living embodiments of their goddess's will. Vaktar warriors spend their lives in glorious battle and distant wandering. Should one die honourably with a pure purpose, Wintra herself may bear that soul to her halls, where the fallen feast, gather new strength and at last return to the world among her chosen warriors.

## Training

The Einherjar master the carving and use of runes, cultivate command over the magic of cold and carry an oath of righteous vengeance against the enemies of Vaktar-Jorden and its dragon goddess. Their presence inspires companions and fills foes with dread, for every Ascended is a living legend who kept a cool head even in the face of death.

## Attributes

The traditional weapons of the Ascended are light fighting and throwing axes of the kind used for hunting in the northern lands. Each is wrought from a rare northern metal, tempered in Wintra's own breath and inscribed with runes that lend it power and precision.
