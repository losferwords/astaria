# Chapter 041 — The Wedding at Ulehey

*[[Дикоземье|Wildlands]], 104 NE, autumn*

On their way back, [[Вигдис Меткая|Vigdis]] and [[Томас Робинсон|Tom]] spotted [[Мэй Ву|Mei Wu]]. Vigdis recognised her as a spy and brought her into camp. Mei was forced to her knees beside the captured archer.

Vigdis's questioning was brutal and precise. The knife at the Dju prisoner's back sank ever deeper until he told them they were merely scouting patrols, with no Avatars leading them. When Mei's turn came, she chose a peculiar weapon: the truth. It surprised even those accustomed to expecting fire from her.

Cries of “Dragon!” interrupted the torture. [[Мэйлун|Meilong]] had appeared in the camp. Mei broke free and rushed to him. An Avgar named Jamsar tried to lure [[Урист Китин|Urist]] away from camp and stab him in the back, but the young dragon stopped the traitor and forced him to kill himself. Later, a second traitor attacked Urist with an axe, and Meilong saved him again.

Urist understood that Mei's dragon had twice preserved his life. He and Mei reconciled, and she joined the company no longer as a secret observer, but as someone determined to protect her country from the nomad invasion.

That night, Urist and Vigdis tasted honey, then Avgar moonshine, and their contentious intimacy ended in a tempestuous night. Tom spoke with the wounded [[Рон Эллиот|Ron]] about [[Мерката|Mercate's]] temple and the powers of the [[Тень|Shadows]], then suffered a grim dream of the goddess and disasters threatening southern Astaria.

In the morning, the force reached [[Деревня Улехей|Ulehey]], Urist's native village. Neglect lay everywhere: the forges were silent and scarcely any people remained, though livestock and crops proved the place still lived. An old man named Bangor said the mine had been abandoned and the men who entered it never returned.

Urist's mother greeted her son in tears and told him that his father too had gone into the mines and vanished. She then asked Urist to don his grandfather's ceremonial dress and join the celebration. He assumed they rejoiced at his return, only to learn the truth: he had long ago been betrothed to [[Ли Сан|Li San]].

The village began the wedding. The bride was brought to the table, while Vigdis watched her with the cold fury of someone who already considered Urist her own.
